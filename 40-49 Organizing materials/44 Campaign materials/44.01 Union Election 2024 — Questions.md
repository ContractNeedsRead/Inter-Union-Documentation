# Union Election 2024 — Questions

## Purpose

A question list for auditing a local union nomination and election against the [local] Constitution and By-Laws and the APWU national Constitution. Used by the complainant in the [local] election challenge (appeal E-24-35) to frame requests for documentation; a template for any member checking whether an election was constitutionally run.

## Content

1. Nomination Meeting Date and Procedures

• Was the nomination meeting held between September 7 and September 14, as outlined in Article V, Section 8(a) of the [local] Constitution and By-Laws?

• If not, was there a vote by the membership to change the meeting date, as required by Article V, Section 8(a)?

• If a vote was held to change the meeting date, was quorum reached during that meeting to make the decision valid, as required by Article 2 of the By-Laws (15 members, including officers)?

• Can the minutes from the meeting where the change was voted on be provided for review?

2. Quorum for Nominations and Elections

• How was quorum verified during the nomination meeting on September 17th, given the rescheduling outside of the assigned dates?

• Can the union provide records or minutes indicating how many active members (those with voice and vote) were present to ensure quorum was reached, as required by Article 2 of the By-Laws (15 members, including officers)?

3. Announcement of Nominations and Elections

• How were union members notified about the upcoming nomination meeting and election?

• Were these notifications distributed according to the rules outlined in Article V, Section 8(a) (meeting notice sent at least 15 days before the nomination meeting)?

• Was the notification for the nomination meeting communicated publicly, such as through the union bulletin board or other standard communication methods (General Membership Rights)?

4. Accessibility of Election Results

• Were members informed that election results were only available on the union website, and were they given instructions on how to register for an account to access this information, as outlined in Article V, Section 9?

• Is there any evidence showing how many members were able to access the website, and why election results were not also posted on the union's public bulletin board (general transparency requirements)?

5. Uncontested Elections

• If the nominated candidates were uncontested, was the proper procedure followed in declaring them elected, as stipulated in Article V, Section 7?

• Were union members made aware that the election results could be reviewed or contested, especially given the limited accessibility of the website?

6. Transparency and Member Participation

• What steps did the union take to ensure that all members were informed and able to participate in the nomination and election process, as required by Article V, Section 8(a)?

• Was there any deliberate effort to limit the number of members who participated in the election by restricting notifications or access to election results?
