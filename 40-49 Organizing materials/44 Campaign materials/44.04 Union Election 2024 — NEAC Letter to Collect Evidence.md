# Union Election 2024 — NEAC Letter to Collect Evidence

## Purpose

The National Election Appeals Committee's acknowledgment of appeal E-24-35 and its evidence demand. It is the definitive checklist of what an election appeal file must contain — useful to any member preparing or defending a local-election challenge.

## Content

# American Postal Workers Union, AFL-CIO  
1300 L Street, NW, Washington, DC 20005  

October 31, 2024  

**Sent First Class Mail and Certified Mail Return Receipt:**   
[complainant]  

**Sent First Class Mail and Certified Mail Return Receipt:** 
Chairperson, Local Election Committee  
[local]  
PO Box 607  
South Easton, MA 02375  

Dear [complainant] and LEC Chairperson:  

The National Election Appeals Committee (NEAC) is in receipt of [complainant]'s election appeal, dated October 29, 2024, and received October 31, 2024, concerning the election of the [local].  

This letter serves as an acknowledgment that the NEAC is in receipt of the above-mentioned correspondence. The appeal has been assigned the following number for tracking purposes: **E-24-35**.  

The NEAC is requesting that the Local Election Committee and the appellant submit any further information to support the appeal, including, but not limited to:  

1. Copy of the Appellant's appeal(s) to the [local]'s Election Committee.  
2. Copy of the [local] Election Committee's denial letter.  
3. Date candidates must have been a member in good standing to run.  
4. Copy of the Election Notice.  
5. Copy of the Nomination Notice.  
6. Copy of the Election Rules.  
7. Copy of the ballot(s).  
8. Date ballot(s) were mailed.  
9. First and last day to request duplicate ballots.  
10. Date and time ballot(s) were picked up.  
11. Date ballots were counted.  
12. Election results, including ballot tally summary.  
13. Date election rules were certified and published.  
14. All other information relied upon by the LEC to deny the appellant's election grievance.  

Please find enclosed a Contact Sheet. Please complete this form in its entirety and return it, along with any additional information requested to the NEAC, no later than **November 15, 2024**.  

Information being sent to the NEAC in response to this letter must be received no later than **November 15, 2024**. If this information is not received by this date, the NEAC will base their decision on the information in the file.  

**IMPORTANT:** Please write "**NEAC E-24-35**" on the top of all further correspondence regarding this appeal as well as on each document submitted to the NEAC.  

The documents will be reviewed to determine if the election appeal is proper before the NEAC, and further correspondence will be forthcoming concerning this issue. Your cooperation in this matter is appreciated. With kind regards, I remain  

Yours in union solidarity,  

**National Election Appeals Committee**  
National Election Appeals Committee  

cc: Members, National Election Appeals Committee  
President, [local]
