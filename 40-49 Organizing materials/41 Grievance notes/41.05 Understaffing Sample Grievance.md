# Maintenance Understaffing — Sample Grievance

## Background

Used when the Postal Service has an approved staffing package and authorized complement and is below that complement. The purpose is to require USPS to staff consistent with its survey and to achieve a monetary remedy for the failure to properly staff.

## Contract basis

- [[APWU Article 19 — Handbooks and Manuals]] — the staffing package is made pursuant to MMO-125-20 and ASM 531.7 (see [[32.15 Maintenance MMOs]] and the MS-63 reference in the source).
- [[APWU §38.4]] — the posting obligation for the positions the authorized complement creates.
- [[APWU Article 32 — Subcontracting]] — cross-reference any related subcontracting grievance.
- Personnel Operations EL-312, Section 211, is the source's cited hiring-register authority.

## Grievance text / suggested language

**Grievance:**

The Postal Service has failed to staff the installation with the proper number of employees as required by the current authorized staffing package. The staffing package performed pursuant to MMO-125-20 and ASM 531.7 requires ____ Maintenance employees. The Service has been below authorized complement since __________ and has not completed required maintenance work.

Inconsistencies with the staffing required by the MMO-125-20 staffing package and bypassed required maintenance include:

**Remedy:**

- Bring staffing to levels consistent with authorized staffing.
- Compensate the APWU bargaining unit employees designated by the Union, at the appropriate rate, for all unworked and bypassed hours as set forth in the current authorized staffing package.

## Notes

- **Documentation for filing:** approved staffing package pursuant to MMO-125-20; management staffing survey made pursuant to MMO 28-97; data relating to bypass of work from eMARS/eCBM; all overtime used by all bypassed work (MARS reports).
- **Documentation for proving:** number of employees below authorized complement (track at all times until staffing reaches complement); all correspondence on Maintenance Staffing to District or Area; all approval/authorization letters; current staffing by occupational group; any related subcontracting grievance, labor/management meeting minutes, and e-mail.
- **On-point awards** (author's citations): Blackwell, Case #C90T-1C-C95006449, AIRS #26601; Benn, Case #I90T-1I-C93036556, AIRS #24351; H1C5LC19345 (apwu.org/node/23610).
- All facilities should reference eWHEP — see [[42.01 Labor Checklist]] for the data-collection workflow that feeds this grievance.
- The source includes a "Proper Completion" variant (grievance over failure to complete the staffing process at all); that variant is [[41.02 Failure to Complete Staffing Package]].
