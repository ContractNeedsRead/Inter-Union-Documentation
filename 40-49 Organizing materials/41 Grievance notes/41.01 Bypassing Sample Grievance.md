# Bypassing Required Preventive Maintenance — Sample Grievance

## Background

When the Service is not keeping mail processing equipment in a safe and proper operating condition by bypassing the preventive maintenance (PM) that its own handbooks require. This grievance is used when PM routes required by the staffing survey are being skipped, producing hazards and mail delay.

## Contract basis

- [[APWU Article 19 — Handbooks and Manuals]] — management must follow its own handbooks, manuals, and published regulations, here MMO-125-20 (the Workhour Estimating Guide) and Management Instruction MI AS-530-90-5 (Preventive Maintenance for Mail Processing Equipment).
- [[32.15 Maintenance MMOs]] — the in-vault note for the maintenance management orders this grievance enforces (MMO-125-20 and related).
- The source text also invokes the Postal Reorganization Act's service mandate as background for the "mission of the organization" language.

## Grievance text / suggested language

**Grievance:**

The Service is bypassing preventive maintenance required by MMO-125-20 and Management Instruction MI AS-530-90-5. This work is required to keep the equipment in a safe and proper operating order. Failure to perform preventive maintenance results in hazards and mail delay and undermines the mission of the organization pursuant to the Postal Reorganization Act and other applicable laws, regulations, directives, and manuals.

**Remedy:**

- Cease and desist all unnecessary bypassing of preventive maintenance.
- Make all necessary staffing adjustments so that PMs, required by the staffing survey, are performed.
- Pay, to the employee named by the Union, all bypassed preventive maintenance hours.

## Notes

- Pair with the documentation request: pull the PM Route Bypass reports from eMARS (see [[42.02 eMARS User Guide]]) and the staffing survey before filing.
- The author's source cites MMO-125-20 (current MMO for the workhour estimating guide) and MI AS-530-90-5, and references the Administrative Support Manual 531.313 record-keeping requirement — see the staffing strategy guide ([[42.05 Maintenance Staffing Strategy and Grievance Guide]]) for the fuller "Bypassing" framework.
- Remedy style follows the staffing-grievance pattern: stop the practice, fix the staffing, and pay the bypassed hours to a union-named employee.
