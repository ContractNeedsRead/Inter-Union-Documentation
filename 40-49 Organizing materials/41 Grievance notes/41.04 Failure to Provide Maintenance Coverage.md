# Failure to Provide Maintenance Coverage on Equipment During Operating Tours — Sample Grievance

## Background

Used when the Postal Service unnecessarily resorts to "risk management" to save money — operating mail processing equipment without an appropriate mechanic or technician present. The purpose is to ensure that an appropriate mechanic or technician is present when mail processing equipment is operating, even if overtime is required.

## Contract basis

- [[APWU Article 19 — Handbooks and Manuals]] — the USPS Headquarters Directive, Maintenance Coverage, November 29, 1983, is an Article 19 document.
- [[APWU Article 15 — Grievance-Arbitration Procedure]] — the procedural track for the grievance.
- [[APWU §8.4]] — overtime: the violation is operating without coverage in an attempt to avoid overtime, and the remedy is overtime-rate pay.
- The binding Step 4 settlement, case number H8T-4K-C 35791, is the contractual anchor for the directive's enforceability.

## Grievance text / suggested language

**Grievance:**

As per the Step 4 settlement to case number H8T-4K-C 35791, the Postal Service issued the November 29, 1983 directive titled "Maintenance Coverage". That settlement and directive requires the Postal Service to staff properly and make every effort in short-term situations, including the use of overtime, to provide proper maintenance coverage for operating equipment.

On ______________, the Postal Service operated the equipment without proper maintenance coverage and without compliance with the Management directive and Step 4 settlement. The Employer did so in an attempt to avoid overtime. These actions were in violation of the directive and the Step 4 settlement.

**Remedy:**

- Pay to the applicable employees who should have been brought in to provide coverage at the appropriate pay at the overtime rate, including out of schedule overtime or premium, for all hours the Postal Service operated the equipment without proper coverage.

## Notes

- Identify the equipment, tour, and hours operated without coverage; cross-check eMARS dispatch and schedule records (see [[42.02 eMARS User Guide]]).
- Cite the Step 4 settlement number verbatim in the grievance — it is the enforcement hook for the 1983 directive.
- This is the "no coverage on an operating tour" category of the staffing framework in [[42.05 Maintenance Staffing Strategy and Grievance Guide]].
