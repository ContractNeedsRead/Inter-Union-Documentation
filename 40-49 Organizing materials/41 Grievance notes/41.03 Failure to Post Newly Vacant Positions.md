# Failure to Post and Fill Newly Vacant Positions — Sample Grievance

## Background

Used when the Postal Service fails to post and fill positions authorized by its staffing survey — either positions newly created by a recently approved survey or existing positions. The goal is to get all authorized positions posted within 30 days of approval and filled within another 29 days, and to win out-of-schedule premium, higher-level pay, and retroactive promotions for employees kept out of the positions to which they were contractually entitled.

## Contract basis

- [[APWU Article 38 — Maintenance Craft]] — Sections 4 and 5 govern posting and filling of maintenance vacancies.
- [[APWU §38.4]] and [[APWU §38.5]] — the posting and selection-method provisions the grievance enforces.
- [[25.02 Posting & Bidding]] — the cross-craft hub for the posting/bidding framework.
- The author's source also files this under MMO-125-20 (the staffing survey that authorizes the positions), [[APWU §8.4]] (overtime/out-of-schedule premium), ELM 434.6, and EL 401 Section D.

## Grievance text / suggested language

**Grievance:**

The Postal Service has failed to post and fill newly created or existing vacancies consistent with Article 38 Sections 4 and 5. Positions authorized by an approved staffing survey are newly created positions and must be posted and filled pursuant to Article 38.

The following positions were not posted and filled pursuant to the above.

**Remedy:**

- Post and fill all newly created and existing positions from PARS, PERS, transfer requests, and in-service registers that were in place at the time the position(s) should have been posted.
- Pay out of schedule premium or overtime to all employees who were delayed in being placed into these positions and all employees who moved to different days and hours related to the posting of these positions (i.e., all residual movement).
- Make all employees whole, including appropriate higher-level pay and retroactive promotions.

## Notes

- **On-point arbitration award** (author's citation): Blackwell, Case #C90T-1C-C95007424, AIRS #26341.
- List every affected position with the date it should have been posted; the 30-day posting / 29-day filling clocks in [[APWU §38.4]] run from survey approval.
- For the staffing-survey groundwork that creates the vacancy, see [[42.05 Maintenance Staffing Strategy and Grievance Guide]].
