# How to Collect WebEOR Data

## Purpose

The week-by-week WebEOR export workflow that feeds the staffing investigation: 52 weeks of data, exported, organized, and backed up off the USPS systems.

## Content

### Complete workflow

1. Export 52 weeks of data.
2. Organize the files in your project directory.
3. Email copies to your personal account for backup.

### Exporting (once per week, 52 times)

- Open **Microsoft Edge**, type `webeor` in the address bar, press Enter.
- Select your facility; click the `>>` button **7 times** (sets the 7-day range).
- Click **Refresh Data → CSV**.
- In the save dialog, save into your project directory's `WebEOR Data/` folder (a consistent local path).

### Organizing and backing up

- Keep the folder structure stable so the staffing-package comparison can be rebuilt at any time.
- Email each export to a **personal** account — never rely on USPS systems as the only copy.
