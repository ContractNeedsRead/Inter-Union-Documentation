# Harassment, EEO & OWCP — Procedure Kit

## Purpose

The reusable procedural skeleton of a harassment/retaliation case with an OWCP occupational-disease claim running alongside — four tracks (grievance, safety, EEO, OWCP), the evidence discipline, and the form/template set. Reconstructed from a real, fully-documented case; every personal detail has been replaced with [brackets].

## The four tracks

### 1. Grievance track

- File at the earliest step the facts support; a harassment grievance is grievable under [[28.01 Grievance Procedure]] regardless of the discipline track.
- A separate **ELM-violation grievance** (management violated a USPS manual provision) is its own document.
- The discipline-side defenses live in [[24.01 Discipline]] and [[24.02 Due Process]] — every adverse action must meet just cause, and the discussion-before-paper rule applies.

### 2. Safety track

- Every hazard incident gets a PS Form 1767 ([[31.09 ELM 824]]); keep the number sequence — the gaps in sequence are evidence of suppression.
- If the local channel is suppressed: OSHA complaint + the §11(c) retaliation protection ([[34.04 OSH Act 19 29 USC 668]], [[42.07 OSHA Complaint — Citation Map]]).
- Inside USPS: ELM 666.3 is the whistleblower-protection machinery — OIG hotline, preliminary review, and the stay-of-personnel-action ladder (VP Labor Relations within 3 business days, 120-day stays, Administrative Law Judge 45-day stays, and the appeal tie-in) — [[31.12 ELM 666.3 Whistleblower Protection]].
- Participation rights without fear of restraint/interference/coercion/discrimination/reprisal: [[31.13 ELM 814.1 Safety Rights]].

### 3. EEO track

- Harassment on a protected basis has the separate EEO-redress route; record the EEO counselor contact and any PS 2565 response in your chronology.
- The protected-basis anchor statutes: [[34.15 Rehabilitation Act 29 USC 794]] (disability — expressly names the Postal Service) and [[34.16 VEVRAA 38 USC 4212]] (uniformed-service status); the contract's no-discrimination clause is the APWU Article 2 hub.

### 4. OWCP track (occupational disease claim)

Forms, in order:

| # | Form | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | ECOMP registration | ecomp.dol.gov with a **personal** email — never the work email |
| 2 | OSHA-301 | Injury/illness incident report, within 4 calendar days of filing |
| 3 | CA-2 | Notice of Occupational Disease and Claim for Compensation — the primary form |
| 4 | CA-35 series | "Evidence Required in Support of a Claim for Occupational Disease" — the checklist for stress claims |
| 5 | CA-7 / CA-7a | Claim for Compensation / Time Analysis — file if wages were lost (LWOP) |
| 6 | CA-17 | Duty Status Report — work restrictions, completed by the treating professional |
| 7 | CA-20 | Attending Physician's Report — the medical narrative |

Timeline facts: the agency completes its portion within **10 working days**; you get **at least 60 days** to respond to OWCP requests; file CA-7 promptly if you miss work. The governing statute is [[34.13 FECA 5 USC 8102]]; the regulations are [[34.17 OWCP Regulations 20 CFR 10]] (partial transcription).

## Evidence discipline

- **Chronology:** one dated, continuous narrative — every incident, witness, document, and management response in order.
- **Medical:** records + a causation statement from the treating professional ("in my professional opinion, the diagnosed condition is causally related to the described workplace stressors" — have it written, not assumed).
- **Witnesses:** brief signed statements; ask early, people transfer.
- **Management documents:** emails, 1767s, 3971s, grievance responses, staffing/MMO documents ([[42.06 Requests for Information]]).
- **Regulatory exhibits:** the MMOs and manual sections the other side violated ([[32.17 MMO Rubric Library — Index]], [[31.09 ELM 824]]).
