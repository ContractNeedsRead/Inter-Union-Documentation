# FMLA — Steward Guide

## Purpose

A steward-facing orientation to the Family and Medical Leave Act for APWU-represented postal employees: who is eligible, how much leave exists, how leave may be taken, the serious-health-condition and qualifying-exigency definitions, the certification workflow, and the forms. Built from the statute ([[34.19 FMLA 29 USC 2612]]) and the union's guidance booklet (APWU FMLA Book, June 2016).

## Content

### Eligibility

- Employed by the Postal Service at least **12 months** (not necessarily consecutive), and
- worked at least **1,250 hours** during the 12 months before leave begins, and
- works at a location where the employer employs **50 or more employees within 75 miles**.
- COP, OWCP time, military leave, and court leave count toward the hours/service requirements (per the joint APWU/USPS Q&A).

### Entitlements (29 U.S.C. §2612)

- **12 workweeks** in any 12-month period for: birth and care of a child; adoption or foster placement; care of a spouse, son, daughter, or parent with a serious health condition; the employee's own serious health condition; or a **qualifying exigency** arising from a family member's covered active duty (or call-up notice).
- **26 workweeks** in a single 12-month period for **military caregiver leave** to care for a covered servicemember (spouse, son, daughter, parent, or next of kin); the combined total in that period is capped at 26.
- Leave is **unpaid**, with substitution of accrued paid leave (employee election or employer requirement) per §2612(d); intermittent or reduced-schedule leave is available for medical needs (with a temporary-transfer alternative-position rule for planned treatment).

### Certification workflow

1. Employee requests FMLA leave (or a foreseeable need) — foreseeable leave requires 30 days' notice or "as is practicable."
2. Employer may require certification from the health care provider (employee's own condition, family member's condition, or servicemember care) — APWU Form 1 / Form 2, or the sample forms.
3. Management responds to the request; when approved, leave is designated FMLA-protected and the job (and health benefits) are protected for the entitlement period.
4. On return, the employee is restored to the same or equivalent position.

### The union guidance source (APWU FMLA Book, June 2016, 53 pp.)

Structure: President's letter → **A Guide to the FMLA** (who can use leave; how much; how leave can be taken; when; what constitutes a "serious health condition"; "qualifying exigency"; caring for a covered servicemember; job protection; the request procedure; required documentation; management's response; returning from leave) → **Forms section** with APWU Form 1 and Form 2 plus the sample certification forms.

**FMLA Forms folder (10 sample forms, by name):** Certification of Exigency for Military FMLA; Certification of Employee's Serious Health Condition; Certification of Family Member's Serious Health Condition; Sample Form Child Chronic Asthma; Sample Form Employee Absence Plus Treatment; Sample Form Employee Cancer; Sample Form Employee Diabetes; Sample Form Employee Hospital Stay; Sample Form Employee Migraine; Sample Form Employee Musculoskeletal; Sample Form Employee Permanent Long Term Care; Sample Form Employee Pregnancy; Sample Form Multiple Treatments; Sample Form Spouse Absence Plus Treatment; Sample Form Spouse Hospital Stay; Sample Form Spouse Permanent Long Term Care; Sample Form Spouse Pregnancy.
