# PSE Issues (APWU Pre-Convention Workshop 2022)

## Purpose

The APWU 2022 Pre-Convention Workshop "PSE Issues" is the union's own rights summary for Postal Support Employees in the Clerk Craft: appointment and reappointment rules, the PSE caps, hours and overtime, pay, leave, holidays, discipline, grievance access, and conversion to career. Summarized here by subject for rank-and-file use. The companion Richardson-Brooks Q&A (2021–2024 National Agreement) is scanned with no text layer and is not transcribed.

## Content

### Appointment and reappointment (Article 7.1; MOU Appendix A)

PSEs are hired from an appropriate register for terms not to exceed **360 calendar days** per appointment, based on operational need, and are placed on the rolls in their hiring installation by date and test score (Article 37.2.D.4 seniority tie-breakers apply only to same-date, same-score hires). On reappointment, PSEs have a **break in service of at least 5 days**; the break need not occur on the 360th day. As long as breaks do not exceed 5 days, PSEs become eligible for FEHB health insurance after completing a 360-day term — and PSEs are now eligible for USPS health insurance on day one. There is no intent to separate a PSE more than 5 days to defeat health benefits. Working a PSE continuously beyond the 360-day appointment without the 5-day break is a contract violation — **do not ask to have the PSE converted**; the proper remedy is to compensate the bargaining unit for all hours the PSE worked in violation of the contract. The same remedy logic applies to any clerk craft work performed in violation of the CBA.

### The PSE caps (Article 7.1.B.3)

Two Clerk Craft caps: the **District Cap** (20% of career Mail Processing (F1) clerk craft employees in the District, except in accounting periods 3 and 4; 20% of career Retail/Customer Service (F4) clerk craft employees — usable in F1 without counting against the F1 cap; monitored and enforced at HQ level) and the **Installation Window Cap** (10% of career window clerks in Level 22-and-above offices; 20% in Level 21-and-below; the 0.5-and-above rounding rule applies; monitored and enforced locally). The USPS report includes all PSE SSDAs (D/A 81-4 in F4); the D/A must be corrected to 81-3 when a PSE is not window trained and does not work the window, and to 81-4 when an 81-3 PSE works the window; the report includes borrowed (HUB) PSEs and must be monitored locally. The PSA agreement adds 10 PSEs per each of the 46 Package Support Annexes (460 additional PSEs, assignable anywhere in the installation); Grade 6 PSEs assigned to a Level 4 RMPO do not count against the cap (with a 12-per-district exception in the 2021 CBA; under the 2021 CBA Level 4 RMPO PSEs are not counted toward the district cap at all). In addition to the accounting periods 3–4 exception, there are 4 weeks preceding AP 3 in which the cap increases 1% per week (1% → 2% → 3% → 4%) for both F1 and F4. When PSE window hours demonstrate the need for a full-time preferred duty assignment, the assignment is posted for bid within the section — but not when the PSE merely covers an already established duty assignment (temporarily vacant, long-term sick leave, etc.).

### Hours of work and overtime (Article 8)

- The PSE service week is a calendar week beginning at 12:01 a.m. Saturday and ending at 12 midnight Friday; the service day is the calendar day on which the majority of work is scheduled. These are the only items in Article 8.2.
- All PSEs will be offered a minimum of one nonscheduled day per service week (exceptions during the peak season).
- PSEs cannot be worked over 8 hours in a service day or 40 hours in a service week except as provided in Articles 8.4.C, 8.4.E, and 8.4.F of the CBA; PSEs may be required to work overtime on a nonscheduled day only when all available employees on the appropriate Overtime Desired List have worked up to 12 hours in a day or 60 in a week.
- PSE penalty overtime and the December rules follow the Article 8.4 structure (see [[APWU §8.4]]).

### Annual leave and leave rules (MOU page 288–289; JCIM Article 10)

- **Accrual:** in all Level 4 RMPOs, upon completion of two 360-day appointments and immediately upon reappointment thereafter, PSEs earn one additional hour of leave per pay period.
- **Credit at beginning of appointments:** in all non-POStPlan offices (including APOs), upon completion of an initial 360-day appointment and immediately upon reappointment thereafter, PSEs are advanced **40 hours of annual leave** (prorated to the end of the 360-day term on initial implementation). In all Level 4 RMPOs the same 40-hour advance applies after **two** 360-day appointments.
- PSEs may apply for LWOP without exhausting their annual leave; approval/disapproval is at management's discretion per ELM 514.22.
- PSEs have the same obligation as career employees to maintain their assigned schedule and avoid unscheduled absences, and must provide acceptable evidence for absences when required.
- **FMLA:** PSEs with 12 accumulated months of USPS employment over the past 7 years and 1,250 hours in the 12 months immediately preceding the leave are eligible for FMLA-protected leave.
- **Military leave:** non-career employees such as PSEs may be absent for military leave but are not eligible for paid military leave (ELM 517.22).
- **Wounded Warrior Leave:** PSEs with a single or combined service-connected disability rating of 30% or more are credited **104 hours of WWL** retroactive to their EOD date or the current leave year, and 104 hours on the first day of each leave year for use through the last day of the leave year (no carry-over).

### Holidays (Article 11; MOU Article 11, Holidays)

PSEs receive holiday leave pay for the 6 major holidays — New Year's Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving Day, Christmas Day — subject to the Article 11.2 eligibility guidelines. Pay: **8 hours** in 200-man-year offices, **4 hours** in POStPlan offices, **6 hours** in all other offices. PSEs who work a holiday may elect to have their annual leave balance credited with 4, 6, or 8 hours (as applicable) in lieu of holiday leave pay. PSEs are scheduled to work a holiday or designated holiday **after** all full-time volunteers are scheduled; PSEs are scheduled to the extent possible **before** any full-time volunteers or non-volunteers work a nonscheduled day or any full-time non-volunteers are required to work their holiday; a locally negotiated pecking order prevails.

### Excessing, light duty, and grievance access (Articles 12–15)

- In addition to residual duty assignments PSEs have opted into, the parties identify other duty assignments occupied by PSEs to minimize the impact of excessing on full-time career employees (Article 12.5.B.2; JCIM Art. 12, page 11).
- Article 13 does not apply to PSEs, but it does not prohibit assigning PSEs to light duty; non-APWU bargaining unit employees on light/limited duty or rehab assignments in an APWU craft are not counted as career employees for PSE cap purposes.
- **Article 15 applies to PSEs** for those provisions the parties have agreed apply to them; nothing waives the employer's NLRA obligations; PSEs will not be discharged for exercising grievance-arbitration rights.
- The separation of PSEs at the end of a 360-day term, and the decision not to reappoint, are not grievable. PSEs may be separated for lack of work at any time; such separation is not grievable **except where alleged to be pretextual**. PSEs separated for lack of work before the end of their term get reappointment preference over other applicants for one year.

### Discipline (MOU PSE Discipline 2013; JCIM Article 16)

PSEs may be disciplined or removed within their appointment term for **just cause**, grievable-arbitrable, provided that within the immediately preceding six months the employee completed 90 work days or was employed 120 calendar days (whichever comes first). Removal for cause within the term requires advance written notice of the charges per Article 16. Discipline must be corrective, not punitive — progressive (corrective) discipline applies to PSEs — and PSEs will not be non-scheduled because of misconduct as a substitute for discipline.

### Conversion — filling residual vacancies (2021 CBA pages 430–434; MOU page 432)

After the Postal Service completes steps 1–5 of the residual vacancy pecking order and a duty assignment on eReassign remains a residual vacancy, the **senior PSE within the installation is converted to career status** and placed in the vacancy. Paragraph 6.b also permits reassignment of full- and part-time Clerk Craft employees, limited to the 1-in-4 and 1-in-6 rules; if a residual remains under step 7, Clerk Craft PTFs and PSEs within a 50-mile radius are selected by highest installation standing through the eReassign Canvass posting, with conversion no later than the first day of the third full pay period after the close of the posting cycle or rejection of the offer/request. For the **first full-time opportunity in each block of 4 or 6**, if the PSE's relative standing date within the installation (bid cluster) is older than the date of the transfer request, the PSE is converted — continuous, in blocks of 4 or 6. For F4 residual assignments with window duties, conversion is deferred until the highest-standing PSE is given an opportunity to train and qualify; a PSE who fails window qualification remains a PSE with the same relative standing and serves the 180-day restriction of Article 37.3.F.7.b. When PTF vacancies are created/filled: conversion of Clerk Craft PSEs within the installation by relative standing, transfer of PTF Clerks through regular eReassign within 50 miles by seniority, then offers to PTFs/PSEs within 50 miles (highest standing selected), with the same third-pay-period deadline.

### POStPlan restrictions (MOU POStPlan)

PSEs cannot work in Administrative Post Office (APO) Level-18 offices (with exceptions) and cannot be assigned to Level-6 Remotely Managed Post Offices (RMPO) (with exceptions). Level-6 PSEs may work in Level-4 RMPOs at the Level-6 rate without counting against the cap (12-per-district exception noted above), and may be used within the cluster (Level-18 office and RMPOs) to relieve career employees on window duties for short-term absences (AL/SL/LWOP) normally not to exceed 2 pay periods, only after all PTF hours are used.

### Orientation, Article 19, and the 2016 agreement

An orientation program is required for newly hired career employees; Article 19 applies to PSEs for all published regulations; the April 2016 agreement between the parties provides the terms referenced in the deck. PSEs assigned as SSDA (D/A 81-4) have the associated designation rules noted under the caps. The 2014 POStPlan Q&As and the 2015 PSE fingerprint compensation Q&A are reproduced in the deck (PSEs must undergo fingerprinting; the 2015-07-15 Q&A covers compensation).
