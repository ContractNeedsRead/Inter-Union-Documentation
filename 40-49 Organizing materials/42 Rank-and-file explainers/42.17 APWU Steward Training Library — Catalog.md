# 42.17 APWU Steward Training Library — Catalog

The user's `_sources/Steward Training/` archive is the APWU's own training library: convention workshop decks, steward books, discipline and harassment training, the Postal Bulletin archive, and USPS handbooks/manuals/publications. This catalog lists every top-level folder, what it contains, and which vault notes cover the subject — so the rank-and-file material can be found from the vault and the transcribed authorities can be found from the training.

## Steward's Training

The core steward course: `Shop Steward's Training Manual, Revised July 2022.pdf`, the steward training PowerPoint and documents folder, writeable grievance forms, and the COVID-19 OWCP claim guide. → Vault: [[42.04 Shop Steward's Training Manual]]; grievance forms practice: [[28.01 Grievance Procedure]].

## Fighting Discipline

`DISCIPLINE BOOK Revised 2023.pdf` and the Fighting Discipline PowerPoint (2023). → Vault: [[24.01 Discipline]], [[24.02 Due Process]], [[APWU Article 16 — Discipline Procedure]]; NPMHU parallel: [[NPMHU Article 16 — Discipline Procedure]].

## Family and Medical Leave Act

`APWU FMLA Book.pdf`, `FMLA Questions and Answers.pdf`, and the FMLA forms folder. → Vault: [[23.01 Leave]]; the ELM leave core is [[31.07 ELM 510 Leave]] (the FMLA/ELM 515 full text is a gap).

## Retirement Planning Workshop

`Retirement Planning August 12 2022.pdf` and the planning documents folder. → Vault: [[34.09 CSRS 5 USC 8336]], [[34.10 FERS 5 USC 8412]], [[21.01 Compensation]].

## PSE Issues

`PSE Issues Power Point.pdf`, the Documents for PSE Issues folder, and the 2021–2024 National Agreement Q&A by Richardson-Brooks (scanned; no text layer). → Vault: [[42.20 PSE Issues]], [[APWU Article 7 — Employee Classifications]], [[15.02 APWU MOUs]].

## Large Office Issues Workshop

Function 1 documents and the RI-399 documents folders. → Vault: no dedicated note yet; the RI-399/craft-designation family is covered on the NPMHU side by [[15.04 NPMHU MOUs]] ("Regional Instruction 399", "Mail Processing Work Assignment Guidelines").

## Protecting Clerk Work in Smaller Offices - Function 4

`Protecting Clerk Work in Smaller Offices - Function 4.pdf` and the 2022 small-office class documents folder. → Vault: the craft-jurisdiction provisions [[APWU §1.6]] and [[APWU §37.3]]; NPMHU parallels in [[NPMHU §1.6]].

## Article 37.3.A.1 and MDAT Training

`Article 37 Duty Assignments and MDAT PP 070822.pdf`, the Clerk Craft Residual Vacancy MOU Q&As, Reversion or Abolishment, the MDAT tutorial, and the duty-assignment documents folder. → Vault: [[APWU §37.3]], [[APWU §12.5]]; NPMHU side: [[NPMHU §12.3]] and the "Filling of Residual Vacancies" MOU in [[15.04 NPMHU MOUs]].

## Maintenance Article 12 Excessing Guide Workshop

`Article 12 Maintenance Basics PowerPoint.pdf`, `Article 12 Maintenance Guide PowerPoint.pdf`, and the guide documents folder. → Vault: [[APWU §12.5]], [[25.03 Reassignment & Transfer]]; NPMHU side: [[NPMHU §12.6]] and the excessing MOUs in [[15.04 NPMHU MOUs]].

## Maintenance MS 1 Handbook TL 6 Training

`MS-1 Training PowerPoint.pdf` and the MS-1 reference material folder (MS-1 itself is not supplied — a plain-text gap noted in [[32.08 MS-47 Housekeeping]]). → Vault: [[APWU Article 38 — Maintenance Craft]], [[32.13 MS-63 Maintenance Management]], [[32.15 Maintenance MMOs]].

## Motor Vehicle Service Division Workshop

Article 32 transportation subcontracting, DOT licensing and testing, MAXIMIZATION full-time flexible, MVS annual bidding, and VMF subcontracting decks. → Vault: [[APWU §32.1]], [[29.02 Subcontracting]]; NPMHU side: [[NPMHU §32.1]].

## Innovative Organizing Training

2022 organizing journal, organization department deck, welcome booklet, PSE and career benefits fliers, member applications, and more. → Vault: no dedicated organizing note yet; membership/checkoff law: [[APWU §17.2]] and [[APWU Article 17 — Representation]].

## Leadership Development Training

`Leadership Development Book, Revised 2022.pdf`, volunteer application, and weekly plan. → Vault: no dedicated note yet.

## Labor Law Fundamentals Workshop

`Labor Law.pdf` (the Pre-Convention NLRB primer). → Vault: [[42.18 Labor Law Fundamentals]], [[34.03 NLRA 8(d) 29 USC 158]], [[27.01 Union Rights]], [[27.02 Representation]].

## Workplace Harassment

`USPS Workplace-Harassment-Powerpoint.pdf`, `Improving Your Workplace Environment.pdf` (APWU brochure), Steps-for-Managers (Sept 2009), and PS Form 1767. → Vault: [[42.19 Workplace Harassment Training]], [[42.08 Harassment, EEO & OWCP — Procedure Kit]], [[32.19 EL-603 EEO Complaint Processing]], [[APWU Article 2 — Nondiscrimination and Civil Rights]].

## Window Training Guides

Lead Sales and Service Associate workbooks/guides (on-the-job training, administrator, participant, student, facilitator), PO-209 Retail Operations Handbook, and related window-training documents. → Vault: no dedicated note yet; window duties relate to [[APWU §37.3]] and the craft-jurisdiction provisions.
