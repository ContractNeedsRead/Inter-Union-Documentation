# OSHA Complaint — Citation Map

## Purpose

A reusable template for mapping workplace safety violations to the exact OSHA standard each one breaches — the skeleton of a complaint or whistleblower case. Fill the incident column with your own facts; the citations and quoted standard language are the fixed part.

## Why this matters across crafts

Every contract's safety article ([[26.01 Safety & Health]]) obligates management to safe conditions, but the enforcement teeth are statutory: OSHA standards bind the Postal Service directly, and the OSH Act's whistleblower provision protects the employee who refuses unsafe work or reports it. A case built as incident → citation → quoted standard is the form both OSHA and arbitrators can process.

## Citation map

| Incident pattern | Citation | Standard language (quoted) |
|---|---|---|
| Management refuses to allow a documented lockout/tagout procedure | 29 CFR 1910.147(c)(4)(i) | "Procedures shall be developed, documented and utilized for the control of potentially hazardous energy…" |
| A stale energy-control procedure offered as current | 1910.147(c)(4)(ii) | "The procedures shall clearly and specifically outline the scope, purpose, authorization, rules, and techniques to be utilized…" |
| No annual periodic inspection of energy control procedures | 1910.147(c)(6)(i) | "The employer shall conduct a periodic inspection of the energy control procedure at least annually…" |
| No certification of the annual inspections | 1910.147(c)(6)(ii) | "The employer shall certify that the periodic inspections have been performed…" |
| Employees not retrained after equipment/procedure changes | 1910.147(c)(7)(ii) | "Retraining shall be provided whenever there is a change in job assignments, a change in machines, equipment or processes…" |
| Supervisor dismisses the written energy-control program | 1910.147(c)(1) | "The employer shall establish a program consisting of energy control procedures…" |
| Work on/near energized circuits without the documented safe-work procedure | 1910.333(b)(2)(iv)(B) | Electrical safety-related work practices — qualified-person procedures for energized work |
| Missing or inadequate protective equipment for electrical work | 1910.335(a)(1)(i) | Safeguards for personnel protection |
| PPE not provided/assessed | 1910.132(a), (d)(1) | "The employer shall assess the workplace to determine if hazards are present…" and provide PPE |
| Injury/illness not reported or recorded | 1904.39; OSH Act §8(c)(2) | Reporting and recordkeeping duties |
| Adverse action after protected safety activity | OSH Act §11(c)(1) | Whistleblower protection — discrimination for refusing unsafe work or filing a complaint |
| Contradictory orders forcing a choice between safety rules and pay | Constructive-discharge / coercive-retaliation theory (NLRB-OSHA precedent) | Forcing an employee to choose between violating safety rules and losing pay is coercive retaliation |

## Supporting authority

- Lockout/tagout program references in the maintenance world: MMO-120-19 (energy control), MMO-116-19, EL-810 — full PDFs in [[32.17 MMO Rubric Library — Index]].
- The USPS-side hazard channel: PS Form 1767, per [[31.09 ELM 824]].
- Whistleblower protection inside the Postal Service's own manual: [[31.12 ELM 666.3 Whistleblower Protection]] — OIG hotline route, stay machinery, and appeal rights.
- The right to participate without fear of reprisal: [[31.13 ELM 814.1 Safety Rights]].

## How to use with the vault

1. Fill the incident column with facts from your own case — never leave names or facility details in the note.
2. Pair each row with the contemporaneous 1767s ([[31.09 ELM 824]]) and the relevant MMO from [[32.17 MMO Rubric Library — Index]].
3. The retaliation rows (§11(c)) ride the same protection as [[31.12 ELM 666.3 Whistleblower Protection]]; the discipline context is [[24.01 Discipline]].
4. For the companion personal-claim track, see [[42.08 Harassment, EEO & OWCP — Procedure Kit]].
