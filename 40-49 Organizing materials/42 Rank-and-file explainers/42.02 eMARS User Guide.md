# eMARS User Guide

## Purpose

A steward-facing guide to eMARS (eMaintenance Asset Reporting System, emars.usps.gov) — the system where maintenance work assignments, PM routes, work orders, bypasses, and personnel schedules live. It is the data source behind the [[42.01 Labor Checklist]]; the reports it produces are the evidence in staffing and bypass grievances.

### The steward-relevant core (preserved from the source)

**1.0 Login.** Click the eMARS link (http://emars.usps.gov), log in with valid credentials, and select the right site from the dropdown list.

**3.0 Assign Work.** The Assign Work module facilitates daily maintenance workload scheduling and assignment. WORK ORDERS is selected by default with Crew/Tour 001; the Sched Date calendar is the date work will be assigned (defaults to today). After an employee is selected, the upper grid shows all work orders planned under the selected crew and currently available; the lower grid lists work already assigned to the employee for the selected date. Click PREVENTIVE MAINTENANCE to switch to PM work. Sched Date selections more than 5 days old are rejected and reset to the current date. An assigned PM Route or Work Order turns the row light blue; unassigned rows stay white. **Important: ensure all PM Routes and Work Orders in the weekly schedule are assigned.** The employee dropdown's right-hand column shows work status for the day: ON (scheduled to work), OFF (not scheduled), L (leave), T (training) — work can still be assigned to OFF/L/T employees, which is the flexibility used for OT scheduling. Assign/un-assign via the ASSIGN/UN-ASSIGN buttons; COPY and MOVE replicate or transfer assignments between employees and weekdays; the Other Crews tab pulls work orders from other crews; Combine Daily and Combine Senior boxes merge PM routes sharing subsite ID, acronym, work code, frequency, and route numbers (no eCBM routes combine).

**3.3 Scheduling Work Orders.** Click WORK ORDERS; all work orders are listed in the upper grid; the same assign/un-assign/copy/move procedures apply.

**4.0 Reports.** Click Reports for the module behind the Labor Checklist. Contracts (5 categories: Contractor Listing, Contract Listing, Contract Costs, Contract Maint Cost, Transaction Listing); Equipment (Inventory Listing, Equipment History, Informational); Inventory (Issues, Orders, Receipts, Updates, Transaction Summary, Purchases, MISP, Toolbox); Manager; Misc; Personnel (assignments, training, leave, HR data); Prevent Maint (PM route completion, compliance, scheduling); React Maint (reactive calls, response times, troubleshooting); Scheduling (assignments, crew utilization, scheduling compliance); Work Orders (creation, completion, status, costs).

**6.0 Reactive Maintenance.** (Permissions required.) View current calls via Call Finder (open or all); ADD CALL to log a reactive call (locate acronym/equipment number, locate the trouble, SAVE, then dispatch an employee); DISPATCH to add employees; REMOVE to take them off; CLOSE CALL selects the Trouble/Verb/Noun and closes the call. EMPS ON CALL selects who is available for calls (all, by tour, or individual).

**7.0 Completed Actions.** Process Worksheet types the worksheet number or finds it, selects Action and Reason, enters Time, and steps through with Next until the worksheet is complete. +PM adds a PM route to the current worksheet (Type: Daily, Senior, Combined Daily, Combined Senior; select the PM, Action Code, Reason only if the action is Bypassed Route, Actual Time, SAVE). +CM adds a CM work order. Print Daily Entries produces the daily labor-transaction report (PDF).

**8.0 Form 4774** (Equipment Breakdown Investigation Report). Fields with turquoise backgrounds are not editable; complete the breakdown details, investigation findings, and corrective actions, then SAVE.

## How to use with the vault

- Work the [[42.01 Labor Checklist]] pull list against the Reports module: PM Route Listings, Work Load Schedules, Personnel/Training, Work Orders, PM Route Bypass, Reactive Activity, Form 4774 breakdowns, and the EQR Match Report (see Equipment → EOR MATCH REPORT).
- Bypass records (PM routes marked Bypassed in Completed Actions) are the raw material for [[41.01 Bypassing Sample Grievance]]; schedule and dispatch records support [[41.04 Failure to Provide Maintenance Coverage]].
- The underlying maintenance-management handbook is [[32.13 MS-63 Maintenance Management]] (MS-63, Maintenance Management Class A Offices).
- The MMO layer (MMO-125-20 workhour estimating guide) lives at [[32.15 Maintenance MMOs]].
