# 00.01 Conventions & Design

This is the canonical design document for the vault. It adapts the approved build prompt and records every workspace-specific decision made during Phase 0. Anything not stated here defaults to the build prompt; deviations are listed in §10 and logged in [[00.06 Build Log]].

## 1. Mission

Two missions, in order:

1. **Legibility across crafts.** A rank-and-file member of any of the four crafts (NALC, APWU, NRLCA, NPMHU) opens a topic hub and sees, side by side, what each contract says, where they converge and diverge, and which law and arbitration construes each provision. The hubs are the product.
2. **Self-sufficiency.** Every cited passage exists as transcription inside the vault, in markdown, near its interpretive material. A reader never needs to download a PDF or follow a link to read the law. Links to originals are provenance, not the reading path.

## 2. Three orthogonal layers

| Layer | Job | Rules |
|---|---|---|
| Johnny Decimal (JD) | Address book — where a note lives | Containers only: areas, categories, article hubs, topic hubs, source collections. One-level article-folder exception (§4). |
| Typed links | Meaning — how notes relate | Only the edge vocabularies in [[00.03 Relationship types]]. Recorded in frontmatter as forward edges; backlinks are a bonus, never a substitute. |
| Controlled tags | Subject — what a note is about | Only tags in [[00.02 Taxonomy]], hierarchical `topic/…` namespace. A topic never appears in a folder name; a location never appears in a tag; a reference never appears as a JD number. |

## 3. Areas and JD numbering

- Exactly the ten areas from the build prompt (`00-09`, `10-19`, `20-29`, `30-39`, `40-49`, and spare `50-99`). Hard ceiling; no new areas or categories without user approval.
- JD numbers are assigned per [[00.00 JD Index]].
- Article hubs: `11.<NN>` = NALC Article NN, `12.<NN>` = APWU, `13.<NN>` = NRLCA, `14.<NN>` = NPMHU.

## 4. Atomic units

- **An article is a hub.** A **section is a note.** A **subsection** becomes its own note only when it passes the split threshold: independently cited; contains a distinct obligation/rate/timeline/procedure; will be linked from three or more notes; long enough to be painful inside its parent; or has clear cross-contract counterparts. Otherwise it lives as a heading or block reference inside the section note.
- **Article-folder exception (one level):** each article hub lives in a folder named `<JD> Article <N> — <Title>` (e.g., `11.08 Article 8 — Hours of Work`) containing the hub note (same name) and the section notes as un-numbered leaves. Section and subsection notes never get JD numbers.
- Prefer smaller notes over mega-notes. Over-fragmentation is easier to fix than a mega-note.

## 5. The vault is the archive

- Every cited passage is transcribed into markdown in the note that cites it. `## Full text` is the canonical reading surface and is **verbatim** (see [[00.08 Transcription conventions]]).
- External URLs and original files are provenance, never the primary reading path. A note that links to an original but does not contain its text is incomplete.
- **Never invent a citation.** If a section is referenced but the source text is not in the workspace, create a stub with `status: missing-source`, the citation, and a link to the gap report in [[00.05 Workspace Inventory]]. Never fabricate text, page numbers, award numbers, or case citations.

## 6. Provenance block (frontmatter only)

Every transcribed note carries this fixed block. Property names are frozen — no improvised variants.

```yaml
source_file:          # path to the archive copy; resolves relative to the VAULT ROOT (always ../_sources/...)
source_url:           # canonical URL, if any
source_pages:         # page range in the source's own pagination (e.g., "44-45"); for web-born sources: section range
source_locator:       # precise citation (e.g., "§8.5(a)–(c), pp. 44–45")
source_checked:       # date the transcription was checked against the source (YYYY-MM-DD)
transcribed:          # date transcribed (YYYY-MM-DD)
transcription_notes:  # OCR uncertainty, illegibility, amendment flags
```

- The block renders in the properties panel and queries cleanly; it never clutters the reading view.
- **No body-prose provenance.** There are no bare "see the original" sentences and no body links to source files or URLs. There is no `## Source` section. Provenance lives in frontmatter only.
- A `source_file` that does not resolve is an audit failure.

## 7. Enumerated frontmatter values

- `type:` article-hub | section | subsection | topic-hub | elm | handbook | arbitration | statute | case | agency-decision | mou | lmou | interpretive-guide | stub | grievance-note | explainer | meta | index | log | audit | template
- `status:` complete | partial | missing-source | superseded
- `provision_type:` definition | rate | procedure | remedy | right | obligation | timeline
- `relation:` (counterparts) analogous | parallel | divergent | supersedes | unverified. **The string `same` is prohibited.** Counterpart edges are typed judgments, never equivalence. Read both provisions fully before writing the edge; if you cannot, record `relation: unverified` and flag it in the audit.

## 8. References and the ELM

- **References are textual facts:** record only references a reader would actually follow. Boilerplate cross-citations are noise.
- **The ELM is a comparison surface, not neutral ground.** Where the ELM restates contract language: `relation: restates`. Where it diverges: `relation: diverges` with a one-line note describing the divergence. Divergence is a finding, not an error.
- Every `references` target resolves to a note or a `missing-source` stub.

## 9. Hubs

- Hub tables of provisions are populated by Dataview; the **comparison table at the heart of each hub is hand-written**, reads the provisions, and states material differences plainly. It is not generated.
- Hub body order: `## Why this matters across crafts` → `## Provisions by contract` (Dataview) → `## Comparison table` → `## Legal layer` → `## Divergences worth noting` → `## Related topics`. A hub may optionally add `## Primary sources` listing the transcribed notes it aggregates.

## 10. Workspace layout and decisions made in Phase 0

1. **Vault root** is `<workspace>/Vault/` (this folder). **Raw sources** live outside the vault at `<workspace>/_sources/` — a sibling at the same filesystem level. The vault is markdown-only. Rationale: graph, search, and sync all perform better without binaries, and transcription makes the PDF unnecessary for reading.
2. **`source_file` resolution:** the field is always written relative to the vault root, starting `../_sources/…`. (The build prompt's `../../_sources/…` example assumed different nesting; the uniform rule above is the one audited.)
3. **Original filenames kept.** The user's filenames encode the agreement term years (e.g., `2023-2026-NALC-National-Agreement.pdf`), which is strictly more information than the prompt's example convention. Canonical short IDs are maintained in [[00.09 Source archive index]]. ELM chapter files keep their USPS names (`elmc1.pdf` …).
4. **LMOU folder name** is `16 LMOU — local agreements` (en dash). The prompt's literal `/` was avoided because Obsidian wikilinks parse `/` as a path separator, which would corrupt links to notes inside the folder.
5. **Page vs. section markers** (see [[00.08 Transcription conventions]]): page markers `<!-- p.42 -->` for paginated PDFs (contracts, JCAM/JCIM/CIM); section markers `<!-- §432.6 -->` for web-born / manual-format sources (ELM, ASM, POM, and any future HTML/eCFR sources).
6. **NPMHU extraction:** the 2022–2025 NPMHU agreement's text layer is present but reading-order-scrambled and duplicated. Extraction uses coordinate-ordered reconstruction (PyMuPDF) with deduplication — not OCR. Every NPMHU transcription is spot-checked against the rendered page. See [[00.05 Workspace Inventory]] §2.
7. **JCAM extraction:** the 2025 JCAM is a Paper-Capture PDF (scanned pages + OCR text layer). Treat every JCAM transcription as OCR-derived: spot-check each passage against the rendered page and mark uncertainty per [[00.08 Transcription conventions]].
8. **Web-born sources:** `source_pages` is meaningless for HTML/manual sources; use section ranges in `source_locator` and section markers in the body, never page markers.
9. **Extraction tooling:** `tools/` at the workspace root (outside the vault) holds Python helpers for extraction and verification. **Engine policy (established 2026-10-26 after cross-engine audits):** poppler (`pdftotext -layout`) is the primary extraction engine for all sources except the NPMHU agreement, where PyMuPDF coordinate-ordered extraction is primary (poppler's order is scrambled there). Rationale: several files contain text runs that PyMuPDF omits but poppler extracts — NALC §8.4.E–G and §8.5.A (invisible text runs after soft hyphens), ELM Exhibit 434.141a and other tables, JCAM pages. **Every transcribed page passes a cross-engine yield check** (per-page char/word comparison); pages whose engines disagree materially are escalated to render verification by an image-capable reviewer. The vault itself stays markdown-only.

## 11. Schema freeze and scale discipline

- Phase 0 produced the schemas ([[00.04 Templates]]), the taxonomy ([[00.02 Taxonomy]]), and the edge vocabulary ([[00.03 Relationship types]]). **No contract notes are mass-created before the Phase 0 gate is approved.** Re-tagging thousands of notes is the most expensive mistake available.

## 12. Ambiguity handling

When a decision is not covered here: (1) preserve the three-layer separation; (2) prefer the smaller note; (3) prefer transcribing more source text over linking out; (4) record uncertainty explicitly (`status: partial`, `relation: unverified`, `missing-source`, `<!-- OCR uncertain -->`) rather than papering over it; (5) log the decision in [[00.06 Build Log]]; (6) if the decision would change the schema, taxonomy, area structure, or transcription conventions, **stop and ask the user.**
