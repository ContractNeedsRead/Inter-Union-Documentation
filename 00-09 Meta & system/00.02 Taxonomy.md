# 00.02 Taxonomy

The single controlled list of approved tags. Tags are the **subject** layer (see [[00.01 Conventions & Design]] §2). They are Obsidian-native hierarchical tags in the `topic/` namespace. `provision_type` and `status` are frontmatter enums, not tags, and never appear as tags.

Rules:

- A note carries only tags from this list. No ad-hoc tags at scale.
- If a needed tag is missing: add it here in the same session **and** note the addition in [[00.06 Build Log]].
- A provision carries 1–4 tags: the most specific subject plus its ancestors' relevant cross-cut (e.g., `topic/overtime`, `topic/overtime/penalty`, `topic/compensation`).
- Topic hubs query the hub's primary tag **and its descendants**.
- Tag co-membership is never legal sameness. `counterparts.relation` is where judgment lives.

## Separate namespaces (not tags)

- **`provision_type`** — definition | rate | procedure | remedy | right | obligation | timeline
- **`status`** — complete | partial | missing-source | superseded
- **`type`** — article-hub | section | subsection | topic-hub | elm | handbook | arbitration | statute | case | agency-decision | mou | lmou | interpretive-guide | stub | grievance-note | explainer | meta | index | log | audit | template (grievance-note and explainer added 2026-10-26 for areas 41–44 per user instruction — see build log)
