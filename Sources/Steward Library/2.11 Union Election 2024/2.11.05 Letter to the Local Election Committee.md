# E-24-35

**Subject: Formal Request for Validation of Election Procedures and Notification of Election Appeals**

Dear Local Election Committee,

Since the results of the recent election for Local #3844 were never formally posted, I am reaching out directly to request clarification and validation of the election process. I have made election appeals both in this letter and at the October 27th meeting, where I formally requested that the leadership and the LEC members present explain why the posting was never made and why the nomination date was held on the 15th, in contravention of the constitution.

I am formally requesting validation of each procedural component of the election. Below, I have outlined specific concerns regarding potential violations of election processes and member rights. In instances where a violation is not yet certain, I request supporting documentation to determine compliance with constitutional requirements.

---

### 1. **Notice of Election**
   - Members have been denied timely notification of the election if the Notice of Election was not issued or distributed according to union requirements.
   - **Relevant Citations:**
      - **Local Constitution, Article V, Section 9** and **APWU National Constitution, Article 12, Section 1**.
      - **APWU Members’ Bill of Rights, Item 6**: "Every member has the right to participate in the activities of this Union," which includes receiving adequate notice to exercise their voting rights.
   - **Request for Documentation:** Please provide the original election notice, the method of distribution, and the date it was posted or mailed to members.

---

### 2. **Nomination Process and Notice**
   - The nomination process did not follow designated timelines, and members were not informed of amendments in advance, potentially preventing full participation.
   - **Relevant Citations:**
      - **Local Constitution, Article V, Section 8(a)**, mandating that nomination meetings be held from **September 7 through September 14**.
      - **Article VIII, Section 1**, requiring amendments to election dates or processes to have:
         - **A two-thirds vote of present members**,
         - **At least 15 days’ prior written notice** to members before the motion’s final meeting.
      - **APWU Members’ Bill of Rights, Item 3**: "Every member has the right to be heard," which includes being informed of meetings to participate in the nomination process.
   - **Request for Documentation**: 
      - A copy of the Nomination Notice, including original and altered dates.
      - Record of the member vote to change the nomination schedule, including confirmation of a two-thirds majority.
      - Proof that members received 15-day written notice of the amendment meeting.

---

### 3. **Eligibility to Run and Hold Office**
   - If candidates did not meet eligibility standards, members’ rights to vote for qualified candidates may have been infringed.
   - **Relevant Citations:**
      - **APWU National Constitution, Article 10** and **Local Constitution, Article V**, specifying candidates must be members in good standing.
      - **APWU Members’ Bill of Rights, Item 8**: "Every member has the right to support the candidate of his/her choice," which is compromised if candidates do not meet eligibility requirements.
   - **Request for Documentation**: Verification of each candidate’s eligibility status, including membership standing and dues as of the required date.

---

### 4. **Election Rules and Certification**
   - If election rules were not certified and published, members’ rights to understand and participate according to the established guidelines may have been compromised.
   - **Relevant Citations:**
      - **APWU National Constitution, Article 12, Section 1**, requiring certified and published election rules.
      - **APWU Members’ Bill of Rights, Item 5**: "Every member has the right to freedom of the press," which includes the right to access union rules governing fair participation in elections.
   - **Request for Documentation**: Certified Election Rules, including the publication date and methods used to communicate these rules to all members.

---

### 5. **Posting of Election Results**
   - Members’ rights may have been compromised if election results were not posted in an accessible manner, preventing grievances from being filed within the allowed time.
   - **Relevant Citations:**
      - **Local Constitution, Article V, Section 10(a)**, requiring accessible election results postings for 72 hours to allow for grievances.
      - **APWU Members’ Bill of Rights, Item 9**: "Every member has the right to a fair trial, to be represented by an individual of his or her choice, and to proper appeal procedures," which includes timely result postings for grievances.
   - **Request for Documentation**: Confirm the date, time, and methods used to post election results, including locations where results were accessible.

---

### 6. **Grievance Process Availability**
   - If the grievance process was inaccessible, members’ rights to appeal election procedures may have been infringed.
   - **Relevant Citations:**
      - **Local Constitution, Article V, Section 10(a)**, stipulating a 72-hour window for grievances post-results.
      - **APWU Members’ Bill of Rights, Item 9**: "Every member has the right to a fair trial, to be represented by an individual of his or her choice, and to proper appeal procedures," which necessitates an accessible grievance process.
   - **Request for Documentation**: Records of grievances filed within the 72-hour window, including acknowledgment of receipt and responses.

---

I am submitting these requests to confirm the integrity of our election process and to ensure compliance with constitutional requirements. These procedures are essential to maintaining trust in our local elections and preserving the rights of all members as outlined in our union’s governing documents. Your cooperation is essential for establishing clarity and reinforcing confidence among our members.

Thank you for your attention to this matter. Please feel free to contact me if any clarification is needed regarding these requests.

**In solidarity,**
