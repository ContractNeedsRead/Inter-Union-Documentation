**Outline of Formal Complaint Regarding Election Violations – Local **
### I. Introduction
- **Complaint Submitted To:** National Election Appeals Committee
- **Subject of Complaint:** Alleged election and procedural violations impacting the integrity and fairness of the nomination and election process of Local, APWU.
- **Requested Remedy:** Nullification of the current election process and rerun of a fair, constitutionally compliant election.

### II. Violations of Election Posting Requirements -- Area Local Constitution, Article V, Section 10(a)
```
"Any member who feels aggrieved in connection with the conduct of an election of this local shall file their grievance with the election committee within 72 hours after the results of the election have been posted. The grievance shall be in writing and shall contain all the relevant facts on which the appeal is based."
```

   - **Allegation:** Election results were improperly posted on a restricted online section under “Members” > “2024 Election and Appointments,” requiring login, thereby limiting member access and awareness.
   - **Violation Details:**
      - Results were inaccessible to members not previously registered on the members-only portal.
      - Constitution mandates accessible postings for a minimum 72-hour grievance period; this limited access prevented members, particularly non-favored candidates, from timely contesting results.
      - **Supporting Evidence:** Restricted access screenshots showing the delayed visibility of postings (Figures 1–3).

### III. Direct Interference by Union Officials
   - **Alleged Incident Date:** October 27, 2024, Local Meeting.
   - **Constitutional Reference:** APWU National Constitution, Members’ Bill of Rights, Item 8.
     - **Text:** "Every member has the right to support the candidate of his/her choice and to participate in that right with others."
   - **Violation Details:**
      - Clerk Craft Director Tom O’Brien’s dismissive response to a member’s question regarding election grievance accessibility reflects an obstruction of members' rights.
      - O’Brien responded dismissively to concerns about the absence of physical postings, undermining members who rely on such notifications.
   - **Impact:** Reflects an attitude and practice undermining members’ constitutionally guaranteed rights to accessible and fair grievance processes, potentially discouraging members from supporting non-favored candidates.

### IV. Unannounced and Unposted Constitutional Amendments
   - **Violation Date:** August 2024 Membership Meeting.
   - **Constitutional Reference:** Local Constitution, Article VIII, Section 1.
     - **Text:** "The Constitution may be amended by two-thirds vote of those present and voting at any regular meeting, provided that the amendments shall have been submitted to the membership in writing at least thirty (30) days prior to the vote."
   - **Violation Details:**
      - Seven constitutional amendments were passed without prior member notice, preventing any opportunity for review and informed participation.
      - Members were informed post-factum that changes were accessible on the website, yet no documentation was found after thorough search attempts.
      - **Impact:** Violates the requirement for transparent communication on amendments affecting governance and election procedures, limiting members' rights to informed decision-making on impactful constitutional changes.

### V. Unconstitutional Scheduling of Nomination Meeting
   - **Constitutional Reference:** 
      - Local Constitution, Article V, Section 8(a).
        - **Text:** "At this August meeting, the membership shall establish a nomination meeting to be held during the period September 7 through September 14."
      - APWU National Constitution, Article 12, Section 1.
        - **Text:** "The officers of this Union shall be elected by secret ballot by plurality vote…"
   - **Violation Details:**
      - Nomination meeting held on September 15, outside the authorized period of September 7-14, without securing the required two-thirds vote by the membership.
      - Minutes from the September 2024 meeting lack any record of a vote to alter the nomination date.
      - **Statement by Local President:** During the October 27 meeting, President Steve Curreri confirmed this scheduling change but did not produce documented proof of the two-thirds member vote.
      - **Impact:** Violates constitutionally mandated timelines and procedures for election nominations, thereby affecting election validity.

### VI. Retaliatory Actions Against Complainant
   - **Incident Date:** Post-October 27, 2024, Meeting.
   - **Constitutional Reference:** APWU National Constitution, Members’ Bill of Rights, Item 10.
     - **Text:** "Every member has the right to be secure in his or her basic rights without fear of political, economic, physical or psychological intimidation."
   - **Violation Details:**
      - Following the complainant’s questioning of election procedures, he was removed from his position as an Alternate Steward, leaving the Maintenance Craft with limited representation.
      - The acting Maintenance Craft Director, who assumed the role, has minimal records of filed grievances beyond personal overtime bypasses, suggesting potential negligence in member representation.
      - **Impact on Membership:** The retaliatory removal of the Maintenance Craft Director and lack of active representation in the Maintenance Craft undermine the union's commitment to member protection and discourage member participation in union governance.

### VII. Summary of Requested Actions
   - **Remedy Sought:**
      1. Nullify the current election due to procedural non-compliance and constitutional violations.
      2. Ensure a re-run with:
         - Clearly accessible and transparent posting of election results, both physically and digitally.
         - Adherence to mandated timelines and proper member notification for constitutional amendments.
         - Equal opportunity for all members to participate in and contest the election process as stipulated under the Members’ Bill of Rights.
