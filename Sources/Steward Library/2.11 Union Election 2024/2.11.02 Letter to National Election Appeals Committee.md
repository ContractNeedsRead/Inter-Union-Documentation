Subject: Formal Complaint Regarding Election Violations – Local 

Dear National Election Appeals Committee,

I am writing to formally file a complaint regarding the recent nomination and election process conducted by my local union, Local. Multiple procedural violations occurred during this process, violating both the local and national constitutions. These breaches significantly impacted the election’s fairness and transparency, ultimately disadvantaging members and curtailing their right to participate in union governance. Below, I outline the specific violations and request that the election be nullified and re-run.

According to **Article V, Section 10(a)** of the Local Constitution and By-Laws, the union is required to post election results in a manner allowing members 72 hours to file any grievances. However, the results were neither posted in a physical location nor made readily accessible online. Instead, they were posted exclusively in a restricted online section under **“Members” > “2024 Election and Appointments”**, accessible only to those with pre-approved member logins. This placement avoided the public “News” or “News Archives” sections, which require no login and would have been easily accessible to all members. Updating this limited-access page is entirely different from “posting” the results in a location where they would be accessible and visible to the full membership. This choice appears to have been deliberately made to prevent visibility and, consequently, any contestation by members, especially those who may have been candidates not favored by the current leadership team.

At the **October 27th meeting**, I directly questioned **Clerk Craft Director Tom O’Brien**, asking, “How am I supposed to contest the election if the results weren’t posted?” His response was dismissive; he insisted that grievances had to be filed within 72 hours of the results posting, and when I emphasized the importance of physical postings, he replied that it was “too bad.” This attitude reflects a disregard for members who rely on physical notices and have a legitimate expectation of accessible communication. Other officers also argued that members should proactively search for information online, entirely disregarding the union’s obligation to provide essential updates transparently and accessibly.

Additionally, at the **August 2024 Membership Meeting**, “seven constitutional changes were voted on unanimously” without prior notice to members or an opportunity for review. Members were subsequently informed that these changes could be found on the website. However, after a thorough search, I found no documentation or announcements regarding these amendments. This failure to post or communicate the changes contravenes **Article VIII, Section 1**, which mandates that amendments be clearly communicated to members with proper notice. Members have the right to understand the content and implications of amendments before they are ratified, particularly those impacting election procedures.

The union scheduled the nomination meeting outside the constitutionally established dates without securing the necessary two-thirds vote, as required by **Article VIII, Section 1**. The **September 2024 Membership Meeting Minutes** show no evidence that such a vote was conducted, rendering the change in dates invalid​. According to **Article V, Section 8(a)**, nomination meetings should occur between **September 7 and September 14**. However, **Local President Steve Curreri** confirmed during the **October 27th meeting** that the nomination meeting was held on **September 15th**, violating this timeline. Curreri claimed a vote was taken to alter the nomination date but failed to provide proof of the required two-thirds approval. This sequence of events highlights a series of constitutional oversights.

Due to these substantial procedural violations, I formally request that the nomination process and election be **nullified**. A new election should be conducted that adheres to both the local and national constitutions, including:

- Proper notice for all procedural changes,
- Transparent election results postings available both physically and digitally,
- Adherence to constitutionally mandated timelines, and
- Ensuring all members have an equal opportunity to participate and file grievances as needed.

These requests reflect the rights and principles outlined in the **Members’ Bill of Rights** and in the APWU Constitution, which aim to protect members' access to fair and democratic union practices.

I trust that the National Election Appeals Committee will review this matter with the seriousness it deserves and take corrective action to uphold the integrity of the APWU's election procedures. Please feel free to contact me should you need further information or clarification.

**In solidarity,**


Alternate Steward  
APWU Local #3844

Despite this, I believe wholeheartedly in our union’s purpose and aspire only to be a committed member and steward, if possible.
