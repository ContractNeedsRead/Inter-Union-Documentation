# E-24-35

**Subject: Explanation of Procedural Request to Local Election Committee**

Dear National Election Appeals Committee,

I am writing to provide clarity on my recent actions concerning the election process in Local, as per your request for additional documentation to support my appeal (Case No. E-24-35). I have submitted a formal letter to the Local Election Committee (LEC), which I have attached to this packet of evidence.

In my letter to the LEC, I have made election appeals both in writing and verbally at the October 27th meeting. During that meeting, I formally requested that the leadership and the LEC members present explain why the election results were never formally posted and why the nomination date was held on the 15th, contrary to our constitutional provisions.

The letter to the LEC details specific concerns regarding potential violations of our local and national constitutions, as well as the APWU Members’ Bill of Rights. I am seeking validation for each procedural component of the election and requesting documentation to confirm compliance with constitutional requirements or to clarify any potential discrepancies. This request serves several purposes:

1. **Addressing Potential Procedural Omissions**: Given that members were potentially denied timely notice, accessible information, and clear communication regarding amendments to election procedures, I wanted to ensure all relevant documents were formally requested from the LEC. By outlining specific procedural concerns tied to our governing documents, I aim to obtain confirmation of any missed requirements directly from the LEC.

2. **Supporting the Integrity of My Appeal**: Although I escalated my concerns to the NEAC due to the perceived lack of adherence to posting and accessibility requirements by the LEC, my intent has always been to ensure transparency and fairness in the process. By documenting these requests to the LEC now, I am reinforcing my commitment to due process and to exhausting all steps available to validate procedural adherence at the local level.

3. **Protecting Members’ Rights as Outlined in the APWU Constitution**: Each item in my request is specifically aligned with provisions of the APWU Members’ Bill of Rights. By submitting this structured request to the LEC, I am attempting to confirm whether these rights were fully upheld during the election process. This approach will either substantiate my concerns regarding rights violations or provide assurance of procedural compliance.

### Documentation Requested from the LEC

In the letter to the LEC, I have requested detailed documentation on the following components:

1. **Notice of Election**
   - **Concern**: Members may have been denied timely notification if the Notice of Election was not issued or distributed according to union requirements.
   - **Relevant Citations**:
     - *Local Constitution*, Article V, Section 9.
     - *APWU National Constitution*, Article 12, Section 1.
     - *APWU Members’ Bill of Rights*, Item 6.
   - **Request**: The original election notice, the method of distribution, and the date it was posted or mailed to members.

2. **Nomination Process and Notice**
   - **Concern**: The nomination process did not follow designated timelines, and members were not informed of amendments in advance, potentially preventing full participation.
   - **Relevant Citations**:
     - *Local Constitution*, Article V, Section 8(a); Article VIII, Section 1.
     - *APWU Members’ Bill of Rights*, Item 3.
   - **Request**:
     - A copy of the Nomination Notice, including original and altered dates.
     - Record of the member vote to change the nomination schedule, including confirmation of a two-thirds majority.
     - Proof that members received 15-day written notice of the amendment meeting.

3. **Eligibility to Run and Hold Office**
   - **Concern**: If candidates did not meet eligibility standards, members’ rights to vote for qualified candidates may have been infringed.
   - **Relevant Citations**:
     - *APWU National Constitution*, Article 10.
     - *Local Constitution*, Article V.
     - *APWU Members’ Bill of Rights*, Item 8.
   - **Request**: Verification of each candidate’s eligibility status, including membership standing and dues as of the required date.

4. **Election Rules and Certification**
   - **Concern**: If election rules were not certified and published, members’ rights to understand and participate according to the established guidelines may have been compromised.
   - **Relevant Citations**:
     - *APWU National Constitution*, Article 12, Section 1.
     - *APWU Members’ Bill of Rights*, Item 5.
   - **Request**: Certified Election Rules, including the publication date and methods used to communicate these rules to all members.

5. **Posting of Election Results**
   - **Concern**: Members’ rights may have been compromised if election results were not posted in an accessible manner, preventing grievances from being filed within the allowed time.
   - **Relevant Citations**:
     - *Local Constitution*, Article V, Section 10(a).
     - *APWU Members’ Bill of Rights*, Item 9.
   - **Request**: Confirmation of the date, time, and methods used to post election results, including locations where results were accessible.

6. **Grievance Process Availability**
   - **Concern**: If the grievance process was inaccessible, members’ rights to appeal election procedures may have been infringed.
   - **Relevant Citations**:
     - *Local Constitution*, Article V, Section 10(a).
     - *APWU Members’ Bill of Rights*, Item 9.
   - **Request**: Records of grievances filed within the 72-hour window, including acknowledgment of receipt and responses.

By submitting these requests to the LEC, I aim to ensure compliance with our governing documents and to address any procedural omissions directly. This action supports the integrity of my appeal by demonstrating my commitment to due process and to exhausting all available steps at the local level.

Please let me know if additional information or clarification is needed. I remain committed to ensuring a fair and compliant resolution to these election concerns.

**In solidarity,**
