### **II. APWU Grievance Documentation & Analysis System**  
I've developed a comprehensive digital toolkit that automates grievance documentation and analysis, which I'd like to offer to our union. This system:  

**Solves Key Challenges**:  
- Reduces grievance prep time from weeks to hours  
- Automates staffing calculations using MMO standards  
- Generates RFIs and complete Step 1 packages  
- Validates WebEOR data to prove violations  

**Core Features**:  
1. **WebEOR Analyzer**: Processes CSV exports to identify contractual violations  
2. **Staffing Calculator**: Applies arbitrated and contractual standards to prove understaffing  
3. **Auto-Documentation**: Generates RFIs and grievance packages with embedded evidence  

**Member Benefits**:  
- 85% reduction in documentation time  
- Consistent application of MMO standards  
- Error-proof violation identification  
- Professional-grade grievance packages  

I'd appreciate the opportunity to demonstrate this system and discuss how it could support our locals nationwide. The complete system is ready for deployment at no cost to APWU.
