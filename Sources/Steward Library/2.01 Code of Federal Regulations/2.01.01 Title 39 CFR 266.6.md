## Summary
This section outlines the procedures by which individuals may request inspection, copying, or amendment of personal records within a Privacy Act System of Records.

## Section Details

### § 266.6 Procedures for Requesting Inspection, Copying, or Amendment of Records

#### (a) Submission of Requests
- **Manner of Submission**:
  Requests should be made in writing to the custodian of the record, if known, or to the Manager, Records Office, U.S. Postal Service, 475 L’Enfant Plaza SW., Washington, DC 20260, telephone (202) 268–2608. Requests to the Office of Inspector General should be directed to the Freedom of Information Act/Privacy Officer, Office of Inspector General, 1735 North Lynn Street, Arlington, Virginia, 22209–2020. Mark inquiries clearly as ‘‘Privacy Act Request’’.

- **Details Required**:
  Provide information as published under ‘‘Notification’’ in the FEDERAL REGISTER. If information is insufficient, the requester will be notified and informed of any additional information required. Non-Postal Service employees should designate their preferred post office for reviewing or obtaining copies of records.

- **Amendment Requests**:
  Contest the relevance, accuracy, timeliness, or completeness of the record, including a statement of the desired amendment.

- **Third Party Inquiries**:
  Written consent of the person to whom the records pertain is required to review or copy their records.

- **Response Period**:
  Custodian will acknowledge receipt within ten (10) days and inform if a specific record exists.

#### (b) Compliance with Request for Access
- **Notification of Inspection**:
  Custodian will notify the requester where and when the record will be available for inspection or copying. Normally available during regular business hours at the postal facilities where they are maintained.

- **Identification Required**:
  Requester must present valid identification such as a driver’s license, Medicare card, or employee ID card before reviewing records.

- **Responsibilities of Requester**:
  - Must not remove official records from Postal Services premises unless given a copy.
  - Sign a statement after reviewing specific record(s).
  - May be accompanied by a person of choosing if written authorization is provided for the review.

- **Special Rules for Medical Records**:
  Disclosed unless a medical officer determines access could adversely affect the individual. In such cases, disclosure may be restricted.
