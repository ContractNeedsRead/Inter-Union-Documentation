[[2.10.02.01 Federal Labor Relations Authority]]

# RIGHTS OF EMPLOYEES

## Section 7. [§ 157]

Employees shall have the right to self-organization, to form, join, or assist labor organizations, to bargain collectively through representatives of their own choosing, and to engage in other concerted activities for the purpose of collective bargaining or other mutual aid or protection. They shall also have the right to refrain from any or all such activities, except to the extent that such right may be affected by an agreement requiring membership in a labor organization as a condition of employment as authorized in [[2.10.02.02 NLRA Section 8(a)(3)]]
