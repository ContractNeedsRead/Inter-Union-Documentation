**_Article 17, Section 3_** requires the Employer to provide for a review of all documents, files, and other records necessary in processing a grievance. **_Article 31, Section 2_** requires that the Employer make available for inspection by the unions all relevant information necessary for collective bargaining or the enforcement, administration, or interpretation of the agreement. Under **_Section 8a(5) of the National Labor Relations Act_**, it is an Unfair Labor Practice for the Employer to fail to supply relevant information for the purpose of collective bargaining. Grievance processing is an extension of the collective bargaining process.

Requests for Information are submitted to investigate potential grievances, grievances and to defend employees through the collective bargaining process.
