1. **January 15**: 
   - Posting for Choice Vacation Period on bulletin boards.

2. **February 15**: 
   - Closing date for Choice Vacation Period solicitation.

3. **March 1**: 
   - Notification to employees of approval/disapproval for the Choice Vacation Period.

4. **March 7**: 
   - Resubmission deadline for Choice Vacation Period requests.

5. **March 15**: 
   - Notification of resubmission approvals and posting of the total leave chart.
   - Final day for employees to submit their Choice Vacation period request if previously unavailable due to sickness/injury.

6. **April 1**: 
   - Start of the Choice Vacation Period.
   - Start of vacation period for Tour 1 employees (2330 hours).

7. **September 15**: 
   - Posting for Winter School vacation periods and Thanksgiving week vacation slots on bulletin boards.

8. **September 30**: 
   - End of the Choice Vacation Period.

9. **October 1**: 
   - Closing date for Winter School vacation period solicitation.

10. **October 15**: 
    - Notification to employees of approval/disapproval for Winter School vacation period.

11. **October 22**: 
    - Resubmission deadline for Winter School vacation period requests.

12. **October 29**: 
    - Notification of resubmission approvals and posting of total leave chart for Winter School vacation period.

13. **November 1**: 
    - Employer must publish the beginning date of the new leave year by this date.

14. **November 15**: 
    - Final day for employees to submit Winter School vacation period requests if previously unavailable due to sickness/injury.

1. **Monthly Labor-Management Meeting**:
   - A labor-management meeting must be held every month, typically during the first full week of each month, except for December when the Christmas meeting takes its place.
   - Agenda items must be exchanged two days in advance of the meeting.
   - Any unresolved items from the previous meeting must be discussed first.

2. **Christmas Meeting**:
   - The Christmas meeting replaces the labor-management meeting for the month of December.

3. **Posting of Leave Lists**:
   - Annual leave lists for the upcoming service week must be posted on the Wednesday before the service week.

4. **Rotation of Safety Talks**:
   - Safety talks must be rotated weekly so that all non-scheduled employees have an opportunity to participate.

5. **Posting and Review of Seniority List**:
   - The seniority list must be updated and posted quarterly, with copies furnished to the union.
