# Memorandum of Understanding  
**Between the U.S.P.S., Brockton MA**  
**And the A.P.W.U. S.S.A.L. 3844**

---

## 1. Additional or Longer Wash-up Periods:

All clerk craft personnel are to receive a five (5) minute wash-up period prior to the meal break and a five (5) minute wash-up period prior to the end of their tour of duty.

All maintenance craft employees will receive a five-minute wash-up period prior to lunch and prior to the end of tour. Recognizing that many maintenance employees are engaged in dirty or toxic work, additional reasonable time will be granted consistent with the National Agreement as well as with past practices.

All motor vehicle craft employees will receive a five-minute wash-up period prior to lunch and prior to the end of tour. Recognizing that many motor vehicle employees are engaged in dirty or toxic work, additional reasonable time will be granted consistent with the National Agreement as well as with past practices.

---

## 2. The Establishment of a Regular Work Week of Five Days with Either Fixed or Rotating Days Off:

All regular employees shall have a fixed basic work week schedule consisting of five (5) scheduled days and two (2) non-scheduled days. Management will make reasonable effort not to schedule part-time flexibles more than six days a week. The beginning time must be posted by the employer on the Wednesday prior to the service week whenever possible. Management agrees to utilize its best efforts, consistent with service needs, to provide flexibles with a more consistent schedule.

When new job assignments are made or additional positions created, management shall notify the union president as far in advance as possible, with at least a 21-day notice provided. Management must inform and discuss with the union working days, days off, hours, schemes, duty station, and other items pertinent to the new job assignment or position. The parties agree to maintain the intent of Article VIII, Section 2-C of the National Agreement relative to consecutive non-scheduled days.

---

## 3. Guidelines for the Curtailment or Termination of Postal Operations Due to Emergency Conditions:

### A.
The parties are aware of their responsibilities for the development of appropriate guidelines for the curtailment or temporary halt of postal operations due to emergency conditions.

### B.
Termination of postal operations to conform to orders of local authorities, or as local conditions warrant because of emergency conditions, shall be based upon information available and received from local, state, and national authorities and other official sources.

The installation head, or his designee, in making a determination on absences, must consider the following:

1. Availability of public transportation in lieu of use of private vehicles.
2. Proximity of other employees available for carpool types of transportation.
3. Employees within walking distance to the post office.
4. Intensity and possible duration of the storm, or other disaster.
5. Known physical disability of the employee.
6. The percentage of employees able to report to work.
7. Recommendations made by state, local, regional, or federal authorities.

In making determinations for early dismissal, the installation head, or his designee, must consider the following:

1. Driving conditions.
2. On-the-job safety factors.
3. Intensity, density, and predicted extended weather forecast.
4. Possible lack of work due to the storm.
5. Availability and impact of incoming sources of mail.
6. Recommendations made by local, state, regional, or federal authorities.

### C.
Management must consult with union representatives concerning appropriate action when the emergency is of such a nature that advance notice is possible.

### D.
When a determination has been made that such an emergency condition exists, or any other situation prevents groups of employees from working or reporting to work, the installation head or designee shall consult with the union President or designee as soon as possible. They shall also utilize public media to notify employees of any changes resulting from such conditions.

The Union representatives, if available and willing, shall call employees scheduled to report to curtailed tours. Employees required to work beyond the calling of administrative leave shall be compensated with an hour off for each hour worked during their normal work schedule under emergency conditions. Such leave shall be granted within 30 days, subject to current leave policy.

### E.
Maintenance officials will make employee safety a prime concern, taking appropriate actions to protect employees, such as rest breaks to warm up or cool down and reassignment to warmer or cooler work areas.

---

## 4. Formulation of Local Leave Program:

### A.
Care shall be exercised to assure that no employee is required to forfeit any part of his/her annual leave. Cancellations of scheduled leave, as required by a declaration of emergency, will be done by inverse seniority, but only after voluntary cancellations and maximum overtime have been solicited. Unused weeks or surrendered weeks caused by employee cancellation during the choice vacation period shall be made available to appropriate craft employees on a first-come, first-served basis. This can be in units of five (5) days or leave requests for less than five (5) days.

### B.
The employer shall post solicitations for the Choice Vacation Period as follows:

1. Post on bulletin boards by January 15  
2. Closing date for notice: February 15  
3. Notification of approval/disapproval: March 1  
4. Resubmission for Choice Vacation: March 7  
5. Notification of resubmissions and posting of leave chart: March 15  

Any unused Choice Vacation slots after this period shall be considered on a first-come, first-served basis until the maximum number of employees allowed off is reached.

The employer shall post solicitations for the Winter School vacation periods and the week of Thanksgiving as follows:

1. Post on bulletin boards by September 15  
2. Closing date for notice: October 1  
3. Notification of approval/disapproval: October 15  
4. Resubmission for Winter School vacation periods: October 22  
5. Notification of resubmissions and posting of leave chart: October 29  

Unused Winter School vacation slots after this period shall be considered on a first-come, first-served basis until the maximum number of employees allowed off is reached.

### C.
After completing this procedure, a copy of the vacation schedule shall be given to the union. Any employee unavailable due to sickness or injury will be contacted by management regarding available weeks. They have until March 15 (or November 15 for Winter school vacation periods) to submit their request for the Choice Vacation period.

### D.
If an employee becomes ill while on Choice Vacation leave, he/she may have the leave charged to sick leave upon request in writing. If approved, the employee shall be eligible for another available period, provided it does not deprive any other employee of his/her first-choice scheduled vacation.

### E.
Call-in AAL Resolution for Clerk Craft:

Efforts must be made to allow a liberal amount of advanced annual leave and ensure adequate staffing to meet mail processing commitments. The liberal leave amounts include the allowed percentage by local contract and beyond when reduced mail volumes are anticipated, or arrangements can be made for staffing needs.

The following procedures will be implemented:

1. Leave requests shall be granted as long as the contractual limit has not been reached.  
2. Requests for the following day may be submitted until a reasonable time before the end of a tour.  
3. Requests beyond the limit will be considered, and approval will be attempted where possible, even with voluntary overtime to cover absences.  
4. The tour General Supervisor or operations manager will leave a note with the time scale indicating where leave may be allowed the following day. On each tour, a minimum of one clerk may call in no later than 90 minutes prior to the start of his/her tour and request advanced annual leave. If there is an opening, this request will be granted.  

AAL leave categories will apply to the following breakdowns:

- Mail processing clerks (Manual, Automation & Expediter)
- SPBS

Whenever possible, management will make reasonable effort to grant additional AAL requests.

Procedures will be developed among supervisors and stewards to ensure daily administration of these actions.

---

## 5. The Duration of the Choice Vacation Period:

The Choice Vacation period shall begin on April 1 and end on September 30. Winter School vacation periods, as well as the service week containing Thanksgiving, shall be considered as part of the Choice Vacation period.

---

## 6. The Beginning Day of an Employee's Vacation Period:

### A.
The beginning day of an employee's vacation period shall be on the first day of their basic work week.

### B.
Exceptions may be granted by agreement between the employee, the union, and the employer.

### C.
Vacation periods for Tour 1 personnel shall commence at 2330 hours Sunday and end at 0800 hours the following Saturday.

---

## 7. Whether Employees May Request Two Selections During the Choice Vacation Period:

All employees earning twenty to twenty-six days of leave may request three (3) sections of annual leave in units of five (5) days, either concurrently or as desired. Additional time may be granted if it has not been selected by another employee and the maximum number of employees off has not been reached. First consideration shall be given to requests in units of five (5) days.

---

## 8. Whether Jury Duty and Attendance at National or State Conventions Shall Be Charged to the Choice Vacation Period:

Jury duty and attendance at national, state, or regional conventions cannot be determined by the employee and shall not be charged to the Choice Vacation period. Employees shall be entitled to an additional selection.

---

## 9. Determination of the Maximum Number of Employees Receiving Leave Each Week During the Choice Vacation Period:

### A. Clerical Craft Employees:
1. One to five employees - 1 off  
2. Six to twelve employees - 2 off  
3. Thirteen and over - no less than 15% off  

Additional annual leave may be granted at management's discretion.

### B. Annual Leave in the Clerk Craft:

1. By tour  
2. By building  
3. Window clerks  
4. Manual clerks  
5. Automation  
6. Administrative offices  
7. SPBS  

### C. Maintenance and Motor Vehicle Departments:

1. One to five employees - 1 off  
2. Six to twelve employees - 2 off  
3. Thirteen and over - no less than 16.5%  

In the Maintenance Department, annual leave will be granted based on the above numbers per occupational code. In the Motor Vehicle Department, leave will be granted as above, provided additional leave will be granted as per past practice unless unforeseen circumstances arise.

---

## 10. Issuance of Official Notices to Employees of Their Approved Vacation Schedule:

Each employee shall be notified by return of duplicate Form 3971 (approved or disapproved) and by posting a total leave chart. All advance commitments for granting annual leave shall be honored.

---

## 11. Date and Means of Notifying Employees of the Beginning of the New Leave Year:

The employer shall, no later than November 1, publish on bulletin boards and by other means the beginning date of the new leave year, which will begin with the first day of the first full pay period of the calendar year.

---

## 12. Procedures for Submission of Applications for Annual Leave During Non-Choice Vacation Period:

The remainder of the employee's annual leave may be granted at other times of the year as requested. The employee will submit 3971 forms in triplicate to the supervisor. Requests not answered within 48 hours are considered granted.

The minimum number of employees allowed off on annual leave outside of the Choice Vacation period shall be:

1. One to six employees - 1 off  
2. Seven to fifteen employees - 2 off  
3. Over fifteen employees - no less than 12%  

Additional employees may be granted leave on a first-come, first-served basis at the supervisor’s discretion.

For Maintenance and Motor Vehicle crafts:

1. One to six employees - 1 off  
2. Seven to fifteen employees - 2 off  
3. Over fifteen employees - no less than 12.5%  

---

## 13. Method of Selecting Employees to Work on a Holiday:

The provisions of Article XI, Section 6 of the National Agreement will be followed. Management will apply the following order:

1. Full-time regulars who volunteer to work on the holiday.  
2. PSEs, even if overtime is required.  
3. Full-time regulars who volunteer to work on their non-scheduled day.  
4. Full-time regulars on the overtime desired list, by inverse seniority.  
5. Full-time regulars not on the overtime desired list, by inverse seniority.  
6. Full-time regulars who do not volunteer for holiday work, by inverse seniority.  

Management shall post on Tuesday before the service week the list of all full-time regular employees mandated to work on their holiday. Management may solicit all qualified employees within the installation before mandatory scheduling.

---

## 14. Overtime Desired Lists:

### A. In the Clerk Craft:
The Overtime Desired List shall be established per Article VIII of the National Agreement. Overtime lists shall reflect both non-scheduled and scheduled day overtime requests. Employees can opt for ten-hour or twelve-hour overtime. Whenever possible, at least a one (1) hour notice will be given to full-time regular employees.

### B. In the Maintenance and Motor Vehicle Crafts:
The Overtime Desired List shall be installation-wide and maintained for both non-scheduled and scheduled days, with employee options for ten-hour or twelve-hour overtime. A two (2) hour notice will be given (or one hour in the Maintenance craft), whenever possible.

---

## 15. Duty Assignments Reserved for Light Duty Assignments:

There shall be mutual agreement between management and the APWU on the hours of work and number of duty assignments for employees crossing crafts or occupational groups for light duty purposes before the assignment is made.

---

## 16. Reserving Light Duty Assignments:

Management and the APWU shall implement Article XIII to ensure that no regularly assigned member of the workforce is adversely affected by light duty assignments.

---

## 17. Light Duty Assignments in Each Craft:

### A.
Light duty administered by the Installation head is a valued asset for employees.

### B.
Consultations of individual cases will be held between management and the union as needed.

### C.
Supervisors and stewards on all tours shall be kept up to date on employees on light duty.

### D.
There shall be mutual agreement on the hours of work and number of duty assignments for employees crossing crafts for light duty.

### E.
Light duty must align with productive needs and not be created solely to accommodate specific requests or conditions.

### F.
Requests for light duty must include full medical evaluations and descriptions of the condition, including the duration of the need for light duty.

---

## 18. Identification of Assignments to Be Considered Light Duty:

Sections shall be identified by tour in the clerk craft. Sections will be identified by tour and occupational code in the maintenance and motor vehicle crafts.

---

## 19. Assignment of Employee Parking Spaces:

Parking shall be on a first-come, first-served basis, after assignment of government vehicles. Management shall consult with the Union before changing parking policy.

---

## 20. Annual Leave for Union Activities:

Union activities cannot be determined by the employee, and an employee attending union activities shall not have this time charged to the choice vacation period.

---

## 21. Items Subject to Local Negotiations as Provided in the Craft Provisions:

### A. Monthly Labor-Management Meetings:
1. The Christmas meeting shall replace the labor-management meeting for December.  
2. Meetings shall last at least one hour unless mutually agreed otherwise.  
3. The union president and one other representative shall attend each meeting.  
4. Meetings will be scheduled seven (7) calendar days before the first full week of each month.

### B. Agenda:
1. Agenda items will be exchanged two (2) days in advance.  
2. Items not resolved from previous meetings shall be discussed first.  
3. Non-agenda items may be discussed by mutual consent.

### C.
The union president and designated stewards may use USPS telephones for local calls for official union business, with supervisor permission. Long-distance calls must use a credit card charge system.

### D.
If the union removes its phone, this section becomes null and void.

---

## 22. Local Implementation of Agreement Relating to Seniority, Reassignments, and Posting:

### A. Seniority and Reassignments:
1. Management shall recognize seniority in job assignment preferences whenever possible.  
2. The seniority list will be posted quarterly, with copies furnished to the union.  
3. All bidding on preferred assignments shall be done on an installation-wide basis.  
4. Employees performing higher-level work on detail or during absence shall be compensated at the higher rate.  
5. No employee shall be bumped from an assignment once assigned, except for training or O.J.I. purposes.

### B. Posting and Reassignments:
1. Bids shall be posted for seven (7) days.  
2. Bids posted on Monday shall be removed the following Monday, unless it is a holiday.  
3. Results of bids will be posted seven (7) days after closing.  
4. The successful bidder shall be placed in the new assignment within ten (10) days of posting, except in December.  
5. No bid shall close on a Monday if it is a holiday.  

### C. Steward Assignments and Authorizations:
1. Union representation requests shall not be unreasonably denied.  
2. Requests for representation shall not be unduly delayed.  
3. Additional stewards may be released to ensure timely grievance processing.  
4. The employer shall provide office space for grievance processing.

### D. E.L.R., Training, Environmental, EEO:
1. No employee shall be subject to sexual harassment.  
2. Public address programming shall continue per Postal Bulletin guidelines.  
3. Radios with headphones shall be allowed, provided they do not pose a safety hazard.  
4. Annual leave lists shall be posted by Wednesday before the service week.  
5. Safety talks shall rotate to ensure participation by all employees.  
6. Supervisors are prohibited from performing bargaining unit work except as allowed by Article I, Section 6(A-1 through A-5).

### E.
If a new facility affects the clerical craft, union officials shall inspect the installation at least 120 days in advance.

---

## Floor Seniority:

Employees shall express job assignment preferences at the start of a normal shift.

No employee shall be bumped from an assignment once assigned, except for training or O.J.I. purposes.

LSM and SPLSM operators may exercise floor seniority when assigned to the manual distribution area.

Floor seniority will not be recognized for employees voluntarily working on non-scheduled days or holidays.

---

**Addendum B**  
The intent of Article 30, Item 22.A.1 is to ensure that employees in Brockton receive full consideration for their seniority. Management's rights must be served, and application of seniority will be recognized unless it causes disruption or delay. Seniority will be considered for unexpected assignments arising from emergencies or unforeseen fluctuations in mail or equipment needs. Employees may be assigned work to ensure proficiency of their bid skills.
