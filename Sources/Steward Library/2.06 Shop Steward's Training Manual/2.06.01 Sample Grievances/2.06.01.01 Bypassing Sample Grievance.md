### Bypassing Required Preventive Maintenance
### Bypassing Sample Grievance

**Grievance:**  
The Service is bypassing preventive maintenance required by [[MMO-125-20]] and Management Instruction [[2.03.02.03 MI AS-530-90-5]]. This work is required to keep the equipment in a safe and proper operating order. Failure to perform preventive maintenance results in hazards and mail delay and undermines the mission of the organization pursuant to the Postal Reorganization Act and other applicable laws, regulations, directives, and manuals.

### Bypassing Remedy

**Remedy:**  
- Cease and desist all unnecessary bypassing of preventive maintenance.
- Make all necessary staffing adjustments so that PMs, required by the staffing survey, are performed.
- Pay, to the employee named by the Union, all bypassed preventive maintenance hours.
