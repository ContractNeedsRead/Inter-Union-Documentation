## 3.0 Assign Work

### 3.1 Contracts Scheduling Principles

The Assign Work module in eMARS facilitates daily maintenance workload scheduling and assignment to employees. The objective is to improve and prolong building and equipment life, avoid unplanned maintenance activity, and lower overall maintenance costs. The timely assignment of PM Routes and Work Orders eliminates breakdown maintenance and significantly reduces the number and frequency of corrective maintenance actions.

**To Access Assign Work:**

1. In the eMARS main page click on the **Assign Work** button on the top navigation menu

Notice that the WORK ORDERS option is selected by default and the Crew/Tour 001 is selected by default. The Sched Date calendar is the date when the work is going to be assigned, and is set to current date by default.

After an employee is selected, two grid areas below will be activated. The upper grid shows all work orders which were originally planned under the selected crew and are currently available to be assigned. The lower grid lists all work that has been assigned to the employee for the selected date. Within the grid areas the sort options are available by clicking on the column headers.

**To switch to PM work orders:**

Click on the **PREVENTIVE MAINTENANCE** button. All available PM will be listed in the upper grid area.

> **NOTE**: The upper grid data is locked from the calendar.

Notice that the Sched Date is defaulted to current date. To assign work for a different date, click on the Sched Date calendar and click on the applicable date. If the date selected on the Sched Date calendar is more than 5 days old a message appears and the Sched Date will be reset to current date.

After a PM Route or Work Order is assigned to an employee, the background color of the row turns to light blue. If it has not yet been assigned to any employees, the background remains white.

**Important**: Ensure all PM Routes and Work Orders in the weekly schedule are assigned!

### 3.2 Scheduling PM Routes

At the end of the scheduling process, all PM Routes should be highlighted blue, and none should be left white.

When the **PREVENTIVE MAINTENANCE** button is pressed, the grid displays all the PM Routes available for the employee selected.

> **Note**: Within the dropdown list of Employees, the column to the right of the employee number indicates employee work status for the day:
> - **ON** - Employee scheduled to work
> - **OFF** - Employee not scheduled to work
> - **L** - Employee on leave
> - **T** - Employee in training

You can still assign work to the employee if the system indicates the employee is not scheduled to report to work which allows flexibility for OT scheduling. The next column indicates if the employee has been assigned at least one CM or PM work order.

**To assign a work order:**

1. Select the Work Order from the upper grid
2. Click the **ASSIGN** button
3. The selected work order row will be moved to the lower grid

**To un-assign a work order:**

1. Highlight the work order from the list of assigned work orders
2. Click the **UN-ASSIGN** button

**To copy the assignment between employees and week days:**

1. Click the **COPY** button
2. The Copy Scheduled Tasks window appears
3. Click the **Destination** tab
4. Scroll down to crew if not the one displayed
5. Click on button and the employees under that crew is displayed
6. Under **Employee Selection** tab, click on employee to whom the work is to be copied
7. More than one employee can be selected and will be designated as selected by a red X
8. Click **COPY** to confirm

The work has now been copied from the original employee and assigned to the new employees.

**To exit:**

Click on **EXIT** to leave the module.

The copied results can be verified in the previous window (Assign Work).

**To move work instead of copy:**

1. Click the **MOVE** button
2. Follow the same steps as copying
3. Click on **EXIT** to leave the module

Notice that the work has now been removed from the original employee.

**To assign work from other crews:**

1. Click on the **Other Crews** tab on the left hand side
2. A new window will be opened, which lists all work orders from the other crews
3. Select the desired rows
4. Click **ASSIGN** button

Notice that the selected rows are assigned to the employee.

**To combine PM work orders:**

- Check the **Combine Daily** box - the daily routes PM routes which have the same subsite ID, acronym, work code, frequency and route numbers will be combined. No eCBM routes will be combined.
- Check the **Combine Senior** box - senior routes PM routes which have the same subsite ID, acronym, work code, frequency and route numbers will be combined.

### 3.3 Scheduling Work Orders

1. Click on the **WORK ORDERS** button
2. All Work orders will be listed in the upper grid area

Follow the same procedures as outlined in [[#3.2 Scheduling PM Routes|Scheduling PM Routes]] for assigning, un-assigning, copying, and moving work orders.

## 4.0 Reports

The Report module in eMARS facilitates various reports. 

**To Access Reports:**

In the eMARS main page click on the **Reports** button.

### 4.1 Contracts

**To Access Contract Reports:**

In the Reports main page click on the **Contracts** button and the main page appears.

The Contracts Reports has 5 categories:

1. **Contractor Listing**
2. **Contract Listing**
3. **Contract Costs**
4. **Contract Maint Cost**
5. **Transaction Listing**

**Contractor Listing** report is categorized by Master Listing, By State and By Company Name.

**Contract Listing** report lists all the Contractors with the Contract ID.

**Contract Costs** report lists Contract Cost categorized by Subsite - Acronym, By Contractor and By Dollars based on the date range selection.

**Contract Maint. Cost** report lists all the Contract Maintenance Cost.

**Transaction Listing** report lists Contract by Transaction Date.

### 4.2 Equipment

**To Access Equipment Inventory Reports:**

In the Reports main page click on the **Equipment Inventory** button and the main page appears.

The Equipment Inventory Reports has 3 categories:

1. **Inventory Listing**
2. **Equipment History**
3. **Informational**

**Inventory Listing** report lists Active, Inactive Equipment by Acronym categorized by Catalog Listing, By Subsite and Acronym and By Manufacturer.

**Equipment History** report lists Active, Inactive Equipment by Acronym categorized by Equipment History Reports, Modification Orders based on the date range selection.

**Informational** report lists Equipments categorized by Equipment Listing For Unexpired Warranty Dates and Equipment Listing For Inspection/Overhaul Dates.

### 4.3 Inventory

**To Access Inventory Management Reports:**

In the Reports main page click on the **Inventory Management** button and the main page appears.

The Inventory Management Reports has 8 categories:

1. **Issues**
2. **Orders**
3. **Receipts**
4. **Updates**
5. **Transaction Summary**
6. **Purchases**
7. **MISP**
8. **Toolbox**

The **Issues** report lists Parts/Supplies Issued categorized by Non-Stocked Issues, Repairable Due Back to Stockroom based on the date range selection.

The **Orders** report lists Orders based on NSN, Nomenclature, Work Order No, Order Date categorized by Orders, Local Orders based on the date range selection.

The **Receipts** report lists Receipts based on date range selection.

The **Updates** report lists catalog updates and modifications.

The **Transaction Summary** report provides summary of all inventory transactions.

The **Purchases** report lists all purchase-related transactions.

The **MISP** report provides Maintenance Inventory Support Process data.

The **Toolbox** report lists toolbox inventories and assignments.

### 4.4 Manager

Manager reports provide supervisory and management-level reports for oversight and planning purposes.

### 4.5 Misc Reports

Miscellaneous reports include various specialized reports not categorized under other report types.

### 4.6 Personnel Reports

Personnel reports provide information about employee assignments, training, leave, and other HR-related data.

### 4.7 Prevent Maint

Preventive Maintenance reports provide data on PM route completion, compliance, and scheduling.

### 4.8 React Maint

Reactive Maintenance reports provide data on reactive calls, response times, and troubleshooting activities.

### 4.9 Scheduling

Scheduling reports provide information about work assignments, crew utilization, and scheduling compliance.

### 4.10 Work Orders

Work Order reports provide detailed information about work order creation, completion, status, and costs.

## 6.0 Reactive Maintenance

> **Note**: This module is only available to users with proper permissions.

### View Current Calls

**To View all current calls (open and closed):**

1. Click on the **Call Finder** button
2. The Reactive Call Finder window will pop up
3. Select open or all

### Add Reactive Call

**To Add a Reactive Call:**

1. Click on the **ADD CALL** button
2. Locate Acronym and Equipment Number, or click on **FIND** button to select the Acronym and Equipment Number
3. Locate the Trouble by scrolling down
4. Click on **SAVE** button
5. The message "Would you like to dispatch an employee now?" appears
6. Select an Employee from the drop down menu
7. Click the **dispatch** button
8. Click on the **Save** button
9. The dispatched employee name will be displayed in the Dispatched Employees grid

### Dispatch Additional Employee

**To Dispatch an Additional Employee to a Reactive Call:**

1. Click on **CALL FINDER**
2. Double click on call to select
3. Under the Dispatched Employees section, scroll down to select the employee
4. Click the **DISPATCH** button
5. Notice that the additional employee is added under the Dispatched Employees section

### Remove Employee from Call

**To Remove an Additional Employee from a Reactive Call:**

1. Click on **CALL FINDER**
2. Scroll down to select call
3. Locate the employee to be removed from the call
4. Click the **REMOVE** button

### Close Reactive Call

**To Close a Reactive Maintenance Call:**

1. Click on **CALL FINDER**
2. Scroll down to select call
3. Select the Trouble from the drop down menu
4. Select the Verb by scrolling
5. Locate Noun by scrolling down
6. Click on **CLOSE CALL** button
7. Confirm on the Confirmation prompt
8. A confirmation message appears to indicate that the call has been closed
9. The employee name is removed from the Dispatched Employee section

### Select Reactive Maintenance Employees

**To select Reactive Maintenance Employees:**

1. Click the **EMPS ON CALL** button
2. Select Yes - all employees will be available for calls
3. Select No - will clear previous selections
4. Select by Tour - only employees on the selected Tour will be available for calls
5. Select individual employees by highlighting the row name
6. Click on **CHANGE AVAILABILITY** tab
7. Click on **SAVE** button
8. Notice that the selected Employee Availability has been changed

## 9.0 Inventory

### 9.1 Inventory Issues

#### 9.1.1 ISSUE STOCKED ITEM TO A WORK ORDER

1. From the eMARS main menu click on the **INV** button at the top of the screen
2. Click on the **Issues** button at the left of the screen
3. Click on the **Issue Stocked Parts** button
4. Enter search criteria (NSN, nomenclature, stockroom, location, or OEM)
   - Not all fields need to be filled out
   - One field besides stockroom must be filled in
   - Use scroll down to select stockroom if unknown
   - Click on the **GO** button
5. Click on the correct part and **SELECT** button OR:
6. Select correct Stockroom using scroll down
7. Type in the NSN and press **Enter**
8. Type in the Quantity
   > **Note**: System will display message if this issue will bring BOH to zero - click **OK**
9. Press **Enter**
10. Click **CHOOSE WORK ORDER** button
11. Enter known information (register, work order number, or acronym) and press **GO**
12. Scroll down and highlight correct work order, click **SELECT** button
13. If selecting standing work order with equipment number OPMS, must have equipment number in Equip No/Serial No field
14. Click **PROCESS ISSUE** button when completed

> **Note**: When a repairable part is issued, a note pops up stating: "This is a repairable item. The carcass must be returned to the stockroom to create a 1 for 1 order with Topeka. The 1 for 1 will not be ordered until Topeka receives the part. You may use the generated order to receipt in the part."

#### 9.1.2 ISSUE STOCKED ITEM TO A PREVENTIVE MAINTENANCE ROUTE

1. From eMARS main menu click on **INV** button
2. Click on **Issues** button
3. Click on **Issue Stocked Parts** button
4. Enter search criteria and click **GO**
5. Click on correct part and **SELECT** button OR:
6. Select correct Stockroom
7. Type in NSN and press **Enter**
8. Type in Quantity
   > **Note**: System displays message if BOH will be zero - click **OK**
9. Press **Enter**
10. Use scroll down to select employee
11. Press **Enter**
12. Type in **000** or use scroll down to select **000-PM** line
13. Type in the route number
14. If part is repairable, process completed

> **Note**: Same repairable part note applies as in 9.1.1
