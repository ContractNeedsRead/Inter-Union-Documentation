# Leave

## Why this matters across crafts

Three of the four contracts are near-clones here — the same ELM Subchapter 510 backbone, the same choice-vacation ladder, the same .01-hour charge units. The rural contract is a different instrument entirely: leave gated on leave-replacement availability, a free-Saturday rule, and a silent-approval clock that auto-approves unanswered requests. The divergences are where a steward's time gets won or lost.

## Divergences worth noting

1. **The rural article shares nothing structural with the other three** — no choice-vacation program, no ELM-510 adoption, no .01-hour units. Rural leave turns on leave-replacement availability instead ([[NRLCA §10.2\|NRLCA §10.2]]).
2. **Only NRLCA gives a free Saturday** when leave spans the week — and a work-day-Saturday holiday frees the preceding Friday ([[NRLCA §10.4\|NRLCA §10.4]]).
3. **Only NRLCA has silent approval:** an unanswered, properly-acknowledged request auto-approves after three days ([[NRLCA §10.7\|NRLCA §10.7]]). In the other crafts, silence is just silence.
4. **NPMHU's 23-week default choice period** (last Saturday in April onward) kicks in only when locals deadlock — the other two leave the fallback to local implementation ([[NPMHU §10.5\|NPMHU §10.5]]).
5. **NALC's ELM-510 adoption is scoped by effect** ("to the extent they set wages, hours, and working conditions"), APWU's and NPMHU's by employee class (PSE/MHA carve-outs) — three different doors into the same manual ([[NALC §10.2\|NALC §10.2]], [[APWU §10.2\|APWU §10.2]], [[NPMHU §10.2\|NPMHU §10.2]]).
6. **NRLCA non-career accrual is the only 20-hours-per-leave-hour economy** (max 4 hours per pay period) — subs and RCAs earn leave on a piecework clock the other crafts' non-career classes don't share ([[NRLCA §10.5\|NRLCA §10.5]]).
