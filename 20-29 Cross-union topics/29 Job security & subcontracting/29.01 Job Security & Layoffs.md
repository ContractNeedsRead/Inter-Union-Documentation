# Job Security & Layoffs

## Why this matters across crafts

No-layoff protection splits the four crafts into two legal worlds. The hourly crafts share the Healy Award framework — lifetime protection for the September 15, 1978 workforce, a six-year earn-in for everyone after, a 90/60-day notice machine, two-year recall, and ELM severance. The rural contract replaces all of that with one sentence: career employees cannot be involuntarily laid off for the life of the agreement.

## Legal layer

- **[[34.08 RIF 5 USC 3502]]** — the federal retention-order statute beneath the contractual layoff machinery (Ch. 35).
- **[[34.09 CSRS 5 USC 8336]]** — §8336(d)(2), the RIF-volunteer early retirement the preconditions reference.
- **[[34.11 USERRA 38 USC 4311-4312]]** — the military-service protections the coverage clauses count as "work."
- **[[31.05 ELM 435 Severance Pay]]** — the severance the protective-benefits clauses point to.
- **[[34.14 MSPB Adverse Actions 5 USC 7513]]** — the preference-eligible appeal route the §6.7 election rules coordinate with.

## Divergences worth noting

1. **The rural clause is one sentence; the hourly framework is eight sections.** NRLCA gives career employees an absolute, term-length, involuntary-layoff bar with no thresholds, no notice machine, no recall list, and no severance pointer — because none is needed ([[NRLCA §6\|NRLCA §6]]).
2. **The rural bar is narrower too:** substitutes, RCAs, RCRs, and ARCs are outside the protection entirely ([[NRLCA §6\|NRLCA §6]]).
3. **Entry points for layoff grievances differ:** NALC admits at Step B; APWU at the Regional Level; NPMHU at Regional/Area Step 3 ([[NALC §6.7\|NALC §6.7]], [[APWU §6.F\|APWU §6.F]], [[NPMHU §6.7\|NPMHU §6.7]]).
4. **APWU and NPMHU add PSE/MHA separation and termination-incentive solicitation to the preconditions** — NALC's list stops at casuals and hour minimization ([[APWU §6.B\|APWU §6.B]], [[NPMHU §6.3\|NPMHU §6.3]]).
5. **NPMHU alone writes retreat rights into the recall clause** ([[NPMHU §6.5\|NPMHU §6.5]]).
