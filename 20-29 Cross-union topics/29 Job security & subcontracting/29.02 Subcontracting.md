# Subcontracting

## Why this matters across crafts

All four contracts begin from the same five-factor test — public interest, cost, efficiency, equipment availability, employee qualifications — and then diverge in what the union can actually see and force before the work leaves. The rural contract carries the only real conversion weapon: a CDS renewal cost comparison that can turn contracted routes back into bargaining-unit delivery. The other three get notice, meetings, and information rights — gates, not vetoes.

## Legal layer

- **[[28.01 Grievance Procedure]]** — the enforcement route: NRLCA makes Article 32 disputes expressly grievable; the other crafts' significant-impact violations ride the same grievance ladder (see [[NRLCA §32.5\|NRLCA §32.5]]).
- **[[29.01 Job Security & Layoffs]]** — subcontracting's sibling; Article 6 layoff protection and Article 32 interact in every RIF scenario.
- No subcontracting-specific arbitration awards are in `_sources/` yet (gap G1).

## Divergences worth noting

1. **Only NRLCA can win work back.** The CDS renewal cost comparison converts a contracted route to rural delivery when the rural cost is lower — a substantive conversion right none of the other three contracts contain ([[NRLCA §32.4\|NRLCA §32.4]]). NALC's information right and APWU's 60-day package are process protections, not outcomes.
2. **NRLCA also carries the only freeze:** contract service cannot expand except through Article 32 itself, with violations grievable ([[NRLCA §32.5\|NRLCA §32.5]]).
3. **APWU's highway-movement package is the deepest disclosure** — equipment justifications, renewal vs. new solicitation status, PVS-replacement specifics — while NALC gets cost information only on request ([[APWU §32.2\|APWU §32.2]] vs [[NALC §32.1\|NALC §32.1]]).
4. **NPMHU is the only contract that obligates bargaining-unit employees to serve the contractor** — platform loading/unloading assistance for star-route vehicles ([[NPMHU §32.2\|NPMHU §32.2]]).
5. **The "no final decision before discussion" gate appears word-for-word at NALC and NRLCA** but APWU and NPMHU phrase it as CAR-stage consultation — same function, different paper trail.
