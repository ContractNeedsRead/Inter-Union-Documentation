# Seniority

## Why this matters across crafts

Seniority is the currency every posting, bid, and reassignment is paid in — but each contract mints it differently: different probation gates, different restart rules, different tie-breakers, and wildly different bid budgets. A carrier can bid six times a term; a clerk five; a mail handler nine. Knowing your contract's version of this article is knowing what your years are actually worth.

## Legal layer

- **[[34.08 RIF 5 USC 3502]]** — the federal retention-order floor beneath every contract's excessing rules (Article 6 layoff protection is the adjacent topic — see [[29.01 Job Security & Layoffs]]).
- **[[34.12 Veterans Preference 5 USC 2108]]** — preference eligibility feeds the §3502 retention ladder and the discipline-article appeals.
- **[[32.05 F-15 Travel and Relocation]]** and **[[32.04 EL-312 Rural Handbooks]]** — stubs for the relocation-benefit and rural machinery the reassignment sections invoke (sources not yet supplied).

## Divergences worth noting

1. **Bid budgets are arbitrary and unequal:** six bids (NALC), five (APWU), nine (NPMHU), and no cap at all (NRLCA). The "closer to home" exception exists in all three capped contracts.
2. **Only NRLCA breaks ties by Social Security number** — a cascade that ends in the last-four-digits lottery rather than a seniority judgment ([[NRLCA §12.2\|NRLCA §12.2]]).
3. **Seniority restart dates differ by craft generation:** NALC keys to July 21, 1973; APWU to November 20, 1994 — the same principle, different grandfather dates ([[NALC §12.2\|NALC §12.2]] vs [[APWU §12.2\|APWU §12.2]]).
4. **NRLCA has no retreat-right machinery** — no comparative work-hour report; the other three give the union a numbers-based check on excessing ([[NRLCA §12.5\|NRLCA §12.5]]).
5. **Mutual exchanges are a rural-only instrument**, binding once signed but barred same-office and cross-craft ([[NRLCA §12.5\|NRLCA §12.5]]).
6. **NPMHU pays you to move:** up to five days' annual leave or LWOP for an approved transfer — no other contract does ([[NPMHU §12.7\|NPMHU §12.7]]).
