# Reassignment & Transfer

## Why this matters across crafts

When an installation closes, consolidates, or excesses, the reassignment articles decide who moves, with what notice, at whose expense, and with what right to come back. The cores are shared — minimum dislocation, no bumping, national briefing on major relocations — but the trigger lists, notice ladders, and retreat-right machinery differ enough that a closure hits each craft differently.

## Divergences worth noting

1. **NRLCA has no retreat-right machinery** — no comparative work-hour report, no numbers check on an excessing decision; the other three all give the union one ([[NRLCA §12.5\|NRLCA §12.5]]).
2. **Mutual exchanges are rural-only**, binding once signed, but barred within an office and across crafts ([[NRLCA §12.5\|NRLCA §12.5]]).
3. **APWU's eight trigger events** (including motor-vehicle changes and PTF over-quota) are the widest list; NALC's fifth item ends at reductions beyond attrition with 6–8 left reserved ([[APWU §12.5\|APWU §12.5]] vs [[NALC §12.5.A\|NALC §12.5.A]]).
4. **NPMHU pays for the move** — up to five days' annual leave or LWOP for an approved transfer ([[NPMHU §12.7\|NPMHU §12.7]]).
5. **APWU limits excessing frequency** — once per three months per Postal Area ([[APWU §12.5\|APWU §12.5]]).
