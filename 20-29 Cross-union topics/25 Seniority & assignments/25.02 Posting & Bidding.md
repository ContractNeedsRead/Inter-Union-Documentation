# Posting & Bidding

## Why this matters across crafts

Posting and bidding is where seniority becomes an assignment — and the machinery is radically different by craft. The rural contract runs the richest ladder (30-day vacancy posting, a seven-rung consideration cascade, 10-day awards), NPMHU runs a 28-day regime with precise reposting triggers, and NALC and APWU push almost everything into their craft articles with only a bid budget on top.

## Divergences worth noting

1. **Only NRLCA writes the full award ladder in the national agreement** — the other three defer posting mechanics to craft articles, leaving NRLCA's 10-day award / 21-day placement rule the only national-level deadline of its kind ([[NRLCA §12.3\|NRLCA §12.3]]).
2. **NPMHU's reposting triggers are the most precise in any contract** — a 50% duty change or a one-hour start-time shift forces a repost ([[NPMHU §12.3\|NPMHU §12.3]]).
3. **The rural consideration cascade reaches seven levels deep** — mutual-exchange bidders and withheld-for-excess carriers sit above PTFs and substitutes ([[NRLCA §12.3\|NRLCA §12.3]]).
4. **NPMHU alone pins down what a "section" is** for posting purposes — nine enumerated bases through local implementation ([[NPMHU §12.4\|NPMHU §12.4]]).
