# Overtime

## Why this matters across crafts

Overtime is where the four contracts' wage systems collide most visibly: city carriers, clerks, and mail handlers share an hourly 1.5× structure with quarterly Overtime Desired Lists, while rural carriers work under an evaluated, FLSA §7(b)(2) annual-guarantee system with no overtime list at all — overtime is a rate, not a queue. Who can be forced, who can decline, what the hours are worth, and where the ceiling sits are materially different answers in each book, and each difference is an enforceable right.

## Divergences worth noting

1. **Mail handlers have no penalty overtime.** NPMHU §8.4 tops out at 1.5× — no 2× rate, unlike NALC and APWU. ELM Exhibit 434.141c confirms Mail Handlers: No. This is the single biggest pay gap between the hourly crafts.
2. **NALC-only 2.5×** for hours beyond 12/60, and NALC-only **Work Assignment list** — city carriers who refuse the OTDL can still put themselves on the WAL.
3. **Distribution philosophy splits the crafts:** NALC mandates *equitable* distribution with weekly posted hours; APWU and NPMHU use *seniority rotation*; NRLCA uses a twice-yearly relief-day list.
4. **The rural system is a different species:** no OTDL, no 12/60 ladder — a FLSA §7(b)(2) annual guarantee (2,080 guaranteed / 2,240 cap / 56-hour trigger) with overtime computed on the regular rate. Comparing "rural overtime" to city overtime requires translating between systems, not equating clauses.
5. **The 60-hour ceiling is not universal.** NALC and APWU state 12/60 for ODL employees; NPMHU states only a 12-hour daily cap with no weekly number; NRLCA states none at all.
6. **Non-OTDL floor differs:** NALC and APWU give non-OTDL employees the 4-of-5/10/8/6 shield; NPMHU gives only 10/6 (no 4-of-5); NALC alone adds 11.5/60 and explicit protection from discipline for leaving at those limits.
7. **ELM is not neutral ground here.** §434.133's general penalty-overtime category reads as if it covers everyone, but its own Exhibit 434.141c excludes mail handlers — the ELM papers over the NPMHU carve-out only if read without the exhibit.
8. **December means different things:** penalty-overtime exclusion (NALC/APWU), limits exclusion (NALC/APWU/NPMHU), and Christmas-OT accounting (NRLCA).
