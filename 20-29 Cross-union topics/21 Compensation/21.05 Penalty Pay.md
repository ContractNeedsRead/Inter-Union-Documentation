# Penalty Pay

## Why this matters across crafts

Penalty pay — double-time and beyond — is where the wage floor most visibly splits by craft: city carriers and clerks get 2× (and city carriers alone get 2.5× beyond 12/60), while mail handlers and rural carriers get nothing above time-and-one-half. Two crafts punish forced overtime; two crafts pay it at the same rate as voluntary overtime.

## Legal layer

- **[[31.03 ELM 434.141 Eligibility and Exhibit]]** — Exhibit 434.141c is the authoritative eligibility table; it excludes mail handlers outright.
- **[[31.02 ELM 434.1 Overtime]]** — §434.133's definitional text, which defers to the agreements ("hours described in applicable labor agreements").
- **[[33.01 Das 2013 — OTDL priority includes penalty overtime]]** — the award holding that "overtime work" priority scheduling includes penalty overtime (historical §8.5.H).
- **[[33.02 Mittenthal 1987 — Penalty overtime and holiday scheduling]]** — management may not reshuffle the holiday pecking order to dodge penalty pay.

## Divergences worth noting

1. **Mail handlers and rural carriers have no penalty rate at all.** For them, forced overtime costs the employer exactly what voluntary overtime costs — the economic deterrent the 2× rate is meant to be simply does not exist ([[NPMHU §8.4\|NPMHU §8.4]], [[NRLCA §8.3\|NRLCA §8.3]]).
2. **NALC's 2.5× beyond 12/60 is unique** — no other craft escalates again past double time ([[NALC §8.4\|§8.4.G]]).
3. **The ELM reads universal but isn't:** §434.133's "two times" category exists for everyone on paper, and its own Exhibit 434.141c says Mail Handlers: No. Reading the ELM without the exhibit creates a phantom mail-handler penalty entitlement ([[31.02 ELM 434.1 Overtime\|§434.133]] + [[31.03 ELM 434.141 Eligibility and Exhibit\|Exhibit 434.141c]]).
4. **The December exclusion** applies only where a penalty rate exists to exclude — it means nothing at NPMHU and NRLCA ([[NALC §8.4\|NALC §8.4.C]]).
5. **The Mittenthal rule** — no holiday-scheduling manipulation to avoid penalty pay — protects only the two crafts that have penalty pay to protect ([[33.02 Mittenthal 1987 — Penalty overtime and holiday scheduling\|Mittenthal 1987]]).
