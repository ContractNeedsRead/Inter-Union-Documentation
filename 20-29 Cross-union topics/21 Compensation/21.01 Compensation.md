# Compensation

## Why this matters across crafts

The pay articles look interchangeable until you line up the terms: three contracts raise wages 1.3/1.4/1.5% on staggered calendars, and the NPMHU's expired 2022–2025 term delivers 1.3% three years running — the cost of the stalled bargaining now heading into PRA arbitration. And beneath the general-increase rows, the rural craft runs an entirely different compensation universe: evaluated hours, route schedules, mileage allowances.

## Legal layer

- **[[34.01 FLSA 207 Maximum Hours]]** — the §7(b)(2) annual guarantee is the statutory frame for the entire rural compensation article ([[NRLCA §9.2.A.1\|NRLCA §9.2.A.1]]).
- **[[31.11 ELM 444 Rural Letter Carrier Compensation]]** — the ELM's rural-compensation subchapter (transcribed).
- **[[34.02 Postal Reorganization Act Labor Framework]]** — §1207 is the arbitration machinery that now sets the NPMHU's next wage ladder; the 2022–2025 term's flat 1.3% row is exactly what that machinery is about to replace.

## Divergences worth noting

1. **NPMHU's expired term is the pay outlier:** 1.3% × 3 with no escalation, against 1.3/1.4/1.5% ladders at the other three — and the difference is heading into §1207 interest arbitration ([[NPMHU §9.1\|NPMHU §9.1]], [[34.02 Postal Reorganization Act Labor Framework\|PRA §1207]]).
2. **Step-wait economies differ sharply:** NPMHU's 88-week early-step waits are double the 44-week waits of the other crafts' early steps ([[NPMHU §9.2\|NPMHU §9.2]] vs [[APWU §9.2\|APWU §9.2]]).
3. **Only NALC pays a one-time $1,000 Step P boost** ([[NALC §9.9\|NALC §9.9]]).
4. **The rural compensation system is untranslatable row-for-row:** evaluated hours, route classes, EMA at 48¢/mile, Christmas-allowance procedures — no hourly-craft analog exists ([[NRLCA §9.2.C\|NRLCA §9.2.C]], [[NRLCA §9.2.G\|NRLCA §9.2.G]]).
5. **Non-career raises have different shapes:** NALC's CCA $0.50/hr flat bump, APWU's two-tier PSE steps (+$0.50/+$1.50), NPMHU's Grade 4/5 +$0.50 + Step B, NRLCA's +$0.55 + new 156-week step — four different answers to the same retention problem ([[NALC §9.7\|NALC §9.7]], [[APWU §9.8\|APWU §9.8]], [[NPMHU §9.7\|NPMHU §9.7]], [[NRLCA §9.1.H\|NRLCA §9.1.H]]).
