# Safety & Health

## Why this matters across crafts

Safety is the one article where the unions built nearly identical machinery — the same just-cause-adjacent duty language, the same 50-employee committee threshold, the same quarterly cadence — and then split on two things that matter most in practice: whether individual grievances may even be discussed in the safety committee, and whether the rural craft gets a safety committee at all. The APWU's half-million-dollar ergonomics fund is the only money any contract puts behind the program.

## Legal layer

- **[[34.04 OSH Act 19 29 USC 668]]** — §19 is the statutory authority binding the Postal Service to federal agency safety programs; every Art 14.3 cites it (note: the 1998 amendment excludes USPS from §668(a) itself — transcribed in the note).
- **[[31.09 ELM 824]]** — the ELM's PS Form 1767 hazard-reporting machinery the local committees run on.
- **[[32.09 Publication 52 Hazardous Materials]]** — stub; the hazardous-materials list the committees review (source not yet supplied).
- **[[34.17 OWCP Regulations 20 CFR 10]]** — the Health Service / injury-compensation side of the article (partial).

## Divergences worth noting

1. **The rural craft has no safety committee.** NRLCA routes safety through the Article 31 labor-management meetings — no 50-employee threshold, no three-year terms, no Field Council seats ([[NRLCA §14.3\|NRLCA §14.3]]).
2. **APWU's §14.5 is the mirror image of everyone else's:** individual grievances *may* be discussed in its Local Safety and Health Committee; NALC and NPMHU expressly exclude them ([[APWU §14.5\|APWU §14.5]] vs [[NALC §14.5\|NALC §14.5]]).
3. **Only APWU funds the program** — $500,000 a year for ergonomics, disbursed by the Employer-designated chair with quarterly expenditure reports ([[APWU §14.3\|APWU §14.3]]).
4. **NALC alone briefs automation semiannually** and puts ergonomics of new systems on the National Committee's plate ([[NALC §14.1\|NALC §14.1]]).
5. **NPMHU adds a denial remedy** — unreasonably denied meeting requests are referred to the Union's Regional Office ([[NPMHU §14.7\|NPMHU §14.7]]).
