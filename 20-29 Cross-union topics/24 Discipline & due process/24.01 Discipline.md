# Discipline

## Why this matters across crafts

All four contracts open with the same promise — discipline is corrective, not punitive, and nothing happens without just cause — and then diverge in the machinery that makes the promise real: how a short suspension is served, whether a grievance stalls the effective date, what happens to a letter of warning a year later, and what a preference-eligible employee trades away by choosing an MSPB appeal. Those small differences decide whether a discipline sticks.

- Arbitration awards construing the discipline articles are not yet in `_sources/` (gap G1) — the overtime awards ([[33.01 Das 2013 — OTDL priority includes penalty overtime]], [[33.02 Mittenthal 1987 — Penalty overtime and holiday scheduling]]) show the review standard the legal layer applies.

## Divergences worth noting

1. **The NRLCA "day of reflection" exists nowhere else.** A 14-day paper suspension carries a paid non-duty day preceded by a mandatory joint union-management session — a correction mechanism the other three contracts lack ([[NRLCA §16.3\|NRLCA §16.3]]).
2. **NRLCA letters of warning die after one clean year; everyone else's live two.** A rural carrier's LOW is uncitable twice as fast ([[NRLCA §16.8\|NRLCA §16.8]]).
3. **APWU is the only craft where a grievance stalls a ≤14-day suspension's effective date** until settlement or award ([[APWU §16.4\|APWU §16.4]]); NALC and NPMHU avoid the problem by making ≤14-day suspensions paper-only.
4. **The veterans'-preference election splits three ways:** NALC waives beyond Step B and never delays arbitration; NRLCA waives beyond Step 3; APWU and NPMHU waive beyond Step 3 **and** park the arbitration until the MSPB finishes, without back pay for the wait ([[NALC §16.9\|NALC]] vs [[APWU §16.9\|APWU/NPMHU]]).
5. **NPMHU's mid-tier is unique:** the 14-day suspension carries its own 14-day notice + Step-2 extension machinery between the ≤14 and >14 regimes ([[NPMHU §16.5\|NPMHU §16.5]]).
6. **NPMHU demands a signed, dated review writing;** the others require only review and concurrence ([[NPMHU §16.8\|NPMHU §16.8]]).
