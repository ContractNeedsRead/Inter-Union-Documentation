## Summary

Twenty-six archived Maintenance Management Orders catalog the staffing-hour arithmetic for maintenance machine types: per-equipment totals across MM5/MM6, MM-7, MPE-9, and ET-10 levels, with annual servicing/repair/nonproductive-time summaries and PM checklist time summaries. These figures are the staffing arithmetic for Article 38.4 staffing grievances.

## Cross-union comparison

The MMO hour figures are the staffing arithmetic underlying [[APWU Article 38 — Maintenance Craft]] §38.4 staffing grievances; the Maintenance Craft is APWU-only, so the catalog has no counterpart in the other contracts.
