# 32.24 Postal Bulletin Archive — Index

The user's Steward Training archive holds 89 issues of the **Postal Bulletin** (2020 through May 2023) under `_sources/Steward Training/Postal Bulletins/`, one per published date. The Postal Bulletin is the Postal Service's official publication channel for **ELM revisions, handbooks and manual changes, pay and leave policies, and postal regulations** — the instrument through which the ELM's September 2022 revisions (and later ones) are announced. This index makes the issues findable; the issues themselves are not transcribed in the vault.

## Summary

This index covers the complete Postal Bulletin archive in the Steward Training library (89 issues, 2020–2023). The Postal Bulletin is the source channel for ELM updates — the gap-report item G9's instrument — so the index is the entry point for finding the published text of ELM changes that post-date the September 2022 ELM chapters transcribed in area 31. The issues themselves are not transcribed.
