# ELM §444 — Rural Letter Carrier Compensation

## Full text

<!-- §444 -->
### 444 Rural Letter Carrier Compensation

<!-- §444.1 -->
#### 444.1 Regular Rural Carriers

<!-- §444.11 -->
##### 444.11 Guaranteed Annual Wage

The National Rural Letter Carrier Agreement conforms to Section 7(b)(2) of the FLSA. Certain regular rural carriers assigned to a route of 35 hours or more (31 paid miles or more) receive a guaranteed annual wage based on the number of hours, or miles, assigned to their route.

<!-- §444.12 -->
##### 444.12 Base Rate of Pay

The base rate of pay for these rural carriers equals the base annual rate of a 40 hour evaluated route plus overtime at one and one-half times the base hourly rate for all assigned hours (equivalent miles) in excess of 2080 hours (2184 miles) a year. Hours worked in excess of 12 hours a day, or 56 hours in an FLSA workweek, are payable at 150 percent of the carrier’s regular rate.

<!-- §444.2 -->
#### 444.2 Other Rural Carriers

Rural carriers who are not covered under the guarantee provisions of Section 7(b)(2) of the FLSA are compensated at the rate for a 40-mile route in accordance with the compensation provisions in 433 and 434.

## Summary

Recognizes the FLSA §7(b)(2) exemption for regular rural carriers on routes of 35 hours or more (31 paid miles or more), whose guaranteed annual wage is route-based; sets their base rate as the 40-hour evaluated route annual base plus overtime at 1 1/2 times the base hourly rate for assigned hours over 2080 a year (2184 miles), with 150% of the regular rate beyond 12 hours a day or 56 hours in an FLSA workweek; and routes other rural carriers through the §433/§434 compensation provisions at the 40-mile rate.

## Cross-union comparison

This is the FLSA-administration counterpart to the NRLCA evaluated pay system: the guaranteed annual wage and the 2080-hour/2184-mile overtime trigger are the FLSA §7(b)(2) framework behind NRLCA Article 9's evaluated compensation.
