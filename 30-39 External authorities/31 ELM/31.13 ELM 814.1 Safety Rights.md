# ELM §814.1 — Employee Rights and Responsibilities (Safety)

## Full text

<!-- §814.1 -->
### 814.1 Rights

Employees have the right to:

- a. Become actively involved in the Postal Service's safety and health program and be provided a safe and healthful work environment.
- b. Report unsafe and unhealthful working conditions using PS Form 1767, *Report of Hazard, Unsafe Condition, or Practice*.
- c. Consult with management through appropriate employee representatives on safety and health matters such as program effectiveness.
- d. Participate in inspection activities where permissible.
- e. Participate in the safety and health program without fear of:
  - (1) Restraint,
  - (2) Interference,
  - (3) Coercion,
  - (4) Discrimination, or
  - (5) Reprisal.

<!-- §814.2 -->
### 814.2 Responsibilities

All employees are responsible for:

- a. Complying with all OSHA and Postal Service safety and health regulations, procedures, and practices…

## Summary

The ELM's safety-participation charter: every employee may participate in the safety and health program, report hazards on PS Form 1767, consult through representatives, join inspections, and do all of it free of restraint, interference, coercion, discrimination, or reprisal.

## Cross-union comparison

USPS-wide — applies identically across the four crafts; the contract-level counterpart machinery is each union's Article 14 (see [[26.01 Safety & Health]]).
