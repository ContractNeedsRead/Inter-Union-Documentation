# 5 U.S.C. §7513 — Cause and Procedure (MSPB Adverse Actions)

## Full text

<!-- §7513 -->

§ 7513. Cause and procedure

<!-- §7513(a) -->

(a) Under regulations prescribed by the Office of Personnel Management, an agency may take an action covered by this subchapter against an employee only for such cause as will promote the efficiency of the service.

<!-- §7513(b) -->

(b) An employee against whom an action is proposed is entitled to—
- (1) at least 30 days’ advance written notice, unless there is reasonable cause to believe the employee has committed a crime for which a sentence of imprisonment may be imposed, stating the specific reasons for the proposed action;
- (2) a reasonable time, but not less than 7 days, to answer orally and in writing and to furnish affidavits and other documentary evidence in support of the answer;
- (3) be represented by an attorney or other representative; and
- (4) a written decision and the specific reasons therefor at the earliest practicable date.

<!-- §7513(c) -->

(c) An agency may provide, by regulation, for a hearing which may be in lieu of or in addition to the opportunity to answer provided under subsection (b)(2) of this section.

<!-- §7513(d) -->

(d) An employee against whom an action is taken under this section is entitled to appeal to the Merit Systems Protection Board under section 7701 of this title.

<!-- §7513(e) -->

(e) Copies of the notice of proposed action, the answer of the employee when written, a summary thereof when made orally, the notice of decision and reasons therefor, and any order effecting an action covered by this subchapter, together with any supporting material, shall be maintained by the agency and shall be furnished to the Board upon its request and to the employee affected upon the employee’s request.

(Added Pub. L. 95–454, title II, § 204(a), Oct. 13, 1978, 92 Stat. 1136.)

## Summary

Section 7513 is the CSRA adverse-action core: an agency may take an action covered by the subchapter (removal, suspension over 14 days, reduction in grade or pay, furlough of 30 days or less) only for cause that promotes the efficiency of the service. Before action, the employee gets at least thirty days' advance written notice stating specific reasons (shortened on reasonable cause to believe an imprisonable crime), at least seven days to answer orally and in writing with affidavits and documentary evidence, representation by an attorney or other representative, and a written decision with specific reasons at the earliest practicable date. Agencies may add a hearing by regulation. The affected employee may appeal to the Merit Systems Protection Board, and the agency maintains and discloses the proposal, answer, decision, and supporting material. The four contracts' Article 16.9 clauses (Veterans' Preference) key their MSPB-appeal waivers and arbitration-scheduling holds to this cause-and-procedure regime — the "MSPB appeal" those clauses name is the §7513(d) appeal right.

## Cross-union comparison

All four craft agreements route preference eligibles' adverse-action appeals through the MSPB under this section, with contract language choosing between the §7513(d)/MSPB route and the grievance-arbitration procedure; §7513's 30-day notice and efficiency-of-service cause standard are the statutory backdrop for the contracts' own 30-day discipline notices.
