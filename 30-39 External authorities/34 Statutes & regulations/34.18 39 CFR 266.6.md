# 39 CFR §266.6 — Appeal procedure

## Full text

<!-- §266.6 -->
**§ 266.6 Appeal procedure.**

**(a) Appeal procedure.**

(1) If a request for notification of or to inspect, copy, or amend a record is denied, in whole or in part, or if no determination is made within the period prescribed by this part, the requester may appeal to the General Counsel, U.S. Postal Service, 475 L’Enfant Plaza SW., Washington, DC 20260–1101.

(2) The requester must submit an appeal in writing within 90 days of the date of denial, or within 90 days of such request if the appeal is from a failure of the custodian to make a determination. The letter of appeal should include, as applicable:

(i) Reasonable identification of the record to which the requester sought notification, access, or amendment;

(ii) A statement of the Postal Service action or failure to act, and of the relief sought; and

(iii) A copy of the request, of the notification of denial, and of any other related correspondence, if any.

(3) Any record found on appeal to be incomplete, inaccurate, not relevant, or not timely, must be appropriately amended within 30 working days of the date of such findings.

(4) The decision of the General Counsel constitutes the final decision of the Postal Service on the right of the requester to be notified of; inspect, copy, or otherwise have access to; or change or update a record. The decision on the appeal must be in writing and, in the event of a denial, must set forth the reasons for such denial and state the individual’s right to obtain judicial review in a district court. An indexed file of decisions on appeals must be maintained by the General Counsel.

## Summary

39 CFR Part 266 implements the Privacy Act (5 U.S.C. §552a) for Postal Service records. §266.6 is the appeal stage of that machinery: a requester whose request to be notified of, inspect, copy, or amend a record is denied — or who gets no timely determination — may appeal in writing to the USPS General Counsel within 90 days, identifying the record, the action or failure to act, and the relief sought, with copies of the underlying request and denial. Records found on appeal to be incomplete, inaccurate, irrelevant, or untimely must be amended within 30 working days. The General Counsel's written decision is final agency action: a denial must state reasons and the right to district-court judicial review, and the General Counsel keeps an indexed file of appeal decisions. The user's archived note describes the companion §266.5 machinery: written requests to the record custodian or the Manager, Records Office (or the OIG FOIA/Privacy Officer for OIG records), marked "Privacy Act Request," with Federal Register identification details; acknowledgment within 10 days; inspection during business hours with identification; and no removal of records from premises.

## Cross-union comparison

Not a bargaining subject — this is the USPS's Privacy Act regulation, uniform across all four crafts and their members. It is the regulatory route members use when contract information requests (each agreement's Article 31) do not reach personal records.
