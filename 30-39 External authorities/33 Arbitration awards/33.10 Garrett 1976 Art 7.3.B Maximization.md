## Holding

The seven Taunton grievances alleging violation of Article VII (7), Section 3 — the requirement that the Employer maximize the number of full-time employees and minimize part-time flexibles without fixed schedules, including the six-month/8-hours-within-10 conversion trigger — are returned to the parties for local settlement under the opinion. The Taunton Postmaster should promptly schedule at least one part-time flexible Clerk on an experimental basis (8 hours within 10, same 5 days each week, same assignment); if after a fair trial such scheduling impairs efficiency it may be discontinued and the grievances withdrawn; if no significant inefficiency results over six months, the assignment converts to a full-time regular position — repeated experimentally until maximization is achieved as required by the Article.

## Summary

Garrett set the procedural template for enforcing the maximization clause at the local level: schedule a PTF experimentally on a full-time pattern, measure efficiency over six months, convert absent demonstrated inefficiency, and repeat. The award reads the six-month rule as an evidentiary trigger and directs local implementation rather than a flat order.

## Cross-union comparison

The 90%-full-time floor for 200+-workyear offices and the six-month PTF conversion trigger are the shared Article 7.3 framework the NALC's Maximization MOUs ([[15.01 NALC MOUs]]) implement for city carriers; Garrett is the APWU clerk-craft enforcement template for the same clause.
