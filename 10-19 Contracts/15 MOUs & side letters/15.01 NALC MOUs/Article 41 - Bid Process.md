# Re: Article 41 - Bid Process

## Full text

<!-- p.226 -->

Re: Article 41 - Bid Process

The parties agree that where telephone bidding is an alternative form of bidding, bids may be submitted by telephone. When computerized and telephone bidding are available to all employees in an installation, telephone and computerized bidding is mandatory. Date: August 14, 2000

## Summary

Permits telephone bids where telephone bidding is an alternative form, and makes telephone and computerized bidding mandatory where both are available to all employees in an installation.
