# Re: CCA Advanced Annual Leave

## Full text

Re: CCA Advanced Annual Leave

The parties agree that, upon completion of an initial 360-day appointment as a City Carrier Assistant (CCA), and immediately upon reappointment to any subsequent appointments thereafter, CCAs will be advanced forty (40) hours of annual leave. Upon initial implementation, CCAs will receive annual leave prorated to the end of their 360day term.

## Summary

Advances 40 hours of annual leave to CCAs upon completing an initial 360-day appointment and upon each reappointment, prorated at initial implementation.
