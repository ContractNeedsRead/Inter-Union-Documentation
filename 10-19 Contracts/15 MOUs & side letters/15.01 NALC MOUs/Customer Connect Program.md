# Re: Customer Connect Program

## Full text

<!-- p.223 -->

Re: Customer Connect Program

The National Association of Letter Carriers, AFL-CIO and United States Postal Service mutually recognize that revenue growth through the increased use of Postal Service products and services is vital to the current and future success of the Postal Service. To that end, the parties jointly developed the Customer Connect Program in which city letter carriers use their access and special relationships with customers to encourage increased use of Postal Service products and services. The parties reemphasize their joint commitment to the growth and long-term success of the Customer Connect Program and pledge to continue to work jointly at all levels of our organizations to enhance this important effort. Date: September 11, 2007

## Summary

Reemphasizes the joint Customer Connect Program, in which city carriers use customer relationships to encourage Postal Service product and service use, with continued joint work at all levels.
