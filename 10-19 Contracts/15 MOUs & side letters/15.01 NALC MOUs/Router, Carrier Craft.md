# Re: Router, Carrier Craft

## Full text

<!-- p.227 -->

Re: Router, Carrier Craft

1. Router is a Grade 1 city letter carrier assignment. 2.   Router duties consist of casing, routing and sequencing of mail for a specific group of routes. Assignments may include specific street duties as reflected in the assignment posting. 3. Router assignments shall be formed and bid as full-time duty assignments. Part-time router work assign- ments may be utilized consistent with 4 below. 4. The number of full-time router assignments shall be determined consistent with Article 7, Section 3 of the National Agreement. 5. The notice inviting bids shall include a listing of routes for which router’s duties will be performed by the posted assignment.

<!-- p.229 -->

6. A router may be temporarily moved from his/her bid assignment only in “unanticipated circumstances,” pursuant to the provisions of Article 41, Section l.C.4. of the National Agreement. 7.   A Grade 1 replacement router may be utilized where practical to cover the nonscheduled days of other router assignments. Date: November 21, 2001

## Summary

Router is a Grade 1 city letter carrier assignment (casing, routing, and sequencing for a group of routes, with possible street duties); full-time router assignments are formed and bid consistent with Article 7.3, with the bid notice listing covered routes; temporary moves only in unanticipated circumstances under Article 41.1.C.4; Grade 1 replacement routers may cover nonscheduled days.
