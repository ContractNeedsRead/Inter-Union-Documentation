# Re: Elimination of Step CC Pay Rate in CCA Schedule

## Full text

<!-- p.174 -->

Re: Elimination of Step CC Pay Rate in CCA Schedule

1. Effective June 19, 2021, the Step CC pay rate in RSC Q4 and Q5 (Table Three) will be eliminated. Step BB and its pay rate will become the new entry step for new CCA hires. The new waiting period from Step BB to Step AA will be 52 weeks.

2. All CCAs in Step CC of RSC Q4 and Q5 as of June 19, 2021, will advance to Step BB and will maintain their current time-in-step credit toward Step AA.

3. CCAs in Step BB of RSC Q4 and Q5 as of June 19, 2021, will have 12 weeks added to their current time-in-step credit toward Step AA.

## Summary

Effective June 19, 2021, eliminates the Step CC rate in RSC Q4/Q5 (Table Three), makes Step BB the entry step with a 52-week BB-to-AA wait, advances Step CC CCAs to BB with time-in-step credit, and adds 12 weeks of credit to Step BB CCAs toward AA.
