# Re: City Carrier Assistant Transfers

## Full text

<!-- p.190 -->

Re: City Carrier Assistant Transfers

The parties agree to establish a one-year pilot program in at least two Districts to study possible approaches to facilitate the voluntary reassignment of city carrier assistants (CCAs) from one installation to a different installation within the same District. The parties may extend or expand this pilot program to include opportunities beyond district boundaries by mutual agreement. This memorandum expires with the 2023 National Agreement.

## Summary

Establishes a one-year pilot in at least two Districts to study voluntary CCA reassignment within a District, extendable by mutual agreement; expires with the 2023 Agreement.
