# Re: Managed Service Point Scans

## Full text

Re: Managed Service Point Scans

The parties agree that the Managed Service Points (MSPs) will be removed from the street delivery portions of city letter carrier routes. The removal of the scans will take place no later than 60 days from the ratification date of the 2019 collective bargaining agreement.

## Summary

Removes Managed Service Point scans from the street delivery portions of city carrier routes within 60 days of the 2019 Agreement's ratification.
