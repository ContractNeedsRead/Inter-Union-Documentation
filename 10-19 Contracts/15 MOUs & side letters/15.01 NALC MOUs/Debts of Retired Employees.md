# Re: Debts of Retired Employees

## Full text

<!-- p.218 -->

Re: Debts of Retired Employees

If a retiree receives an invoice or notice of debt determination from the Postal Service after his/her separation date, the retiree may initiate a grievance though the local branch of his/her former employing office. Such dispute must be initiated directly to Step B. The grievance must be received at Step B within thirty days from the date the retiree first learned, or may reasonably have been expected to have learned, of the Postal Service’s intent to collect the debt. Upon receipt of a dispute initiated under this memorandum, the Step B team will obtain any necessary records relevant to the debt from the relevant office (i.e. Eagan Accounting Services or the former employee’s installation). Once the records are obtained, the Step B team will give priority consideration to discuss and issue a decision on the case. Step B impasses appealed to arbitration will be processed pursuant to Article 15.4.C of the National Agreement. If a retiree also files an appeal under the Debt Collection Act, the grievance shall be closed.

## Summary

A retiree receiving a post-separation debt notice may grieve directly to Step B within 30 days of learning of the debt; the Step B team obtains the relevant Eagan or installation records and gives the case priority; impasses arbitrate under Article 15.4.C; a Debt Collection Act appeal closes the grievance.
