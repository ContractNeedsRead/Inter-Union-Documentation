# Re: Article 17.7.D Payroll Deductions/Allotments

## Full text

Re: Article 17.7.D Payroll Deductions/Allotments

No later than January 4, 2008, the Postal Service will increase the maximum allotments in the existing program by providing one additional allotment for the use of NALC bargaining unit employees. Date: September 11, 2007

## Summary

By January 4, 2008, the Postal Service adds one additional payroll allotment for NALC bargaining unit employees.
