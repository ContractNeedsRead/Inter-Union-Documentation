# Re: Mutual Exchanges

## Full text

Re: Mutual Exchanges

The parties agree that in applying the relevant provisions of Section 351.6 of the Employee and Labor Relations Manual, city letter carriers in grades CC-01 and CC-02 are considered as being in the same grade. This agreement applies solely to determining whether employees are eligible for mutual exchanges. Date: September 11, 2007

## Summary

For ELM 351.6 mutual-exchange eligibility, treats city letter carriers in grades CC-01 and CC-02 as being in the same grade.
