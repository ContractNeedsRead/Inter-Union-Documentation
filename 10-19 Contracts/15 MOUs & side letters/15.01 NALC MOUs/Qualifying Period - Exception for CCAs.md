# Re: Qualifying Period - Exception for City Carrier Assistants

## Full text

<!-- p.183 -->

Re: Qualifying Period - Exception for City Carrier

Assistants The parties agree that City Carrier Assistants with a minimum of 90 days of continuous service as a CCA prior to conversion

<!-- p.185 -->

to career status will be exempt from the 90-Day Qualifying Period in ELM 512.313. Any break in service as required by Appendix B, Section I.1.b will not impact this continuous service requirement. The ELM will be updated to reflect this change.

## Summary

Exempts CCAs with 90 or more days of continuous service from the ELM 512.313 90-day qualifying period upon conversion, with the Appendix B break-in-service unaffected; the ELM will be updated.
