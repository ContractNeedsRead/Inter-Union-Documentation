# Non-Traditional Full-Time Duty Assignments in Retail Operations, Level 20 and Below Offices

## Full text

<!-- p.289 -->
MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING
BETWEEN THE
UNITED STATES POSTAL SERVICE
AND THE
AMERICAN POSTAL WORKERS UNION, AFL-CIO
Re: Non-Traditional Full-Time Duty Assignments in Retail
Operations, Level 20 and Below Offices
The parties agree that for retail operations in Level 20 and
below offices, non-traditional full-time duty assignments may
be created when the Union can demonstrate the need for such
non-traditional duty assignments and it is economically and
operationally advantageous to do so.

## Summary

In retail operations of Level 20 and below offices, NTFT duty assignments may be created when the Union demonstrates the need and it is economically and operationally advantageous.

Modifies the Clerk Craft posting framework for small-office retail NTFTs: [[APWU Article 37 — Clerk Craft]]
