# Pending Qualifications for In-Service, Transfers and Entrance Hiring

## Full text

<!-- p.427 -->
MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING
BETWEEN THE
UNITED STATES POSTAL SERVICE
AND THE
AMERICAN POSTAL WORKERS UNION, AFL-CIO
Re: Pending Qualifications for In-Service, Transfers and
Entrance Hiring
Employees entering the Maintenance Craft through in-
service, transfers and entrance hiring shall be subject to the
same provisions as existing Maintenance Craft employees
contained in Articles 38.5.C.3 and 38.5.C.4, provided there
were training requirements contained on the original Notice
of Intent.
If the employee successfully completes prerequisite course(s)
but fails the requisite training requirement(s) identified on
the original Notice of Intent, the employee shall be placed
in an authorized vacant skilled or semi-skilled occupational
group duty assignment for which they are qualified within the
installation. If there are no skilled or semi-skilled vacancies,
<!-- p.428 -->
the employee shall be placed into a vacant Level 4 Laborer
Custodial occupational group position within the installation.
If there are no vacancies, the employee will be an unassigned
regular (UAR) as a Laborer Custodial and mirror the tour and
nonscheduled days identified on the original Notice of Intent,
even if the authorized Level 4 Laborer Custodial staffing
levels are exceeded. If there are multiple positions available
for skilled, semi-skilled, or Laborer Custodial positions the
employee will have a choice of which position they receive.

## Summary

Employees entering the Maintenance Craft through in-service, transfers, or entrance hiring are subject to Articles 38.5.C.3 and 38.5.C.4 where the original Notice of Intent carried training requirements; those failing the requisites are placed in a vacant qualified skilled or semi-skilled assignment, then a vacant Level 4 Laborer Custodial position, then unassigned Laborer Custodial status mirroring the NOI tour and nonscheduled days (even above authorized staffing), with position choice where multiple vacancies exist.
