# Extend Minimizing Excessing MOU

## Full text

<!-- p.325 -->
MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING
BETWEEN THE
UNITED STATES POSTAL SERVICE
AND THE
AMERICAN POSTAL WORKERS UNION, AFL-CIO
Re: Extend Minimizing Excessing MOU
The parties agree to extend as indicated below provisions of the
Memorandum of Understanding (MOU), Minimizing Excessing,
which expire with the term of the 2024-2027 Agreement.
• The parties agree that the provisions of the above
referenced MOU are applicable and will continue until
September 20, 2027, or for the term of the 2024 National
Agreement, whichever is longer, except as provided
for in the Step 4 resolution in case number Q10C-4Q-C
14317802/HQTT20140774 or as modified by any other
agreements.

## Summary

Extends the Minimizing Excessing MOU provisions through September 20, 2027 (or the agreement's term, whichever is longer), except as provided in the Step 4 resolution Q10C-4Q-C 14317802/HQTT20140774 or as modified by other agreements.

## Contract callout

Incorporates the Minimizing Excessing protections for the agreement's term: [[APWU §12.5]]
