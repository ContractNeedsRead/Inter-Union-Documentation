# Contracting or Insourcing of Contracted Service

## Full text

<!-- p.363 -->
MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING
BETWEEN THE
UNITED STATES POSTAL SERVICE
AND THE
AMERICAN POSTAL WORKERS UNION, AFL-CIO
Re: Contracting or Insourcing of Contracted Service
It is understood that if the service can be performed at a cost equal
<!-- p.364 -->
to or less than that of contract service, when a fair comparison
is made of all reasonable costs, the work will be performed in-
house.

## Summary

Where a fair comparison of all reasonable costs shows the service can be performed at a cost equal to or less than contract service, the work will be performed in-house.

## Contract callout

Implements the Article 32.1 cost-comparison standard for insourcing: [[APWU §32.1]]
