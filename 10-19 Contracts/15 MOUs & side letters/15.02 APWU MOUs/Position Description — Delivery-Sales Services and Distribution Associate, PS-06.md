# Position Description: Delivery/Sales Services and Distribution Associate, PS-06

## Full text

<!-- p.378 -->
MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING
BETWEEN THE
UNITED STATES POSTAL SERVICE
AND THE
AMERICAN POSTAL WORKERS UNION, AFL-CIO
Re: Position Description: Delivery/Sales Services and
Distribution Associate, PS-06
1. The Employer agrees to establish the position of Delivery/
Sales Services and Distribution Associate, Standard
Position SP-2643, PS-6, to be filled by the senior qualified
bidder in the Clerk Craft. The Delivery/Sales Services and
Distribution Associate position will contain the existing
duties and responsibilities of Sales Services and Distribution
Associate, PS-6 and Clerk/Special Delivery Messenger,
PS-6.
2.	The Postal Service agrees to implement this combined
position description by July 2, 2011.

## Summary

Establishes Delivery/Sales Services and Distribution Associate, Standard Position SP-2643, PS-6, filled by the senior qualified Clerk Craft bidder, combining Sales Services and Distribution Associate PS-6 and Clerk/Special Delivery Messenger PS-6 duties, implemented by July 2, 2011.

Modifies the Article 37 position list with the combined SP-2643 position: [[APWU Article 37 — Clerk Craft]]
