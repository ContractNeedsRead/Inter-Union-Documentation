# Postal Uniform & Work Clothing Program Task Force

## Full text

<!-- p.355 -->
MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING
BETWEEN THE
UNITED STATES POSTAL SERVICE
AND THE
AMERICAN POSTAL WORKERS UNION, AFL-CIO
Re: Postal Uniform & Work Clothing Program Task Force
The parties agree to establish a uniform and Work Clothing
Program Task Force to identify alternative methods of
administering the uniform and work clothing program, such
as through a centralized program that reduces the number
of uniform venders or the elimination of the uniform and
work clothing program allowances due to the Postal Service
providing uniform items directly to employees.
The Task Force will be administered by the Vice President, Labor
Relations and President, American Postal Workers Union, or
their designees. The Task Force will meet within ninety (90) days
of the ratification of the 2024-2027 National Agreement. This
Task Force is effective with the signing of this MOU and will
<!-- p.356 -->
continue for the term of the 2024-2027 National Agreement.
The Task Force will consist of three (3) members appointed by the
APWU and three (3) members appointed by the Postal Service.
Additionally, either party reserves the right to provide a
subject matter expert(s) for consultation to this Task Force.
This Task Force is not intended to alter or amend the provisions
of the Uniform Control Committee and the rules of procedure
established in Article 26. In addition to the identification of
alternative methods, this Task Force will be responsible for
monitoring the application and success of any new methods
of administration of the uniform and work clothing program.

## Summary

Establishes a uniform and Work Clothing Program Task Force (three and three members, subject-matter experts permitted) to identify alternative administration methods such as vendor consolidation or direct item provision, administered by the VP Labor Relations and APWU President, meeting within 90 days of ratification for the agreement's term; does not alter the Uniform Control Committee or Article 26 procedures.
