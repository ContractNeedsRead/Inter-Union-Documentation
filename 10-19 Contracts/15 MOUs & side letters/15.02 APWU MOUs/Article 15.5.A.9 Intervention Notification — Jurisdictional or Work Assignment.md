# Article 15.5.A.9 Intervention Notification - Jurisdictional or Work Assignment

## Full text

<!-- p.343 -->
MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING
BETWEEN THE
UNITED STATES POSTAL SERVICE
AND THE
AMERICAN POSTAL WORKERS UNION, AFL-CIO
Re: Article 15.5.A.9 Intervention Notification – Jurisdictional
or Work Assignment
Pursuant to Article 15.5.A.9, the parties agree that in any
arbitration proceeding in which a Union feels that its interests
may be affected, it shall be entitled to intervene and participate
in such arbitration proceeding. The parties agree that within 30
days of receiving the appeal to arbitration of jurisdictional or
work assignment cases the interested non-party Union shall be
provided a copy of the moving papers.
Immediately upon scheduling the jurisdictional or work assignment
cases for arbitration, the interested non-party Union shall be
entitled to intervene, and shall be informed of the date, time and
location of the arbitration.

## Summary

Under Article 15.5.A.9, an interested non-party Union receives the moving papers within 30 days of the arbitration appeal in jurisdictional or work assignment cases and is entitled to intervene, with notice of the hearing's date, time, and location upon scheduling.

## Contract callout

Implements the Article 15.5.A.9 intervention procedure: [[APWU §15.5]]
