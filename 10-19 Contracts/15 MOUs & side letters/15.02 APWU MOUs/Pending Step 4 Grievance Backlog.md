# Pending Step 4 Grievance Backlog

MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING
BETWEEN THE
UNITED STATES POSTAL SERVICE
AND THE
AMERICAN POSTAL WORKERS UNION, AFL-CIO
Re: Pending Step 4 Grievance Backlog
The parties mutually agree that within 60 days of ratification
of the Tentative Agreement, the National parties will set up a
process to meet and discuss all pending Step 4 Appeals with
the aim of engaging in good faith discussions and negotiations
to address pending National disputes. All pending cases
will be reviewed and discussed with the goal to resolve the
grievances, through settlement or closure, beginning with,
and with particular attention to, cases filed prior to 2015. The
review will be completed within 90 days after it commences,
unless the parties have mutually agreed to extend the process.
In addition, after discussion of the disputes listed below, it is
determined that there is no National interpretive issue and the
grievances, will be administratively closed without prejudice
to either party’s position within 60 days of ratification. All
Regional cases held behind each National case listed below,
on a one-time basis, will also be administratively closed
provided that the issue of the National case is the sole issue
contained in the Regional cases held in abeyance.

## Summary

Within 60 days of ratification the National parties establish a process to review and resolve all pending Step 4 appeals (90-day review unless extended), with eight listed National cases administratively closed without prejudice within 60 days (Regional cases held solely on those issues closed once) and three listed NRP cases settled.
