# Article 32 Exceptions

## Full text

<!-- p.366 -->
MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING
BETWEEN THE
UNITED STATES POSTAL SERVICE
AND THE
AMERICAN POSTAL WORKERS UNION, AFL-CIO
Re: Article 32 Exceptions
Article 32.1.A does not apply to this list of work items below. If
other work is being combined with a work item from this list it is
understood that the provisions of Article 32.1.A may be applicable
to the other work.
This list of exceptions shall not preclude the Postal Service from
assigning this work to the bargaining unit. This list of items below
does not prejudice either party from contending Article 32.1 does
or does not apply to the work because of its exclusion from the
list below.
1. Septic (sewer) tank work and outflow to leach (drain) field.
2. Exterior trenching or excavation with (driven) heavy
equipment related to structural repair and utilities work
outside the building.
3. Underground storage tank work.
4. Gasoline, oil, diesel dispensing equipment work except for
preventative maintenance, repair or replacement of hoses
and nozzles.
5.	 Initial installation or total replacement of irrigation systems.
6. Replacement of roof top mounted chiller unit rated over 40
tons.
7. Roof gutter replacement, including downspout.
<!-- p.367 -->
8.	 Complete roof replacement.
9.	Laying new asphalt and resurfacing of paved surfaces
that requires milling; sealing or resealing more than
15,000 square feet of pavement.
10. Concrete work (site preparation, framing, placing, etc.) in
excess of four (4) yards concrete.
11. Work involving Class I or Class II Asbestos Containing
Building Materials (including PACBM).
12. Electrical work involving 400 AMP or greater.
13. Escalator work.
14. Building security systems work. Does not include gates,
doors, mag locks, CCTV and other physical security
components.
15.	 Mold abatement.
16.	 Animal or pest control.
17. LAN/MPI complete system installs (refer to Q00C-4Q
04003182 Das Award).
18. Lawncare in excess of 300,000 square feet (refer to
B7T-3D-C 22868 Step 4).
19. Elevator service.
20. Water well repairs and maintenance.
21. Installation or replacement of permanent security
fencing.

## Summary

Lists twenty-one work items to which Article 32.1.A does not apply (septic/sewer work through permanent security fencing, with the LAN/MPI and lawncare items cross-referenced to the Q00C-4Q 04003182 Das award and B7T-3D-C 22868 Step 4); combining other work with a listed item may bring Article 32.1.A back into play; the list does not preclude bargaining-unit assignment and does not prejudice either party's Article 32.1 contentions.
