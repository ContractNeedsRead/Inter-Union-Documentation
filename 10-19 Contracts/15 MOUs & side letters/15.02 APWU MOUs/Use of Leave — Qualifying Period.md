# Use of Leave - Qualifying Period

## Full text

<!-- p.312 -->
MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING
BETWEEN THE
UNITED STATES POSTAL SERVICE
AND THE
AMERICAN POSTAL WORKERS UNION, AFL-CIO
Re: Use of Leave – Qualifying Period
The parties agree that postal support employees (PSEs) with
a minimum of 90 days of continuous service as a PSE prior to
conversion to career status will be exempt from the 90-day
<!-- p.313 -->
qualifying period as written in the Employee and Labor Relations
Manual (ELM) 512.313. Any break in service as required by
Appendix A, Section 1.b will not impact this continuous service
requirement.

## Summary

PSEs with at least 90 days of continuous service before conversion are exempt from the ELM 512.313 90-day qualifying period; the Appendix A Section 1.b break in service does not affect the continuous-service requirement.

## Contract callout

Modifies the ELM 512.313 qualifying period for converting PSEs: [[APWU §10.2]]
