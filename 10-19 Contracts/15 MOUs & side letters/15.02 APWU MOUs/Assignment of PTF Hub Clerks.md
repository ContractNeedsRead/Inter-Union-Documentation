# Assignment of PTF Hub Clerks

## Full text

<!-- p.379 -->
MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING
BETWEEN THE
UNITED STATES POSTAL SERVICE
AND THE
AMERICAN POSTAL WORKERS UNION, AFL-CIO
Re: Assignment of PTF Hub Clerks
This provision covers part-time flexible (PTF) Clerk Craft
employees who may be required to work in installations other
than their home office (installation).
PTFs shall be scheduled for work outside their home office
(installation) in accordance with the following:
1. within thirty (30) days of the effective date of the 2024
National Agreement, PTFs shall notify their postmaster
in writing their preference to not work in offices in other
installations or indicate the offices where they will
volunteer to work within a 50-mile radius of their home
office (may indicate all). PTFs unavailable for the entire
thirty (30) day period due to leave shall provide their written
preference within thirty (30) days of returning to work;
2.	 within fourteen (14) days of the effective date of the
conversion, hiring, or assignment, PTFs shall notify their
postmaster in writing their preference to not work in offices
and/or installations or indicate the offices where they
will volunteer to work within a 50-mile radius of their
home office (may indicate all);
3. if a PTF wants to rescind or modify his or her preference
to not work in other offices and/or installations, the
employee must provide fourteen (14) days notice to his or
her postmaster in writing. The change will be effective no
later than fourteen (14) days from the date of notice;
4. preference modifications will be allowed no more than
three (3) times in a calendar year. Upon request, a copy of
each written preference modification will be provided to
the appropriate Union official.
<!-- p.380 -->
5. The pecking order for assignment will be:
a. PTFs who have indicated a preference to work outside
their installation;
b. available and qualified postal support employees;
c. PTFs that have preferenced not to work in offices in
other installations.
6. A part-time flexible clerk who is required to work
outside his/her home office is entitled to compensable
travel time or mileage as determined by applying the
applicable provisions of Section 438 of the ELM and
Chapter 7 of Handbook F-15 and as documented in
accordance with Section 260 of Handbook F-21. The
part-time flexible clerk shall properly complete a PS
Form 1164 or PS Form 1164e and submit it to the home
office postmaster or designee on a weekly basis.
This eliminates the continuation of the MOU, Re: Pilot
Program – Memorandum of Understanding (MOU), Re:
Assignment of PTF Hub Clerks, on page 438 of the 2021-
2024 National Agreement.
(See Questions and Answers regarding Part-Time Flexible Clerks
Working in Other Installations and Memo)

## Summary

PTF Hub Clerk rules: 30-day (and 14-day for new conversions/hires) written preferences to work or not work outside the home office within a 50-mile radius, three calendar-year modifications with Union copies; the pecking order runs PTF volunteers, then available qualified PSEs, then non-volunteering PTFs; outside-home work earns compensable travel time or mileage under ELM 438, Handbook F-15 Chapter 7, and F-21 Section 260 documentation on PS Forms 1164/1164e; replaces the 2021-2024 pilot MOU.
