# Local Implementation

## Full text

<!-- p.359 -->
MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING
BETWEEN THE
UNITED STATES POSTAL SERVICE
AND THE
AMERICAN POSTAL WORKERS UNION, AFL-CIO
Re: Local Implementation
It is hereby agreed by the United States Postal Service and the
American Postal Workers Union, AFL-CIO, that the following
procedures will apply to the implementation of Article 30 during
the 2024 local implementation period.
1. The 30 consecutive day period for 2025 local
implementation will commence on October 6, 2025, and
terminate on December 5, 2025.
If no party provides written notification of its intent to invoke
the local implementation process prior to October 20,
2025, presently effective Memoranda of Understanding
not inconsistent or in conflict with the 2024 National
Agreement shall remain in effect during the term of this
Agreement. Initial proposals must be exchanged within the
first twenty-one (21) days of the 30 consecutive day local
implementation period.
2. In the event that any issue(s) remains in dispute at the end
of the thirty (30) consecutive day local implementation
period, each party shall identify such issue(s) in writing.
Initialed copies of this written statement and copies of all
proposals and counterproposals pertinent to the issue(s)
in dispute will be furnished by the appropriate Local party
to the appropriate management official at the Grievance/
Arbitration Processing Center, of the Employer with copies
<!-- p.360 -->
to the postmaster, Local Union President and the Union’s
Regional representative no later than January 9, 2026.
Inclusion of any matter in the written statement does not
necessarily reflect the agreement of either of the parties
that such matter is properly subject to local implementation.
3. The appropriate management official at the Area office
and the Regional Union representative shall attempt to
resolve the matters in dispute within seventy-five (75) days
after the expiration of the 60-day local implementation
period. The appropriate management official at the Area
office and the Regional Union representative will have full
authority to resolve all issues still in dispute.
4. If the parties identified in Paragraph 3 above are unable to
reach agreement at the Regional Level during the seventy-
five (75) day period provided for above, the issue(s) may be
appealed to final and binding arbitration by the National
Union President or the Vice President, Labor Relations no
later than twenty-one (21) days of the end of the seventy-
five (75) day period.
5. The parties at the Area Level will select sufficient
arbitrators from the Regular Contract Panel to ensure that
issues appealed are heard within 60 days of the appeal to
arbitration.
6. Where there is no agreement and the matter is not
referred to the appropriate management official at the
Grievance/Arbitration Processing Center or to arbitration,
the provision(s), if any, of the former Local Memorandum
of Understanding shall apply unless inconsistent with or in
conflict with the 2024 National Agreement.
7. Where a dispute exists as to whether an item in the former
Local Memorandum of Understanding is inconsistent or in
conflict with the 2024 National Agreement, such dispute
will be processed in accordance with the procedures
outlined in two (2) through four (4) above. Items declared
to be inconsistent or in conflict shall remain in effect until
four (4) months have elapsed from the conclusion of the
<!-- p.361 -->
local implementation period under the 2024 National
Agreement.
This Memorandum of Understanding expires with the expiration
of the 2024 National Agreement.

## Summary

Article 30 implementation calendar: the 30-day period runs October 6 through December 5, 2025; LMOUs continue absent October 20, 2025 notification; initial proposals exchange within 21 days; written dispute identification with proposals and counterproposals to the Processing Center by January 9, 2026; 75-day Regional resolution; 21-day National arbitration appeal; arbitrator selection for 60-day hearings; former LMOU provisions apply where nothing is referred; inconsistent/conflicting items remain in effect four months past the implementation period; expires with the agreement.
