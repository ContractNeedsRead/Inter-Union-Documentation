# Clerical Work

## Full text

<!-- p.273 -->
MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING
BETWEEN THE
UNITED STATES POSTAL SERVICE
AND THE
AMERICAN POSTAL WORKERS UNION, AFL-CIO
Re: Clerical Work
When non-managerial or non-supervisory work not otherwise
excluded by Article 1.2 which was being performed by supervisors
<!-- p.274 -->
is no longer performed by supervisors, then it must be assigned
to Clerk Craft employees.

## Summary

Non-managerial, non-supervisory work not excluded by Article 1.2 that supervisors stop performing must be assigned to Clerk Craft employees.

## Contract callout

Incorporates the Article 1.6 bargaining-unit-work rule for supervisory clerical work: [[APWU §1.6]]
