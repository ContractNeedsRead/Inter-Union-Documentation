# Article 37 eReassign Task Force

## Full text

<!-- p.403 -->
MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING
BETWEEN THE
UNITED STATES POSTAL SERVICE
AND THE
AMERICAN POSTAL WORKERS UNION, AFL-CIO
Re: Article 37 eReassign Task Force
The parties have agreed in the Memorandum of Understanding
(MOU) on Residual Vacancies – Clerk Craft to establish a Task
Force, within six (6) months of the signing of the 2015 Collective
Bargaining Agreement, to explore ways to expedite the eReassign
posting and placement process.
Among the issues to be addressed will be the feasibility of
combining the 21-day Clerk Craft posting and the regular
eReassign posting processes.
The Task Force will consist of three (3) members appointed by the
APWU and three (3) members appointed by the Postal Service.

## Summary

Task Force (three and three members) established within six months of the 2015 Agreement to explore expediting the eReassign posting and placement process, including combining the 21-day Clerk Craft posting with regular eReassign.
