# Pilot Grievance-Arbitration Procedures

## Full text

<!-- p.341 -->
MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING
BETWEEN THE
UNITED STATES POSTAL SERVICE
AND THE
AMERICAN POSTAL WORKERS UNION, AFL-CIO
Re: Pilot Grievance-Arbitration Procedures
The parties agree to meet within 90 days from the signing of the
National Agreement to jointly develop and pilot new grievance/
arbitration procedures designed to effectively ensure local
contract compliance, improve the labor climate and foster more
professional relationships.

## Summary

The parties meet within 90 days of signing to jointly develop and pilot new grievance/arbitration procedures designed to ensure local contract compliance, improve the labor climate, and foster professional relationships.
