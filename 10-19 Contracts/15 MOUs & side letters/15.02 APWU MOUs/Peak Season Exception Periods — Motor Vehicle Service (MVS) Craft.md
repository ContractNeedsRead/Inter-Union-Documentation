# Peak Season Exception Periods - Motor Vehicle Service (MVS) Craft

## Full text




<!-- p.429 -->
## Summary

August 17, 2016 memorandum: reporting-periods 3 and 4 peak season for Function 3A with Holiday Transportation Assistants (HTAs); annuitant reemployment priority consistent with NDAA; HTA/annuitant pay at the PSE MVO Level 7 or PSE TTO Level 8 rates (exceptions by Union concurrence); PTF straight-time preference; OTDL priority before HTA overtime with Article 8.4.G applying and PTF priority scheduling; weekly National reports of HTA hires, rates, and hours.

## Contract callout

Modifies the Article 39 staffing with the MVS peak season exception: [[APWU Article 39 — Motor Vehicle Craft]]
