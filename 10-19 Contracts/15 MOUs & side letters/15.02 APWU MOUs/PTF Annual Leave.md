# PTF Annual Leave

## Full text

<!-- p.313 -->
MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING
BETWEEN THE
UNITED STATES POSTAL SERVICE
AND THE
AMERICAN POSTAL WORKERS UNION, AFL-CIO
Re: PTF Annual Leave
The parties agree that forty (40) hours of annual leave will be
advanced to part-time flexible (PTF) employees, prorated to
the end of the leave year for their first leave year as a PTF, and
<!-- p.314 -->
annually thereafter, unless and until the employee converts to
full-time status.

## Summary

PTFs are advanced 40 hours of annual leave, prorated to the leave-year end for their first PTF leave year and annually thereafter, until conversion to full-time.

Modifies the PTF annual leave advance rules: [[APWU §10.2]]
