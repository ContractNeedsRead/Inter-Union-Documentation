# Return of Aging Residual Duty Assignments to Local Installation

## Full text

<!-- p.428 -->
MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING
BETWEEN THE
UNITED STATES POSTAL SERVICE
AND THE
AMERICAN POSTAL WORKERS UNION, AFL-CIO
Re: Return of Aging Residual Duty Assignments to Local
Installation
1. Upon mutual agreement between management and the
Local Union President (or their designee), a bid for a
vacant Maintenance Craft duty assignment that was
previously posted at that installation, and has been
<!-- p.429 -->
residual for 120 days or more, may be recalled and
reposted one (1) time at that installation unless it is
currently in a posting or offer phase.
2. A Notice of Intent (NOI) for filling that vacancy shall be
reposted in accordance with the posting requirements
set forth in Article 38, and filled in accordance with
Article 38.8 Order for Filling Vacant Maintenance
Positions. No duty assignments will be identified for
reversion or alteration in an NOI posted under this
provision.

## Summary

On mutual agreement, a vacant Maintenance Craft assignment residual for 120 days or more may be recalled and reposted once at its installation (unless in a posting or offer phase), with the NOI reposted per Article 38 and filled per Article 38.8, with no reversion or alteration identifications.
