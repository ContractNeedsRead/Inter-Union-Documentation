# Article 12.5.B.2

## Full text

<!-- p.316 -->
MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING
BETWEEN THE
UNITED STATES POSTAL SERVICE
AND THE
AMERICAN POSTAL WORKERS UNION, AFL-CIO
Re: Article 12.5.B.2
The parties agree that Article 12.5.B.2 shall be amended to include
the need to identify duty assignments within the appropriate
radius currently held by PSE employees which shall be made
available for the reassignment of excess career employees.

## Summary

Amends Article 12.5.B.2 to require identifying duty assignments within the appropriate radius currently held by PSEs, which must be made available for the reassignment of excess career employees.

Modifies the Article 12.5.B.2 vacancy identification: [[APWU §12.5]]
