# Virtual Arbitrations

## Full text

<!-- p.344 -->
MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING
BETWEEN THE
UNITED STATES POSTAL SERVICE
AND THE
AMERICAN POSTAL WORKERS UNION, AFL-CIO
Re: Virtual Arbitrations
The parties agree expedited arbitrations as defined in the
Expedited Arbitration MOU will be heard exclusively in
virtual format beginning no later than one full year from the
ratification date of the 2024-2027 National Agreement. Prior
to implementation of virtual hearings in the field pursuant to
this MOU, the parties at the National Level will develop the
rules governing virtual hearings.
It is understood this Memorandum of Understanding does not
change either party’s right to refer an expedited case to regular
arbitration in accordance with the applicable procedures of
Article15, Section 5.C., of the National Agreement.
The parties at the National Level will continue to attempt
to identify and agree upon additional issues to be referred
to expedited arbitration at the Area/Regional Level in
accordance with Section 5.C of Article 15 of the National
Agreement.
The National parties will meet throughout this contract term
to evaluate the progress, effectiveness, and any feedback
received related to virtual hearings. The goal of the meetings
and evaluations will be to improve the effectiveness and
efficiency of virtual hearings.
This memorandum will expire with the expiration of the term
of the 2024-2027 National Agreement or may be terminated
by either party with at least ninety (90) days advance written
notice.

## Summary

Expedited arbitrations (per the Expedited Arbitration MOU) are heard exclusively in virtual format beginning no later than one year after ratification, with National rules developed first; regular-arbitration referral rights are unchanged; the parties meet throughout the term to evaluate virtual hearings; expires with the agreement or on 90 days' notice.
