# Article 35: Employee Assistance Program Subject of Discussion

## Full text

<!-- p.370 -->
MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING
BETWEEN THE
UNITED STATES POSTAL SERVICE
AND THE
AMERICAN POSTAL WORKERS UNION, AFL-CIO
Re: Article 35: Employee Assistance Program Subject of
Discussion
The parties recognize the issues of child and elder care and the
potential challenges many employees may face as a result of
these issues during their postal careers.
Therefore, the parties agree that for the term of the 2024
National Agreement child and elder care means, methods,
and programs may be included as a topic of discussion of the
Employee Assistance Program Joint Committee established under
Article 35.

## Summary

For the agreement's term, child and elder care means, methods, and programs may be included as a topic of discussion for the Article 35 Employee Assistance Program Joint Committee.
