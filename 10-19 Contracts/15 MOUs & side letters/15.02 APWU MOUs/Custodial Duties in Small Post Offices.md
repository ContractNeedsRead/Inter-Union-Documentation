# Custodial Duties in Small Post Offices

## Full text

<!-- p.418 -->
MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING
BETWEEN THE
UNITED STATES POSTAL SERVICE
AND THE
AMERICAN POSTAL WORKERS UNION, AFL-CIO
Re: Custodial Duties in Small Post Offices
The parties agree that when in small post offices there are two
(2) hours or less of contracted out custodial duties in a day that
<!-- p.419 -->
cannot be combined with other maintenance duties to create
a duty assignment, those duties will be assigned to an existing
APWU bargaining unit duty assignment in the facility.

## Summary

In small post offices, two hours or less of daily contracted custodial duties that cannot be combined with other maintenance duties are assigned to an existing APWU bargaining unit duty assignment in the facility.

## Contract callout

Modifies the Article 38 assignment rules for small-office custodial work: [[APWU Article 38 — Maintenance Craft]]
