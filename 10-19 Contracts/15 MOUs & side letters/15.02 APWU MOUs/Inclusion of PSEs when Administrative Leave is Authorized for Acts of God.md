# Inclusion of PSEs when Administrative Leave is Authorized for Acts of God

## Full text

<!-- p.314 -->
MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING
BETWEEN THE
UNITED STATES POSTAL SERVICE
AND THE
AMERICAN POSTAL WORKERS UNION, AFL-CIO
Re: Inclusion of PSEs when Administrative Leave is
Authorized for Acts of God
<!-- p.315 -->
The parties agree that Postal Support Employees (PSEs) will
be included and entitled to Administrative Leave when such
leave is authorized for Acts of God, as described in Subsection
519.21 of the Employee and Labor Relations Manual (ELM).
The application of the ELM Subsection 519.21 will be applied
to PSEs as follows:
ELM 519.214 Early Dismissal Due to Acts of God
When a PSE is dismissed early from duty while at work due to
an Act of God, PSEs will be compensated as follows:
1. If they have not reached their guaranteed work hours
as provided in the USPS-APWU National Agreement,
they will receive administrative leave for the remainder
of their guarantee, or, if scheduled and assigned begin
and end tour, they will receive administrative leave for
the remainder of their scheduled hours for that day.
2. If they have worked more than their guaranteed
work hours as provided in the USPS-APWU National
Agreement, they are entitled to credit for hours worked,
plus administrative leave for the remainder of their
scheduled work hours. The combination of time worked
and administrative leave may not exceed 8 hours in a
service day.
ELM 519.215 Employees Prevented from Reporting
PSEs scheduled to report who are prevented from reporting
or, who after reporting, are prevented from working by an Act
of God, may receive administrative leave in an amount equal
to their guaranteed work hours as provided in the USPS-
APWU National Agreement or, if scheduled and assigned
begin and end tour, they will receive administrative leave for
their scheduled hours for that day.
ELM 519.216 Employees on Annual Leave, Sick Leave, or LWOP
PSEs on annual leave, sick leave, or LWOP remain in such
status and are not entitled to administrative leave.
<!-- p.316 -->
This MOU applies only to Acts of God as described in the ELM
Subsection 519.21.

## Summary

PSEs receive administrative leave for Acts of God under ELM 519.21: early dismissal compensation to the guarantee or scheduled hours (with the 8-hour daily cap), prevented-reporting leave to the guarantee or scheduled hours, and no administrative leave for PSEs on annual, sick, or LWOP status; applies only to ELM 519.21 Acts of God.
