# Terminal Pay Process

## Full text

<!-- p.356 -->
MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING
BETWEEN THE
UNITED STATES POSTAL SERVICE
AND THE
AMERICAN POSTAL WORKERS UNION, AFL-CIO
Re: Terminal Pay Process
Any employee who is eligible for terminal payout(s) can contact
the office from which they retired or separated from and make
<!-- p.357 -->
the appropriate arrangements. Management at such offices
shall promptly distribute terminal checks according to the request
of the former employee.

## Summary

Employees eligible for terminal payouts contact the office from which they retired or separated to make arrangements; management promptly distributes terminal checks according to the former employee's request.
