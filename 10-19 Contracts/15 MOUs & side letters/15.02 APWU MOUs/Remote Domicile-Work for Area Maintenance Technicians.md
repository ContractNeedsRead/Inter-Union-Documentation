# Remote Domicile/Work for Area Maintenance Technicians

## Full text

<!-- p.428 -->
MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING
BETWEEN THE
UNITED STATES POSTAL SERVICE
AND THE
AMERICAN POSTAL WORKERS UNION, AFL-CIO
Re: Remote Domicile/Work for Area Maintenance Technicians
Upon execution and for the life of the 2024 APWU-USPS
Collective Bargaining Agreement, AMTs will be afforded an
opportunity to request a change from their current reporting
station. The written requests will be submitted to the respective
Field Maintenance Operations Supervisor. If approved, the
AMT’s official domicile office will remain unchanged.

## Summary

For the agreement's life, AMTs may request a change from their current reporting station through the Field Maintenance Operations Supervisor; approvals leave the official domicile office unchanged.
