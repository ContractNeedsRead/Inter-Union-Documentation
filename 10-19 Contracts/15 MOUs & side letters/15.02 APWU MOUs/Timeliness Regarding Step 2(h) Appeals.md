# Timeliness Regarding Step 2(h) Appeals

## Full text

<!-- p.341 -->
MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING
BETWEEN THE
UNITED STATES POSTAL SERVICE
AND THE
AMERICAN POSTAL WORKERS UNION, AFL-CIO
Re: Timeliness Regarding Step 2(h) Appeals
When the Union incorrectly appeals a grievance under Article
15.2 Step 2(h) to Step 3 rather than to arbitration, and can
show the appeal was made timely, management will not consider
timeliness as a waiver of the grievance. If no timely appeal to
Step 3 can be established by the Union then management retains
the right to raise the timeliness issue. This Memorandum includes
the scenario where the Union incorrectly appeals a grievance
under Article 15.2 Step 2(h) directly to arbitration that should
have been appealed to Step 3. The grievance will not be waived
as untimely provided the Union can show a timely appeal to
arbitration. If no timely appeal can be established by the Union,
<!-- p.342 -->
then management retains the right to raise the timeliness issue.

## Summary

Where the Union incorrectly appeals an Article 15.2 Step 2(h) grievance to Step 3 instead of arbitration (or directly to arbitration instead of Step 3) and shows a timely appeal, management will not treat timeliness as a waiver; if no timely appeal is established, management retains the timeliness defense.

Modifies the Article 15.2 Step 2(h) timeliness waiver rule: [[APWU §15.2]]
