# MVS Training Initiatives Committee

## Full text

<!-- p.436 -->
MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING
BETWEEN THE
UNITED STATES POSTAL SERVICE
AND THE
AMERICAN POSTAL WORKERS UNION, AFL-CIO
Re: MVS Training Initiatives Committee
The parties reaffirm their commitment to provide employees
with training on the latest technologies for all current and new
vehicles. The parties agree to provide this training on a fair and
equitable basis on all current fleet vehicle models. Additionally,
the parties recognize the benefits of affording employees with
opportunities for development and career advancement.
Within ninety (90) days from the effective date of the National
Agreement, a Joint MVS Committee on training will be
established at the National Level, which will consist of up to six
(6) representatives of each party. At least one representative
<!-- p.437 -->
from each of the parties will be from the National Level. The
committee will discuss concerns, revisions, or updates to existing
training curriculum, and/or additional training opportunities or
programs. The committee will submit its findings to the Director,
APWU Contract Administration, and the Director, Motor Vehicle
Service of the American Postal Workers Union, AFL-CIO.

## Summary

Joint MVS Committee on training (up to six representatives per party, at least one National each) established within 90 days of the effective date to discuss curriculum revisions, updates, and additional training, reporting findings to the APWU Contract Administration and Motor Vehicle Service Directors.
