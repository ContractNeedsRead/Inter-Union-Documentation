# Removal of Social Security Number References

## Full text

<!-- p.362 -->
MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING
BETWEEN THE
UNITED STATES POSTAL SERVICE
AND THE
AMERICAN POSTAL WORKERS UNION, AFL-CIO
Re: Removal of Social Security Number References
The parties agree that the Postal Service intends to strive to
remove social security numbers from all bid-related forms (e.g.,
PS Forms 1717 and 1717A), PS Form 1723, Notice of Assignment,
and any other postal form where the social security number is not
necessary to the form’s processing. In such cases, the Employee
Identification Number (EIN) will be substituted.

## Summary

The Postal Service will remove social security numbers from bid-related forms (PS Forms 1717/1717A), PS Form 1723, and other forms where the SSN is unnecessary, substituting the Employee Identification Number (EIN).
