# Establishment of the Job Bid Management (JBM) Tool Work Group

## Full text

<!-- p.407 -->
MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING
BETWEEN THE
UNITED STATES POSTAL SERVICE
AND THE
AMERICAN POSTAL WORKERS UNION, AFL-CIO
Re: Establishment of the Job Bid Management (JBM) Tool
Work Group
The parties agree to establish a work group to identify the
ability of the Job Bid Management (JBM) Tool to assess
contractual restrictions and properly award duty assignments
to appropriate successful bidders.
The work group will be administered by the Director, USPS
Contract Administration (APWU) and APWU Clerk Division,
Director, or their designees. This work group will consist of
no more than three (3) members appointed by the APWU
and three (3) members appointed by the Postal Service.
Additionally, either party reserves the right to provide a
subject matter expert(s) for consultation to this work group,
taking into consideration the JBM Tool is used across multiple
crafts within the Postal Service.
<!-- p.408 -->
This work group is not intended to alter or amend the
provisions of Article 37 of the National Agreement, or the
rules or procedures established to fill bargaining unit positions
in the Clerk Craft.
In addition to the evaluation of the ability to meet the needs
of job posting, bidding and application within the JBM Tool,
this work group will be responsible for assessing the tool’s
ability to process the placement of Clerk Craft bargaining unit
employees into vacant Postal Service positions in accordance
with the National Agreement by both manual and automated
means.

## Summary

Work group (up to three and three members plus subject-matter experts) to assess the Job Bid Management Tool's ability to apply contractual restrictions and award duty assignments, and to process Clerk Craft placement by manual and automated means; does not alter Article 37 or the Clerk Craft fill procedures.
