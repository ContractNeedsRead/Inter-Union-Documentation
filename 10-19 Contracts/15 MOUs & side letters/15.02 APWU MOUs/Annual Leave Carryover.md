# Annual Leave Carryover

## Full text

<!-- p.307 -->
MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING
BETWEEN THE
UNITED STATES POSTAL SERVICE
AND THE
AMERICAN POSTAL WORKERS UNION, AFL-CIO
Re: Annual Leave Carryover
The parties agree that, as soon as practicable after the signing
of the 2024 National Agreement, the applicable handbooks
and manuals will be modified to provide revised regulations for
annual leave carryover as follows:
a. Regular workforce employees covered by this Agreement
may carry over 520 hours of accumulated annual leave
beginning with leave carried over from leave year 2025 to
leave year 2026.
b. Employees who fall under the provisions of Public Law 83-
102 and who have maintained a carryover of more than
520 hours cannot increase their present ceiling.
<!-- p.308 -->
In all other respects, the ELM provisions for payment
of accumulated leave are not changed because of this
Memorandum.

## Summary

Regular workforce employees may carry over 520 hours of accumulated annual leave beginning with the 2025-to-2026 leave year; employees under Public Law 83-102 with carryovers above 520 hours cannot increase their present ceiling; other ELM accumulated-leave payment provisions are unchanged.
