# Overtime Administration Prior To Peak Season

## Full text

<!-- p.301 -->
MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING
BETWEEN THE
UNITED STATES POSTAL SERVICE
AND THE
AMERICAN POSTAL WORKERS UNION, AFL-CIO
Re: Overtime Administration Prior To Peak Season
During the term of the 2024-2027 National Agreement, during
the four (4) week period prior to accounting period 3, when
needed, overtime work for full-time employees shall be scheduled
among available and qualified employees doing similar work in the
<!-- p.302 -->
work location where the employees regularly work. The following
conditions for the assignment of overtime are for the purpose of
protecting the rights of Overtime Desired List employees during
the four (4) week period prior to peak season.
1. During the four (4) week period prior to accounting period
3, when an opportunity exists for overtime for qualified
and available full-time employees doing similar work in the
work location where the employees regularly work, prior
to using a PSE in excess of eight (8) hours in a service day
or forty (40) hours in a service week, such qualified and
available full-time employees on the appropriate Overtime
Desired List will be selected to perform such work in order
of their seniority on a rotating basis up to ten (10) hours in
a day or fifty-six (56) hours in a service week.
2.	This MOU is not intended to change, modify or eliminate
any Article 30 Local Memorandum of Understanding
language addressing overtime scheduling which may be in
conflict with this MOU.

## Summary

During the four weeks before accounting period 3, overtime goes to qualified available full-time employees doing similar work in the work location, with OTDL employees selected by seniority on a rotating basis up to 10/56 hours before PSEs work over 8/40; the MOU does not change conflicting Article 30 LMOU overtime language.

## Contract callout

Modifies the Article 8.5 OTDL sequencing for the pre-peak ramp: [[APWU §8.5]]
