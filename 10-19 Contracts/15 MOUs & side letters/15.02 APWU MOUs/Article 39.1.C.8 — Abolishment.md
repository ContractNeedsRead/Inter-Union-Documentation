# Article 39.1.C.8 - Abolishment

## Full text

<!-- p.434 -->
MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING
BETWEEN THE
UNITED STATES POSTAL SERVICE
AND THE
AMERICAN POSTAL WORKERS UNION, AFL-CIO
Re: Article 39.1.C.8 – Abolishment
Frequently Asked Question:
What is an example of a valid reason for abolishing a
Motor Vehicle Craft duty assignment?
Response:
On the effective date of the abolishment, the majority of
the work assigned to that duty assignment would no longer
be performed.

## Summary

Q&A on Article 39.1.C.8: a valid abolishment example is where, on the effective date of the abolishment, the majority of the work assigned to that duty assignment would no longer be performed.

Incorporates an interpretive example for the Article 39.1.C.8 abolishment definition: [[APWU §39.1]]
