# Mail Equipment Shop Prior MOUs

## Full text

<!-- p.437 -->
MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING
BETWEEN THE
UNITED STATES POSTAL SERVICE
AND THE
AMERICAN POSTAL WORKERS UNION, AFL-CIO
Re: Mail Equipment Shop Prior MOUs
The parties agree that the following MOUs listed in the 2015-
<!-- p.438 -->
2018 Collective Bargaining Agreement:
• MOU Re: Subcontracting Mail Equipment Shops page 339
• MOU Re: Work Clothes Program-MES page 390
• MOU Re: Training Opportunities Mail Equipment Shops
page 390
• MOU Re: Mail Equipment Shop Operations page 391
• MOU Re: Overtime at the Mail Equipment Shops page 391,
as well as references within Articles 41.2.G.1, 41.3.F.8, and
• MOU Re: Operation of Powered Industrial Equipment for
Material Support Craft Employees from the 2018 – 2021
Collective Bargaining Agreement, will not be included
or printed in the next Agreement. However, the parties
acknowledge that should the Mail Equipment Shop work
return, the above listed MOUs will be in full force and
effect.

## Summary

The five listed Mail Equipment Shop MOUs from the 2015-2018 Agreement (Subcontracting Mail Equipment Shops, Work Clothes Program-MES, Training Opportunities, Mail Equipment Shop Operations, and Overtime at the Mail Equipment Shops, with the Article 41.2.G.1/41.3.F.8 references) and the Operation of Powered Industrial Equipment MOU from the 2018-2021 Agreement are not printed in the current Agreement, but return to full force and effect if Mail Equipment Shop work returns.
