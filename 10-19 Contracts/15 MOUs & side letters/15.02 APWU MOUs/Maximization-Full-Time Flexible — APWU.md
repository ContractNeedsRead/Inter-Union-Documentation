# Maximization/Full-Time Flexible - APWU

## Full text

<!-- p.288 -->
MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING
BETWEEN THE
UNITED STATES POSTAL SERVICE
AND THE
AMERICAN POSTAL WORKERS UNION, AFL-CIO
Re: Maximization/Full-Time Flexible – APWU
Where a part-time flexible has performed duties within his/her
craft and occupational group within an installation at least 40
hours a week (8 within 9, or 8 within 10, as applicable), five (5)
days a week, over a period of six (6) months, the senior part-time
flexible shall be converted to full-time status.
<!-- p.289 -->
This criteria shall be applied to postal installations with 125 or
more workyears of employment.
It is further understood that part-time flexibles converted to full-
time under this criteria will have flexible reporting times, flexible
nonscheduled days, and flexible reporting locations within
the installation depending upon operational requirements as
established on the preceding Wednesday.
The parties will implement this in accordance with their past
practice.
Date: July 21, 1987

## Summary

July 21, 1987 memorandum: where a PTF performs 40-hour, five-day weeks (8 within 9, or 8 within 10) over six months, the senior PTF converts to full-time; applies to installations with 125 or more workyears; converted employees carry flexible reporting times, nonscheduled days, and reporting locations; implemented per past practice.
