# Article 1.6.B

## Full text

<!-- p.274 -->
MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING
BETWEEN THE
UNITED STATES POSTAL SERVICE
AND THE
AMERICAN POSTAL WORKERS UNION, AFL-CIO
Re: Article 1.6.B
The parties recognize that it is important to resolve issues
concerning postmasters and supervisors performing bargaining
unit work. To that end, the parties agree to the following:
The parties agree to conduct a joint analysis of the Clerk Craft
bargaining unit workload in post offices Levels 15 through 18. The
parties will utilize this analysis to determine the amount of bargaining
unit work, or the basis for establishing the amount of such work, that
may be performed by a postmaster in these level offices.
<!-- p.275 -->
After the parties have conducted and discussed this joint analysis,
they will conduct a pre-arbitration review at the National Level of
grievances currently pending on this issue.

## Summary

Joint analysis of Clerk Craft workload in Levels 15-18 post offices to determine how much bargaining unit work a postmaster may perform, followed by a National Level pre-arbitration review of pending grievances on the issue.
