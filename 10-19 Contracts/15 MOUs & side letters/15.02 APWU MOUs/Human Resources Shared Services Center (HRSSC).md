# Human Resources Shared Services Center (HRSSC)

## Full text

<!-- p.438 -->
MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING
BETWEEN THE
UNITED STATES POSTAL SERVICE
AND THE
AMERICAN POSTAL WORKERS UNION, AFL-CIO
Re: Human Resources Shared Services Center (HRSSC)
The parties agree that it is in their mutual best interest to resolve
continuing issues with posting and bidding which have developed
through the implementation of computerized bidding through
the Human Resources Shared Services Center (HRSSC).
To further this effort, the parties agree to meet at the Headquarters
Level within ninety (90) days after the signing of this Agreement
to discuss these matters and explore opportunities for resolution.
The parties will continue to meet as necessary to discuss issues
and to improve communication.

## Summary

The parties meet at Headquarters Level within 90 days of signing to resolve posting and bidding issues arising from computerized bidding through the HRSSC, continuing to meet as necessary to discuss issues and improve communication.
