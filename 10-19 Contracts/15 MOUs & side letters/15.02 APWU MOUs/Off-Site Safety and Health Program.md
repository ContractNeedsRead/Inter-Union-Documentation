# Off-Site Safety and Health Program

## Full text

<!-- p.331 -->
MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING
BETWEEN THE
UNITED STATES POSTAL SERVICE
AND THE
AMERICAN POSTAL WORKERS UNION, AFL-CIO
Re: Off-Site Safety and Health Program
It is the responsibility of management to provide safe working
conditions. The Union will cooperate with and assist management
to live up to this responsibility.
The Postal Service Safety and Health Program and OSHA
standards and regulations cover Postal Service employees who
perform Postal Service duties in private employers’ establishments
and while delivering mail and performing other activities off
<!-- p.332 -->
Postal Service property. To ensure that employees are protected,
safe and healthful working conditions must be provided through
engineering and administrative controls, personal protective
equipment, enforcement of safe work practices, withdrawal of
employees from the private sector facility, and, if necessary,
curtailment of mail. The purpose of routine safety and health
program evaluations is to measure the effectiveness of the Postal
Service Safety and Health Program at each organizational level,
ensure OSHA compliance, and promote a model for effective
safety and health programs. The Postal Service will ensure that
the work area(s) and equipment for APWU represented employees
in off-site locations are safe.
The National Joint Labor-Management Safety and Health
Committee will discuss and work toward creating an
implementation process to ensure employees in off-site locations
are fully protected by the Postal Service’s Safety and Health
Program.

## Summary

Management responsibility for safe working conditions extends off Postal Service property: the Safety and Health Program and OSHA standards cover employees in private establishments and during delivery; protections include engineering controls, PPE, safe-practice enforcement, withdrawal, and mail curtailment; the National Joint Labor-Management Safety and Health Committee develops the off-site implementation process.
