# Expedited Arbitration

## Full text

<!-- p.336 -->
MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING
BETWEEN THE
UNITED STATES POSTAL SERVICE
AND THE
AMERICAN POSTAL WORKERS UNION, AFL-CIO
Re: Expedited Arbitration
The United States Postal Service and the American Postal
Workers Union, AFL-CIO, agree to hear the following issues in
the expedited arbitration forum:
1. Individual Overtime Issues;
2. Withholding of Step Increases;
3. Individual Leave Requests Concerning Annual Leave, Sick
Leave, Leave Without Pay, Court Leave, Restricted Sick
Leave, and Requests for Medical Certification;
4. AWOL;
5. Holiday Scheduling Issues;
6.	 Letters of Warning and Suspensions of 14 Days or Less
(Except Emergency Suspensions);
7. Article 25, Higher-Level Assignments;
8. Employee Claims;
9.	 Letters of Demand of Less Than $2,500;
10. Individual Clerk Craft Seniority Disputes;
11. Steward Time;
12. Grievances solely pertaining to requests for information
(no other issues attached to the grievance);
13. Individual Disputes Over Bereavement Leave;
14. Individual Uniform Allowance Issues;
15. Post-Separation Letters of Indebtedness;
16. Revocation of Driving Privileges;
17. Employee Bulletin Boards;
18. Voting Leave;
19. Leave to Donate Blood;
<!-- p.337 -->
20. Such Other Matters as are Mutually Agreeable at the Area/
Regional Level.
This Agreement does not change either party’s right to refer
an expedited case to regular arbitration in accordance with the
applicable procedures of Article 15, Section 5.C, of the National
Agreement.
The parties at the National Level will continue to attempt to
identify and agree upon additional issues to be referred to
expedited arbitration at the Area/Regional Level in accordance
with Section 5.C of Article 15 of the National Agreement.

## Summary

The expedited arbitration forum hears nineteen listed issue categories (individual overtime, step withholding, leave requests, AWOL, holiday scheduling, LOWs and suspensions of 14 days or less, higher level, employee claims, letters of demand under $2,500, clerk seniority, steward time, information requests, bereavement, uniform issues, post-separation indebtedness, driving privileges, bulletin boards, voting leave, blood-donation leave) plus mutually agreeable matters; referral-to-regular-arbitration rights under Article 15.5.C are unchanged, and the parties continue identifying additional expedited issues.
