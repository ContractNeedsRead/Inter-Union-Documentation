# NPMHU MOU — Higher Level Pay for Temporary Details

## Full text

<!-- p.210 -->

**HIGHER LEVEL PAY FOR TEMPORARY DETAILS**

When level 4 mail handlers are temporarily detailed to level 5 mail handler duties, higher level pay will be designated to the same step within level 5 as the employees occupy in their level 4 assignments.

## Summary

Agrees that when level 4 mail handlers are temporarily detailed to level 5 mail handler duties, higher level pay is payable as set out in the MOU.

## Contract callout

This MOU is the instrument referenced from [[NPMHU §25.2]] (relation: modifies): Higher level pay when level 4 mail handlers are temporarily detailed to level 5 duties.
