# NPMHU MOU — Step Increase, Unsatisfactory Performance

## Full text

<!-- p.209 -->

**STEP INCREASE, UNSATISFACTORY PERFORMANCE**

The Parties agree that periodic step increases will not be withheld for reason of unsatisfactory performance and that all other aspects of the current step increase procedures remain unchanged, unless otherwise provided for by the 2022 National Agreement.

## Summary

Agrees that periodic step increases will not be withheld for reason of unsatisfactory performance, with the terms set out in the MOU.

## Contract callout

This MOU is the instrument referenced from [[NPMHU §9.5]] (relation: modifies): Periodic step increases will not be withheld for unsatisfactory performance.
