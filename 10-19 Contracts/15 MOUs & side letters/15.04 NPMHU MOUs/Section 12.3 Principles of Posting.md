# NPMHU MOU — Section 12.3 Principles of Posting

## Full text

<!-- p.176 -->

**SECTION 12.3 PRINCIPLES OF POSTING**

When either Article 12.3B4, B5 or B6 are applicable, requiring a bid to be reposted while the total number of bids in the section remains the same, an expedited selection process shall be applied. The duty assignments encumbered by the employees junior to the senior employee whose bid is reposted will be offered, in seniority order, to the employees remaining in the section beginning with the senior employee whose bid is reposted. An employee(s) declining to make a selection when canvassed shall be assigned to the duty assignment(s) remaining in the section after the expedited selection process has been completed.

The results of the above listed actions shall be effective at the beginning of the succeeding pay period.

The bid being reposted in accordance with Article 12.3B4, B5 or B6 will be posted for the installation and not included in the expedited selection process.

After the expedited bidding process, the junior employee left without a duty assignment shall retain the right to retreat to the section from which withdrawn as detailed in Section 12.6C4c.

<!-- p.177 -->

BETWEEN THE

UNITED STATES POSTAL SERVICE

AND THE

NATIONAL POSTAL MAIL HANDLERS UNION

## Summary

Provides that when Article 12.3B4, B5 or B6 require a bid to be reposted while the total number of bids in the section remains the same, an expedited selection process applies: duty assignments encumbered by employees junior to the senior employee whose bid is reposted are offered in seniority order, employees declining are assigned the remaining assignments, results are effective at the beginning of the succeeding pay period, the reposted bid is posted installation-wide, and the junior employee left without an assignment retains the right to retreat under Section 12.6C4c.
