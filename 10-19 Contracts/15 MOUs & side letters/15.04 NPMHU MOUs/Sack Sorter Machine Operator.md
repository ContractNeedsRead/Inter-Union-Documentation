# NPMHU MOU — Sack Sorter Machine Operator

## Full text

<!-- p.173 -->

**SACK SORTER MACHINE OPERATOR**

The parties hereby agree that effective July 21, 1987, all future postings of the position, Sack Sorting Machine Operator, salary level MH-5, standard position 2-438, shall be filled by the senior qualified bidder meeting the qualification standard for the position.

<!-- p.174 -->
