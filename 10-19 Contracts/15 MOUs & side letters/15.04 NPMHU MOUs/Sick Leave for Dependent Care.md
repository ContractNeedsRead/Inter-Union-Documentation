# NPMHU MOU — Sick Leave for Dependent Care

## Full text

<!-- p.168 -->

**SICK LEAVE FOR DEPENDENT CARE**

During the term of the 2022 National Agreement, sick leave may be used by an employee to give care or otherwise attend to a family member having an illness, injury or other condition which, if an employee had such condition, would justify the use of sick leave by the employee. Family members shall include son or daughter, parent and spouse as defined in ELM Section 515.2. Up to 80 hours of sick leave may be used for dependent care in any leave year. Approval of sick leave for dependent care will be subject to normal procedures for leave approval.

<!-- p.169 -->

MEMORADUM OF UNDERSTANDING

## Summary

Allows up to 80 hours of sick leave per leave year during the term to give care or otherwise attend to a family member (son or daughter, parent, or spouse as defined in ELM Section 515.2) with an illness, injury, or other condition that would justify the employee's own sick leave use, with approval terms set out in the MOU.

## Contract callout

This MOU is the instrument referenced from [[NPMHU §10.6]] (relation: modifies): Up to 80 hours of sick leave per leave year for dependent care.
