# NPMHU MOU — Purge of Warning Letters

## Full text

<!-- p.207 -->

**PURGE OF WARNING LETTERS**

The parties agree that there will be a one-time purge of Official Disciplinary Letters of Warning from the personnel folders of all employees represented by the National Postal Mail Handlers Union. To qualify to be purged, a Letter of Warning must:

Have an issue date prior to the effective date of the 2022 Na1. tional Agreement between the parties;

2. Have been in effect for 6 months or longer and not cited as an element of prior discipline in any subsequent disciplinary action; and

3. Not have been issued in lieu of a suspension or a removal action.

All grievances associated with discipline that is purged as a result of this Memorandum shall be withdrawn.

(The preceding MOU, Purge of Warning Letters, shall apply to Mail Handler Assistant employees.)

## Summary

Agrees that there will be a one-time purge of Official Disciplinary Letters of Warning, with the terms set out in the MOU.
