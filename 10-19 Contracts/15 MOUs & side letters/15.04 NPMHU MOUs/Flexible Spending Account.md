# NPMHU MOU — Flexible Spending Account

## Full text

<!-- p.160 -->

**FLEXIBLE SPENDING ACCOUNT**

The United States Postal Service agrees the maximum allowable employee Flexible Spending Account (FSA) health care contribution will be the amount permitted by the Internal Revenue Service (IRS).

<!-- p.161 -->

## Summary

Agrees the maximum allowable employee Flexible Spending Account (FSA) health care contribution will be the amount permitted by the Internal Revenue Service (IRS).

## Contract callout

This MOU is the instrument referenced from [[NPMHU §21.1]] (relation: implements): Sets the FSA health care contribution maximum at the IRS-permitted amount.
