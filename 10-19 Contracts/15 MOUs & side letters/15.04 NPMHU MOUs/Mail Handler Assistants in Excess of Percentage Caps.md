# NPMHU MOU — Mail Handler Assistants in Excess of Percentage Caps

## Full text

<!-- p.141 -->

**MAIL HANDLER ASSISTANTS IN EXCESS OF PERCENTAGE CAPS**

The parties acknowledge that there may be situations of limited duration that occur during the course of the year when the Employer needs to employ MHAs in excess of the cap for the total number of MHAs in an installation.

Any local or Area/Regional agreements to allow the employment of MHAs in excess of the percentage caps requires concurrence by the parties at the National level.

BETWEEN THE

UNITED STATES POSTAL SERVICE

AND THE

NATIONAL POSTAL MAIL HANDLERS UNION

## Summary

Acknowledges limited-duration situations in which the Employer needs to employ MHAs in excess of the installation cap, and requires concurrence of the parties for any local or Area/Regional agreements allowing such excess employment.

## Contract callout

This LOI is the instrument referenced from [[NPMHU §7.1]] (relation: modifies): Allows MHA employment above the Article 7.1B3 caps in limited-duration situations with party concurrence.
