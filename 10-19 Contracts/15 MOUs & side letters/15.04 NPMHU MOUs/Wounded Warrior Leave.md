# NPMHU MOU — Wounded Warrior Leave

## Full text

<!-- p.170 -->

**WOUNDED WARRIOR LEAVE**

The parties agree that MHAs shall be covered by the instructions written by the Postal Service under the Wounded Warrior Federal Leave Act of 2015.

## Summary

Agrees MHAs shall be covered by the instructions written by the Postal Service under the Wounded Warrior Federal Leave Act of 2015.

## Contract callout

This MOU is the instrument referenced from [[NPMHU §10.2]] (relation: implements): Extends the Wounded Warrior Federal Leave Act of 2015 instructions to MHAs.
