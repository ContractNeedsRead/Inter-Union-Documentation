# NPMHU MOU — Re: Article 12.1 – Probationary Period – Bidding

## Full text

<!-- p.173 -->

**Re: Article 12.1 – Probationary Period – Bidding**

The parties agree to the following regarding bidding during a ninety (90) calendar day probationary period:

Full-time career mail handlers who are serving a probationary period pursuant to Article 12.1 of the National Agreement and applicable memoranda of understanding are eligible to bid for vacant duty assignments in accordance with Article 12.3 of the National Agreement.

Seniority for full-time career mail handlers during their probationary period will be computed for the purpose of bidding pursuant to this Agreement. This computation of such seniority does not create any additional obligation or entitlement for application of seniority not otherwise provided for in the National Agreement.

## Summary

Agrees full-time career mail handlers serving a probationary period under Article 12.1 are eligible to bid for vacant duty assignments in accordance with Article 12.3, with the seniority terms set out in the MOU.
