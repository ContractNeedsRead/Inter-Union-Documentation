# 15.04 NPMHU MOUs and Letters of Intent

The 2022 NPMHU National Agreement states that "Memorandums of Understanding and Letters of Intent are included in the text of the Agreement" with a cross-reference index mapping each instrument to its article. The instruments are printed at pages 135–243. Each leaf below carries the verbatim full text (page markers `<!-- p.NNN -->`; printed signature blocks omitted with a note line), a summary, and the typed callout edge to the contract provision it modifies, implements, incorporates, or supplies procedure for.
