# NPMHU MOU — Potential for Mail Handler Assistant PTF Opportunities

## Full text

<!-- p.189 -->

**POTENTIAL FOR MAIL HANDLER ASSISTANT PTF OPPORTUNITIES**

For the term of this Agreement, any newly established or vacant part-time career positions will be offered to Mail Handler Assistants (MHAs) within fifty (50) miles of the position for conversion to career based on relative standing. Any disputes arising from the terms of this MOU will be referred to the National NPMHU-USPS Article 12 Task Force for resolution.

## Summary

Sets the potential for Mail Handler Assistant part-time flexible opportunities, effective as of October 4, 2014 except as otherwise provided, applied both to MHAs hired on or after that date and to existing MHAs as set out in the MOU.

## Contract callout

This MOU is the instrument referenced from [[NPMHU Article 7 — Employee Classifications]] (relation: procedural): MHA PTF opportunities; effective October 4, 2014, applied to MHAs hired on or after that date.
