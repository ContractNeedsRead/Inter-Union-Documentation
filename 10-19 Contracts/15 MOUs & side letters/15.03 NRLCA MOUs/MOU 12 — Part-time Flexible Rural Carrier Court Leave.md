# NRLCA MOU 12 — Part-time Flexible Rural Carrier Court Leave

## Full text

<!-- p.150 -->
1. A part-time flexible rural carrier will be eligible for court leave as defined in Employee and Labor Relations Manual sections 516.1 and 516.31, if the employee would otherwise have been in a work status or annual leave status.
2. If eligibility is established under paragraph 1, the specific amount of court leave for an eligible part-time flexible rural carrier shall be determined on a daily basis as set forth below:
   - **a.** If previously scheduled for a full assignment(s) on a regular and/or auxiliary route, the evaluation of the full assignment on the regular and/or auxiliary route(s).
   - **b.** If previously scheduled to perform auxiliary assistance, the number of hours the part-time flexible rural carrier would have reasonably worked in the performance of the assignment(s) at the attained straight time rate.
   - **c.** If previously scheduled for annual leave, the number of hours at the attained straight time rate with the hours previously requested and granted annual leave restored to the employee’s annual leave balance.
3. The amount of court leave for part-time flexible rural carriers shall not result in more than 40 hours in a service week when combined with work hours and any other leave.

<!-- signature block omitted -->

## Summary

Extends court leave (ELM 516.1/516.31) to PTF rural carriers who would otherwise have been in work or annual leave status, computed daily by the scheduled assignment's evaluation, by reasonably-worked auxiliary-assistance hours, or by straight-time hours with annual leave restored, capped at a combined 40 hours per service week.
