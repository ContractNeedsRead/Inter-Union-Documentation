# NRLCA MOU 13 — Assistant Rural Carrier

## Full text

<!-- p.151 -->
The parties agree that an assistant rural carrier (ARC) will be a non-career, bargaining-unit employee with limited contractual benefits, performing service either on Saturdays, Sundays, and holidays consistent with current practice, or parcel delivery on any day of the week.

The assistant rural carrier will receive hourly compensation in accordance with Table Four, Rural Carrier Associate***/Assistant Rural Carrier**** Straight-Time Hourly Wage Rates.

All other principles and work rules for assistant rural carriers will be outlined in the Implementation Guidelines.

<!-- signature block omitted -->

## Summary

Defines the ARC: a non-career bargaining-unit employee with limited benefits, working Saturdays, Sundays, and holidays per current practice or parcel delivery any day, paid at Table Four hourly rates, with remaining work rules in the Implementation Guidelines.
