# NRLCA MOU 2 — Recognition of Principles of Evaluated System

## Full text

<!-- p.131 -->
The parties recognize the existing evaluated system of compensation to be a fair, reasonable, and equitable method of payment. The parties further recognize that the evaluated system is comprised of elemental standards which may not in every instance be adequate for an individual carrier; nevertheless, the parties agree that the standards, when examined collectively, represent a fair day’s pay for rural carriers.

Where discrepancies develop in the element of the present route evaluation system, which has been in use for many years, and into the time standards of the system that has been applied to office and road duties, the Employer may undertake studies to correct such discrepancies so long as the changes are consistent with the principles of this memorandum and Article 34.

<!-- signature block omitted -->

## Summary

The parties' joint endorsement of the evaluated compensation system: collectively the elemental standards are a fair day's pay even if individually imperfect, and the employer may study and correct discrepancies in the route evaluation system so long as changes stay consistent with this memorandum and Article 34 (work/time standards).
