# NRLCA MOU 4 — Grievance Time Limits

## Full text

<!-- p.133 -->
The parties agree that the time limits for filing a grievance appeal concerning a proposed removal shall begin from the date of receipt of the notice of proposed removal; therefore, once a grievance is filed concerning such notice, it is not necessary to also file a grievance on the letter of decision. In addition, receipt of a notice of proposed removal starts the 30-day advance notice period of Article 16.4.

<!-- signature block omitted -->

## Summary

The grievance clock on a proposed removal runs from receipt of the notice of proposed removal — a grievance filed on the notice need not be refiled on the letter of decision — and the same notice starts the Article 16.4 30-day advance-notice period. Removes the double-filing trap in removal cases.
