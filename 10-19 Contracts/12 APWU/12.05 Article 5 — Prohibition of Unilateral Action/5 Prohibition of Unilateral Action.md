# APWU §5 — Prohibition of Unilateral Action

## Full text

<!-- p.8 -->
The Employer will not take any actions affecting wages, hours and other terms and conditions of employment as defined in Section 8(d) of the National Labor Relations Act which violate the terms of this Agreement or are otherwise inconsistent with its obligations under law.
(The preceding Article, Article 5, shall apply to PSEs)

## Summary

The Employer may not take unilateral actions affecting wages, hours, or other terms and conditions of employment, as defined in NLRA §8(d), that violate the Agreement or are otherwise inconsistent with its legal obligations. Article 5 applies to PSEs.

## Cross-union comparison

The one-sentence prohibition tracking NLRA §8(d) is identical in function to the parallel clauses in the other craft agreements, anchoring the status-quo and unilateral-change doctrines for each craft.
