# APWU §14.6 — Employee Participation

## Full text

<!-- p.78 -->
### Section 6. Employee Participation

It is the intent of this program to ensure broad exposure to employees, to develop interest by active participation of employees, to ensure new ideas being presented to the Committee and to make certain that employees in all areas of an installation have an opportunity to be represented. At the same time, it is recognized that for the program to be effective, it is desirable to provide for a continuity in the committee work from year to year. Therefore, except for the Chairperson and Secretary, the
<!-- p.79 -->
committee members shall serve three-year terms and shall at the discretion of the Union be eligible to succeed themselves.

## Summary

The program aims for broad employee exposure, active participation, new ideas, and representation across all areas of an installation, balanced by continuity: committee members other than the Chairperson and Secretary serve three-year terms and may succeed themselves at the Union's discretion.

## Cross-union comparison

The three-year staggered-term design for continuity is the shared postal approach to local safety committee membership.
