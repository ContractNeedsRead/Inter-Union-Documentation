# APWU §14.4 — Local Safety Committee

## Full text

<!-- p.77 -->
### Section 4. Local Safety Committee

At each postal installation having 50 or more employees, a Joint Labor-Management Safety and Health Committee will be established. In installations having fewer than 50 employees,
<!-- p.78 -->
installation heads are encouraged to establish similar committees
when requested by the Union. Where no Safety and Health Committee exists, safety and health items may be placed on the agenda and discussed at Labor-Management meetings. There shall be equal representation on the Committee between the Union and management. The representation on the Committee to be specifically determined by the Employer and the Union shall include one (1) person from the Union, except in installations with two (2) or more APWU crafts where up to two (2) persons may be designated by the Union, and appropriate management representatives. The Chairperson will be designated by the Employer.
It is recognized that under some circumstances, the presence of an additional employee employed at the installation will be useful to the Local Safety and Health Committee because of that employee’s special expertise or experience with the agenda item being discussed. Under these circumstances, which will not normally be applicable to most agenda items, the employee may, at the request of the Union, be in attendance only for the time necessary to discuss that item. Payment for the actual time spent at such meetings by the employee will be at the applicable straight-time rate, providing the time spent is a part of the employee’s regular workday.

## Summary

Joint Labor-Management Safety and Health Committees are mandatory at installations of 50 or more employees, encouraged below 50 on union request, with safety items discussed at Labor-Management meetings where none exists. Representation is equal: one union member (up to two in multi-craft installations) plus management representatives, with an Employer-designated Chairperson. An additional employee with special expertise may attend for a specific agenda item at the Union's request, paid straight time for time within the regular workday.

## Cross-union comparison

The 50-employee trigger and equal-representation structure are the postal-standard local safety committee rules; the two-union-member allowance for multi-craft installations is the APWU-specific accommodation of its six-unit structure.
