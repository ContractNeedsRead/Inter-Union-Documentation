# APWU §14.9 — Field Federal Safety and Health Councils

## Full text

<!-- p.82 -->
### Section 9. Field Federal Safety and Health Councils

In those cities where Field Federal Safety and Health Councils exist, one (1) representative of the Union who is on the Local Safety and Health Committee in an independent postal installation in that city and who serves as a member of such Councils, will be
<!-- p.83 -->
permitted to attend the meetings. Such employee will be excused from regularly assigned duties without loss of pay. Employer authorized payment as outlined above will be granted at the applicable straight-time rate, provided the time spent in such meetings is a part of the employee’s regular work day.
(See Memos, pages 331-334)
(The preceding Article, Article 14, shall apply to PSEs)

## Summary

In cities with Field Federal Safety and Health Councils, one union representative serving on both the Council and the Local Safety and Health Committee may attend Council meetings, excused from duty without loss of pay at the straight-time rate when the meeting falls within the regular workday. Article 14 applies to PSEs.

## Cross-union comparison

Council-attendance release time is a common federal-sector safety provision; the PSE extension follows the APWU pattern of PSE-applicability parentheticals.
