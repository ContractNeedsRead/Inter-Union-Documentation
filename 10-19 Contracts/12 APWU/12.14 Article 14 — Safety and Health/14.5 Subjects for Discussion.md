# APWU §14.5 — Subjects for Discussion

## Full text

<!-- p.78 -->
### Section 5. Subjects for Discussion

Individual grievances may be made the subject of discussion during Local Safety and Health Committee meetings, in accordance with Article 14, Section 2.

## Summary

Individual grievances may be discussed at Local Safety and Health Committee meetings under the §14.2 procedures.

## Cross-union comparison

The grievance-referral to the local safety committee is the same device the other crafts use to route safety complaints through their committees.
