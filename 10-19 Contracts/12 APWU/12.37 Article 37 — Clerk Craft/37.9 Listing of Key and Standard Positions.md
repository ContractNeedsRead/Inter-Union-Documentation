# APWU §37.9 — Listing of Key and Standard Positions

## Full text

<!-- p.179 -->
### Section 9. Listing of Key and Standard Positions

The Employer will continue to furnish to the Union at the National Level copies of key and standard positions including qualification standards in the Clerk Craft.

## Summary

The Employer continues furnishing the Union, at the National Level, copies of key and standard Clerk Craft positions with their qualification standards.

## Cross-union comparison

The key-and-standard-position disclosure is the clerk-specific analog of the same obligation in the Maintenance Craft article.
