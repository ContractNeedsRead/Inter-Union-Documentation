# APWU §37.7 — Scheme Committee

## Full text

<!-- p.178 -->
### Section 7. Scheme Committee

- **A.** The Employer agrees to having as part of the National Labor-Management Committee, a Labor-Management Subcommittee on schemes for the consideration of appropriate matters relating to schemes.
- **B.** Subject to any criteria established in the future by the National Labor-Management Committee, Local Level Scheme Committees will continue operation as presently constituted.
- **C.** There shall be no annual or periodic scheme examinations.

## Summary

A Labor-Management Subcommittee on schemes sits within the National Labor-Management Committee; Local Level Scheme Committees continue as presently constituted; and there are no annual or periodic scheme examinations.

## Cross-union comparison

The scheme subcommittee structure is the clerk-specific scheme governance; the no-periodic-examinations bar protects scheme-qualified clerks.
