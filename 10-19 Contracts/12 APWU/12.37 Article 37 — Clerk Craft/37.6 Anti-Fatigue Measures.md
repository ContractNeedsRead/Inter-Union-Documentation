# APWU §37.6 — Anti-Fatigue Measures

## Full text

<!-- p.178 -->
### Section 6. Anti-Fatigue Measures

- **A.** The subject of fatigue as it relates to the safety and health of an employee is a proper subject for the consideration of the Joint Labor-Management Safety Committee as provided in Article 14 of the National Agreement. The Employer will continue to furnish adjustable platform stools for periods of sustained distribution as heretofore.
- **B.** The feasibility of a study of seating devices, including seats with back supports, for the purpose of improving upon and eventually replacing the equipment termed “adjustable platform stools” heretofore supplied, as “sit-stand” devices is a proper subject for determination by the National Labor-Management Committee.

## Summary

Fatigue as a safety-and-health subject belongs to the Joint Labor-Management Safety Committee under Article 14; adjustable platform stools continue for sustained distribution; and a seating-device study (back-support seats replacing platform stools as sit-stand devices) is for the National Labor-Management Committee.

## Cross-union comparison

The fatigue referral to the safety committee is the clerk-specific echo of the shared safety machinery.
