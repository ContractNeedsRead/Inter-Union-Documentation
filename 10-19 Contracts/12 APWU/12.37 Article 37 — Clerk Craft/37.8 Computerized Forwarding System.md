# APWU §37.8 — Computerized Forwarding System

## Full text

<!-- p.178 -->
### Section 8. Computerized Forwarding System

The application of a rotation system for the Computerized Forwarding System and the subject of fatigue as it pertains to
<!-- p.179 -->
the Computerized Forwarding System will be consistent with the requirements of the applicable provisions of this Agreement.
(See Memo, page 387)

## Summary

Computerized Forwarding System rotation and fatigue handling must be consistent with the applicable Agreement provisions.

## Cross-union comparison

The CFS rotation-and-fatigue consistency clause is the clerk-specific automation safeguard.
