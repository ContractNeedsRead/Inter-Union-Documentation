# APWU §31.3 — Information

## Full text

<!-- p.133 -->
### Section 3. Information

The Employer will make available for inspection by the Union all relevant information necessary for collective bargaining or the enforcement, administration or interpretation of this Agreement, including information necessary to determine whether to file or to continue the processing of a grievance under this Agreement. Upon the request of the Union, the Employer will furnish such information, provided, however, that the Employer may require the Union to reimburse the USPS for any costs reasonably incurred in obtaining the information.
Requests for information relating to purely local matters should be submitted by the Local Union representative to the installation head or his/her designee. All other requests for information shall be directed by the National President of the Union to the Vice President, Labor Relations.
Nothing herein shall waive any rights the Union may have to obtain information under the National Labor Relations Act, as amended.
(The preceding Article, Article 31, shall apply to PSEs)

## Summary

The Employer makes available for inspection all relevant information necessary for bargaining or for enforcing, administering, or interpreting the Agreement — including grievance-assessment information — with possible reimbursement of reasonable costs. Local requests go through the Local Union representative to the installation head; all others from the National President to the VP of Labor Relations. NLRA information rights are preserved. Article 31 applies to PSEs.

## Cross-union comparison

The relevant-information duty with the NLRA preservation clause is the postal-standard information provision shared across the crafts.
