# APWU §21.4 — Injury Compensation

## Full text

<!-- p.117 -->
### Section 4. Injury Compensation

Employees covered by this Agreement shall be covered by Subchapter I of Chapter 81 of Title 5, and any amendments thereto, relating to compensation for work injuries. The Employer will promulgate appropriate regulations which comply with applicable regulations of the Office of Workers’ Compensation Programs and any amendments thereto.

## Summary

Covered employees get federal workers' compensation under 5 U.S.C. Chapter 81, Subchapter I, with Employer regulations complying with OWCP's applicable regulations.

## Cross-union comparison

The FECA incorporation with OWCP-compliant regulations is identical across the postal agreements.
