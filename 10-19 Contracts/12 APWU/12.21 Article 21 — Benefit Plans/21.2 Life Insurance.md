# APWU §21.2 — Life Insurance

## Full text

<!-- p.117 -->
### Section 2. Life Insurance

The Employer shall maintain the current life insurance program in effect during the term of this Agreement.

## Summary

The Employer maintains the current life insurance program for the agreement's term.

## Cross-union comparison

The continuation-of-program clause is the standard postal life-insurance provision.
