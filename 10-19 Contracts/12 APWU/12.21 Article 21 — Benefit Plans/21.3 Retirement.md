# APWU §21.3 — Retirement

## Full text

<!-- p.117 -->
### Section 3. Retirement

The provisions of Chapters 83 and 84 of Title 5 U.S. Code, and any amendments thereto, shall continue to apply to employees covered by this Agreement.

## Summary

The CSRS and FERS chapters (5 U.S.C. Chapters 83 and 84, with amendments) continue to apply to covered employees.

## Cross-union comparison

The Title 5 retirement incorporation is identical across the postal agreements.
