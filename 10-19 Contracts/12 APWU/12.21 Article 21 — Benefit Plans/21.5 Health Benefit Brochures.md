# APWU §21.5 — Health Benefit Brochures

## Full text

<!-- p.117 -->
### Section 5. Health Benefit Brochures

When a new employee who is eligible for enrollment in the Postal Service Health Benefits Program enters the Postal Service, the employee shall be furnished a copy of the Health Benefit Plan brochure of the Union signatory to this Agreement which represents the craft in which the employee is to be employed.

## Summary

New PSHB-eligible employees receive the brochure of the union health plan for the craft in which they will be employed.

## Cross-union comparison

The union-plan brochure distribution is a standard postal benefit provision across the crafts.
