### Preamble (verbatim)

This Agreement (referred to as the 2024 National Agreement) is entered into by and between the United States Postal Service (hereinafter referred to as the “Employer”) and the American Postal Workers Union, AFL-CIO (hereinafter referred to as the “Union”). The effective date of this Agreement is September 21, 2024, except that all newly negotiated work rules are effective as of July 11, 2025, unless otherwise agreed.
