# APWU §1.1 — Union

## Full text

<!-- p.1 -->
### Section 1. Union

The Employer recognizes the Union designated below as the exclusive bargaining representative of all employees in the bargaining unit for which each has been recognized and certified at the National Level:
American Postal Workers Union, AFL-CIO – Maintenance Employees
American Postal Workers Union, AFL-CIO – Motor Vehicle Employees
American Postal Workers Union, AFL-CIO – Postal Clerks
- The Special Delivery Messengers were merged into the Clerk Craft by Memorandum of Understanding dated November 20, 1997.
American Postal Workers Union, AFL-CIO – Mail Equipment Shops Employees
American Postal Workers Union, AFL-CIO – Material Distribution Centers Employees
American Postal Workers Union, AFL-CIO – Operating Services and Facilities Services Employees.

## Summary

The Postal Service recognizes the APWU as the exclusive bargaining representative of the nationally certified units: Maintenance, Motor Vehicle, Postal Clerks (including the Special Delivery Messengers merged into the Clerk Craft in 1997), Mail Equipment Shops, Material Distribution Centers, and Operating Services and Facilities Services employees.

## Cross-union comparison

The exclusive-recognition structure is the standard opening of each craft's agreement; APWU alone recognizes six certified units, reflecting its multi-craft bargaining structure rather than NALC's, NRLCA's, or NPMHU's single-craft units.
