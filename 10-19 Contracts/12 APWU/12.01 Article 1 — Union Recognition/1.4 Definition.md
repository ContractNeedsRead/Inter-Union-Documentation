# APWU §1.4 — Definition

## Full text

<!-- p.2 -->
### Section 4. Definition

Subject to the foregoing exclusions, this Agreement shall be applicable to all employees in the regular workforce of the U.S. Postal Service, as defined in Article 7, at all present and subsequently acquired installations, facilities, and operations of the Employer, wherever located.

## Summary

The Agreement covers all employees in the regular workforce, as defined in Article 7, at every present and future installation, facility, and operation of the Postal Service, subject to §§1.2–1.3's exclusions.

## Cross-union comparison

The coverage-by-reference pattern (regular workforce defined in the classification article) parallels NALC's and NPMHU's recognition articles; the "wherever located" breadth is common to all four agreements.
