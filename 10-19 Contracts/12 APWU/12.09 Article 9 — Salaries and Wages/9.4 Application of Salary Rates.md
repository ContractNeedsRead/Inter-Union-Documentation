# APWU §9.4 — Application of Salary Rates

## Full text

<!-- p.29 -->
### Section 4. Application of Salary Rates

The Employer shall continue the current application of salary rates for the duration of this Agreement.

## Summary

The Employer continues the current application of salary rates for the life of the Agreement — a continuation clause freezing the existing rate-application practice in place.

## Cross-union comparison

A one-line continuance of the status quo on rate application, parallel to the continuation clauses the other crafts use to carry forward unwritten pay practices.
