# APWU §9.2 — Step Progression Schedule

## Full text

<!-- p.26 -->
### Section 2. Step Progression Schedule

The step progression for the salary schedule shall be as follows:

| For PS Grades | Steps (In Weeks) | Waiting Period |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | All | 44 |
| 4 through 7 | All | 36 |
| 8 through 11 | All | 30 |

Effective no later than the first full pay period 180 days after contract ratification, Grade 3 in RSC P7/P9, K7/K9, and C7/C9, will be upgraded to Grade 4.

Career employees appointed on or after May 23, 2011, will progress as follows:

| PS Grades | Entry Step | Top Step |
|---|---|---|
| 3-4 | JJ | I |
| 5 | FF | K |
| 6-7 | FF | L |
| 8 | A | P |
| 9-11 | D | P |

Effective no later than the first full pay period 180 days after contract ratification, Step JJ will be eliminated from Grade 4 in RSC P7, K7, and C7.

<!-- p.27 -->
Effective September 19, 2026 (PP21-2026), a new top step will be added for Grades 4 – 7 in RSC P7, K7, and C7.

## Summary

Step waiting periods are 44 weeks for Grade 3, 36 for Grades 4–7, and 30 for Grades 8–11. Grade 3 in RSC P7/P9, K7/K9, and C7/C9 upgrades to Grade 4 within 180 days of ratification; employees appointed on or after May 23, 2011 progress from the listed entry steps to the listed top steps by grade band; Step JJ is eliminated from Grade 4 in those RSCs within 180 days; and a new top step is added for Grades 4–7 in those RSCs effective September 19, 2026.

## Cross-union comparison

The waiting-period and entry/top-step tables are the APWU's step-ladder mechanics; NALC's and NPMHU's wage articles carry their own step tables, with the 2011-appointment split being the APWU-specific feature.
