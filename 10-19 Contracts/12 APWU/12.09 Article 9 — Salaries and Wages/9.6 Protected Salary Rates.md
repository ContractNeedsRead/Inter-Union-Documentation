# APWU §9.6 — Protected Salary Rates

## Full text

<!-- p.29 -->
### Section 6. Protected Salary Rates

- **A.** The Employer shall continue the current salary rate protection program for the duration of this Agreement.
- **B.** Employees who qualify for “saved grade” will receive “saved grade” for an indefinite period of time subject to the conditions contained in Article 4, Section 3, and Article 37.4.C.6.b.

## Summary

The current salary rate protection program continues for the life of the Agreement; employees qualifying for saved grade hold it indefinitely, subject to the conditions of Article 4.3 and Article 37.4.C.6.b.

## Cross-union comparison

Saved-grade protection tied to mechanization and craft-posting conditions is the APWU expression of the saved-grade/saved-rate protections the other crafts attach to their own wage and reassignment provisions.
