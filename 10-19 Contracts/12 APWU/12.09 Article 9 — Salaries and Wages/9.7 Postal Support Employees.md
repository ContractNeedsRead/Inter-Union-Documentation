# APWU §9.7 — Postal Support Employees

## Full text

<!-- p.29 -->
### Section 7. Postal Support Employees

The hourly rates for PSEs shall be adjusted by the general increases provided for in Article 9.1. PSEs will receive the following wage adjustments:
- PSEs will receive annual 1% wage increases in addition to the general wage increases provided above for career employees (i.e., 2.3%, 2.4%, and 2.5%).
All percentage increases are applied to the wage rates in effect September 20, 2024. (Table 4.1)

<!-- p.39 -->
### Table 4.1

```
Postal Support Employee (PSE) Hourly Rates
Effective September 20, 2024

Grade             5        6        7        8
Hourly Rates    19.35    20.48    21.68    22.09
```

## Summary

PSE hourly rates move with the §9.1 general increases plus an additional annual 1% (2.3%, 2.4%, 2.5% total), all applied to the September 20, 2024 rates in Table 4.1 (Grades 5–8: $19.35–$22.09).

## Cross-union comparison

The PSE rate schedule with the extra 1% annual bump is the APWU non-career pay structure, paralleling the CCA and MHA wage provisions in the NALC and NPMHU agreements.
