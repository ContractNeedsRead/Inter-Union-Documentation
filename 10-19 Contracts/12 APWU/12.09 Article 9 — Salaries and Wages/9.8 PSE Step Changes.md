# APWU §9.8 — PSE Step Changes

## Full text

<!-- p.29 -->
### Section 8. PSE Step Changes

- **A.** Effective no later than the first full pay period 180 days after contract ratification, a new step will be created for PSEs. PSEs will reach this new step after a 26-week waiting period. This new step will be $0.50 higher than the PSE wage rate at time of implementation.
<!-- p.30 -->
  This step will remain $0.50 higher than the entry step for PSEs in perpetuity there will be no separate calculation for increases applied to this wage rate.
  Upon initial implementation, any PSE with 26 weeks or more of relative standing will move into the new step.
  (See Table 4.2 on page 39 as an example)
- **B.** PSEs in RMPO Step Changes. Effective no later than the first full pay period 180 days after contract ratification, a new step will be created for PSEs in Remotely Managed Post Offices (RMPOs). RMPO PSEs will reach this new step after a 208-week waiting period. This new step will be $1.50 higher than the PSE entry wage rate at time of implementation.
  This step will remain $1.50 higher than the entry step for PSEs in perpetuity; there will be no separate calculation for increases applied to this wage rate.
  Upon initial implementation, any RMPO PSE with 208 weeks or more of relative standing will move into the new step.
  (See Table 4.2 on page 39 as an example)

## Summary

Within 180 days of ratification a new PSE step opens after 26 weeks of relative standing, permanently $0.50 above the entry step; RMPO PSEs get their own step after 208 weeks, permanently $1.50 above entry. PSEs already past those thresholds move in on implementation (Table 4.2, effective December 13, 2025, illustrates). Table 3 records the legacy Step GG (for PSEs auto-converting to career after 24 months in Grades 5–7), eliminated within 180 days of ratification in RSC P7, K7, and C7.

## Cross-union comparison

The PSE step structure with the RMPO premium is the APWU's non-career wage ladder; the CCA and MHA schedules in the other crafts carry their own step arrangements without an RMPO analogue.
