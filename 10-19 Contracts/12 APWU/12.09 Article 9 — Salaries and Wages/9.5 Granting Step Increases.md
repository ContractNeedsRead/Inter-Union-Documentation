# APWU §9.5 — Granting Step Increases

## Full text

<!-- p.29 -->
### Section 5. Granting Step Increases

The Employer will continue the program on granting step increases for the duration of this Agreement.

## Summary

The Employer continues the existing step-increase program for the life of the Agreement.

## Cross-union comparison

Another continuation clause; the operative step-increase rules live in the ELM and the parties' practice, as with the other crafts' corresponding continuance language.
