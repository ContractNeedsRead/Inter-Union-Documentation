# APWU §9.1 — Basic Annual Salary

## Full text

<!-- p.26 -->
### Section 1. Basic Annual Salary

The basic annual salary schedules, with proportional application to hourly rate employees, for those employees covered under the terms and conditions of this Agreement shall be increased as follows:
Effective November 16, 2024—the basic annual salary for each grade and step shall be increased by an amount equal to 1.3% of the basic annual salary for the grades and steps in effect on September 20, 2024. (Table 1)
Effective November 15, 2025—the basic annual salary for each grade and step shall be increased by an amount equal to 1.4% of the basic annual salary for the grades and steps in effect on September 20, 2024. (Table 1)
Effective November 14, 2026—the basic annual salary for each grade and step shall be increased by an amount equal to 1.5% of the basic annual salary for the grades and steps in effect on September 20, 2024. (Table 1)

## Notes (source)

- 1: Effective no later than the first full pay period 180 days after contract ratification, Grade 3 in RSC P7/P9, K7/K9, and C7/C9, will be upgraded to Grade 4.
- 2: Effective no later than the first full pay period 180 days after contract ratification, Step JJ will be eliminated from Grade 4 in RSC P7, K7, and C7.
- 3: Effective September 19, 2026 (PP21-2026), a new top step will be added for Grades 4 - 7 in RSC P7, K7, and C7.

## Summary

Basic annual salary schedules (applied proportionally to hourly employees) increase 1.3% effective November 16, 2024, 1.4% effective November 15, 2025, and 1.5% effective November 14, 2026, each computed on the grades and steps in effect September 20, 2024. Tables 1 (full-time regular annual) and 1.2 (PTF hourly) carry the September 20, 2024 schedules with the New 2011/overlap/pre-2011 annotations and the three table footnotes.

## Cross-union comparison

The three-year percentage pattern over a fixed base date is the standard postal general-increase structure shared with NALC's and NPMHU's wage articles; the 2011 schedule transition (New 2011 vs pre-5/23/2011 step ladders) is the APWU-specific legacy reflected in the tables.
