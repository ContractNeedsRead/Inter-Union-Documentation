# APWU §6.G — Intent

## Full text

<!-- p.15 -->
### G. Intent

The Employer shall not lay off, reduce in force, or take any other action against a non-protected employee solely to prevent the attainment of that employee of protection status.

## Summary

The Employer may not lay off, reduce in force, or act against a non-protected employee for the purpose of preventing that employee from attaining protected status.

## Cross-union comparison

The anti-evasion clause protecting the six-year path to no-layoff status is the APWU counterpart of the good-faith intent clauses in NALC's and NPMHU's layoff protections.
