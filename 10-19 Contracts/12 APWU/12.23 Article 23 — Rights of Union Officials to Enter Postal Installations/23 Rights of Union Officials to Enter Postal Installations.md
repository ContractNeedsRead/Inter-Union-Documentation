# APWU §23 — Rights of Union Officials to Enter Postal Installations

## Full text

<!-- p.118 -->
Upon reasonable notice to the Employer, duly authorized representatives of the Union shall be permitted to enter postal installations for the purpose of performing and engaging in official union duties and business related to the Collective Bargaining Agreement. There shall be no interruption of the work of employees due to such visits and representatives shall adhere to the established security regulations.
(See Memo, page 353)
(The preceding Article, Article 23, shall apply to PSEs)

## Summary

With reasonable notice, duly authorized union representatives may enter postal installations for official union duties and business related to the Agreement, without interrupting work and in compliance with security regulations. Article 23 applies to PSEs.

## Cross-union comparison

The reasonable-notice access right with the no-interruption and security conditions is the postal-standard union-access provision shared across the crafts.
