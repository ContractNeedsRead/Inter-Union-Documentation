# APWU §15.7 — Outstanding Debts of Retired Employees

## Full text

<!-- p.100 -->
### Section 7. Outstanding Debts of Retired Employees

A dispute over payroll or collection disputes may be filed as a grievance at Step 1; Article 15, Section 1.
(See Memo, page 337)
(The preceding Article, Article 15, shall apply to PSEs)

## Summary

Disputes over payroll or collection issues may be filed as grievances at Step 1 under §15.1. Article 15 applies to PSEs.

## Cross-union comparison

The payroll/collection-dispute filing right and the PSE extension round out the article consistently with the other crafts' grievance scope.
