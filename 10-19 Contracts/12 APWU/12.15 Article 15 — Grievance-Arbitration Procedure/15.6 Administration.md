# APWU §15.6 — Administration

## Full text

<!-- p.100 -->
### Section 6. Administration

On a weekly basis, the Employer will furnish to the President of the Union or his/her designee an electronic file detailing cases pending arbitration. This report will contain the following sortable fields or their equivalents:
- a. Area;
- b. Performance Cluster;
- c. Union;
- d. Appeal Step;
- e. Grievance Arbitration Tracking System Number.

## Summary

Weekly, the Employer sends the Union President an electronic file of pending arbitration cases with sortable fields for Area, Performance Cluster, Union, Appeal Step, and Grievance Arbitration Tracking System Number.

## Cross-union comparison

The weekly electronic case-file report is the APWU's administration clause; the other crafts carry comparable reporting obligations in their grievance-arbitration articles.
