# APWU §11.5 — Holiday on Non-Work Day

## Full text

<!-- p.45 -->
### Section 5. Holiday on Non-Work Day

- **A.** When a holiday falls on Sunday, the following Monday will be observed as the holiday. When a holiday falls on Saturday, the preceding Friday shall be observed as the holiday.
- **B.** When an employee’s scheduled non-work day falls on a day observed as a holiday, the employee’s scheduled workday preceding the holiday shall be designated as that employee’s holiday.

## Summary

Sunday holidays are observed the following Monday and Saturday holidays the preceding Friday. When an observed holiday falls on an employee's non-work day, the scheduled workday before the holiday becomes that employee's designated holiday.

## Cross-union comparison

The Saturday/Sunday observation rules and the preceding-workday designation are the postal-standard holiday mechanics shared across the four agreements — the same rules the Mittenthal 1987 award construed for the holiday pecking order.
