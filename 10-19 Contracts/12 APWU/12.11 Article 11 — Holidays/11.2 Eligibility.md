# APWU §11.2 — Eligibility

## Full text

<!-- p.44 -->
### Section 2. Eligibility

To be eligible for holiday pay, an employee must be in a pay status the last hour of the employee’s scheduled workday prior to or the first hour of the employee’s scheduled workday after the holiday.

## Summary

Holiday pay eligibility requires pay status during the last hour of the scheduled workday before the holiday or the first hour of the scheduled workday after it.

## Cross-union comparison

The last-hour-before/first-hour-after pay-status test is the shared postal holiday-eligibility rule, stated in the same terms by NALC and NPMHU.
