# APWU §11.6 — Holiday Schedule

## Full text

<!-- p.45 -->
### Section 6. Holiday Schedule

- **A.** The Employer will determine the number and categories of employees needed for holiday work and a schedule shall be posted as of the Tuesday preceding the service week in which the holiday falls.
  (See Memo, page 303)
<!-- p.46 -->
- **B.** As many full-time and part-time regular schedule employees as can be spared will be excused from duty on a holiday or day designated as their holiday. Such employees will not be required to work on a holiday or day designated as their holiday unless all PSEs and part-time flexibles are utilized to the maximum extent possible even if the payment of overtime is required, and unless all full-time and part-time regulars with the needed skills who wish to work on the holiday have been afforded an opportunity to do so.
- **C.** An employee scheduled to work on a holiday who does not work shall not receive holiday pay, unless such absence is based on an extreme emergency situation and is excused by the Employer.
- **D. Postal Support Employees**
  PSEs will be scheduled for work on a holiday or designated holiday after all full-time volunteers are scheduled to work on their holiday or designated holiday. They will be scheduled, to the extent possible, prior to any full-time volunteers or non-volunteers being scheduled to work a nonscheduled day or any full-time non-volunteers being required to work their holiday or designated holiday. If the parties have locally negotiated a pecking order that would schedule full-time volunteers on a nonscheduled day, the Local Memorandum of Understanding will apply.

## Summary

The holiday schedule posts by the Tuesday before the holiday's service week. As many full-time and part-time regular schedule employees as can be spared are excused, and they cannot be required to work unless all PSEs and part-time flexibles are used to the maximum extent possible (even at overtime pay) and all willing regulars with needed skills have had the opportunity. A scheduled employee who does not work forfeits holiday pay unless the absence is an excused extreme emergency. PSEs are scheduled only after all full-time volunteers, and before full-timers are worked on nonscheduled days or non-volunteers on their holiday; a locally negotiated pecking order for volunteers on nonscheduled days prevails.

## Cross-union comparison

The pecking order (non-career and flexibles to the maximum extent, then regular volunteers, before non-volunteers) is the same provision the Mittenthal 1987 award held management may not deviate from to avoid penalty overtime; the Tuesday posting is the APWU timing (the 1984 version said Wednesday).
