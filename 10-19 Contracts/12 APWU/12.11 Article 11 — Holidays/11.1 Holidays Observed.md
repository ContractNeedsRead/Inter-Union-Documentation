# APWU §11.1 — Holidays Observed

## Full text

<!-- p.44 -->
### Section 1. Holidays Observed

The following eleven (11) days shall be considered holidays for full-time and part-time regular scheduled employees hereinafter referred to in this Article as “employees”:
New Year’s Day
Martin Luther King, Jr.’s Birthday
Washington’s Birthday (Presidents’ Day)
Memorial Day
Juneteenth National Independence Day
Independence Day
Labor Day
Columbus Day
Veterans Day
Thanksgiving Day
Christmas Day

## Summary

Eleven holidays are observed for full-time and part-time regular scheduled employees: New Year's Day, Martin Luther King Jr.'s Birthday, Washington's Birthday (Presidents' Day), Memorial Day, Juneteenth National Independence Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Columbus Day, Veterans Day, Thanksgiving Day, and Christmas Day.

## Cross-union comparison

The eleven-holiday list (with Juneteenth added) matches the holiday lists in the other postal agreements; APWU's designation of "employees" for the article's purposes is full-time and part-time regular scheduled employees, with separate sections for PTFs and PSEs.
