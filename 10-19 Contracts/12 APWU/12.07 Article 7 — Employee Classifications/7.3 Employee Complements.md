# APWU §7.3 — Employee Complements

## Full text

<!-- p.19 -->
### Section 3. Employee Complements

- **A.** The Employer shall staff all postal installations in the regular workforce as of the date of this Agreement as follows:
  - 1. With respect to the Clerk Craft, there will no longer be part-time flexible (PTF) employees working in Function 1 or in post offices Level 21 and above. Part-time flexible (PTF) employees may work in Function 4 offices Level 20 and
<!-- p.20 -->
    below. Offices, Level 20 and below, remain subject to the Article 7.3.B obligations to maximize the number of full-time employees and minimize the number of part-time flexible employees who have no fixed work schedules.
    There will no longer be part-time regular (PTR) employees in the Clerk Craft.
  - 2. With respect to the Motor Vehicle Craft, part-time flexible (PTF) employees will be capped at twenty percent (20%) of the Motor Vehicle Craft career complement by installation. The rounding up rule of .5 shall apply.
    (See Memo, page 432)
  - 3. With respect to all other crafts, installations shall be staffed in accordance with the provisions of this Agreement.
    (See Memos, pages 284-293 and 379)
- **B.** The Employer shall maximize the number of full-time employees and minimize the number of part-time employees who have no fixed work schedules in all postal installations.
- **C.** A part-time flexible employee working eight (8) hours within ten (10), on the same five (5) days each week and the same assignment over a six-month period will demonstrate the need for converting the assignment to a full-time position.
  (See Memo, page 288)
- **D.** The total number of part-time regular employees who may be employed shall not exceed 2.5% of the total number of Maintenance Craft career employees covered by this Agreement.

## Summary

Installations are staffed so that the Clerk Craft has no PTFs in Function 1 or Level 21+ offices (PTFs remain only in Level 20-and-below Function 4 offices) and no part-time regulars; MVS PTFs are capped at 20% of the career complement by installation with .5 rounding; other crafts staff per the Agreement. The Employer must maximize full-time and minimize flexible part-time employment everywhere; a PTF working eight-in-ten on the same five days for six months demonstrates the need to convert the assignment to full-time; and Maintenance Craft part-time regulars are capped at 2.5% of Maintenance career employees.

## Cross-union comparison

The complement-staffing mandates are the APWU side of the postal maximization rules (the NALC/NPMHU equivalents drive their own conversion and complement obligations); the craft-specific PTF/PTR eliminations and caps have no direct counterpart in the single-craft agreements.
