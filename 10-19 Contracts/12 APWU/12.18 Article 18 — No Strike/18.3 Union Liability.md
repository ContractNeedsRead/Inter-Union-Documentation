# APWU §18.3 — Union Liability

## Full text

<!-- p.113 -->
### Section 3. Union Liability

It is agreed that the Union or its Local Unions (whether called Locals or by other names) which comply with the requirements of this Article shall not be liable for the unauthorized action of their members or other postal employees.

## Summary

Unions complying with this Article's requirements are not liable for members' or other postal employees' unauthorized action.

## Cross-union comparison

The compliance-shields-liability rule is standard across the postal no-strike clauses.
