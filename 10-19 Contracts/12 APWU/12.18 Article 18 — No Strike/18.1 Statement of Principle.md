# APWU §18.1 — Statement of Principle

## Full text

<!-- p.112 -->
### Section 1. Statement of Principle

The Union on behalf of its members agree that it will not call or sanction a strike or slowdown.

## Summary

The Union agrees on behalf of its members not to call or sanction a strike or slowdown.

## Cross-union comparison

The no-strike pledge is the boilerplate shared by all four postal agreements.
