# APWU §25.2 — Higher-Level Pay

## Full text

<!-- p.119 -->
### Section 2. Higher-Level Pay

An employee who is detailed to higher-level work shall be paid at the higher level for time actually spent on such job. An
<!-- p.120 -->
employee’s higher-level rate shall be determined as if promoted to the position. An employee temporarily assigned or detailed to a lower-level position shall be paid at the employee’s own rate.

## Summary

Employees detailed to higher-level work are paid at the higher level for time actually worked, with the rate determined as if promoted; temporary lower-level assignments keep the employee's own rate.

## Cross-union comparison

The pay-for-time-actually-spent rule at the as-if-promoted rate is the postal-standard higher-level pay provision.
