# APWU §25.5 — Leave Pay

## Full text

<!-- p.120 -->
### Section 5. Leave Pay

Leave pay for employees detailed to a higher-level position will be administered in accordance with the following:
Employees working short term on a higher-level assignment or detail will be entitled to approved sick and annual paid leave at the higher-level rate for a period not to exceed three (3) days. Short term shall mean an employee has been on an assignment or detail to a higher-level for a period of 29 consecutive work days or less at the time leave is taken and such assignment or detail to the higher-level position is resumed upon return to work. All
<!-- p.121 -->
short-term assignments or details will be automatically canceled if replacements are required for absent detailed employees.
Long term shall mean an employee has been on an assignment or detail to the higher-level position for a period of 30 consecutive workdays or longer at the time leave is taken and such assignment or detail to the higher-level position is resumed upon return to work.
Terminal leave payments resulting from death will be paid at the higher level for all employees who are assigned or detailed to higher-level assignments on their last workday.

## Summary

Short-term details (29 consecutive workdays or less, resumed on return) earn approved sick and annual leave at the higher-level rate for up to three days, and short-term assignments cancel automatically if replacements are needed; long-term details run 30 consecutive workdays or more with the same resumption condition. Terminal leave payments from death are paid at the higher level for employees on higher-level assignments on their last workday.

## Cross-union comparison

The short/long-term leave-pay split with the three-day higher-level leave window and the death-payment rule is the postal-standard higher-level leave provision.
