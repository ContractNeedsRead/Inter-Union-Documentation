# APWU §10.1 — Funding

## Full text

<!-- p.40 -->
### Section 1. Funding

The Employer shall continue funding the leave program so as to continue the current leave earning level for the duration of this Agreement.

## Summary

The Employer keeps funding the leave program to maintain the current leave-earning level for the life of the Agreement.

## Cross-union comparison

A funding-continuation clause; the operative earning levels live in the ELM, as with the other crafts' leave-funding provisions.
