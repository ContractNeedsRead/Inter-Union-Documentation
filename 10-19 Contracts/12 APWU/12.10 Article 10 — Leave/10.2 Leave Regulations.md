# APWU §10.2 — Leave Regulations

## Full text

<!-- p.40 -->
### Section 2. Leave Regulations

- **A.** The leave regulations in Subchapter 510 of the Employee and Labor Relations Manual, insofar as such regulations establish wages, hours and working conditions of employees covered by this Agreement, other than PSEs, shall remain in effect for the life of this Agreement.
  (See Memos, pages 306-314)
- **B.** Career employees will be given preference over noncareer
<!-- p.41 -->
  employees when scheduling annual leave. This preference will take into consideration that scheduling is done on a tour-by-tour basis and that employee skills are a determining factor in this decision.
  (The preceding paragraph, Article 10.2.B applies to PSEs)

## Summary

ELM Subchapter 510's leave regulations stay in effect for the life of the Agreement for covered employees other than PSEs. Career employees get preference over noncareer employees in annual leave scheduling, accounting for tour-by-tour scheduling and skill needs; the preference paragraph applies to PSEs.

## Cross-union comparison

The ELM-subchapter incorporation and the career-over-non-career leave preference are the same devices NALC and NPMHU use, with the PSE parenthetical the APWU-specific extension.
