# APWU §35.1 — Programs

## Full text

<!-- p.140 -->
### Section 1. Programs

The Employer and the Union express strong support for programs of self-help. The Employer shall provide and maintain a program which shall encompass the education, identification, referral, guidance and follow-up of those employees afflicted by the disease of alcoholism and/or drug abuse. When an employee is referred to the EAP by the Employer, the EAP staff will have a reasonable period of time to evaluate the employee’s progress in the program.
This program of labor-management cooperation shall support the continuation of the EAP for alcohol and/or drug abuse at the current level. In addition to the current EAP, the EAP will be expanded, as provided in Section 2 hereof, to encompass the education, identification, referral and guidance of:
<!-- p.141 -->
- 1. employees’ family members afflicted with alcoholism and/or drug abuse which could or does have a negative impact on the employee’s work performance; and
- 2. those employees and their families experiencing other family and/or personal problems which could or do have a negative impact on the employee’s work performance.
An employee’s voluntary participation in the EAP for assistance with alcohol and/or drug abuse will be considered favorably in disciplinary action proceedings.

## Summary

The Employer maintains an EAP covering education, identification, referral, guidance, and follow-up for employees with alcoholism or drug abuse, with a reasonable evaluation period for Employer referrals. The program continues at its current level and expands to employees' family members with alcohol/drug problems and employees and families with other personal problems affecting work performance. Voluntary EAP participation for alcohol/drug assistance is considered favorably in discipline.

## Cross-union comparison

The EAP structure with the discipline-favorability clause is the postal-standard program shared across the crafts.
