# APWU §24.1 — Continuation of Benefits

## Full text

<!-- p.118 -->
### Section 1. Continuation of Benefits

Any employee on leave without pay to devote full or part-time service to the Union signatory to this Agreement shall be credited with step increases as if in a pay status. Retirement benefits will accrue on the basis of the employee’s step so attained, provided the employee makes contributions to the retirement fund in accordance with current procedure. Annual and sick leave will be earned in accordance with existing procedures based on hours worked.

## Summary

Employees on LWOP for full or part-time union service are credited with step increases as if in pay status; retirement benefits accrue on the attained step with the employee's contributions; and annual and sick leave accrue per existing procedures on hours worked.

## Cross-union comparison

The step-credit and retirement-accrual continuation for union LWOP is the postal-standard benefit-continuation provision shared with the other crafts.
