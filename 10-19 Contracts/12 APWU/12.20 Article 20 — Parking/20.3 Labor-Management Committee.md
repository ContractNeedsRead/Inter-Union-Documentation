# APWU §20.3 — Labor-Management Committee

## Full text

<!-- p.115 -->
### Section 3. Labor-Management Committee

Parking is a proper subject for discussion at Local Labor-Management Committee meetings. The location of new, additional, or improved parking facilities; the number of parking spaces; security and lighting in the parking areas, as well as similar
<!-- p.116 -->
subjects, are proper agenda items for such meetings. The Local Labor-Management Committee may make recommendations to the installation head concerning such subjects.
(The preceding Article, Article 20, shall apply to PSEs)

## Summary

Parking — facility locations, space counts, security, lighting, and similar subjects — is a proper Local Labor-Management Committee agenda item, with recommendations to the installation head. Article 20 applies to PSEs.

## Cross-union comparison

The parking-as-LM-committee-subject provision is the standard closing of the postal parking articles.
