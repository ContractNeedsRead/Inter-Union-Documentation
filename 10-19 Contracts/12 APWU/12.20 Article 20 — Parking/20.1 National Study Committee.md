# APWU §20.1 — National Study Committee

## Full text

<!-- p.115 -->
### Section 1. National Study Committee

The existing parking program will remain in effect. There shall be established at the National Level, as a subcommittee of the National Joint Labor-Management Committee, a National Study Committee on Parking in order to improve the parking program at existing facilities and to recommend such programs for new facilities.

## Summary

The existing parking program continues, and a National Study Committee on Parking — a subcommittee of the National Joint Labor-Management Committee — is established to improve parking at existing facilities and recommend programs for new ones.

## Cross-union comparison

The study-committee device for parking is the shared postal approach in the crafts' parking articles.
