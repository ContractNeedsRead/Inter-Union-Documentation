# APWU §43.2 — Duration

## Full text

<!-- p.250 -->
### Section 2. Duration

Unless otherwise provided, this Agreement shall be effective July 11, 2025, and shall remain in full force and effect to and including 12 midnight September 20, 2027, and unless either party desires to terminate or modify it, for successive annual periods. The party demanding such termination or modification must serve written notice of such intent to the other party, not less than 90 or more than 120 days before the expiration date of the Agreement.
(The preceding Article, Article 43, shall apply to PSEs)

## Summary

The Agreement is effective July 11, 2025 through midnight September 20, 2027, then continues for successive annual periods unless either party serves written termination-or-modification notice between 90 and 120 days before expiration. Article 43 applies to PSEs.

## Cross-union comparison

The duration and notice provisions are the standard postal contract-duration language shared across the crafts.
