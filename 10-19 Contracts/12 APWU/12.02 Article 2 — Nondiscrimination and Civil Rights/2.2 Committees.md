# APWU §2.2 — Committees

## Full text

<!-- p.5 -->
### Section 2. Committees

There are established at the National and APWU Regional/USPS Area Levels Joint Committees on Human Rights. The Committees will be composed of responsible representatives of the Union and responsible management officials. The Committees may develop affirmative action proposals on all matters affecting minority groups. The Committees will also be advised of the plan for site selection for facilities planned for national postal mail networks and major metropolitan areas, and review availability of adequate housing and public transportation. The Committees shall meet as required at mutually agreeable times.

## Summary

Joint Committees on Human Rights are established at the National and Regional/Area levels, with union and management representatives, to develop affirmative action proposals affecting minority groups, to be advised of facility site-selection plans for national networks and major metropolitan areas, and to review housing and public transportation availability. Meetings occur as required at mutually agreeable times.

## Cross-union comparison

The Human Rights committee structure is a feature of the APWU and NALC agreements addressing the same postal-civil-rights machinery; NPMHU and NRLCA carry narrower civil-rights bodies.
