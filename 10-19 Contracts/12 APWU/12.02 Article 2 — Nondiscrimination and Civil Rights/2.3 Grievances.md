# APWU §2.3 — Grievances

## Full text

<!-- p.5 -->
### Section 3. Grievances

Grievances arising under this Article may be filed at Step 2 of the grievance procedure within fourteen (14) days of when the employee or the Union has first learned or may reasonably have been expected to have learned of the alleged discrimination, unless filed directly at the National Level, in which case the provisions of this Agreement for initiating grievances at that level shall apply.
(The preceding Article, Article 2, shall apply to PSEs)

## Summary

Discrimination grievances may be filed at Step 2 within fourteen days of when the employee or Union first learned, or reasonably should have learned, of the alleged discrimination; national-level filings follow the agreement's rules for initiating grievances at that level. Article 2 applies to PSEs.

## Cross-union comparison

The discrimination-specific fourteen-day Step 2 entry point is an APWU/NALC-style carve-out from the ordinary grievance time limits; the other crafts route discrimination claims through their general procedures.
