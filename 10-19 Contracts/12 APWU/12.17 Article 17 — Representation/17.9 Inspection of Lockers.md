# APWU §17.9 — Inspection of Lockers

## Full text

<!-- p.112 -->
### Section 9. Inspection of Lockers

Except as provided in Article 39.3.C, the Employer agrees that, a steward or the employee shall be given the opportunity to be present at any inspection of employees’ lockers, except in matters where there is reasonable cause to suspect criminal activity. For a general inspection where employees have had prior notification of at least a week, the above is not applicable.

## Summary

Except as Article 39.3.C provides, a steward or the employee must be given the opportunity to be present at locker inspections, unless there is reasonable cause to suspect criminal activity; the right does not apply to general inspections announced at least a week in advance.

## Cross-union comparison

The steward-presence right for locker inspections is a shared postal representation protection with the same criminal-cause and announced-inspection carve-outs.
