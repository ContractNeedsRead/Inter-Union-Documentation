# APWU §17.1 — Stewards

## Full text

<!-- p.105 -->
### Section 1. Stewards

Stewards may be designated for the purpose of investigating, presenting and adjusting grievances.

## Summary

Stewards are designated to investigate, present, and adjust grievances.

## Cross-union comparison

The steward purpose clause is the standard opening of the postal representation articles.
