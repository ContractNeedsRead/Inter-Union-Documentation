# APWU §17.4 — Payment of Stewards

## Full text

<!-- p.107 -->
### Section 4. Payment of Stewards

The Employer will authorize payment only under the following conditions:
Grievances:
Steps 1 and 2 The aggrieved and one (1) Union steward — (only as permitted under the formula in Section 2.A)
<!-- p.108 -->
for time actually spent in grievance handling, including investigation and meetings with the Employer. The Employer will also compensate a steward for the time reasonably necessary to write a grievance. In addition, the Employer will compensate any witnesses for the time required to attend a Step 2 meeting.
Meetings called by the Employer for information exchange and other conditions designated by the Employer concerning contract application.
Employer authorized payment as outlined above will be granted at the applicable straight time rate, providing the time spent is a part of the employee’s or steward’s (only as provided for under the formula in Section 2.A) regular work day.
(The preceding Section, Article 17.4, shall apply to PSEs)

## Summary

The Employer pays the aggrieved and one formula-certified steward for time actually spent in Steps 1–2 grievance handling, including investigation, meetings, reasonable grievance-writing time, and witnesses' Step 2 attendance; also for Employer-called information-exchange meetings on contract application. Payment is at the straight-time rate for time within the regular workday, and §17.4 applies to PSEs.

## Cross-union comparison

The paid-time-for-grievance-handling scheme scoped to the steward formula is the postal-standard steward payment provision; the witness and information-exchange extensions are shared features.
