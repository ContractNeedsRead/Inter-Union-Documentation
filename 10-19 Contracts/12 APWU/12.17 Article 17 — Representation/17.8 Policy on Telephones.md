# APWU §17.8 — Policy on Telephones

## Full text

<!-- p.112 -->
### Section 8. Policy on Telephones

The parties recognize that telephones are for official USPS business. However, the Employer at the Local Level shall establish a policy for the use of telephones by designated Union representatives for legitimate business related to the administration of the National Agreement, subject to sound business judgment and practices.

## Summary

Telephones are for official USPS business, but the Employer must establish a Local-Level policy allowing designated union representatives to use telephones for legitimate agreement-administration business, subject to sound business judgment.

## Cross-union comparison

The telephone-use policy obligation is a standard postal union-rights provision across the crafts.
