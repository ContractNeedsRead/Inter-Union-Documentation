# APWU §3 — Management Rights

## Full text

<!-- p.5 -->
The Employer shall have the exclusive right, subject to the provisions of this Agreement and consistent with applicable laws and regulations:

- **A.** to direct employees of the Employer in the performance of official duties;
- **B.** to hire, promote, transfer, assign, and retain employees in positions within the Postal Service and to suspend, demote, discharge, or take other disciplinary action against such employees;
<!-- p.6 -->
- **C.** to maintain the efficiency of the operations entrusted to it;
- **D.** to determine the methods, means, and personnel by which such operations are to be conducted;
- **E.** to prescribe a uniform dress to be worn by designated employees; and
- **F.** to take whatever actions may be necessary to carry out its mission in emergency situations, i.e., an unforeseen circumstance or a combination of circumstances which calls for immediate action in a situation which is not expected to be of a recurring nature.
  (The preceding Article, Article 3, shall apply to PSEs)

## Summary

Management holds the exclusive right — subject to the Agreement and applicable law — to direct employees, hire, promote, transfer, assign, retain, suspend, demote, and discharge; to maintain efficiency; to determine methods, means, and personnel; to prescribe uniforms; and to act in genuine emergencies, defined as unforeseen, non-recurring circumstances calling for immediate action. Article 3 applies to PSEs.

## Cross-union comparison

The APWU management-rights list is materially the same as NALC's and NPMHU's, including the emergency definition that Article 8's NTFT Q&As and the layoff provisions invoke; the "subject to the provisions of this Agreement" opening is the contract-law hook shared across all four agreements.
