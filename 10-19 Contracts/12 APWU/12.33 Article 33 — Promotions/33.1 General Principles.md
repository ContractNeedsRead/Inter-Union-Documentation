# APWU §33.1 — General Principles

## Full text

<!-- p.137 -->
### Section 1. General Principles

The Employer agrees to place particular emphasis upon career advancement opportunities. First opportunity for promotions will be given to qualified career employees. The Employer will assist employees to improve their own skills through training and self-help programs, and will continue to expand the Postal Employee Development Center concept.

## Summary

The Employer emphasizes career advancement, gives qualified career employees first promotion opportunity, assists skill improvement through training and self-help programs, and continues expanding the Postal Employee Development Center concept.

## Cross-union comparison

The career-first promotion principle is the shared postal promotion policy across the crafts.
