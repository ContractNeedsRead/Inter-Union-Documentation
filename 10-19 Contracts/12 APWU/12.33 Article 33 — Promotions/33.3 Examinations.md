# APWU §33.3 — Examinations

## Full text

<!-- p.138 -->
### Section 3. Examinations

When an examination is given, there shall be no unreasonable limitation on the number of examinations that may be taken by an applicant.

## Summary

Applicants may take examinations without unreasonable limits on the number of attempts.

## Cross-union comparison

The no-unreasonable-limits examination rule is standard postal promotion language.
