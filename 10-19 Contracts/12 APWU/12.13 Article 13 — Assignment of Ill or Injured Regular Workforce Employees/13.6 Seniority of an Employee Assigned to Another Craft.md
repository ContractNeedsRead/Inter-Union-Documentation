# APWU §13.6 — Seniority of an Employee Assigned to Another Craft

## Full text

<!-- p.73 -->
### Section 6. Seniority of an Employee Assigned to Another Craft

- **A.** Except as provided for in Section 4.I above, a full-time regular employee assigned to another craft or occupational group in the same or lower level in the same installation shall take the seniority for preferred tours and assignments, whichever is the lesser of (a) one (1) day junior to the junior full-time regular employee in the craft or occupational group, (b) retain the seniority the employee had in the employee’s former craft.
- **B.** A part-time flexible employee who is permanently assigned to a full-time regular or part-time flexible assignment in another craft, under the provisions of this Article, shall begin a new period of seniority. If assigned as a part-time flexible, it shall be at the foot of the part-time flexible roll.

## Summary

A full-time regular assigned cross-craft takes the lesser of one day junior to the junior full-time regular in the new craft or their former-craft seniority (except §13.4.I cases); a PTF permanently assigned cross-craft starts new seniority, at the foot of the PTF roll if assigned as a PTF.

## Cross-union comparison

The lesser-of rule for cross-craft seniority mirrors the Article 12.5.C.1.c displacement formula; the same principle appears in the other crafts' reassignment provisions where cross-craft movement exists.
