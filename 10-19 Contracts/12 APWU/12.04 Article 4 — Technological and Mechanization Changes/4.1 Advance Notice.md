# APWU §4.1 — Advance Notice

## Full text

<!-- p.6 -->
### Section 1. Advance Notice

The Union party to this Agreement will be informed as far in advance as practicable, but no less than 30 days in advance, of implementation of technological or mechanization changes which affect jobs, including new or changed jobs in the area of wages, hours or working conditions. When major new mechanization or equipment is to be purchased and installed, the Union at the National Level will be informed as far in advance as practicable, but no less than 90 days in advance.

## Summary

The Union gets at least thirty days' advance notice of technological or mechanization changes affecting jobs (wages, hours, or working conditions), and at least ninety days at the National Level when major new mechanization or equipment is to be purchased and installed.

## Cross-union comparison

The 30/90-day notice structure tracks NALC's and NPMHU's mechanization articles; the second sentence's National-Level trigger for major equipment purchases matches the corresponding NALC provision.
