# APWU §26.3 — Annual Allowance – Work Clothing Program

## Full text

<!-- p.123 -->
### Section 3. Annual Allowance – Work Clothing Program

The annual allowance for eligible employees in the Work Clothes Program and Contract Uniform Program shall be as follows:
Clerical, Motor Vehicle, Maintenance (eligible) – work clothes
- — $116.00 effective May 21, 2026
- — $119.00 effective May 21, 2027
Custodial Maintenance (eligible) – contract uniform
- — $219.00 effective May 21, 2026
- — $224.00 effective May 21, 2027
Vehicle Maintenance (eligible) – contract uniform
- — $276.00 effective May 21, 2026
- — $283.00 effective May 21, 2027
The increase shall become effective on the employee’s anniversary date.
Unused portions of an eligible employee’s annual allowance for uniform and work clothing will be carried over and available for use beginning twelve (12) months after the end of each anniversary year. An eligible employee’s uniform or work clothing allowance balance may not exceed the sum of two (2) years of the employee’s annual allowance entitlement. This uniform and work clothing program adjustment will be implemented no later than twelve (12) months from the effective date of the 2024-2027 Agreement.

## Summary

Work Clothes and Contract Uniform allowances by category (work clothes: $116/$119; custodial contract uniform: $219/$224; vehicle maintenance contract uniform: $276/$283, effective May 21, 2026/2027 on anniversary dates) rise on schedule. Unused allowances carry over, usable twelve months after each anniversary year, with balances capped at two years' entitlement, implemented within twelve months of the agreement's effective date.

## Cross-union comparison

The category-tiered work clothing allowance table is the APWU-specific schedule; NALC's and NPMHU's uniform articles use their own tier structures.
