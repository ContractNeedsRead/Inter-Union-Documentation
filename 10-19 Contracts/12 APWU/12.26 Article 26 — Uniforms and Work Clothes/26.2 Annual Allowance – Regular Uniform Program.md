# APWU §26.2 — Annual Allowance – Regular Uniform Program

## Full text

<!-- p.122 -->
### Section 2. Annual Allowance – Regular Uniform Program

The annual allowance for eligible employees in the regular uniform program shall be as follows:

- **A.** Effective May 21, 2026, the annual allowance for all eligible employees shall be increased from $537.00 per annum to $564.00 per annum; and from $230.00 per annum to $242.00 per annum. The increase shall become effective on the employee’s anniversary date.
  Effective May 21, 2027, the annual allowance for all eligible employees shall be increased from $564.00 per annum to $578.00 per annum; and from $242.00 per annum to $248.00 per annum. The increase shall become effective on the employee’s anniversary date.
- **B.** A newly eligible employee entering the regular uniform program will receive an additional credit to the employee’s allowance, as follows:
  Effective May 21, 2026
  - — $131.00 if entitled to $564.00 per annum;
  - — $32.00 if entitled to $242.00 per annum
  Effective May 21, 2027
  - — $134.00 if entitled to $578.00 per annum;
  - — $33.00 if entitled to $248.00 per annum
  An eligible employee cannot receive this additional credit more than once; however, the current procedures regarding employees
<!-- p.123 -->
  transferring from one allowance category to another shall be continued.

## Summary

Regular uniform program allowances rise to $564.00 (from $537.00) and $242.00 (from $230.00) effective May 21, 2026, and to $578.00 and $248.00 effective May 21, 2027, on each employee's anniversary date. Newly eligible employees get a one-time additional credit ($131/$32 in 2026; $134/$33 in 2027), with existing category-transfer procedures continuing.
