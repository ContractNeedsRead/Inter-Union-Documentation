# APWU §12.3 — Principles of Posting

## Full text

<!-- p.49 -->
### Section 3. Principles of Posting

- **A.** To ensure a more efficient and stable workforce, an employee may be designated a successful bidder no more than five (5) times during the duration of this Agreement unless such bid:
  - 1. is to a job in a higher wage level;
  - 2. is to a duty assignment which does not require a deferment period or additional off-site training;
  - 3. is due to elimination or reposting of the employee’s duty assignment; or
  - 4. enables an employee to become assigned to a station closer to the employee’s place of residence.
- **B.** Specific provisions for posting for each craft are contained in the craft posting provisions of this Agreement.

## Summary

An employee may be a successful bidder at most five times during the agreement, unless the bid is to a higher wage level, requires no deferment period or off-site training, follows elimination or reposting of the employee's assignment, or moves the employee closer to home. Craft-specific posting rules live in the craft articles.

## Cross-union comparison

The five-bid cap with its four exceptions is the APWU's stability device; the other crafts' posting principles achieve the same goal through their own bid-frequency rules, with craft-level detail deferred to the craft articles in each agreement.
