# APWU §12.6 — Transfers

## Full text

<!-- p.66 -->
### Section 6. Transfers

- **A.** Installation heads will consider requests for transfers submitted by employees from other installations.
- **B.** Providing a written request for a voluntary transfer has been submitted, a written acknowledgment shall be given in a timely manner.
  (See Memos, pages 317, 321, and 327)

## Summary

Installation heads consider transfer requests from employees at other installations, and written voluntary-transfer requests must receive a timely written acknowledgment.

## Cross-union comparison

The consider-and-acknowledge duty is the minimal postal transfer framework; the operative transfer rules for each craft are carried in the MOUs the provision cites.
