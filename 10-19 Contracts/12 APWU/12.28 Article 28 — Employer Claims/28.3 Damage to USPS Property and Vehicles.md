# APWU §28.3 — Damage to USPS Property and Vehicles

## Full text

<!-- p.127 -->
### Section 3. Damage to USPS Property and Vehicles

An employee shall be financially liable for any loss or damage to property of the Employer including leased property and vehicles only when the loss or damage was the result of the willful or deliberate misconduct of such employee.

## Summary

Employees are financially liable for loss or damage to Employer property (including leased property and vehicles) only on willful or deliberate misconduct.

## Cross-union comparison

The willful-or-deliberate-misconduct standard is the shared postal rule for property damage liability.
