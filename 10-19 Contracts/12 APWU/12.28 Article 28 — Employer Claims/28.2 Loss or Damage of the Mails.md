# APWU §28.2 — Loss or Damage of the Mails

## Full text

<!-- p.127 -->
### Section 2. Loss or Damage of the Mails

An employee is responsible for the protection of the mails entrusted to the employee. Such employee shall not be financially liable for any loss, rifling, damage, wrong delivery of, or depredation on, the mails or failure to collect or remit C.O.D. funds unless the employee failed to exercise reasonable care.

## Summary

Employees protect the mails entrusted to them and face no financial liability for loss, rifling, damage, wrong delivery, depredation, or C.O.D. collection failures unless they failed to exercise reasonable care.

## Cross-union comparison

The reasonable-care standard for mail loss is identical across the postal employer-claims articles.
