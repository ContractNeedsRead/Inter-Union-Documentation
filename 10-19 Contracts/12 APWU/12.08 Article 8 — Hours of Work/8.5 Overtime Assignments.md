# APWU §8.5 — Overtime Assignments

## Full text

<!-- p.23 -->
### Section 5. Overtime Assignments

When needed, overtime work for regular full-time employees shall be scheduled among qualified employees doing similar work in the work location where the employees regularly work in accordance with the following:

- **A.** Two (2) weeks prior to the start of each calendar quarter, full-time regular employees desiring to work overtime during that quarter shall place their names on an Overtime Desired List.
- **B.** Lists will be established by craft, section, or tour in accordance with Article 30, Local Implementation.
<!-- p.24 -->
- **C.**
  - **1.**
    - **a.** When during the quarter the need for overtime arises, employees with the necessary skills having listed their names will be selected in order of their seniority on a rotating basis.
    - **b.** Those absent or on leave shall be passed over.
- **D.** If the voluntary Overtime Desired List does not provide sufficient qualified people, qualified full-time regular employees not on the list may be required to work overtime on a rotating basis with the first opportunity assigned to the junior employee.
- **E.** Exceptions to C and D above if requested by the employee, may be approved by Local management in exceptional cases based on equity (e.g., anniversaries, birthdays, illness, deaths).
- **F.** Excluding December, no full-time regular employee will be required to work overtime on more than four (4) of the employee’s five (5) scheduled days in a service week or work over ten (10) hours on a regularly scheduled day, over eight (8) hours on a nonscheduled day, or over six (6) days in a service week.
- **G.** Full-time employees not on the Overtime Desired List may be required to work overtime only if all available employees on the Overtime Desired List have worked up to twelve (12) hours in a day or sixty (60) hours in a service week. Employees on the Overtime Desired List:
  - **1.** may be required to work up to twelve (12) hours in a day and sixty (60) hours in a service week (subject to payment of penalty overtime pay set forth in Section 4.D for contravention of Section 5.F); and
  - **2.** excluding December, shall be limited to no more than twelve (12) hours of work in a day and no more than sixty (60) hours of work in a service week.
However, the Employer is not required to utilize employees on the Overtime Desired List at the penalty overtime rate if qualified employees on the Overtime Desired List who are not yet entitled to penalty overtime are available for the overtime assignment.
(See Memos, pages 289, 295, 301, and 303)

## Summary

Overtime among full-time regulars is assigned through a quarterly Overtime Desired List (OTDL), with selection by seniority on a rotating basis among qualified employees doing similar work. Employees absent or on leave are passed over; if the list is exhausted, non-list employees may be compelled by junior-first rotation. Outside December, no full-time regular may be required to work overtime on more than four of five scheduled days, over ten hours on a scheduled day, over eight on a nonscheduled day, or over six days in a service week. Non-list employees can be compelled only after all available OTDL employees have worked up to 12 hours in a day or 60 in a week; OTDL employees themselves are capped at 12/60 excluding December. The Employer need not use an OTDL employee at the penalty rate if another qualified OTDL employee not yet entitled to penalty pay is available.

## Cross-union comparison

The APWU OTDL structure (quarterly sign-up, seniority rotation, junior-first mandatory assignment, 12/60 and 4-of-5-day protections) is materially parallel to NALC's §8.5, though NALC adds the Work Assignment list as an alternative to the OTDL and its §8.5.A permits part-time regulars and PTFs on the list — APWU's list is full-time only per its own language and JCIM Q&A 9. NPMHU mirrors the same assignment order with MHA-specific details; NRLCA has no OTDL at all under evaluated pay.
