# APWU §8.9 — Wash-Up Time

## Full text

<!-- p.26 -->
### Section 9. Wash-Up Time

Installation heads shall grant reasonable wash-up time to those employees who perform dirty work or work with toxic materials. The amount of wash-up time granted each employee shall be subject to the grievance procedure.
(The preceding paragraph, Article 8.9, shall apply to PSEs)

## Summary

Installation heads must grant reasonable wash-up time to employees doing dirty work or working with toxic materials; disputes over the amount granted go through the grievance procedure. The provision applies to PSEs as well.

## Cross-union comparison

Wash-up time for dirty or toxic work is a short obligation shared across the four agreements; APWU makes the grant expressly grievable and extends it to PSEs, matching NPMHU's treatment of its non-career workforce.
