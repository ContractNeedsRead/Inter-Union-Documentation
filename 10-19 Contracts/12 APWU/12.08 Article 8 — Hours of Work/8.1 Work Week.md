# APWU §8.1 — Work Week

## Full text

<!-- p.20 -->
### Section 1. Work Week

The work week for full-time regulars shall be forty (40) hours per week, eight (8) hours per day within ten (10) consecutive hours, provided, however, that in all offices with more than 100
<!-- p.21 -->
full-time employees in the bargaining units the normal work week for full-time regular employees will be forty (40) hours per week, eight (8) hours per day within nine (9) consecutive hours. Shorter work weeks will, however, exist as needed for part-time regulars.
(See Memos, pages 289-301, and 303)

## Summary

The normal work week for full-time regulars is forty hours per week, eight hours per day within ten consecutive hours. In offices with more than 100 full-time bargaining-unit employees, the eight hours must fall within nine consecutive hours — a tighter daily spread for larger offices. Shorter work weeks exist as needed for part-time regulars.

## Cross-union comparison

APWU's §8.1 is a one-paragraph work-week definition with the 100-employee office dividing line between a ten-hour and a nine-hour daily span. NALC's §8.1 adds the further detail that carriers should be assigned eight hours of work on each of five scheduled days. The part-time regular carve-out parallels NALC and NPMHU treatment of shorter work weeks.
