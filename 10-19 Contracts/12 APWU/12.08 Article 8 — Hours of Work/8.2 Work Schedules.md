# APWU §8.2 — Work Schedules

## Full text

<!-- p.21 -->
### Section 2. Work Schedules

- **A.** The employee’s service week shall be a calendar week beginning at 12:01 a.m. Saturday and ending at 12 midnight the following Friday.
- **B.** The employee’s service day is the calendar day on which the majority of work is scheduled. Where the work schedule is distributed evenly over two (2) calendar days, the service day is the calendar day on which such work schedule begins.
- **C.** The employee’s normal work week is five (5) service days, each consisting of eight (8) hours, within ten (10) consecutive hours, except as provided in Section 1 of this Article. As far as practicable the five (5) days shall be consecutive days within the service week.
- **D.** In postal installations which have 200 or more workyears of employment in the regular workforce, career employees in mail processing operations, transportation and vehicle maintenance facility operations, will have consecutive scheduled days off, unless otherwise agreed to by the parties at the Local Level.
  (See Memo, page 289)

## Summary

The service week runs Saturday 12:01 a.m. through Friday midnight; the service day is the calendar day carrying the majority of scheduled work, or the starting day when work splits evenly across two days. The normal work week is five eight-hour service days within ten consecutive hours, consecutive where practicable. Career employees in mail processing, transportation, and vehicle maintenance at installations of 200+ workyears get consecutive scheduled days off unless the local parties agree otherwise.

## Cross-union comparison

The Saturday-to-Friday service week and the majority-of-work service-day test track the NALC and NPMHU definitions of the same concepts; the 200-workyear consecutive-days-off rule is an APWU-specific refinement keyed to career employees in the named operations.
