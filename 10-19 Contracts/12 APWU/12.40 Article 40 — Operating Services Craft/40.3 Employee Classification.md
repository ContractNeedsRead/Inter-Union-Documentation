# APWU §40.3 — Employee Classification

## Full text

<!-- p.223 -->
### Section 3. Employee Classification

The Employer shall staff the bargaining units as defined in Article 1 of this Agreement with full-time employees and postal support
<!-- p.224 -->
employees (PSEs) as deemed necessary to carry out the mission of the U.S. Postal Service.
- **A.** Full-time employees shall be hired pursuant to such procedures as the Employer may establish and shall be assigned to work schedules consisting of five (5) eight (8) hour days in a service week.
- **B.** The PSE workforce shall be comprised of noncareer bargaining unit employees. In the Operating Services Craft, the total number of PSEs used will not exceed 10% of the total number of career Operating Services Craft employees.
  (For PSEs, see Postal Support Employees Memoranda, Section 1, page 251)

## Summary

The bargaining units are staffed with full-time employees (five eight-hour days per service week) and PSEs as needed; PSEs are noncareer employees capped at 10% of career Operating Services Craft employees.

## Cross-union comparison

The PSE percentage cap is the Operating-Services-specific application of the non-career staffing framework.
