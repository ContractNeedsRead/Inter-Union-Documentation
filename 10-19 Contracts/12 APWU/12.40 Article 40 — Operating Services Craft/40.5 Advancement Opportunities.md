# APWU §40.5 — Advancement Opportunities

## Full text

<!-- p.226 -->
### Section 5. Advancement Opportunities

- **A. General Principles**
  The Employer agrees to place particular emphasis upon career advancement opportunities. First opportunity for promotions
<!-- p.227 -->
  shall be given to qualified career employees. The Employer will assist employees to improve their own skills through training and self-help programs.
- **B. Craft Promotions**
  When an opportunity for promotion to a position exists in either of the bargaining units as defined in Article 1, an announcement shall be posted on official bulletin boards in each facility soliciting applications from employees. If an employee is on approved leave and has so requested in writing, stating his/her mailing address, a copy of the announcement soliciting applications shall be mailed to the employee. If the employee is absent for training purposes, the announcement shall be mailed to the employee automatically.
- **C. Best Qualified Selection**
  Employees meeting the qualifications for the position shall be given first consideration. The best qualified applicant shall be selected for the position.

## Summary

Career advancement gets particular emphasis with first promotion opportunity for qualified career employees and skill-improvement assistance; promotion announcements post in each facility with mailings to employees on leave or in training; qualified employees get first consideration and the best qualified applicant is selected.

## Cross-union comparison

The advancement provisions mirror the postal-standard promotion policy of Article 33 at unit scale.
