# NPMHU §21.2 — Life Insurance

## Full text

**Section 21.2 Life Insurance**

The Employer shall maintain the current life insurance program in effect during the term of this Agreement.

## Summary

Maintains the current life insurance program during the Agreement's term.

## Cross-union comparison

Life insurance program continuity is uniform across the agreements.
