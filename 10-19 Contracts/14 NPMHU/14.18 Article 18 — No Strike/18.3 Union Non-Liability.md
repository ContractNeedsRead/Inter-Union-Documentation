# NPMHU §18.3 — Union Non-Liability

## Full text

<!-- p.108 -->
**Section 18.3**

It is agreed that the Union or its local Unions (whether called Area Locals or by other names) which comply with the requirements of this Article shall not be liable for the unauthorized action of their members or other postal employees.

## Summary

Protects the Union and its locals that comply with this Article from liability for unauthorized member or postal employee strike actions.

## Cross-union comparison

The compliance-based non-liability shield is standard in the agreements' no-strike articles.
