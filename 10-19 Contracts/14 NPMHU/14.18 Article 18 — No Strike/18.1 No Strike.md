# NPMHU §18.1 — No Strike

## Full text

<!-- p.108 -->
**Section 18.1**

The Union in behalf of its members agrees that it will not call or sanction a strike or slowdown.

## Summary

Commits the Union, on behalf of its members, not to call or sanction a strike or slowdown.

## Cross-union comparison

A no-strike pledge appears in every postal agreement; NPMHU's is a single sentence.
