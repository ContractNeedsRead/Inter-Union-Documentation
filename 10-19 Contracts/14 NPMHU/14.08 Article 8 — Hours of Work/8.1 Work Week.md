# NPMHU §8.1 — Work Week

## Full text

<!-- p.17 -->
**Section 8.1 Work Week**

The work week for full-time employees shall be forty (40) hours per week, eight (8) hours per day within ten (10) consecutive hours, provided, however, that in all offices with more than 100 full-time employees in the bargaining units the normal work week for full-time employees will be forty hours per week, eight hours per day within nine (9) consecutive hours. Shorter work weeks will, however, exist as needed for part-time regulars.

## Summary

Defines the work week for full-time employees: forty (40) hours per week, eight (8) hours per day within ten (10) consecutive hours. In offices with more than 100 full-time bargaining unit employees, the same forty-hour week compresses to an eight-hour day within nine (9) consecutive hours. Part-time regulars may have shorter work weeks.

## Cross-union comparison

The forty-hour / five-day standard with an eight-hour day matches the full-time standard in the other three agreements' Article 8 provisions (per their hubs: NALC and APWU are parallel; NRLCA is divergent because of evaluated pay). NPMHU-specific details: the 100-employee office threshold that narrows the daily window from ten (10) to nine (9) consecutive hours, and the express allowance for shorter weeks for part-time regulars.
