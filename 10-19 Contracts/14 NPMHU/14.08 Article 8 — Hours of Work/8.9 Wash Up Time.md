# NPMHU §8.9 — Wash Up Time

## Full text

<!-- p.22 -->
**Section 8.9 Wash Up Time**

Installation heads shall grant reasonable wash up time to those employees who perform dirty work or work with toxic materials. The amount of wash up time granted each employee shall be subject to the grievance procedure.

(The preceding Sections, Articles 8.2, 8.3, 8.4, 8.5, 8.7, 8.8, and 8.9, shall apply to Mail Handler Assistant employees to the extent provided in the MOU Re: Mail Handler Assistant Employees or in this Article.)

## Summary

Requires installation heads to grant reasonable wash up time to employees performing dirty work or working with toxic materials; the amount granted is grievable. A trailing clause makes the listed Article 8 sections applicable to MHAs to the extent provided in the MHA MOU or in the Article.

## Cross-union comparison

Wash-up time for dirty or toxic work appears across the agreements in similar form; the NPMHU version leaves the amount to installation heads, subject to the grievance procedure.
