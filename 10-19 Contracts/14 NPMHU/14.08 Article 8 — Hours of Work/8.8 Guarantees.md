# NPMHU §8.8 — Guarantees

## Full text

<!-- p.22 -->
**Section 8.8 Guarantees**

An employee called in outside the employee's regular work schedule shall be guaranteed a minimum of four (4) consecutive hours of work or pay in lieu thereof where less than four (4) hours of work is available. Such guaranteed minimum shall not apply to an employee called in who continues working on into the employee's regularly scheduled shift.

When a full-time employee is called in on the employee's non scheduled day, the employee will be guaranteed eight hours work or pay in lieu thereof. This guarantee will be waived if the employee, with the concurrence of the Union and approval of Management, requests to be released early. The Employer will guarantee all employees at least four (4) hours work or pay on any day they are requested or scheduled to work in a post office or facility with 200 or more man years of employment per year. All employees at other post offices and facilities will be guaranteed two (2) hours work or pay when requested or scheduled to work.

Any MHA who is scheduled to work and who reports to work in an installation with 200 or more man years of employment shall be guaranteed four (4) hours of work or pay. MHAs at smaller installations will be guaranteed two (2) hours work or pay.

## Summary

Guarantees a minimum of four consecutive hours of work or pay to an employee called in outside the regular schedule (unless the call-in continues into the regular shift). A full-time employee called in on a nonscheduled day is guaranteed eight hours, waivable on early release with union concurrence and management approval. On any day employees are requested or scheduled to work, the guarantee is four hours in facilities with 200 or more man years and two hours in smaller offices; MHAs get the same four-hour/two-hour guarantee based on installation size.

## Cross-union comparison

The call-in guarantee is a standard feature across the agreements; NPMHU's distinctive structure is the 200-man-year threshold that raises the floor from two to four hours, plus the explicit eight-hour nonscheduled-day guarantee and the MHA guarantee tied to installation size.
