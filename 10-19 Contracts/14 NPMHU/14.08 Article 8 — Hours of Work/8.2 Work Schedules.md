# NPMHU §8.2 — Work Schedules

## Full text

<!-- p.17 -->
**Section 8.2 Work Schedules**

- A. The employee's service week shall be a calendar week beginning at 12:01 a.m. Saturday and ending at 12 midnight the following Friday.
- B. The employee's service day is the calendar day on which the majority of work is scheduled. Where the work schedule is distributed evenly over two calendar days, the service day is the calendar day on which such work schedule begins.
- C. The employee's normal work week is five (5) service days, each consisting of eight (8) hours, within ten (10) consecutive hours, except as provided in Section 8.1 of this Article. As far as practicable the five days shall be consecutive days within the service week.
- D. In postal installations which have 200 or more work years of employment in the regular work force, career employees in mail processing shall have consecutive scheduled days off, unless otherwise agreed to by the parties at the local level.

## Summary

Defines the service week (calendar week, Saturday 12:01 a.m. through Friday midnight), the service day (the calendar day on which the majority of work is scheduled), and the normal work week (five eight-hour service days within ten consecutive hours, consecutive as far as practicable). In installations with 200 or more work years, career mail processing employees get consecutive scheduled days off unless the parties agree otherwise locally.

## Cross-union comparison

The Saturday-to-Friday service week is the standard postal service week used across the agreements; NPMHU defines it directly in Article 8. The 200-work-year consecutive-days-off rule is a NPMHU-specific mail-processing floor; NALC/APWU reach the same result through local scheduling provisions.
