# NPMHU §13.6 — Seniority of an Employee Assigned to Another Craft

## Full text

<!-- p.76 -->
**Section 13.6 Seniority of an Employee Assigned to Another Craft**

- A. Except as provided for in Section 4I, above, a full-time regular employee assigned to another craft or occupational group in the same or lower level in the same installation shall take the seniority for preferred tours and assignments, whichever is the lesser of (a) one day junior to the junior full-time regular employee in the craft or occupational group, (b) retain the seniority the employee had in the employee's former craft.
- B. A part-time flexible employee who is permanently assigned to a full-time regular or part-time flexible assignment in another craft, under the provisions of this Article, shall begin a new period of seniority. If assigned as a part-time flexible, it shall be at the foot of the part-time flexible roll.

## Summary

For full-time regulars assigned to another craft at the same or lower level, seniority for preferred tours and assignments is the lesser of one day junior to the junior full-time regular in the gaining craft or the seniority held in the former craft (except under §13.4I). PTFs permanently assigned across crafts begin a new seniority period, at the foot of the roll if assigned as a PTF.

## Cross-union comparison

Cross-craft seniority on light duty reassignment follows the shared "lesser of" pattern; NPMHU's one-day-junior formulation and the PTF foot-of-roll rule are its specifics.
