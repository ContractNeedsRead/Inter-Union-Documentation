# NPMHU §33.1 — General Principles

## Full text

<!-- p.125 -->
**Section 33.1 General Principles**

The Employer agrees to place particular emphasis upon career advancement opportunities. First opportunity for promotions will be given to qualified career employees. The Employer will assist employees to improve their own skills through training and self-help programs, and will continue to expand the Postal Employee Development Center concept.

## Summary

Commits the Employer to emphasize career advancement, give qualified career employees first opportunity for promotions, assist skill improvement through training and self-help programs, and expand the Postal Employee Development Center concept.

## Cross-union comparison

Career-advancement emphasis is shared across the agreements' promotion articles.
