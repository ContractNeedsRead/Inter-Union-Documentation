# NPMHU §17.1 — Stewards

## Full text

<!-- p.102 -->
**Section 17.1 Stewards**

Stewards may be designated for the purpose of investigating, presenting and adjusting grievances.

## Summary

Authorizes steward designation for investigating, presenting, and adjusting grievances.

## Cross-union comparison

The steward's grievance function is the shared baseline of the representation articles.
