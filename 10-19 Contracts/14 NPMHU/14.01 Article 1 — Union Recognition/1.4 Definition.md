# NPMHU §1.4 — Definition

## Full text

<!-- p.2 -->
**Section 1.4 Definition**

Subject to the foregoing sections, this Agreement shall be applicable to all employees in the regular work force of the U.S. Postal Service, as defined in Article 7, at all present and subsequently acquired installations, facilities, and operations of the Employer, wherever located.

## Summary

Applies the Agreement, subject to §§1.1–1.3, to all employees in the regular work force as defined in Article 7, at every present and future installation, facility, and operation of the Employer.

## Cross-union comparison

Scope-by-Article-7 is the standard craft-agreement approach: coverage is defined by the employee-classification article rather than repeated here.
