# NPMHU §1.1 — Recognition

## Full text

<!-- p.1 -->
**Section 1.1 Recognition**

The Employer recognizes the Union designated below as the exclusive bargaining representative of all employees in the bargaining unit for which the Union has been recognized and certified at the national level:

National Postal Mail Handlers Union, A Division of the Laborers’ International Union of North America, AFL-CIO.

## Summary

Recognizes the National Postal Mail Handlers Union, A Division of the Laborers’ International Union of North America, AFL-CIO, as the exclusive bargaining representative of all employees in the nationally recognized and certified bargaining unit.

## Cross-union comparison

The four agreements each carry a recognition clause naming their union as the exclusive representative at the national level; this note is the NPMHU instance.
