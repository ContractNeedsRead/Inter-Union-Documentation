# NPMHU §4.1 — Advance Notice

## Full text

<!-- p.5 -->
**Section 4.1 Advance Notice**

The Union at the national level will be informed as far in advance as practicable, but no less than 30 days in advance, of implementation of technological or mechanization changes which affect jobs including new or changed jobs in the area of wages, hours or working conditions. When major new mechanization or equipment is to be purchased and installed, the Union at the national level will be informed as far in advance as practicable, but no less than 90 days in advance.

## Summary

Requires national-level advance notice to the Union of technological or mechanization changes affecting jobs: at least 30 days for changes affecting wages, hours, or working conditions, and at least 90 days when major new mechanization or equipment is purchased and installed.

## Cross-union comparison

Notice-before-automation obligations exist across the agreements; NPMHU's two-tier 30/90-day rule (implementation vs. major equipment purchase) is the craft-specific mechanics.
