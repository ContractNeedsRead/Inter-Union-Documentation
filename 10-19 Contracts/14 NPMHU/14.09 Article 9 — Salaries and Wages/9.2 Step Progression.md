B. Table Two – Career Appointments On or After the Effective Date of the Award, February 15, 2013

Effective no later than the first full pay period 180 days after ratification, the step waiting periods in the RSC M7 wage scale will be reduced from 52 weeks to 48 weeks between all steps.

As of the implementation date, any employee who has at least 48 weeks in time in step credit will advance to the next step and will have their time in step credit reset. All other employees remain in the same step, retaining time in step credit.

## Summary

Sets step waiting periods by schedule. Table One (career appointments before February 15, 2013): Grades 4 and 5 run AA→P with 88-week waits through Step C, 44-week waits through J, then 34, 26, and 24-week waits; Grade 6 runs A→P with 96-week waits through C and the same pattern thereafter. Table Two (appointments on or after February 15, 2013): waiting periods drop from 52 to 48 weeks between all steps no later than the first full pay period 180 days after ratification, with employees holding at least 48 weeks of step credit advancing at implementation and others retaining their credit.

## Cross-union comparison

Step waiting periods are contract-specific in every agreement; NPMHU's dual-table structure (pre/post-February 2013) and the 52-to-48-week reduction are its mechanics, distinct from NALC/APWU/NRLCA step schedules.
