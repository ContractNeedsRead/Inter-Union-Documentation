# NPMHU §9.7 — Mail Handler Assistant Employees

## Full text

<!-- p.29 -->
**Section 9.7 Mail Handler Assistant Employees**

In addition to the general increases provided in Section 9.1 above, MHAs will receive an increase of 1.0% annually, for a total of 2.3% effective November 19, 2022, 2.3% effective November 18, 2023, and 2.3% effective November 16, 2024.

All percentage increases are applied to the wage rates in effect on September 20, 2022.

In addition, the current MHA rate for Grade 4 and Grade 5 will be increased by $0.50 effective (PP25-2022) November 19, 2022. Also, effective no later than the first full pay period 180 days after ratification, a new step, Step B, will be added to the MHA wage scale for Grade 4 and Grade 5. Step B will be $0.50 higher than the Step A rate for RSC M4 for the respective wage in Grade 4 and Grade 5. The step waiting period to reach Step B will be 6 months (26 weeks). RSC M4 Step B will remain $0.50 higher than Step A in perpetuity; there will be no separate calculation for increases applied to this wage rate. Upon initial implementation, any MHA in RSC M4 with 6 months (26 weeks) or more of relative standing will move to Step B.

[See Memo, page 144]

## Summary

MHAs receive the §9.1 general increases plus an additional 1.0% annually (2.3% total) effective November 19, 2022, November 18, 2023, and November 16, 2024, all applied to September 20, 2022 wage rates. Grade 4 and Grade 5 MHA rates rise $0.50 effective PP25-2022 (November 19, 2022); a new Step B ($0.50 above Step A, reached after 6 months/26 weeks, permanently $0.50 above Step A) is added no later than the first full pay period 180 days after ratification, with eligible MHAs (6+ months of relative standing) moving to Step B at implementation.

## Cross-union comparison

Non-career wage rules are the counterpart to APWU's PSE and NALC's CCA wage provisions; NPMHU's MHA-specific 1.0% annual add-on and the RSC M4 Step B structure are craft-specific.
