# NPMHU §9.4 — Application of Salary Rates

## Full text

<!-- p.28 -->
**Section 9.4 Application of Salary Rates**

Except as provided in this Article, the Employer shall continue the current application of salary rates for the duration of this Agreement.

## Summary

Continues the current application of salary rates for the Agreement's duration, except as otherwise provided in Article 9.

## Cross-union comparison

A continuity clause for salary-rate application appears in the other agreements' wage articles as well; the NPMHU version is a single sentence.
