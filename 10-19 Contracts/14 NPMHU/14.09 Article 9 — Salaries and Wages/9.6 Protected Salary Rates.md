# NPMHU §9.6 — Protected Salary Rates

## Full text

<!-- p.28 -->
**Section 9.6 Protected Salary Rates**

- A. The Employer shall continue the current salary rate protection program for the duration of this Agreement.

<!-- p.29 -->
- B. Employees who qualify for "saved grade" will receive "saved grade" for an indefinite period of time subject to the conditions contained in Article 4.4.

[See Memo, page 160]

## Summary

Continues the current salary rate protection program, and gives employees who qualify for "saved grade" that protection for an indefinite period, subject to Article 4.4's conditions.

## Cross-union comparison

Saved-grade and salary protection programs exist across the agreements; NPMHU's runs indefinitely subject to the Article 4.4 conditions.
