# NPMHU §7.2 — Employment and Work Assignments

## Full text

<!-- p.16 -->
**Section 7.2 Employment and Work Assignments**

- A. Normally, work in different crafts, occupational groups or levels will not be combined into one job. However, to provide maximum full-time employment and provide necessary flexibility, management may establish full-time schedule assignments by including work within different crafts or occupational groups after the following sequential actions have been taken:
  - A1. All available work within each separate craft by tour has been combined.
  - A2. Work of different crafts in the same wage level by tour has been combined.
- B. The appropriate representatives of the affected Unions will be informed in advance of the reasons for establishing the combination full-time assignments within different crafts in accordance with this Article.
- C. In the event of insufficient work on any particular day or days in a full-time or part-time employee's own scheduled assignment, management may assign the employee to any available work in the same wage level for which the employee is qualified, consistent with the employee's knowledge and experience, in order to maintain the number of work hours of the employee's basic work schedule.
- D. During exceptionally heavy workload periods for one occupational group, employees in an occupational group experiencing a light workload period may be assigned to work in the same wage level, commensurate with their capabilities, to the heavy workload area for such time as management determines necessary.

[See Memo, page 155]

## Summary

Work in different crafts, occupational groups, or levels is not normally combined into one job, but management may establish combination full-time assignments after first combining all available work within each craft by tour and then combining work of different crafts in the same wage level by tour, with advance notice to the affected unions. Management may also assign employees to available same-wage-level work to maintain scheduled hours when their own work is insufficient, and may shift employees from light to heavy workload areas within the same wage level as necessary.

## Cross-union comparison

Combination-assignment and cross-craft work rules appear in each agreement's classification or assignment articles; NPMHU's sequential two-step combination procedure and the union-notice duty are its specifics.
