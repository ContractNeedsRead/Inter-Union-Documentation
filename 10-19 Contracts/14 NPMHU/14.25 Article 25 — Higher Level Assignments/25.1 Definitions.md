# NPMHU §25.1 — Definitions

## Full text

<!-- p.114 -->
**Section 25.1 Definitions**

Higher level work is defined as an assignment to a ranked higher level position, whether or not such position has been authorized at the installation.

## Summary

Defines higher level work as assignment to a ranked higher level position, whether or not authorized at the installation.

## Cross-union comparison

The higher-level definition is shared across the agreements; the unauthorized-position inclusion is common.
