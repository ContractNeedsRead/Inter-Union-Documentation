# NPMHU §25.2 — Higher Level Pay

## Full text

<!-- p.114 -->
**Section 25.2 Higher Level Pay**

An employee who is detailed to higher level work shall be paid at the higher level for time actually spent on such job. An employee's higher level rate shall be determined as if promoted to the position. An employee temporarily assigned or detailed to a lower level position shall be paid at the employee's own rate.

[See Memo, page 212]

## Summary

Pays employees detailed to higher level work at the higher level rate (as if promoted) for time actually spent on the job; temporary lower level assignments remain at the employee's own rate.

## Cross-union comparison

Higher level pay for actual time worked is the shared rule across the agreements.
