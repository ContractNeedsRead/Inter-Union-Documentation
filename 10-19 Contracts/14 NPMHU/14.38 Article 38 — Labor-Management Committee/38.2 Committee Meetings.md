# NPMHU §38.2 — Committee Meetings

## Full text

<!-- p.132 -->
**Section 38.2 Committee Meetings**

- A. At the national and regional/area levels, the Labor-Management Committees shall meet quarterly, unless additional meetings are scheduled by mutual agreement. Agenda items shall be exchanged at least 15 working days in advance of the scheduled meeting. National level agenda items include those of national concern such as human rights, technological and mechanization changes, subcontracting, jurisdiction, uniforms and work clothes, parking and other labor-management subjects. Regional/Area level agenda items include those of regional/area concern such as human rights and other labor-management subjects.
- B. Union attendance at national level meetings shall be limited to no more than six (6) persons, not including secretarial staff. Union attendance at regional/area level meetings shall be limited to no more than three (3) persons, not including secretarial staff. If the Union requires technical assistance, such technical assistance shall be in addition to the numbers listed above.
- C. Meetings at the national and regional/area (except as to the Christmas operation) levels will not be compensated by the Employer. The Employer will compensate one designated representative from the Union for actual time spent in the meeting at the applicable straight time rate, providing the time spent in such meetings is a part of the employee's regular scheduled work day.
- D. Subject to the provisions of this Agreement, Labor-Management Committee meetings will be separate from other unions.

<!-- p.133 -->
- E. Provided agenda items are submitted, Labor-Management Committee meetings shall be scheduled in all offices in accordance with the following criteria:
  - E1. In offices with a total complement of 300 bargaining unit employees or more, meetings will be held once a month. Complement is defined in this Section as total number of employees currently on the rolls in the installation;
  - E2. In offices with a complement of 100 to 299 bargaining unit employees, meetings will be held bi-monthly; and
  - E3. In offices of less than 100 employees, meetings will be held quarterly.
- F. Agenda items will be exchanged at least 72 hours prior to such meetings. Meetings shall be held at a time and date convenient to both parties. Where agenda items do not warrant a regularly scheduled meeting, discussions may take place by mutual agreement in lieu thereof.

## Summary

Sets the meeting mechanics: quarterly national and regional/area meetings with 15-working-day agenda exchange (national items include human rights, automation, subcontracting, jurisdiction, uniforms, parking); Union attendance caps of six (national) and three (regional/area) persons plus secretarial staff and technical assistance; no Employer compensation for national/regional/area meetings except one Union representative at straight time within the regular workday (Christmas operation excepted); meetings separate from other unions; local meeting frequency by complement (monthly at 300+, bi-monthly at 100–299, quarterly below 100) where agenda items are submitted; and 72-hour agenda exchange with mutually convenient times or agreed discussions in lieu.

## Cross-union comparison

The committee structure with attendance caps and frequency-by-complement is shared across the agreements; NPMHU's compensation exception and frequency thresholds are its specifics.
