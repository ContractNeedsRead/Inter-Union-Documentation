# NPMHU §38.1 — Statement of Principle

## Full text

<!-- p.132 -->
**Section 38.1 Statement of Principle**

The Union through its designated agents shall be entitled at the national, regional/area, and local levels, and at such other intermediate levels as may be appropriate, to participate in regularly scheduled Labor-Management Committee meetings for the purpose of discussing, exploring, and considering with management matters of mutual concern; provided neither party shall attempt to change, add to or vary the terms of this Collective Bargaining Agreement.

## Summary

Entitles the Union, through designated agents, to participate in regularly scheduled Labor-Management Committee meetings at the national, regional/area, local, and appropriate intermediate levels to discuss matters of mutual concern, with neither party attempting to change, add to, or vary the Agreement.

## Cross-union comparison

The labor-management committee framework is shared across the agreements; the no-contract-change proviso is common.
