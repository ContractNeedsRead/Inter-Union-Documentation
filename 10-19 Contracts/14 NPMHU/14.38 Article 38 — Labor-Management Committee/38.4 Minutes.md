# NPMHU §38.4 — Minutes

## Full text

<!-- p.133 -->
**Section 38.4 Minutes**

Minutes of local Labor-Management Committee meetings may be taken by each party.

## Summary

Permits each party to take minutes of local Labor-Management Committee meetings.

## Cross-union comparison

Minutes-taking rights are common across the agreements.
