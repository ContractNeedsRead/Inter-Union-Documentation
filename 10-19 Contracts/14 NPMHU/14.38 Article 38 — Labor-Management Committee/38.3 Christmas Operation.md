# NPMHU §38.3 — Christmas Operation

## Full text

<!-- p.133 -->
**Section 38.3 Christmas Operation**

The policies to be established by management for the Christmas operation will be a subject of discussion at a timely regularly scheduled Labor-Management Committee meeting.

## Summary

Makes the management policies for the Christmas operation a discussion subject at a timely regularly scheduled Labor-Management Committee meeting.

## Cross-union comparison

Christmas operation discussion provisions appear across the agreements.
