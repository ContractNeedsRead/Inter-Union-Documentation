# NPMHU §20.3 — Energy Usage

## Full text

<!-- p.110 -->
**Section 20.3 Energy Usage**

In order to reduce energy usage the Employer and the Union will promote the use of carpooling and public transportation, where available.

## Summary

Commits both parties to promote carpooling and public transportation, where available, to reduce energy usage.

## Cross-union comparison

Carpool/transit promotion is a common feature of the parking articles.
