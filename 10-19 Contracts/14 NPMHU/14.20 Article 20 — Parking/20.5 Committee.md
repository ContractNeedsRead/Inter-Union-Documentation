# NPMHU §20.5 — Committee

## Full text

<!-- p.110 -->
**Section 20.5 Committee**

The parking program is a proper subject for discussion at Labor-Management Committee meetings at the national level provided in Article 38.

(The preceding Article, Article 20, shall apply to Mail Handler Assistant employees.)

## Summary

Makes the parking program a proper subject for the national Labor-Management Committee meetings under Article 38, with a trailing clause extending Article 20 to MHAs.

## Cross-union comparison

Parking as a labor-management committee subject mirrors the other agreements.
