# NPMHU §12.7 — Transfer Request

## Full text

<!-- p.70 -->
**Section 12.7 Transfer Request**

- A. Prior to hiring Mail Handlers, installation heads will consider requests for transfers submitted by Mail Handlers from other installations.
- B. Providing a written request for a voluntary transfer has been submitted, a written acknowledgment shall be given in a timely manner.
- C. An employee whose transfer is approved will be allowed to use up to five (5) days of annual leave or five (5) days leave without pay for purpose of transferring.

[See Memos, pages 179, 182, 184]

## Summary

Requires installation heads to consider Mail Handler transfer requests before hiring, timely written acknowledgment of submitted transfer requests, and up to five days of annual leave or five days of LWOP for an approved transfer.

## Cross-union comparison

Transfer-request priority over outside hiring and transfer leave appear in the other agreements' transfer provisions as well; NPMHU's five-day leave allowance is its specific.
