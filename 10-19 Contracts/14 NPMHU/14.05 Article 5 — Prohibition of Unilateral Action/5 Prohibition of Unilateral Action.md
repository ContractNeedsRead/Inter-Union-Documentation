# NPMHU Art. 5 — Prohibition of Unilateral Action

## Full text

<!-- p.7 -->
**ARTICLE 5 PROHIBITION OF UNILATERAL ACTION**

The Employer will not take any actions affecting wages, hours and other terms and conditions of employment as defined in Section 8(d) of the National Labor Relations Act which violate the terms of this Agreement or are otherwise inconsistent with its obligations under law.

(The preceding Article, Article 5, shall apply to Mail Handler Assistant employees.)

## Summary

Binds the Employer not to take unilateral actions affecting wages, hours, and other terms and conditions of employment as defined in NLRA §8(d) that violate the Agreement or are otherwise inconsistent with its legal obligations. Extends to MHAs by the trailing clause.

## Cross-union comparison

Each agreement pairs its management-rights clause with a unilateral-action prohibition; the NPMHU version keys the protected subjects to NLRA §8(d) by name.
