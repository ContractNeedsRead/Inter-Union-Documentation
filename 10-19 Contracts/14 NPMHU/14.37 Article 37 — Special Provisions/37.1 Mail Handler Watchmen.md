# NPMHU §37.1 — Mail Handler Watchmen

## Full text

<!-- p.130 -->
**Section 37.1 Mail Handler Watchmen**

Former mail handler watchmen, whose positions have been abolished, shall continue to be treated in accordance with the seniority, posting and reassignment provisions of this Agreement.

## Summary

Preserves seniority, posting, and reassignment treatment for former mail handler watchmen whose positions were abolished.

## Cross-union comparison

Abolished-position protections are NPMHU-specific legacy provisions.
