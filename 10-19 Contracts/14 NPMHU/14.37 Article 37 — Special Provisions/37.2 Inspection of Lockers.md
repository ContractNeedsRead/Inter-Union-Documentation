# NPMHU §37.2 — Inspection of Lockers

## Full text

<!-- p.131 -->
**Section 37.2 Inspection of Lockers**

The Employer agrees that, except in matters where there is reasonable cause to suspect criminal activity, a steward or the employee shall be given the opportunity to be present at any inspection of employees' lockers. For a general inspection where employees have had prior notification of at least a week, the above is not applicable.

## Summary

Entitles a steward or the employee to be present at locker inspections, except where there is reasonable cause to suspect criminal activity; the presence right does not apply to general inspections announced at least a week in advance.

## Cross-union comparison

Locker inspection witness rights are shared across the agreements.
