# NPMHU §37.7 — Saved Grade Retention

## Full text

<!-- p.131 -->
**Section 37.7 Saved Grade Retention**

An employee shall not lose Saved Grade by bidding on preferred duty assignments in the position and level assigned.

## Summary

Protects an employee's Saved Grade when bidding on preferred duty assignments in the assigned position and level.

Saved grade protections on bidding appear across the agreements.
