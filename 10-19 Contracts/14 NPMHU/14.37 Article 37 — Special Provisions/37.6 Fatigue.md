# NPMHU §37.6 — Fatigue

## Full text

<!-- p.131 -->
**Section 37.6 Fatigue**

The subject of fatigue, as it relates to the safety and health of mail handler employees, is a proper subject for discussion in local Joint Labor-Management Safety and Health Committee meetings.

## Summary

Makes fatigue, as it relates to mail handler safety and health, a proper subject for local Joint Labor-Management Safety and Health Committee meetings.

## Cross-union comparison

Fatigue discussion provisions appear in the other agreements' safety articles; NPMHU routes it through the local safety committee.
