# NPMHU §37.3 — Local Distribution of Personnel Action Roster Notices

## Full text

<!-- p.131 -->
**Section 37.3 Local Distribution of Personnel Action Roster Notices**

Copies of information bulletins, which contain notification of personnel changes and are currently posted on post office bulletin boards, will be given to the Mail Handlers Union on a regular basis.

## Summary

Requires regular distribution to the Union of information bulletins containing personnel change notifications that are posted on post office bulletin boards.

## Cross-union comparison

Personnel-notice distribution to the Union appears across the agreements.
