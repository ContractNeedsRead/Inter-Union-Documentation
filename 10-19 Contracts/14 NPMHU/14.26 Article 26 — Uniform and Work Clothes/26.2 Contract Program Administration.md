# NPMHU §26.2 — Contract Program Administration

## Full text

<!-- p.116 -->
**Section 26.2 Contract Program Administration**

Employees who are currently furnished uniforms pursuant to the contract program shall continue to be so entitled. Such uniforms shall be issued in a timely manner. The allowance to Mail Handlers under this program shall be as follows:

$ 202 effective May 21, 2023

$ 207 effective May 21, 2024

$ 212 effective May 21, 2025

Each increase shall become effective on the employee's anniversary date following the effective date of change.

## Summary

Continues the contract uniform program for entitled employees with timely issuance, and sets the Mail Handler allowance at $202 (May 21, 2023), $207 (May 21, 2024), and $212 (May 21, 2025), each effective on the employee's anniversary date following the change.

## Cross-union comparison

Uniform allowances are craft-specific across the agreements; NPMHU's three-year schedule is its own.
