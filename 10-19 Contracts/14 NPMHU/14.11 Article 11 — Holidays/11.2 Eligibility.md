# NPMHU §11.2 — Eligibility

## Full text

<!-- p.38 -->
**Section 11.2 Eligibility**

To be eligible for holiday pay, an employee must be in a pay status the last hour of the employee's scheduled workday prior to or the first hour of the employee's scheduled workday after the holiday.

## Summary

Conditions holiday pay eligibility on being in pay status for the last hour of the scheduled workday before the holiday or the first hour of the scheduled workday after it.

## Cross-union comparison

The before-or-after pay-status eligibility test is the standard holiday eligibility rule across the agreements.
