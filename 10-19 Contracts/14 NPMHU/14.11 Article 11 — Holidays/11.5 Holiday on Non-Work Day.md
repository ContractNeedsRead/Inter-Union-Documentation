# NPMHU §11.5 — Holiday on Non-Work Day

## Full text

<!-- p.40 -->
**Section 11.5 Holiday on Non-Work Day**

- A. When a holiday falls on Sunday, the following Monday will be observed as the holiday. When a holiday falls on Saturday, the preceding Friday shall be observed as the holiday.
- B. When an employee's scheduled non-work day falls on a day observed as a holiday, the employee's scheduled workday preceding the holiday shall be designated as that employee's holiday.

## Summary

Moves Saturday holidays to the preceding Friday and Sunday holidays to the following Monday; when an employee's scheduled non-work day falls on the observed holiday, the scheduled workday preceding the holiday is designated as the employee's holiday.

## Cross-union comparison

Saturday/Sunday holiday displacement is uniform across the agreements; the individual designation of the preceding workday is the shared mechanic.
