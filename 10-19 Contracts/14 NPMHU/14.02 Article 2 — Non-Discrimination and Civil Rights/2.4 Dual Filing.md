# NPMHU §2.4 — Dual Filing

## Full text

<!-- p.4 -->
**Section 2.4 Dual Filing**

The Union, at the national and local levels, will take affirmative steps to ensure that bargaining-unit employees are informed that they should not pursue essentially contractual matters simultaneously under the grievance and EEO processes.

The Union, at the national and local levels, will not encourage dual filing of grievances.

(The preceding Article, Article 2, shall apply to Mail Handler Assistant employees.)

## Summary

Obligates the Union at national and local levels to inform employees not to pursue essentially contractual matters simultaneously under the grievance and EEO processes, and not to encourage dual filing. A trailing clause extends Article 2 to MHAs.

## Cross-union comparison

Dual-filing discouragement appears across the agreements as the contract-side complement to the EEO process; the NPMHU version puts the duty on the Union at both national and local levels.
