# NPMHU §2.2 — Committee

## Full text

<!-- p.4 -->
**Section 2.2 Committee**

Non-Discrimination and Civil Rights are proper subjects for discussion at Labor-Management Committee meetings at the national, regional/area and local levels provided in Article 38.

## Summary

Makes non-discrimination and civil rights proper subjects for discussion at the Labor-Management Committee meetings at the national, regional/area, and local levels established by Article 38.

## Cross-union comparison

The other agreements likewise route discrimination subjects through their labor-management committee structures; the NPMHU provision names the Article 38 machinery directly.
