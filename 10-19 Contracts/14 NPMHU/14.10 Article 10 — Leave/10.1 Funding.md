# NPMHU §10.1 — Funding

## Full text

<!-- p.34 -->
**Section 10.1 Funding**

The Employer shall continue funding the leave program so as to continue the current leave earning level for the duration of this Agreement.

## Summary

Obligates the Employer to keep funding the leave program to maintain the current leave earning level for the life of the Agreement.

## Cross-union comparison

Leave-program funding continuity clauses appear across the agreements; the NPMHU version is a single sentence preserving the earning level.
