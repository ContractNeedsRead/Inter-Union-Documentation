# NPMHU §31.2 — Electronic Communication

## Full text

<!-- p.123 -->
**Section 31.2 Electronic Communication**

The Employer shall, on an accounting period basis, provide the Union at its national headquarters with electronic communication containing information as set forth in the Memorandum of Understanding regarding Article 31.

[See Memo and Letter, pages 214-215]

## Summary

Requires the Employer, each accounting period, to provide the Union's national headquarters electronic communication containing the information set out in the Article 31 Memorandum of Understanding.

## Cross-union comparison

Electronic information transmission duties appear across the agreements; the MOU-defined content is NPMHU's specific.
