# NPMHU §32.2 — Special Provisions

## Full text

<!-- p.124 -->
**Section 32.2 Special Provisions**

- A. The Employer and the Union agree that at processing and distribution facilities or post offices where mail handler craft employ-

<!-- p.125 -->
  ees are assigned and on duty on the platform at the time a star route vehicle is being loaded or unloaded exclusively by a star route contract driver, a mail handler(s) will assist in loading and unloading the star route vehicle, unless such requirement delays the scheduled receipt and dispatch of mail or alters the routing or affects the safety requirements provided in the star route contract.
- B. At offices where this Section is applicable, the schedules of mail handlers will not be changed nor will the number of mail handlers be augmented solely on the basis of this Section.

## Summary

Requires mail handlers on duty on the platform to assist in loading and unloading star route vehicles handled exclusively by contract drivers at processing and distribution facilities or post offices (unless it would delay mail receipt/dispatch, alter routing, or affect contract safety requirements), without changing mail handler schedules or augmenting complements solely on this basis.

## Cross-union comparison

Star route assistance provisions are NPMHU-specific protections against contract-driver displacement.
