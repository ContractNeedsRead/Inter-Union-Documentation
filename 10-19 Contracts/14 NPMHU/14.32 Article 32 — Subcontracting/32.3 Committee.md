# NPMHU §32.3 — Committee

## Full text

<!-- p.125 -->
**Section 32.3 Committee**

Subcontracting is a proper subject for discussion at Labor-Management Committee meetings at the national level provided in Article 38.

(The preceding Article, Article 32, shall apply to Mail Handler Assistant employees.)

[See Memo, page 216]

## Summary

Makes subcontracting a proper subject for the national Labor-Management Committee meetings under Article 38, with a trailing clause extending Article 32 to MHAs.

## Cross-union comparison

Subcontracting as a labor-management committee subject mirrors the other agreements.
