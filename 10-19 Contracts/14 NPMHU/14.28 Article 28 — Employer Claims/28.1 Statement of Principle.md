# NPMHU §28.1 — Statement of Principle

## Full text

<!-- p.118 -->
**Section 28.1 Statement of Principle**

The parties agree that continued public confidence in the Postal Service requires the proper care and handling of the U.S.P.S. property, postal funds, and the mails. In advance of any money demand upon an employee for any reason, the employee must be informed in writing and the demand must include the reasons therefor.

## Summary

States the principle that public confidence requires proper care of USPS property, postal funds, and the mails, and requires that any money demand on an employee be preceded by written notice including the reasons.

## Cross-union comparison

The written-demand precondition is common to the agreements' employer claims articles.
