# NPMHU §28.2 — Loss or Damage of the Mails

## Full text

<!-- p.118 -->
**Section 28.2 Loss or Damage of the Mails**

An employee is responsible for the protection of the mails entrusted to the employee. Such employee shall not be financially liable for any loss, rifling, damage, wrong delivery of or depredation on, the mails or failure to collect or remit C.O.D. funds unless the employee failed to exercise reasonable care.

## Summary

Makes employees responsible for protecting entrusted mail, but financially liable for loss, rifling, damage, wrong delivery, depredation, or C.O.D. failures only on failure to exercise reasonable care.

## Cross-union comparison

The reasonable-care standard for mail loss liability is shared across the agreements.
