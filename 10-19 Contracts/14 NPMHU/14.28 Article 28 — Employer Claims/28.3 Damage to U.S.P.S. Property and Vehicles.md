# NPMHU §28.3 — Damage to U.S.P.S. Property and Vehicles

## Full text

<!-- p.118 -->
**Section 28.3 Damage to U.S.P.S. Property and Vehicles**

An employee shall be financially liable for any loss or damage to property of the Employer including leased property and vehicles only when the loss or damage was the result of the willful or deliberate misconduct of such employee.

## Summary

Limits employee financial liability for loss or damage to Employer property (including leased property and vehicles) to loss or damage caused by the employee's willful or deliberate misconduct.

## Cross-union comparison

The willful/deliberate misconduct standard is shared across the agreements.
