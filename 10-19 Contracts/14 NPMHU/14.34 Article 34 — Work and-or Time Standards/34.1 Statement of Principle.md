# NPMHU §34.1 — Statement of Principle

## Full text

<!-- p.126 -->
**Section 34.1 Statement of Principle**

The principle of a fair day's work for a fair day's pay is recognized by the parties to this Agreement.

## Summary

Recognizes the principle of a fair day's work for a fair day's pay.

## Cross-union comparison

The fair day's work/pay principle is shared across the agreements' work standards articles.
