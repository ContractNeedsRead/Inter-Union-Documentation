# NPMHU §34.3 — Difference Resolution

## Full text

<!-- p.127 -->
**Section 34.3 Difference Resolution**

Within a reasonable time not to exceed 10 days after the receipt of such notice, the Union and the Employer shall meet for the purpose of resolving any differences that may arise concerning such proposed work measurement systems or work or time standards.

## Summary

Requires the parties to meet within ten days of the notice to resolve differences over proposed work measurement systems or work or time standards.

## Cross-union comparison

The 10-day difference-resolution meeting is shared across the agreements.
