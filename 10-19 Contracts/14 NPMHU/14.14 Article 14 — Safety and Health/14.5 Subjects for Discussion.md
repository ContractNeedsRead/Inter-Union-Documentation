# NPMHU §14.5 — Subjects for Discussion

## Full text

<!-- p.79 -->
**Section 14.5 Subjects for Discussion**

Individual grievances shall not be made the subject of discussion during Safety and Health Committee meetings.

## Summary

Excludes individual grievances from Safety and Health Committee meeting discussion.

## Cross-union comparison

The grievance exclusion keeps the committee channel separate from the grievance procedure in all the agreements.
