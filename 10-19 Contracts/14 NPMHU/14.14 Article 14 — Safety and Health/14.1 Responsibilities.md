# NPMHU §14.1 — Responsibilities

## Full text

<!-- p.77 -->
**Section 14.1 Responsibilities**

It is the responsibility of management to provide safe working conditions in all present and future installations and to develop a safe working force. The Union will cooperate with and assist management to live up to this responsibility. The Employer agrees to give appropriate consideration to human factors in the design and development of automated systems.

## Summary

Assigns management responsibility for safe working conditions in all present and future installations and for developing a safe work force, with Union cooperation; the Employer must consider human factors in automated system design.

## Cross-union comparison

The shared safety article framework across the agreements starts from the same management-responsibility premise; the human-factors-in-automation commitment is NPMHU's specific addition.
