# NPMHU §6.8 — Intent

## Full text

<!-- p.13 -->
**Section 6.8 Intent**

The Employer shall not layoff, reduce in force, or take any other action against a non-protected employee solely to prevent the attainment by that employee of protected status.

## Summary

Prohibits the Employer from laying off, reducing in force, or acting against a non-protected employee solely to prevent that employee from attaining protected status.

## Cross-union comparison

An anti-evasion intent clause accompanies the layoff protections in the other agreements as well; NPMHU's is a single-sentence prohibition.
