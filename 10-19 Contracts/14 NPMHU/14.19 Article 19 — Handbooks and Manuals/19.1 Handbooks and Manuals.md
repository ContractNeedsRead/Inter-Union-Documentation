# NPMHU §19.1 — Handbooks and Manuals

## Full text

<!-- p.108 -->
**Section 19.1**

Those parts of all handbooks, manuals and published regulations of the Postal Service, that directly relate to wages, hours or working conditions, as they apply to employees covered by this Agreement, shall contain nothing that conflicts with this Agreement, and shall be continued in effect except that the Employer shall have the right to make changes that are not inconsistent with this Agreement and that are fair, reasonable, and equitable. This includes, but is not limited to, the Postal Service Manual and the F-21 Timekeeper's Instructions.

## Summary

Requires that the parts of Postal Service handbooks, manuals, and published regulations directly relating to wages, hours, or working conditions contain nothing conflicting with the Agreement; they continue in effect subject to Employer changes that are not inconsistent with the Agreement and are fair, reasonable, and equitable — including the Postal Service Manual and the F-21 Timekeeper's Instructions.

## Cross-union comparison

The handbook/manual consistency clause is shared across the agreements; the F-21 reference is common.
