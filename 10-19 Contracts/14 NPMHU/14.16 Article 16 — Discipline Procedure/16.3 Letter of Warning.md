# NPMHU §16.3 — Letter of Warning

## Full text

<!-- p.99 -->
**Section 16.3 Letter of Warning**

A letter of warning is a disciplinary notice in writing, identified as an official disciplinary letter of warning, which shall include an explanation of a deficiency or misconduct to be corrected.

[See Memo, page 207]

## Summary

Defines a letter of warning as a written disciplinary notice, identified as an official disciplinary letter of warning, explaining the deficiency or misconduct to be corrected.

## Cross-union comparison

The letter-of-warning definition matches the other agreements' first step of progressive discipline.
