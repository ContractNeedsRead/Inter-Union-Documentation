# NALC §26.2 — Annual Allowance

## Full text

<!-- p.94 -->
### Section 2. Annual Allowance

The annual allowance for eligible employees in the reimbursable uniform program shall be as follows:

- **A.** Effective May 21, 2025 the annual allowance for all eligible employees shall be increased from present $499 per annum to $536 per annum. The increase shall become effective on the employee’s anniversary date.

Effective May 21, 2026 the annual allowance for all eligible employees shall be increased from $536 per annum to $549 per annum. The increase shall become effective on the employee’s anniversary date.

- **B.** A newly eligible employee entering the reimbursable uniform program will receive an additional credit to the employee’s allowance as follows:

Effective May 21, 2025 - $125 if entitled to $536 per annum.

Effective May 21, 2026 - $128 if entitled to $549 per annum.

An eligible employee cannot receive this additional credit more than once; however, the current procedures regarding
<!-- p.95 -->
employees transferring from one allowance category to another shall be continued.

Unused portions of an eligible employee’s annual allowance for uniform and work clothing will be carried over and available for use beginning twelve (12) months after the end of each anniversary year. An eligible employee’s uniform or work clothing allowance balance may not exceed the sum of two (2) years of the employee’s annual allowance entitlement. This uniform and work clothing program adjustment will be implemented no later than twelve (12) months from the date of the Interest Arbitration Award.

## Summary

The annual uniform allowance rises from $499 to $536 on May 21, 2025 and to $549 on May 21, 2026, each effective on the employee's anniversary date. Newly eligible employees get a one-time additional credit — $125 with the $536 allowance, $128 with the $549 — with category-transfer procedures continuing. Unused allowance carries over twelve months after each anniversary year, balances cap at two years' entitlement, and the adjustment is implemented within twelve months of the Interest Arbitration Award.

## Cross-union comparison

The named dollar allowances ($536/$549) with anniversary-date effectiveness and the one-time new-employee credits ($125/$128) are NALC-specific figures for this term. The other crafts' uniform allowances are set elsewhere or in their own amounts, so these numbers are contract-specific.
