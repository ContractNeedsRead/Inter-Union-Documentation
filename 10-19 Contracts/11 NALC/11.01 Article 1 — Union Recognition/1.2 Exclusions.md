# NALC §1.2 — Exclusions

## Full text

<!-- p.1 -->
### Section 2. Exclusions

The employee group set forth in Section 1 above does not include, and this Agreement does not apply to:

1. Managerial and supervisory personnel;
2. Professional employees;
3. Employees engaged in personnel work in other than a purely non-confidential clerical capacity;
4. Security guards as defined in Public Law 91-375, 1201(2);
5. All Postal Inspection Service employees;
6. Employees in the supplemental work force as defined in Article 7;
7. Rural letter carriers;
8. Mail handlers;
9. Maintenance Employees;
10. Special Delivery Messengers;
<!-- p.2 -->
11. Motor Vehicle Employees; or
12. Postal Clerks.

## Summary

The unit described in §1.1 excludes twelve listed groups: managerial and supervisory personnel, professional employees, confidential personnel workers, security guards under the Postal Reorganization Act definition, Inspection Service employees, the supplemental work force, and the other crafts (rural carriers, mail handlers, maintenance employees, special delivery messengers, motor vehicle employees, and postal clerks). The list fixes the city letter carrier unit's boundary against supervision and against the other recognized crafts.

## Cross-union comparison

Every agreement's recognition article excludes the same neighbors — each craft's unit is defined partly by naming the other crafts as excluded. NALC's list is notable for excluding "the supplemental work force as defined in Article 7" by cross-reference and for the statutory security-guard exclusion tied to Public Law 91-375.
