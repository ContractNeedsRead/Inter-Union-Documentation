# NALC §1.1 — Union

## Full text

<!-- p.1 -->
### Section 1. Union

The Employer recognizes the National Association of Letter Carriers, AFL-CIO as the exclusive bargaining representative of all employees in the bargaining unit for which it has been recognized and certified at the national level — City Letter Carriers.

## Summary

This is the recognition clause: the Postal Service recognizes the NALC as the exclusive bargaining representative of the nationally certified city letter carrier bargaining unit. It is the root grant of the union's representative status that every other article presupposes.

## Cross-union comparison

All four agreements open with a recognition clause naming their union and unit, so the shape is universal. NALC's unit description is the broadest in one line — "City Letter Carriers" — while the exclusions that actually define the unit's edges live in §1.2 and §1.3.
