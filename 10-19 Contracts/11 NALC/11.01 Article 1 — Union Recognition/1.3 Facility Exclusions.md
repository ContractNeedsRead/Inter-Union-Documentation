# NALC §1.3 — Facility Exclusions

## Full text

<!-- p.2 -->
### Section 3. Facility Exclusions

This Agreement does not apply to employees who work in other employer facilities which are not engaged in customer services and mail processing, previously understood and expressed by the parties to mean mail processing and delivery, including but not limited to Headquarters, Area Offices, Information Service Centers, Postal Service Training and Development Institute, Oklahoma Postal Training Operations, Postal Academies, Postal Academy Training Institute, Stamped Envelope Agency, Supply Centers, Mail Equipment Shops, or Mail Transport Equipment Centers.

## Summary

The agreement does not cover employees in facilities outside customer services and mail processing — defined by the parties as mail processing and delivery — with a non-exclusive list including Headquarters, Area Offices, information and training centers, supply operations, and mail equipment facilities. It is a geographic and functional exclusion layered on top of the §1.2 occupational exclusions.

## Cross-union comparison

Facility exclusions are common across the four agreements, but each craft's list differs in scope and detail. NALC's is tied to the parties' shared understanding of "mail processing and delivery" and enumerates specific non-operational installations, whereas the rural agreement's unit boundaries are stated more compactly.
