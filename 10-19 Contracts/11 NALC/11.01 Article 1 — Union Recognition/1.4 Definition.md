# NALC §1.4 — Definition

## Full text

<!-- p.2 -->
### Section 4. Definition

Subject to the foregoing exclusions, this Agreement shall be applicable to all employees in the regular work force of the U.S. Postal Service, as defined in Article 7, at all present and subsequently acquired installations, facilities, and operations of the Employer, wherever located.

## Summary

Subject to §§1.2–1.3, the agreement applies to all regular work force employees of the Postal Service, as Article 7 defines that term, at every present and future installation, facility, and operation wherever located. It is the closing, catch-all definition that completes the recognition article.

## Cross-union comparison

The "all present and subsequently acquired installations" phrase is standard postal contract language across crafts. NALC's version defers the definition of "regular work force" to its own Article 7, the same cross-reference structure the other agreements use to their own employee-classification articles.
