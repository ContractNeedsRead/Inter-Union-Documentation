# NALC §41.5 — National Joint City Delivery Committee

## Full text

<!-- p.124 -->
### Section 5. National Joint City Delivery Committee

There will be established at the national level a Joint City Delivery Committee. The Committee will be comprised of representatives of the Employer and five Union representatives appointed by the President of the NALC and will meet for the purpose of advising on problems affecting city delivery service and to present suggested changes and improvements in operating procedures. Such meetings will be held semiannually at Postal Service Headquarters.

Agenda items shall be exchanged 15 working days in advance of the scheduled meeting, and written minutes shall be kept of all such Committee meetings. The City Delivery Committee shall receive notice of any proposed changes in any instructional booklet regarding the mail count and route inspection and adjustment system. Recommendations of the NALC representatives will be considered and may be adopted by mutual agreement of the Committee provided they are not in conflict with the National Agreement.

[see Memo, pages 230 and 234]

## Summary

A national Joint City Delivery Committee — Employer representatives plus five NALC President-appointed union representatives — meets semiannually at Postal Service Headquarters to advise on city delivery problems and suggest operational changes and improvements. Agendas are exchanged fifteen working days ahead, minutes are kept, the committee receives notice of proposed instructional-booklet changes to the mail count and route inspection system, and NALC recommendations may be adopted by mutual agreement if consistent with the national agreement.

## Cross-union comparison

The five-member union side, the fifteen-working-day agenda exchange, and the instructional-booklet notice right are NALC-specific committee mechanics. The rural craft's route evaluation is governed through its own joint machinery, making this city delivery committee structure distinctive.
