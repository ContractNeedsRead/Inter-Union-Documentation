# NALC §20.3 — Labor-Management Committee

## Full text

<!-- p.87 -->
### Section 3. Labor-Management Committee

Parking is a proper subject for discussion at local Labor-Management Committee meetings. The location of new, additional, or improved parking facilities; the number of parking spaces; security and lighting in the parking areas as well as similar subjects are proper agenda items for such meetings. The local Labor-Management Committee may make recommendations to the installation head concerning such subjects.

(The preceding Article, Article 20, shall apply to City Carrier Assistant Employees.)

## Summary

Parking matters — new, additional, or improved facilities, space counts, security, lighting, and similar subjects — are proper local Labor-Management Committee agenda items, and the committee may recommend to the installation head. The CCA parenthetical extends the article to City Carrier Assistants.

## Cross-union comparison

Channeling parking through the local Labor-Management Committee with a named agenda list is the shared postal approach, and the NALC clause matches the general pattern of the other crafts' parking provisions.
