# NALC §20.2 — Security

## Full text

<!-- p.87 -->
### Section 2. Security

Recognizing the need for adequate security for employees in parking areas, and while en route to and from parking areas, the Employer will take reasonable steps, based on the specific needs of the individual location, to safeguard employee security, including, but not limited to, establishing liaison with local police authorities, requesting the assignment of additional uniformed police in the area, improving lighting and fencing, and, where available, utilizing mobile security force patrols.

## Summary

The Employer must take reasonable, location-specific steps to protect employees in parking areas and en route to and from them: police liaison, additional uniformed police, better lighting and fencing, and mobile security patrols where available.

## Cross-union comparison

The enumerated parking security measures — liaison, added police, lighting, fencing, mobile patrols — are NALC-specific in their detail. The other crafts' parking provisions address parking access and assignment rather than this security menu.
