# NALC §20.1 — National Study Committee

## Full text

<!-- p.87 -->
### Section 1. National Study Committee

The existing parking program will remain in effect. A National Study Committee on Parking will be established in order to improve the parking program at existing facilities and to recommend such programs for new facilities.

## Summary

The existing parking program stays in effect while a National Study Committee on Parking works to improve parking at existing facilities and recommend programs for new ones.

## Cross-union comparison

The national parking study committee is a NALC-specific vehicle; the other crafts address parking through local labor-management channels without a named national study body.
