# NALC §13.1 — Introduction

## Full text

<!-- p.47 -->
### Section 1. Introduction

- **A.** Part-time regular schedule employees assigned in the craft unit shall be considered to be in a separate category. All provisions of this Article apply to part-time regular schedule employees within their own category.
- **B.** The U.S. Postal Service and the Union recognizing their responsibility to aid and assist deserving full-time regular or part-time flexible employees who through illness or injury are unable to perform their regularly assigned duties, agree to the following provisions and conditions for reassignment to temporary or permanent light duty or other assignments. It will be the responsibility of each installation head to implement the
<!-- p.48 -->
provisions of this Agreement within the installation, after local negotiations.

## Summary

Part-time regular schedule employees are their own category for the article's purposes. The parties agree to reassign deserving full-time regular or PTF employees who cannot perform their regular duties due to illness or injury to temporary or permanent light duty or other assignments, with each installation head responsible for implementing the provisions after local negotiations.

## Cross-union comparison

The NALC light duty article's dual-category framing — separate treatment of part-time regulars and the full-time/PTF focus of the reassignment machinery — is its own design. The other crafts' injured-employee provisions exist but none opens with this category statement and local-negotiation implementation mandate in the same form.
