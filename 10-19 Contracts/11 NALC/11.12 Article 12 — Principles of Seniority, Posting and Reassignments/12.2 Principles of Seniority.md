# NALC §12.2 — Principles of Seniority

## Full text

<!-- p.35 -->
### Section 2. Principles of Seniority

- **A.** Except as specifically provided in this Article, the principles of seniority are established in the craft Articles of this Agreement.
- **B.** An employee who left the bargaining unit on or after July 21, 1973 and returns to the same craft:
  1. will begin a new period of seniority if the employee returns from a position outside the Postal Service; or
<!-- p.36 -->
  2. will begin a new period of seniority if the employee returns from a non-bargaining unit position within the Postal Service, unless the employee returns within 1 year from the date the employee left the unit.

## Summary

Seniority principles live in the craft articles unless this article provides otherwise. An employee who left the unit on or after July 21, 1973 and returns to the same craft starts a new seniority period, whether returning from outside the Postal Service or from a non-bargaining position within it — with a one-year grace window for the latter.

## Cross-union comparison

The July 21, 1973 cutoff with the one-year return window for non-bargaining positions is NALC-specific seniority-clock machinery. The other crafts handle unit departures and returns through their own seniority articles without this named date and window structure.
