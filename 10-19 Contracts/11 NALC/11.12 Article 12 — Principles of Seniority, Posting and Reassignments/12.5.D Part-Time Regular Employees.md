# NALC §12.5.D — Part-Time Regular Employees

## Full text

<!-- p.47 -->
- **D.** Part-Time Regular Employees

Part-time regular employees assigned in the craft units shall be considered to be in a separate category. All provisions of this Section apply to part-time regular employees within their own category.

## Summary

Part-time regular employees form their own category for §12.5 purposes, and all of the section's provisions apply to them within that category.

## Cross-union comparison

Segregating part-time regulars into their own category for reassignment purposes is a NALC-specific framing. The other crafts' reassignment provisions treat part-time regulars through their own classification structures without this explicit category statement.
