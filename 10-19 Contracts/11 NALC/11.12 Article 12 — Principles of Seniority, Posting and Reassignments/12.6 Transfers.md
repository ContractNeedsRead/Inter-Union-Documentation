# NALC §12.6 — Transfers

## Full text

<!-- p.47 -->
### Section 6. Transfers

- **A.** Installation heads will consider requests for transfers submitted by employees from other installations.
- **B.** Providing a written request for a voluntary transfer has been submitted, a written acknowledgement shall be given in a timely manner.

(Additional reassignment and probationary period provisions regarding City Carrier Assistant Employees are found in Appendix B.)

[see Memos, pages 187-193]

## Summary

Installation heads must consider transfer requests from employees at other installations, and a written voluntary transfer request must be acknowledged in writing in a timely manner. Bracketed notes point to Appendix B for CCA reassignment and probationary provisions and to the transfer memoranda at pages 187–193.

## Cross-union comparison

The written-acknowledgement requirement for voluntary transfer requests is a NALC-specific procedural detail. The other crafts' transfer provisions state consideration and priority rules but generally do not guarantee a timely written acknowledgement as this clause does.
