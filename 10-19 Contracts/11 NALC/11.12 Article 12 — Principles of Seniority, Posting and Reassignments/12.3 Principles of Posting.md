# NALC §12.3 — Principles of Posting

## Full text

<!-- p.36 -->
### Section 3. Principles of Posting

- **A.** To insure a more efficient and stable work force, an employee may be designated a successful bidder no more than six (6) times during the duration of this Agreement unless such bid:
  1. is to a job in a higher wage level;
  2. is due to elimination or reposting of the employee’s duty assignment; or
  3. enables an employee to become assigned to a station closer to the employee’s place of residence.
- **B.** Specific provisions for posting for each craft are contained in the craft posting provisions of this Agreement.

## Summary

An employee may be a successful bidder at most six times during the agreement, with three exceptions: bids to a higher wage level, bids caused by elimination or reposting of the employee's assignment, and bids that move the employee to a station closer to home. Craft-specific posting rules live in the craft articles.

## Cross-union comparison

The six-successful-bids cap for the agreement's duration with its three named exceptions is a NALC-only posting restriction. The other crafts regulate bidding through seniority and craft procedures without a numeric cap of this kind.
