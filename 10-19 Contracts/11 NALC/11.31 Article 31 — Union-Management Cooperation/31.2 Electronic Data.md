# NALC §31.2 — Electronic Data

## Full text

<!-- p.103 -->
### Section 2. Electronic Data

The Employer shall, on a bi-weekly basis, provide the Union at its national headquarters the information set forth in the Memorandum of Understanding regarding Article 31 by encrypted/password protected Electronic File Exchange. If Electronic File Exchange is unavailable, the information will be provided by disc.

[see Memo, page 222]

## Summary

The Employer must bi-weekly provide the union's national headquarters the Article 31 memorandum's information by encrypted or password-protected electronic file exchange, or by disc when unavailable. A memorandum on page 222 is cross-referenced.

## Cross-union comparison

The bi-weekly encrypted electronic data obligation with the disc fallback is NALC-specific in its delivery mechanics. The other crafts' information provisions do not specify this transmission method.
