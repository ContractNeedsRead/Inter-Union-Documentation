# NALC §31.1 — Membership Solicitation

## Full text

<!-- p.103 -->
### Section 1. Membership Solicitation

The Union may, through employees employed by the Employer, solicit employees for membership in the Union and receive Union dues from employees in non-work areas of the Employer’s premises, provided such activity is carried out in a manner which does not interfere with the orderly conduct of the Employer’s operation.

## Summary

The union may solicit membership and collect dues through employed employees in non-work areas of postal premises, so long as the activity does not interfere with operations.

## Cross-union comparison

Non-work-area solicitation rights are the standard postal accommodation shared across crafts. The NALC clause matches the general formulation.
