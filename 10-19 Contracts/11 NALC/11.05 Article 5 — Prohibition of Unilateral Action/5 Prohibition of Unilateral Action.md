# NALC §5 — Prohibition of Unilateral Action

## Full text

<!-- p.7 -->
The Employer will not take any actions affecting wages, hours and other terms and conditions of employment as defined in Section 8(d) of the National Labor Relations Act which violate the terms of this Agreement or are otherwise inconsistent with its obligations under law.

(The preceding Article, Article 5, shall apply to City Carrier Assistant Employees.)

## Summary

The Employer may not act unilaterally on wages, hours, or other terms and conditions of employment, as Section 8(d) of the NLRA defines them, where the action would violate the agreement or other legal obligations. It is the general anti-unilateral-change commitment behind the rest of the contract. The CCA parenthetical extends it to City Carrier Assistants.

## Cross-union comparison

All four agreements contain a no-unilateral-action clause with essentially the same NLRA §8(d) framing, since the language descends from the Postal Reorganization Act's bargaining mandate. The NALC clause is the shortest of the craft versions and adds nothing beyond the statutory baseline — the comparison value is the sameness of the commitment, not its differences.
