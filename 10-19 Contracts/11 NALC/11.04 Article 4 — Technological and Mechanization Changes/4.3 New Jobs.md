# NALC §4.3 — New Jobs

## Full text

<!-- p.6 -->
### Section 3. New Jobs

Any new job or jobs created by technological or mechanization changes shall be offered to present employees capable of being trained to perform the new or changed job and the Employer will provide such training. During training, the employee will maintain his/her rate. It is understood that the training herein referred to is on the job and not to exceed sixty (60) days. Certain specialized technical jobs may require additional and off-site training.

An employee whose job is eliminated, if any, and who cannot be placed in a job of equal grade shall receive rate protection until such time as that employee fails to bid or apply for a position in the employee’s former wage level.

The obligation hereinabove set forth shall not be construed to, in any way, abridge the right of the Employer to make such changes.

## Summary

New jobs created by technological or mechanization changes are offered to present employees who can be trained, with the Employer providing on-the-job training up to sixty days (plus additional off-site training for specialized technical jobs), at the employee's maintained rate. Employees whose jobs are eliminated and who cannot be placed at equal grade receive rate protection until they fail to bid or apply at their former wage level. None of this limits the Employer's right to make the changes.

## Cross-union comparison

The sixty-day on-the-job training ceiling, the rate-maintenance guarantee during training, and the rate-protection condition for displaced employees are NALC-specific details. The other crafts' technology provisions protect employees on change generally, but the NALC clause quantifies training and ties rate protection to the employee's own bidding behavior, which is a distinctive condition.
