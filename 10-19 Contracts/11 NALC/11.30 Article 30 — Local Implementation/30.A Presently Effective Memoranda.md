# NALC §30.A — Presently Effective Memoranda

## Full text

<!-- p.99 -->
- **A.** Presently effective local memoranda of understanding not inconsistent or in conflict with the 2023 National Agreement shall remain in effect during the term of this Agreement unless changed by mutual agreement pursuant to the local implementation procedure set forth below or, as a result of an arbitration award or settlement arising from either party’s impasse of an item from the presently effective local memorandum of understanding (LMOU).

## Summary

Existing local memoranda of understanding that do not conflict with the 2023 National Agreement stay in effect through the term, changeable only by mutual agreement under the local implementation procedure or by arbitration award or settlement from an impasse over a present LMOU item.

## Cross-union comparison

The continuation rule for existing LMOUs is the shared postal framework for local implementation. The NALC clause states the three change routes — mutual agreement, arbitration award, or settlement — in the same general form the other crafts use.
