# NALC §30.C — Impasse Arbitration and Challenges

## Full text

<!-- p.101 -->
- **C.** All proposals remaining in dispute may be submitted to final and binding arbitration, with the written authorization of the National Union President or the Vice President, Labor Relations. The request for arbitration must be submitted within 10 days of the end of the local implementation period. However, where there is no agreement and the matter is not referred to arbitration, the provisions of the former LMOU shall apply. The parties may challenge a provision(s) of an LMOU as inconsistent or in conflict with the National Agreement only under the following circumstances:

1. Any LMOU provision(s) added or modified during one local implementation period may be challenged as inconsistent or in conflict with the National Agreement only during the local implementation period of the successor National Agreement.
2. At any time a provision(s) of an LMOU becomes inconsistent or in conflict as the result of a new or modified provision(s) of the National Agreement.
<!-- p.102 -->
3. At any time a provision(s) of an LMOU becomes inconsistent or in conflict as the result of the amendment or modification of the National Agreement subsequent to the local implementation period.

In such case, the party declaring a provision(s) inconsistent or in conflict must provide the other party a detailed written explanation of its position during the period of local implementation, but no later than seven (7) days prior to the expiration of that period. If the local parties are unable to resolve the issue(s) during the period of local implementation, the union may appeal the impasse to arbitration pursuant to the procedures outlined above. If appealed, a provision(s) of an LMOU declared inconsistent or in conflict will remain in effect unless modified or eliminated through arbitration decision or by mutual agreement.

[see Memo, page 220]

## Summary

Disputed proposals may go to final and binding arbitration with the National President's or Labor Relations Vice President's written authorization, requested within 10 days of the local implementation period's end; without agreement and without arbitration, the former LMOU applies. LMOU provisions can be challenged as inconsistent with the national agreement only in three windows: during the successor agreement's implementation period for newly added or modified provisions, or at any time when a new or amended national agreement provision creates the conflict. A challenging party must give a detailed written explanation during implementation, at least seven days before the period ends, with impasses appealable to arbitration and challenged provisions staying in effect unless changed by arbitration or mutual agreement.

## Cross-union comparison

The three-window challenge rule with the seven-day written explanation requirement and the stay-in-effect rule for challenged provisions is NALC-specific local implementation machinery. The other crafts' local implementation articles state impasse arbitration without this challenge-window structure.
