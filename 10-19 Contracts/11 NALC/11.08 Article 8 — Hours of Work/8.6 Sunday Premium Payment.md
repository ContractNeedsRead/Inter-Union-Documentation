# NALC §8.6 — Sunday Premium Payment

## Full text

<!-- p.23 -->
### Section 6. Sunday Premium Payment

Each employee whose regular work schedule includes a period of service, any part of which is within the period commencing at midnight Saturday and ending at midnight Sunday, shall be paid extra compensation at the rate of 25 percent of the employee’s base hourly rate of compensation for each hour of work performed during that period of service. An employee’s regularly scheduled reporting time shall not be changed on Saturday or Sunday solely to avoid the payment of Sunday premium payment.

## Summary

Any employee whose regular schedule includes any time in the Saturday-midnight-to-Sunday-midnight window is paid a 25 percent Sunday premium on base hourly rate for each hour worked in that period. Management cannot change an employee's Saturday or Sunday reporting time solely to dodge the premium. The provision protects scheduled Sunday service rather than voluntary extra work.

## Cross-union comparison

All four agreements carry a Sunday premium, but NALC expresses it as a flat 25 percent of base hourly rate tied to the midnight-Saturday-to-midnight-Sunday window. APWU's Sunday premium provision uses the same 25 percent figure; the rural craft computes Sunday work within its evaluated system. The anti-manipulation clause (no schedule changes solely to avoid the premium) is stated here in the schedule article itself rather than in a separate pay rule.
