# NALC §8.1 — Work Week

## Full text

<!-- p.18 -->
### Section 1. Work Week

The work week for full-time regulars shall be forty (40) hours per week, eight (8) hours per day within ten (10) consecutive hours, provided, however, that in all offices with more than 100 full-time employees in the bargaining units the normal work week for full-time regular employees will be forty hours per week, eight hours per day within nine (9) consecutive hours. Shorter work weeks will, however, exist as needed for part-time regulars.

## Summary

NALC's basic work week is forty hours, at eight hours per day within ten consecutive hours. In offices with more than 100 bargaining-unit full-time employees, the outer envelope tightens to nine consecutive hours for the same forty-hour, eight-per-day week. Shorter work weeks exist as needed for part-time regulars. The definition is a framework norm; the details of service weeks, service days, and schedule distribution live in §8.2, and exceptions for part-timers and CCAs in §8.3.

## Cross-union comparison

The NALC 40/8 definition is the shared baseline across the four crafts, but NALC alone varies the daily envelope by office size: over 100 bargaining-unit full-timers compresses the allowed span from ten consecutive hours to nine. The rural craft defines a six-day basic week for regular carriers (§8.1 NRLCA), and the APWU and NPMHU agreements state their own 40/8 formulations, so the office-size trigger is a city-carrier-specific feature.
