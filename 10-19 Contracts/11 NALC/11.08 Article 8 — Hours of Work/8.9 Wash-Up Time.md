# NALC §8.9 — Wash-Up Time

## Full text

<!-- p.24 -->
### Section 9. Wash-Up Time

Installation heads shall grant reasonable wash-up time to those employees who perform dirty work or work with toxic materials. The amount of wash-up time granted each employee shall be subject to the grievance procedure.

(The preceding paragraph, Article 8.9, shall apply to City Carrier Assistant Employees.)

## Summary

Installation heads must grant reasonable wash-up time to employees who perform dirty work or handle toxic materials. The amount granted is grievable through the grievance procedure. The CCA parenthetical extends the provision to City Carrier Assistants.

## Cross-union comparison

Wash-up time is a near-universal postal contract feature, but NALC states it in the hours article and ties the amount directly to the grievance procedure rather than to a fixed schedule or local negotiation. The NALC clause is also one of the few Article 8 provisions whose CCA applicability is made explicit, matching the pattern of §§8.4 and 8.7.
