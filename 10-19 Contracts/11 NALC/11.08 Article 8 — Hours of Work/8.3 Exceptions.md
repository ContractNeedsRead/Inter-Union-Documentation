# NALC §8.3 — Exceptions

## Full text

<!-- p.19 -->
### Section 3. Exceptions

The above shall not apply to part-time employees.

Part-time employees will be scheduled in accordance with the above rules, except they may be scheduled for less than eight (8) hours per service day and less than forty (40) hours per normal work week. All PTFs will be guaranteed a minimum of one (1) nonscheduled day each service week, except during the penalty overtime exclusion period. Management will notify PTF employees of their assigned nonscheduled day by the Wednesday preceding the service week.

CCA employees will be scheduled in accordance with Section 2, A and B, of this Article. All CCAs will be guaranteed a minimum of one (1) nonscheduled day each service week, except during the penalty overtime exclusion period. Management will notify CCAs of their assigned nonscheduled day by the Wednesday preceding the service week.

## Summary

The §8.1–§8.2 schedule framework does not apply to part-time employees, who may be scheduled for fewer than eight hours a day and fewer than forty a week. PTFs and CCAs are each guaranteed at least one nonscheduled day per service week, except during the December penalty overtime exclusion period. Management must notify PTFs and CCAs of their assigned nonscheduled day by the Wednesday before the service week.

## Cross-union comparison

The one-nonscheduled-day guarantee for PTFs and CCAs, with a Wednesday notification deadline and a penalty-overtime-exclusion-period carve-out, is a city-carrier-specific construct. The APWU and NPMHU agreements schedule their part-time complements under their own rules, and the rural craft has no CCA classification at all — its part-time rules attach to the evaluated system.
