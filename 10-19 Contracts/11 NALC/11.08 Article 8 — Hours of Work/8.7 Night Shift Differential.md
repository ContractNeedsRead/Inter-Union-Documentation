# NALC §8.7 — Night Shift Differential

## Full text

<!-- p.23 -->
### Section 7. Night Shift Differential

For time worked between the hours of 6:00 p.m. and 6:00 a.m., career employees shall be paid additional compensation at the applicable flat dollar amount at each pay grade and step in accordance with Appendix A attached hereto.

<!-- p.24 -->
(The preceding paragraph, Article 8.7, shall apply to City Carrier Assistant Employees.)

## Summary

Career employees working between 6:00 p.m. and 6:00 a.m. receive a night differential at a flat dollar amount set per pay grade and step in Appendix A. The CCA applicability parenthetical extends the same differential to City Carrier Assistants. The provision is a rate pointer — the dollar values live in Appendix A, not in the article.

## Cross-union comparison

NALC sets night differential as a flat dollar amount by grade and step in an appendix — a table-driven approach the other crafts share in structure (APWU and NPMHU also use appendix tables), while the rural craft's evaluated system handles night work differently. The NALC version explicitly extends the differential to its CCA tier, which has no parallel in the rural agreement.
