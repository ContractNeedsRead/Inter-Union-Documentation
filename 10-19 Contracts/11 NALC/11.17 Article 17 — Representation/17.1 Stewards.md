# NALC §17.1 — Stewards

## Full text

<!-- p.79 -->
### Section 1. Stewards

Stewards may be designated for the purpose of investigating, presenting and adjusting grievances.

## Summary

Stewards are the union's designated grievance actors — investigating, presenting, and adjusting grievances. The section is the article's opening definition of the steward role.

## Cross-union comparison

All four agreements define stewards as grievance representatives, so this clause is the shared baseline. The NALC version's compact three-verb definition (investigating, presenting, adjusting) matches the general postal formulation.
