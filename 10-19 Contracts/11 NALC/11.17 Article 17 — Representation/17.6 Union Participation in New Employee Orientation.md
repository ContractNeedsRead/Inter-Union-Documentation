# NALC §17.6 — Union Participation in New Employee Orientation

## Full text

<!-- p.82 -->
### Section 6. Union Participation in New Employee Orientation

During the course of any employment orientation program for new employees, a representative of the Union representing the craft to which the new employees are assigned shall be provided ample opportunity to address such new employees, provided that this provision does not preclude the Employer from addressing employees concerning the same subject.

Health benefit enrollment information and forms will not be provided during orientation until such time as a representative of the Union has had an opportunity to address such new employees.

## Summary

The union gets ample opportunity to address new employees during orientation, without precluding the Employer from doing the same. Health benefit enrollment materials are withheld from orientation until the union representative has had that opportunity.

## Cross-union comparison

The sequencing rule — no health benefit enrollment materials until the union has addressed the new employees — is a NALC-specific orientation right. The other crafts guarantee union access to orientation in their own terms, but the benefits-enrollment holdback is distinctive.
