# NALC §2.3 — Grievances

## Full text

<!-- p.4 -->
### Section 3. Grievances

Grievances arising under this Article may be filed at Formal Step A of the grievance procedure within fourteen (14) days of when the employee or the Union has first learned or may reasonably have been expected to have learned of the alleged discrimination, unless filed directly at the national level, in which case the provisions of this Agreement for initiating grievances at that level shall apply.

(The preceding Article, Article 2, shall apply to City Carrier Assistant Employees.)

## Summary

Discrimination grievances under Article 2 may be filed at Formal Step A within fourteen days of actual or reasonably expected knowledge of the alleged discrimination, unless initiated directly at the national level, where the national-level initiation provisions apply. The CCA parenthetical extends the article to City Carrier Assistants.

## Cross-union comparison

The fourteen-day discovery rule for discrimination grievances is a distinctive NALC feature: it starts the clock on knowledge rather than on the event, and it preserves the national-level filing alternative. The other crafts route discrimination complaints through their general grievance timeliness rules without this article-specific window.
