# NALC §2.1 — Statement of Principle

## Full text

<!-- p.4 -->
### Section 1. Statement of Principle

The Employer and the Union agree that there shall be no discrimination by the Employer or the Union against employees because of race, color, creed, religion, national origin, sex (including pregnancy), age, or marital status.

In addition, consistent with the other provisions of this Agreement, there shall be no unlawful discrimination against individuals with disabilities, as prohibited by the Rehabilitation Act.

[see Memo, page 139]

## Summary

Both parties commit to nondiscrimination on the listed grounds — race, color, creed, religion, national origin, sex (including pregnancy), age, and marital status — and to no unlawful disability discrimination under the Rehabilitation Act. The pledge binds both the Employer and the Union. A bracketed note points to the Deaf and Hard of Hearing memorandum on page 139.

## Cross-union comparison

All four agreements carry a joint nondiscrimination statement with substantially the same protected-category list. NALC's version is notable for expressly including pregnancy within sex discrimination and for the Rehabilitation Act disability clause with its memorandum cross-reference — the rural and mail handler agreements state the disability commitment with less elaboration.
