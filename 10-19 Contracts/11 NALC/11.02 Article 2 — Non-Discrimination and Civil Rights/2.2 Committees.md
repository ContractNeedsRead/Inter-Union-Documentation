# NALC §2.2 — Committees

## Full text

<!-- p.4 -->
### Section 2. Committees

There are established at the national and area levels Joint Committees on Human Rights. The committees will be composed of a representative of the Union and responsible management officials. The committees may develop affirmative action proposals on all matters affecting minority groups. The committees will also be advised of the plan for site selection for facilities planned for national postal mail networks and major metropolitan areas, and review availability of adequate housing and public transportation. The committees shall meet as required at mutually agreeable times.

## Summary

Joint Committees on Human Rights are established at national and area levels, staffed by a union representative and responsible management officials. The committees may develop affirmative action proposals affecting minority groups and are advised of site-selection plans for national mail network facilities and major metropolitan areas, including review of housing and public transportation availability. They meet as required at mutually agreeable times.

## Cross-union comparison

Joint labor-management human rights committees appear across the postal crafts, but the NALC clause adds functions the other crafts' committee provisions generally do not spell out: site-selection advisement for national mail network facilities and review of housing and public transportation availability.
