# NALC §6.8 — Intent

## Full text

<!-- p.15 -->
- **G.** Intent

The Employer shall not lay off, reduce in force, or take any other action against a non-protected employee solely to prevent the attainment of that employee of protection status.

(Additional no layoffs or reduction in force provisions regarding City Carrier Assistant Employees are found in Appendix B.)

## Summary

The Employer may not lay off, reduce in force, or otherwise act against a non-protected employee solely to stop that employee from reaching protected status. A parenthetical note points to Appendix B for additional no-layoff provisions covering City Carrier Assistants.

## Cross-union comparison

The anti-evasion clause — no action against a non-protected employee solely to prevent vesting into protected status — guards the six-year path defined in §6.1 and §6.2. The other crafts' no-layoff provisions do not state this purpose-specific prohibition in the same terms.
