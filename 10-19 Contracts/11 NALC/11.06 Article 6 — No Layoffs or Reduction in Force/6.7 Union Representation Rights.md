# NALC §6.7 — Union Representation Rights

## Full text

<!-- p.14 -->
- **F.** Union Representation Rights
  1. The interpretation and application of the provisions of this Award shall be grievable under Article 15. Any such grievance may be introduced at Step B and shall be subject to priority arbitration.
  2. The Employer shall provide to the affected Union(s) a quarterly report on all reassignments, layoff and reductions in force made under this Article.
  3. Preference eligibles are not deprived of whatever rights of appeal such employees may have under applicable laws and regulations. If the employee appeals under the Veterans’ Preference Act, however, the time limits for appeal to arbitration and the normal contractual arbitration scheduling procedures are not to be delayed as a consequence of that appeal; if there is an MSPB appeal pending as of the date the arbitration is scheduled by the parties, the
<!-- p.15 -->
grievant waives access to the grievance-arbitration procedure beyond Step B.

## Summary

Disputes over this article's interpretation and application are grievable under Article 15, may start at Step B, and get priority arbitration. The Employer must give the affected unions a quarterly report on all reassignments, layoffs, and reductions in force. Preference eligibles keep their statutory appeal rights, but a pending MSPB appeal as of the scheduled arbitration date means the grievant waives grievance-arbitration access beyond Step B.

## Cross-union comparison

The Step B entry point with priority arbitration for layoff disputes, plus the quarterly union reporting duty, are NALC-specific procedural features. The MSPB-pending waiver in F.3 — arbitration proceeds on schedule and the grievant loses access beyond Step B — is a distinctive coordination rule between the contractual and statutory appeal tracks not written into the other crafts' layoff articles.
