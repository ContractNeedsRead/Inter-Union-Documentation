# NALC §6.5 — Recall Rights

## Full text

<!-- p.13 -->
- **D.** Recall Rights
  1. Employees who are laid off or reduced in force shall be placed on recall lists within their seniority units and shall be entitled to remain on such lists for two years. Such employees shall keep the Employer informed of their current address. Employees on the lists shall be notified in order of craft seniority within the seniority unit of all vacant assignments in the same category and level from which they were laid off or reduced in force. Preference eligibles will be accorded no recall rights greater than non-preference eligibles except as required by law. Notice of vacant assignments shall be given by certified mail, return receipt requested, and a copy of such notice shall be furnished to the local union president. An employee so notified must acknowledge receipt of the notice and advise the Employer of his or her intentions within 5 days after receipt of the notice. If the employee accepts the position offered he or she must report for work within 2 weeks after receipt of notice. If the employee fails to reply to the notice within 5 days after the notice is received or delivery cannot be accomplished, the Employer shall offer the vacancy to the next employee on the list. If an employee declines the offer of a vacant assignment in his or her seniority unit or does not have a satisfactory reason for failure to
<!-- p.14 -->
reply to a notice, the employee shall be removed from the recall list.
  2. An employee reassigned from a losing installation pursuant to B.5 above and who has retreat rights shall be entitled under this Article to exercise those retreat rights before a vacancy is offered to an employee on the recall list who is junior to the reassigned employee in craft seniority.

## Summary

Laid-off or RIF'd employees go on two-year recall lists within their seniority units and are offered vacancies in craft-seniority order by certified mail, with a copy to the local union president. They must acknowledge within 5 days and report within 2 weeks of accepting; silence, declined offers, or unsatisfactory reasons remove them from the list. Reassigned employees with retreat rights exercise those rights before junior employees on the recall list.

## Cross-union comparison

The two-year recall list with certified-mail notice, 5-day reply and 2-week reporting deadlines, and removal for non-response is NALC-specific in its detail. The retreat-rights priority in clause D.2 ties recall to the Article 12 reassignment machinery, a linkage the other crafts handle through their own seniority articles rather than inside their layoff clauses.
