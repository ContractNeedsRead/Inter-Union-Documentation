# NALC §6.6 — Protective Benefits

## Full text

<!-- p.14 -->
- **E.** Protective Benefits
  1. Severance pay. Employees who are separated because of a layoff or reduction in force shall be entitled to severance pay in accordance with Part 435 of the Employee and Labor Relations Manual.
  2. Health and Life Insurance Coverage. Employees who are separated because of a layoff or a reduction in force shall be entitled to the health insurance and life insurance coverage and to the conversion rights provided for in the Employee and Labor Relations Manual.

## Summary

Employees separated by layoff or reduction in force receive severance pay under ELM Part 435, and retain health and life insurance coverage with the conversion rights the ELM provides. Both benefits point entirely to the manual — the article itself sets no amounts or durations.

## Cross-union comparison

Severance and insurance conversion for separated employees is a shared postal framework across crafts, all pointing to the ELM. The NALC clause is purely a pointer provision; it adds nothing beyond the manual, so the comparison value is the uniformity of the entitlement rather than any craft-specific divergence.
