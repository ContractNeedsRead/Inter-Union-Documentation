# NALC §33.1 — General Principles

## Full text

<!-- p.104 -->
### Section 1. General Principles

The Employer agrees to place particular emphasis upon career advancement opportunities. First opportunity for promotions will be given to qualified career employees. The Employer will assist employees to improve their own skills through
<!-- p.105 -->
training and self-help programs, and will continue to expand the Postal Employee Development Center concept.

## Summary

The Employer emphasizes career advancement, giving qualified career employees the first promotion opportunity and helping employees build skills through training, self-help programs, and expansion of the Postal Employee Development Center concept.

## Cross-union comparison

The Postal Employee Development Center expansion commitment is a NALC-specific promotional infrastructure pledge. The other crafts' promotion articles state career-advancement priorities without this named center concept.
