# NALC §33.3 — Examinations

## Full text

<!-- p.105 -->
### Section 3. Examinations

When an examination is given, there shall be no unreasonable limitation on the number of examinations that may be taken by an applicant.

## Summary

Applicants may take promotion examinations without unreasonable limits on the number of attempts.

## Cross-union comparison

The no-unreasonable-limit rule on examination attempts is a NALC-specific promotion safeguard. The other crafts' promotion provisions do not state this retake protection in the same terms.
