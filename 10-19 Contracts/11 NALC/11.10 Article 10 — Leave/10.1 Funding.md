# NALC §10.1 — Funding

## Full text

<!-- p.28 -->
### Section 1. Funding

The Employer shall continue funding the leave program so as to continue the current leave earning level for the duration of this Agreement.

## Summary

The Employer must keep funding the leave program to maintain the current leave-earning level for the life of the agreement. It is a funding guarantee rather than a leave rule.

## Cross-union comparison

A funding-maintenance clause is a common contract opening for leave articles across the crafts. NALC's is one sentence with no stated dollar or accrual figures, deferring to the current earning levels — thinner than the other agreements' leave-funding statements.
