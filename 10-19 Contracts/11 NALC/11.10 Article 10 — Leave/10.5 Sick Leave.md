# NALC §10.5 — Sick Leave

## Full text

<!-- p.30 -->
### Section 5. Sick Leave

The Employer agrees to continue the administration of the present sick leave program, which shall include the following specific items:

- **A.** Credit employees with sick leave as earned.
- **B.** Charge to annual leave or leave without pay (at employee’s option) approved absence for which employee has insufficient sick leave.
<!-- p.31 -->
- **C.** Employee becoming ill while on annual leave may have leave charged to sick leave upon request.
- **D.** For periods of absence of three (3) days or less, a supervisor may accept an employee’s certification as reason for an absence.

## Summary

The present sick leave program continues: employees are credited sick leave as earned, and approved absences for which sick leave is insufficient are charged to annual leave or LWOP at the employee's option. An employee who becomes ill on annual leave may have that leave charged to sick leave on request, and for absences of three days or less a supervisor may accept the employee's own certification as the reason for the absence.

## Cross-union comparison

Sick leave credit as earned and the annual-leave/LWOP substitution option at the employee's choice are standard postal features shared by the other crafts. The NALC clause states only two specific items, leaving the rest of the sick leave program to the continuing administration it references.
