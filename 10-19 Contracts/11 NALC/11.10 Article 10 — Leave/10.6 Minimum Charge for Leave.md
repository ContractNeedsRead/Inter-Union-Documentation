# NALC §10.6 — Minimum Charge for Leave

## Full text

<!-- p.31 -->
### Section 6. Minimum Charge for Leave

The minimum unit charged for sick leave and annual leave for regular work force employees as defined in Article 7, Section 1.A, is one hundredth of an hour (.01 hour).

Employees may utilize annual and sick leave in conjunction with leave without pay, subject to the approval of the leave in accordance with normal leave approval procedures. The Employer is not obligated to approve such leave for the last hour of the employee’s scheduled workday prior to and/or the first hour of the employee’s scheduled workday after a holiday.

[see Memos, pages 176-186]

(Additional leave provisions regarding City Carrier Assistant Employees are found in Appendix B.)

## Summary

Sick and annual leave for regular work force employees is charged in minimum units of one hundredth of an hour. Annual and sick leave may be combined with LWOP under normal approval procedures, but the Employer need not approve such combined leave for the last hour before or the first hour after a holiday. Bracketed notes point to the leave memoranda at pages 176–186 and to Appendix B for CCA leave provisions.

## Cross-union comparison

The one-hundredth-of-an-hour minimum charge is shared postal practice across crafts. The NALC clause adds the holiday-adjacent hour restriction on annual/sick leave combined with LWOP — a guard against using leave fragments to stretch holidays — which is stated here in the contract rather than left to the manual as the other crafts generally do.
