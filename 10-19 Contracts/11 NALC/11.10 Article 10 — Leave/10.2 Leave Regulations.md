# NALC §10.2 — Leave Regulations

## Full text

<!-- p.28 -->
### Section 2. Leave Regulations

The leave regulations in Subchapter 510 of the Employee and Labor Relations Manual, insofar as such regulations establish wages, hours and working conditions of employees covered by this Agreement, shall remain in effect for the life of this Agreement.

## Summary

ELM Subchapter 510's leave regulations remain in effect for the life of the agreement to the extent they set wages, hours, and working conditions of covered employees. The contract adopts the manual as its leave regulatory core.

## Cross-union comparison

Incorporating ELM Subchapter 510 by reference is shared across the postal crafts — all four agreements point to the manual for leave administration. The NALC clause adds the wages-hours-working-conditions qualifier, which is the standard formulation.
