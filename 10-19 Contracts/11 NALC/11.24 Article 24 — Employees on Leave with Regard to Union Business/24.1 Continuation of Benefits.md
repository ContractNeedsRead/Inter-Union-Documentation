# NALC §24.1 — Continuation of Benefits

## Full text

<!-- p.90 -->
### Section 1. Continuation of Benefits

Any employee on leave without pay to devote full or part-time service to the Union shall be credited with step increases as if in a pay status. Retirement benefits will accrue on the basis of the employee’s step so attained, provided the employee makes contributions to the retirement fund in accordance with current procedure. Annual and sick leave will be earned in accordance with existing procedures based on hours worked.

## Summary

Employees on LWOP for union service keep earning step increases as if in pay status, with retirement benefits accruing on that step provided they make retirement fund contributions under current procedure, and annual and sick leave earned under existing procedures based on hours worked.

## Cross-union comparison

Step-increase credit during union LWOP is shared postal practice, but the NALC clause conditions retirement accrual on the employee's own fund contributions and ties leave earning to hours worked — details the other crafts state with their own variations.
