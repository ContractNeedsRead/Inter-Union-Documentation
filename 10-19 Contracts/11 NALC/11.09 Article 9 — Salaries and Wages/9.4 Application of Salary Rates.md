# NALC §9.4 — Application of Salary Rates

## Full text

<!-- p.27 -->
### Section 4. Application of Salary Rates

The Employer shall continue the current application of salary rates for the duration of this Agreement.

## Summary

The Employer must continue applying salary rates as currently applied for the life of the agreement. It is a status-quo preservation clause rather than a pay rule itself.

## Cross-union comparison

Status-quo "continue the current application" clauses are common across the postal contracts during a term. This NALC provision is one sentence with no qualifying conditions, making it the thinnest of the salary-article provisions in any of the four agreements.
