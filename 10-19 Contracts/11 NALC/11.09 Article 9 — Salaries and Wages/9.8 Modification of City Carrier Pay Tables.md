# NALC §9.8 — Modification of City Carrier Pay Tables

## Full text

<!-- p.28 -->
### Section 8. Modification of City Carrier Pay Tables

NALC RSC Q (Table One) and NALC RSC Q7 (Table Two) will be modified in accordance with the Memorandum of Understanding Re: City Carrier Pay Table Step Modifications. These modifications shall be implemented, and effective upon implementation, within 180 days of the date of the Interest Arbitration Award.

## Summary

The Table One and Table Two schedules (NALC RSC Q and RSC Q7) are modified per the City Carrier Pay Table Step Modifications memorandum, with implementation required within 180 days of the Interest Arbitration Award.

## Cross-union comparison

A pay-table modification clause keyed to a specific memorandum and an interest-arbitration implementation deadline is NALC-specific machinery. The other crafts' wage articles modify their schedules directly or through their own MOUs without this award-dated implementation window.
