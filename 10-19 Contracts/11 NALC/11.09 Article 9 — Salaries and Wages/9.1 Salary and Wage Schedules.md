# NALC §9.1 — Salary and Wage Schedules

## Full text

<!-- p.25 -->
### Section 1. Salary and Wage Schedules

Employees with career appointments before January 12, 2013 shall be paid and earn step increases according to the rates and waiting periods outlined in Table One.

Employees appointed to career positions on or after January 12, 2013 shall be paid and earn step increases according to the rates and waiting periods outlined in Table Two.

## Summary

The salary structure splits on the January 12, 2013 appointment date: career employees appointed before it are paid and advance under Table One, and those appointed on or after it under Table Two. The provision is a pointer to the two pay tables.

## Cross-union comparison

The NALC pay system's pre/post-January 2013 two-table split has no counterpart in the rural craft's evaluated compensation system, which pays by route evaluation rather than wage tables. APWU and NPMHU use unified pay schedules without this career-date bifurcation.
