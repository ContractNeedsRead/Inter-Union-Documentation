# NALC §9.9 — Step P Wage Increase

## Full text

<!-- p.28 -->
### Section 9. Step P Wage Increase

The Step P rate in Tables 1 and 2 shall receive a one-time pay increase to an amount that is equal to a flat dollar amount of $1,000. This change shall be implemented, and effective upon implementation, within 180 days of the date of the Interest Arbitration Award.

## Summary

Step P of both pay tables receives a one-time flat $1,000 increase, implemented within 180 days of the Interest Arbitration Award. It is a top-step adjustment layered on the general and COLA increases.

## Cross-union comparison

A flat one-time top-step increase of a named dollar amount is a NALC-specific feature of this term's wage package. The other crafts' wage adjustments for the same period are percentage- or schedule-based, and the rural craft's top pay moves through evaluated route adjustments.
