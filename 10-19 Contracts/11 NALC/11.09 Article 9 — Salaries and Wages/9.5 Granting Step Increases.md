# NALC §9.5 — Granting Step Increases

## Full text

<!-- p.27 -->
### Section 5. Granting Step Increases

The Employer will continue the program on granting step increases for the duration of this Agreement.

[see Memo, page 171]

## Summary

The Employer will continue the step-increase program for the life of the agreement. A bracketed note points to the Granting Step Increases memorandum on page 171.

## Cross-union comparison

The NALC clause merely extends the existing step program without stating waiting periods — those live in the pay tables and the memorandum. The other crafts state step increases within their wage articles or pay schedules, so this continuation-only formulation is a NALC-specific brevity.
