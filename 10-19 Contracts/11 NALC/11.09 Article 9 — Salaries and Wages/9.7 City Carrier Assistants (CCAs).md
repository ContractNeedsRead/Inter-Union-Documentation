# NALC §9.7 — City Carrier Assistants (CCAs)

## Full text

<!-- p.27 -->
### Section 7. City Carrier Assistants (CCAs)

The CCA hourly rates in Table Three shall be adjusted by the general increases provided for in Article 9.2. In addition, CCAs will receive the following wage adjustments:

Effective November 18, 2023, the CCA hourly rates in Table Three shall be increased by 1.0%.

Effective November 16, 2024, the CCA hourly rates in Table Three shall be increased by 1.0%.

Effective November 15, 2025, the CCA hourly rates in Table Three shall be increased by 1.0%.

<!-- p.28 -->
CCA hourly rates in Table Three shall be increased by $0.50 per hour. This wage adjustment shall be implemented, and effective upon implementation, within 180 days of the date of the Interest Arbitration Award.

## Summary

CCA hourly rates in Table Three get the Article 9.2 general increases plus three additional 1.0% adjustments on the same effective dates as the general increases. On top of that, CCA rates rise by $0.50 per hour, to be implemented within 180 days of the Interest Arbitration Award.

## Cross-union comparison

The CCA wage ladder — general increases plus three named 1.0% bumps plus a flat $0.50 hourly add-on — is unique to NALC's noncareer tier. The other crafts' noncareer classifications (PSEs, MHAs, RCAs) have their own separate wage structures, and the rural craft's RCA pay runs through the evaluated system, so no direct counterpart exists.
