# NALC §9.2 — Basic Annual Salary

## Full text

<!-- p.25 -->
### Section 2. Basic Annual Salary

Effective November 18, 2023—the basic annual salary for each grade and step of Table One and Table Two shall be increased by an amount equal to 1.3% of the basic annual salary for the grade and step in effect on the date of this Agreement.

Effective November 16, 2024—the basic annual salary for each grade and step of Table One and Table Two shall be increased by an amount equal to 1.4% of the basic annual salary for the grade and step in effect on the date of this Agreement.

Effective November 15, 2025—the basic annual salary for each grade and step of Table One and Table Two shall be increased by an amount equal to 1.5% of the basic annual salary for the grade and step in effect on the date of this Agreement.

[see Memo, page 173]

## Summary

Three general wage increases apply to every grade and step of both pay tables: 1.3% effective November 18, 2023, 1.4% effective November 16, 2024, and 1.5% effective November 15, 2025, each computed against the salary in effect on the agreement date. A bracketed note points to the Pay Schedule Consolidation memorandum on page 173.

## Cross-union comparison

The NALC general increases are percentage-based across both tables. The other crafts received their own negotiated general increases for the same term years, and the rural craft's adjustments flow through its evaluated system rather than pay tables, so the structures are not directly comparable line for line.
