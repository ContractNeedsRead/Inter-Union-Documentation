# NALC §9.6 — Protected Salary Rates

## Full text

<!-- p.27 -->
### Section 6. Protected Salary Rates

The Employer shall continue the current salary rate protection program for the duration of this Agreement.

## Summary

The Employer must continue the current salary rate protection program for the life of the agreement — protecting employees whose rates would otherwise fall, without stating the program's terms here.

## Cross-union comparison

Rate protection (saved grade/saved salary) exists across the postal crafts, but the NALC clause is a bare continuation commitment. The other agreements describe the protection program's mechanics inside their wage articles, so a reader must go outside this article for NALC's details.
