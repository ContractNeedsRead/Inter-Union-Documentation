# NALC §32.2 — Joint Committee

## Full text

<!-- p.104 -->
### Section 2. Joint Committee

A joint committee is established at the national level to study the problems in this area leading towards a meaningful evolutionary approach to the issue of subcontracting.

(The preceding Article, Article 32, shall apply to City Carrier Assistant Employees.)

## Summary

A national joint committee studies subcontracting problems toward a meaningful, evolutionary approach to the issue. The CCA parenthetical extends the article to City Carrier Assistants.

## Cross-union comparison

National joint committees on subcontracting exist in several postal contracts. The NALC clause's "meaningful evolutionary approach" framing is its own, with the other crafts stating committee purposes differently.
