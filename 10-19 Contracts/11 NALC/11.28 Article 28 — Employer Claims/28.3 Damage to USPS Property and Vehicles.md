# NALC §28.3 — Damage to USPS Property and Vehicles

## Full text

<!-- p.97 -->
### Section 3. Damage to USPS Property and Vehicles

An employee shall be financially liable for any loss or damage to property of the Employer including leased property and vehicles only when the loss or damage was the result of the willful or deliberate misconduct of such employee.

## Summary

Employees are financially liable for loss or damage to Employer property, including leased property and vehicles, only on willful or deliberate misconduct — a stricter standard than the reasonable-care tests in §§28.1–28.2.

## Cross-union comparison

The willful-or-deliberate-misconduct standard for property and vehicle damage is a NALC-specific liability threshold. The other crafts' employer-claims provisions use their own standards, generally negligence-based rather than this heightened misconduct test.
