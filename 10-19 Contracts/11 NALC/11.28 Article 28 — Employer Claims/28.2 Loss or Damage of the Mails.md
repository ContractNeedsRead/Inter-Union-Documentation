# NALC §28.2 — Loss or Damage of the Mails

## Full text

<!-- p.97 -->
### Section 2. Loss or Damage of the Mails

An employee is responsible for the protection of the mails entrusted to the employee. Such employee shall not be financially liable for any loss, rifling, damage, wrong delivery of, or depredation on, the mails or failure to collect or remit C.O.D. funds unless the employee failed to exercise reasonable care.

## Summary

Employees must protect the mail entrusted to them but are financially liable for loss, rifling, damage, wrong delivery, depredation, or uncollected/unremitted C.O.D. funds only when they failed to exercise reasonable care.

## Cross-union comparison

The reasonable-care standard for mail losses is shared across the postal crafts. The NALC clause's enumerated loss categories (rifling, wrong delivery, depredation, C.O.D. failures) match the general postal formulation.
