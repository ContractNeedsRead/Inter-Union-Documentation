# NALC §28.4 — Collection Procedure

## Full text

<!-- p.98 -->
### Section 4. Collection Procedure

- **A.** If a grievance is initiated and advanced through the grievance/arbitration procedure or a petition has been filed pursuant to the Debt Collection Act, regardless of the amount and type of debt, collection of the debt will be delayed until disposition of the grievance and/or petition has (have) been had, either through settlement or exhaustion of contractual and/or administrative remedies.
- **B.** No more than 15 percent of an employee’s disposable pay or 20 percent of the employee’s biweekly gross pay, whichever is lower, may be deducted each pay period to satisfy a postal debt, unless the parties agree, in writing, to a different amount.

(The preceding Article, Article 28, shall apply to City Carrier Assistant Employees.)

## Summary

Collection of a postal debt is delayed while a grievance through the grievance-arbitration procedure or a Debt Collection Act petition is pending, until settlement or exhaustion of remedies. Deductions are capped at 15 percent of disposable pay or 20 percent of biweekly gross pay, whichever is lower, unless the parties agree in writing to a different amount. The CCA parenthetical extends the article.

## Cross-union comparison

The 15%/20% collection caps with the written-agreement escape and the collection hold during grievances or Debt Collection Act petitions are NALC-specific collection rules. The other crafts' employer-claims articles use their own deduction limits and hold rules.
