# NALC §11.2 — Eligibility

## Full text

<!-- p.32 -->
### Section 2. Eligibility

To be eligible for holiday pay, an employee must be in a pay status the last hour of the employee’s scheduled workday prior to or the first hour of the employee’s scheduled workday after the holiday.

## Summary

Holiday pay eligibility turns on pay status at the hour boundaries around the holiday: the employee must be in a pay status either the last hour of the scheduled workday before the holiday or the first hour of the scheduled workday after it.

## Cross-union comparison

The last-hour-before/first-hour-after pay-status test is the standard postal holiday eligibility rule shared by the other crafts. The NALC clause states it without exceptions, leaving the extreme-emergency excuse and CCA-specific rules to §§11.6.C and 11.8.
