# NALC §11.4 — Holiday Work

## Full text

<!-- p.32 -->
### Section 4. Holiday Work

- **A.** An employee required to work on a holiday other than Christmas shall be paid the base hourly straight time rate for each hour worked up to eight (8) hours. Effective with the 2021 Independence Day holiday, employees who work their holiday, at their option, may elect to have their annual leave balance credited with up to eight (8) hours of annual leave or receive the holiday pay to which the employee is entitled as above described.
- **B.** An employee required to work on Christmas shall be paid one and one-half (1 1/2) times the base hourly straight time rate for each hour worked. Effective with the 2021 Independence Day holiday, employees who work their holiday, at their option, may elect to have their annual leave balance credited with up to eight (8) hours of annual leave or receive the holiday pay to which the employee is entitled as above described.
- **C.** Deferred holiday leave credited in accordance with Section 4.A or 4.B, above, will be subject to all applicable rules for requesting and scheduling annual leave and shall be combined with annual leave and counted as annual leave for the purposes of annual leave carryover.

## Summary

Required holiday work on any holiday except Christmas pays the base straight-time rate per hour up to eight hours; Christmas work pays time-and-one-half per hour. Since 2021, employees who work their holiday may elect up to eight hours of annual leave credit or the holiday pay described. Deferred holiday leave elected under A or B follows annual leave request and scheduling rules and counts as annual leave for carryover.

## Cross-union comparison

The Christmas time-and-one-half rate with straight time for all other holidays matches the general postal pattern, but the NALC clause's annual-leave-banking election and the express rule that deferred holiday leave counts toward annual leave carryover are distinctive. The other crafts state their holiday-work rates without this bank-or-pay choice.
