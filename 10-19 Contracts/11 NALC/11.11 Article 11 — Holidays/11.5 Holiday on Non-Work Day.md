# NALC §11.5 — Holiday on Non-Work Day

## Full text

<!-- p.33 -->
### Section 5. Holiday on Non-Work Day

- **A.** When a holiday falls on Sunday, the following Monday will be observed as the holiday. When a holiday falls on Saturday, the preceding Friday shall be observed as the holiday.
- **B.** When an employee’s scheduled non-work day falls on a day observed as a holiday, the employee’s scheduled workday preceding the holiday shall be designated as that employee’s holiday.

## Summary

A Sunday holiday is observed the following Monday and a Saturday holiday the preceding Friday. An employee whose non-work day falls on the observed holiday gets the preceding scheduled workday designated as the holiday.

## Cross-union comparison

The Monday/Friday observance rule and the preceding-workday designation are standard across the postal crafts. The NALC clause states both in two short sentences — the same rule the other agreements carry in near-identical form.
