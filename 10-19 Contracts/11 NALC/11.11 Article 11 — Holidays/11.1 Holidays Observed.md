# NALC §11.1 — Holidays Observed

## Full text

<!-- p.31 -->
### Section 1. Holidays Observed

The following eleven (11) days shall be considered holidays for full-time and part-time regular scheduled employees hereinafter referred to in this Article as “employees”:

- New Year’s Day
- Martin Luther King, Jr.’s Birthday
- Presidents Day
- Memorial Day
- Juneteenth
- Independence Day
- Labor Day
- Columbus Day
- Veterans’ Day
- Thanksgiving Day
- Christmas Day

## Summary

Eleven days are holidays for full-time and part-time regular scheduled employees: the ten traditional postal holidays plus Juneteenth, which the 2023 agreement added to the list. The article's shorthand "employees" refers to these full-time and part-time regular scheduled employees.

## Cross-union comparison

All four agreements observe the same federal-style holiday list, and the addition of Juneteenth is common to the current contracts. The NALC list is stated in the same form as the other crafts', so the comparison value is in the downstream differences (CCA's six holidays in §11.8, payment details) rather than in the names themselves.
