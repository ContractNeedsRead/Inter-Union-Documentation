# NALC §11.7 — Holiday Part-Time Employee

## Full text

<!-- p.33 -->
### Section 7. Holiday Part-Time Employee

A part-time flexible schedule employee shall not receive holiday pay as such. Part-Time Flexible employees other than
<!-- p.34 -->
those in Step AA shall be compensated for the eleven (11) holidays by basing the employee’s regular straight time hourly rate on the employee’s annual rate divided by 1,992 hours. For work performed on December 25, a part-time flexible schedule employee shall be paid in addition to the employee’s regular straight time hourly rate, one-half (1/2) times the employee’s regular straight time hourly rate for each hour worked up to eight (8) hours.

## Summary

PTFs receive no holiday pay as such. Instead, PTFs other than Step AA are compensated for the eleven holidays by computing their regular straight-time hourly rate as annual rate divided by 1,992 hours. For Christmas work, a PTF receives an additional half-time premium per hour worked up to eight hours.

## Cross-union comparison

The annual-rate-divided-by-1,992-hours holiday compensation formula is NALC's mechanism for folding holiday pay into PTF hourly rates — an approach the rural craft approximates through evaluated compensation but which APWU and NPMHU handle through their own part-time holiday rules. The Christmas half-time premium for PTFs parallels the full-time Christmas rate structure.
