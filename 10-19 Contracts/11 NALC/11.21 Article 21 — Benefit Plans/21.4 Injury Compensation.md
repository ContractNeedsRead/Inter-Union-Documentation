# NALC §21.4 — Injury Compensation

## Full text

<!-- p.88 -->
### Section 4. Injury Compensation

Employees covered by this Agreement shall be covered by Subchapter I of Chapter 81 of Title 5, and any amendments thereto, relating to compensation for work injuries. The Employer will promulgate appropriate regulations which comply with applicable regulations of the Office of Workers’ Compensation Programs and any amendments thereto.

## Summary

Work-injury compensation for covered employees runs under Subchapter I of Chapter 81 of Title 5 (FECA), with the Employer promulgating regulations consistent with OWCP's rules and amendments.

## Cross-union comparison

Federal Employees' Compensation Act coverage is statutory and shared across all postal crafts; the NALC clause is a pure incorporation plus the OWCP-consistency regulation duty.
