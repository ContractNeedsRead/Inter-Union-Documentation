# NALC §21.3 — Retirement

## Full text

<!-- p.88 -->
### Section 3. Retirement

The provisions of Chapter 83 and 84 of Title 5 U.S. Code, and any amendments thereto, shall continue to apply to employees covered by this Agreement.

## Summary

Retirement continues under Chapters 83 and 84 of Title 5 — the Civil Service and Federal Employees Retirement Systems — and their amendments apply to covered employees.

## Cross-union comparison

Pointing retirement to the Title 5 chapters is universal across the postal crafts. The NALC clause is a pure statutory incorporation with no craft-specific retirement terms.
