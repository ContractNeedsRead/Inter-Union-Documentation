# NALC §21.2 — Life Insurance

## Full text

<!-- p.88 -->
### Section 2. Life Insurance

The Employer shall maintain the current life insurance program in effect during the term of this Agreement.

## Summary

The current life insurance program must be maintained for the agreement's term — a continuation commitment with no program terms stated.

## Cross-union comparison

A bare continuation clause for the life insurance program is the shared postal formulation across the crafts' benefit articles.
