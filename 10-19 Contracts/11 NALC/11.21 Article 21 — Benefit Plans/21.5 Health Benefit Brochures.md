# NALC §21.5 — Health Benefit Brochures

## Full text

<!-- p.88 -->
### Section 5. Health Benefit Brochures

When a new employee who is eligible for enrollment in the Federal Employee’s Health Benefit Program/Postal Service Health Benefits Program[^1] enters the Postal Service, the employee shall be furnished a copy of the Health Benefit Plan brochure of the Union signatory to this Agreement which represents the craft in which the employee is to be employed.

[^1]: As of January 2025, postal employees will be covered by the Postal Service Health Benefits Program (“PSHB Program”)

(Additional health insurance provisions regarding City Carrier Assistant Employees are found in Appendix B, Section 3.F.)

## Summary

New employees eligible for FEHB/PSHB enrollment must be furnished a copy of the NALC health benefit plan brochure for their craft. The PSHB footnote applies from January 2025, and additional CCA health insurance provisions live in Appendix B, Section 3.F.

## Cross-union comparison

The union-brochure distribution requirement is shared postal practice, but the NALC clause is specific about the craft-representative brochure. The other crafts' benefit articles contain parallel brochure provisions.
