# NALC §14.5 — Subjects for Discussion

## Full text

<!-- p.58 -->
### Section 5. Subjects for Discussion

Individual grievances shall not be made the subject of discussion during Safety and Health Committee meetings.

## Summary

Individual grievances are off-limits as subjects for Safety and Health Committee meetings — the committees address program-level safety matters, not individual cases.

## Cross-union comparison

The express exclusion of individual grievances from safety committee agendas is a NALC-specific boundary rule. The other crafts' safety committee provisions generally describe what the committees may do without stating this negative limitation.
