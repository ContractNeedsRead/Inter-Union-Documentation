# NALC §14.9 — Field Federal Safety and Health Councils

## Full text

<!-- p.62 -->
### Section 9. Field Federal Safety and Health Councils

In those cities where Field Federal Safety and Health Councils exist, one representative of the Union who is on the Local Safety and Health Committee in an independent postal installation in that city and who serves as a member of such Councils, will be permitted to attend the meetings. Such employee will be excused from regularly assigned duties without loss of pay. Employer authorized payment as outlined above will be granted at the applicable straight time rate, provided the time spent in such meetings is a part of the employee’s regular work day.

(The preceding Article, Article 14, shall apply to City Carrier Assistant Employees.)

[see Memos, pages 196, 197]

## Summary

In cities with Field Federal Safety and Health Councils, one union representative who sits on the local Safety and Health Committee and serves on the Council may attend meetings, excused from duty without loss of pay at the straight time rate when the meetings fall within the regular work day. The CCA parenthetical extends the whole article to City Carrier Assistants.

## Cross-union comparison

Council attendance rights for a local safety committee representative, with the straight-time pay condition, is a NALC-specific detail. The other crafts' safety articles do not write Field Federal Safety and Health Council participation into the contract.
