# NALC §14.7 — Local Committee Meetings

## Full text

<!-- p.58 -->
### Section 7. Local Committee Meetings

The Safety and Health Committee shall meet at least quarterly and at such other times as requested by a Committee member and approved by the Chair in order to discuss significant problems or items. The meeting shall be on official time. Each Committee member shall submit agenda items to the Secretary at least three (3) days prior to the meeting. A member of the Health Unit will be invited to participate in the meeting of the Labor-Management Safety and Health Committee when agenda item(s) relate to the activities of the Health Unit.

## Summary

The local Safety and Health Committee meets at least quarterly, plus chair-approved additional meetings for significant problems, all on official time. Agenda items go to the Secretary at least three days ahead, and a Health Unit member joins when the agenda touches Health Unit activities.

## Cross-union comparison

The three-day agenda submission deadline and the Health Unit participation trigger are NALC-specific meeting mechanics. The other crafts' safety committee meeting provisions set their own cadences without these two details.
