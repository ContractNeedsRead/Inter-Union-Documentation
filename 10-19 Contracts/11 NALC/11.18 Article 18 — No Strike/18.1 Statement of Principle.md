# NALC §18.1 — Statement of Principle

## Full text

<!-- p.85 -->
### Section 1. Statement of Principle

The Union in behalf of its members agrees that it will not call or sanction a strike or slowdown.

## Summary

The union commits on behalf of its members not to call or sanction a strike or slowdown.

## Cross-union comparison

All four postal contracts carry the statutory no-strike commitment, since federal employee strikes are barred by law. The NALC clause is the standard one-sentence form shared across the crafts.
