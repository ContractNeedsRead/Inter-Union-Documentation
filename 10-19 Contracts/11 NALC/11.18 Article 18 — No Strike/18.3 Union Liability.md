# NALC §18.3 — Union Liability

## Full text

<!-- p.85 -->
### Section 3. Union Liability

It is agreed that the Union or its local Unions (whether called branches or by other names) which comply with the requirements of this Article shall not be liable for the unauthorized action of their members or other postal employees.

## Summary

A union or local branch that complies with this article's obligations is not liable for unauthorized strikes by its members or other postal employees.

## Cross-union comparison

The compliance-conditioned liability shield for unauthorized wildcat actions is the standard postal no-strike corollary. The NALC clause matches the general formulation used across the crafts.
