# NALC §18.4 — Legal Impact

## Full text

<!-- p.85 -->
### Section 4. Legal Impact

The parties agree that the provisions of this Article shall not be used in any way to defeat any current or future legal action involving the constitutionality of existing or future legislation prohibiting Federal employees from engaging in strike actions. The parties further agree that the obligations undertaken in this Article are in no way contingent upon the final determination of such constitutional issues.

(The preceding Article, Article 18, shall apply to City Carrier Assistant Employees.)

## Summary

The no-strike article cannot be used to defeat legal challenges to the constitutionality of federal employee strike prohibitions, and the union's obligations here do not depend on how those constitutional questions are finally resolved. The CCA parenthetical extends the article to City Carrier Assistants.

## Cross-union comparison

The constitutional-reservation clause preserves the parties' positions on strike-prohibition litigation regardless of outcome — a standard feature of postal no-strike articles. The NALC formulation matches the shared template.
