# NALC §23 — Rights of Union Officials to Enter Postal Installations

## Full text

<!-- p.90 -->
Upon reasonable notice to the Employer, duly authorized representatives of the Union shall be permitted to enter postal installations for the purpose of performing and engaging in official union duties and business related to the Collective Bargaining Agreement. There shall be no interruption of the work of employees due to such visits and representatives shall adhere to the established security regulations.

(The preceding Article, Article 23, shall apply to City Carrier Assistant Employees.)

[see Memo, page 216]

## Summary

Duly authorized union representatives may enter postal installations on reasonable notice to perform official union duties and contract-related business, without interrupting employee work and subject to security regulations. The CCA parenthetical extends the right, and a memorandum on page 216 is cross-referenced.

## Cross-union comparison

Access on reasonable notice with the no-work-interruption and security conditions is the shared postal formulation. The NALC clause matches the general pattern of the other crafts' access provisions.
