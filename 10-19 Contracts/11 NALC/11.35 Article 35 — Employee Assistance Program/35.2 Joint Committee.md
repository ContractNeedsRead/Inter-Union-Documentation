# NALC §35.2 — Joint Committee

## Full text

<!-- p.108 -->
### Section 2. Joint Committee

For the term of this National Agreement, the Employer and the Union agree to establish at the national level a National EAP Committee. The Committee will have responsibility for jointly assessing the effectiveness of EAPs operating inside and outside the USPS, and for developing on an ongoing basis the general guidelines with respect to the level of services and the mechanisms by which the services will be provided.

The Committee is not responsible for day-to-day administration of the program.

The Committee shall convene at such times and places as it deems appropriate during the term of this National Agreement. No action or recommendations may be taken by the Committee except by consensus of its members. In the event that the members of the Committee are unable to agree within a reasonable time on an appropriate course of action with respect to any aspect of its responsibility, the Vice President, Labor Relations, and the National Union President shall meet to resolve such issues.
<!-- p.109 -->
The Committee is authorized to obtain expert advice and assistance to aid its pursuit of its objectives. The apportionment of any fees and expenses for any such experts shall be by consensus of the Committee.

The Employer and the Union agree that they will cooperate fully at all levels towards achieving the objectives of the EAP. This joint effort will continue for the term of this National Agreement.

(The preceding Article, Article 35, shall apply to City Carrier Assistant Employees.)

## Summary

A National EAP Committee operates for the agreement's term, jointly assessing EAP effectiveness inside and outside the USPS and developing service-level and delivery-mechanism guidelines — but not day-to-day administration. It meets as it deems appropriate, acts only by consensus, and escalates deadlocks to the Labor Relations Vice President and the National Union President. It may obtain expert advice with consensus-apportioned costs, and both parties cooperate fully toward EAP objectives for the term. The CCA parenthetical extends the article.

## Cross-union comparison

The consensus-only rule with executive-level escalation and the expert-advice authorization are NALC-specific EAP committee mechanics. The other crafts' EAP provisions create joint committees with their own governance, generally without this escalation path.
