# NALC §16.10 — Employee Discipline Records

## Full text

<!-- p.78 -->
### Section 10. Employee Discipline Records

The records of a disciplinary action against an employee shall not be considered in any subsequent disciplinary action if there has been no disciplinary action initiated against the employee for a period of two years.
<!-- p.79 -->
Upon the employee’s written request, any disciplinary notice or decision letter will be removed from the employee’s official personnel folder after two years if there has been no disciplinary action initiated against the employee in that two-year period.

(Additional discipline procedure provisions regarding City Carrier Assistant Employees are found in Appendix B.)

## Summary

Disciplinary records become unusable in later discipline once two years pass without any new disciplinary action against the employee. On the employee's written request, disciplinary notices and decision letters are removed from the official personnel folder after two clean years. Additional CCA discipline provisions are found in Appendix B.

## Cross-union comparison

The two-year dormancy rule for disciplinary records is a NALC-specific limitation period. The other crafts' discipline articles use their own record-consideration windows, with the NALC two-year figure stated plainly here.
