# NALC §16.9 — Veterans' Preference

## Full text

<!-- p.78 -->
### Section 9. Veterans’ Preference

A preference eligible is not hereunder deprived of whatever rights of appeal are applicable under the Veterans’ Preference Act. If the employee appeals under the Veterans’ Preference Act, however, the time limits for appeal to arbitration and the normal contractual arbitration scheduling procedures are not to be delayed as a consequence of that appeal; if there is an MSPB appeal pending as of the date the arbitration is scheduled by the parties, the grievant waives access to the grievance-arbitration procedure beyond Step B.

## Summary

Preference eligibles keep their Veterans' Preference Act appeal rights. A Veterans' Preference Act appeal does not delay arbitration time limits or scheduling, and if an MSPB appeal is pending when arbitration is scheduled, the grievant waives grievance-arbitration access beyond Step B.

## Cross-union comparison

The coordination rule between statutory appeals and contract arbitration — no delay for the statutory track, but Step B as the ceiling when an MSPB appeal is pending — is NALC-specific in its precision. The other crafts' discipline articles address preference-eligible appeals without this scheduling-waiver structure.
