# NALC §16.3 — Letter of Warning

## Full text

<!-- p.76 -->
### Section 3. Letter of Warning

A letter of warning is a disciplinary notice in writing, identified as an official disciplinary letter of warning, which shall include an explanation of a deficiency or misconduct to be corrected.

## Summary

A letter of warning is an official written disciplinary notice that must identify itself as such and explain the deficiency or misconduct to be corrected.

## Cross-union comparison

The requirement that a letter of warning identify itself as an official disciplinary letter of warning is a NALC-specific formality. The other crafts' discipline articles define the letter of warning with varying formality, none stating the self-identification requirement in exactly these terms.
