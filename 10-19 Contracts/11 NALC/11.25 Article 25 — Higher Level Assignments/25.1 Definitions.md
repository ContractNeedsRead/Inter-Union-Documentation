# NALC §25.1 — Definitions

## Full text

<!-- p.91 -->
### Section 1. Definitions

Higher level work is defined as an assignment to a ranked higher level position, whether or not such position has been authorized at the installation.

## Summary

Higher level work is any assignment to a ranked higher-level position, authorized at the installation or not.

## Cross-union comparison

The NALC definition's breadth — including unauthorized higher-level positions — matches the standard postal higher-level concept the other crafts also define, though the rural craft's evaluated system handles higher-level work differently.
