# NALC §25.2 — Higher Level Pay

## Full text

<!-- p.91 -->
### Section 2. Higher Level Pay

An employee who is detailed to higher level work shall be paid at the higher level for time actually spent on such job. An employee’s higher level rate shall be determined as if promoted to the position. An employee temporarily assigned or detailed to a lower level position shall be paid at the employee’s own rate.

## Summary

Higher level details are paid at the higher level for actual time on the job, with the rate computed as if promoted. Temporary lower-level assignments keep the employee's own rate.

## Cross-union comparison

The as-if-promoted rate rule and the rate protection on lower-level details are the shared postal higher-level framework. The NALC clause states both in three sentences, matching the other crafts' higher-level pay provisions.
