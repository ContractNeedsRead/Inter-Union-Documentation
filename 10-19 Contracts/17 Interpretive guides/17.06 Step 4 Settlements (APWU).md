### 9-5-18 Limited Duty Cross-Craft Assignment

- **Case:** Q15C-4Q-C 17352667 / HQTC20170058 (Class Action)
- **Date:** September 5, 2018
- **Subject:** Whether it violates the National Agreement to assign injured-on-duty employees to work associated with a pilot, experimental, or other similar program.
- **Holding:** Injured-on-duty employees are assigned Clerk work in accordance with Article 37, the MOU Re: Temporary Assignment, Reassignment or Reemployment in APWU Represented Crafts of Employees Injured on the Job (September 10, 2012), and ELM Section 546. Newly created or revised positions from any program are assigned under Article 1.5, consistent with Goldberg Q10C-4Q-C 14011344. Pending local cases resolve under this agreement and local facts.

### Operative paragraph
> 
> September 5, 2018 Mr. Lamont Brooks Assistant Clerk Craft Director American Postal Workers Union, AFL-CIO Re: Q15C-4Q-C17352667/HQTC20170058 1300 L Street, NW Class Action Washington, DC 20005-4128 Washington, DC 20260 Dear Lamont: The parties recently met at Step 4 of the grievance-arbitration process to discuss the above referenced grievance. - **Case:** Q10C-4Q-C 16035805 / APWU-HQTC20151075
- **Date:** July 29, 2016 (per filename; settlement signed thereafter)
- **Subject:** Whether Level 6 PSEs staffing Level 4 RMPOs under the September 22, 2014 POStPlan MOU may be used outside their APO installation and/or in Level 20+ APOs.

### Available for Drug Test Step 4 (3-9-1998)

- **Date:** March 9, 1998
- **Subject:** Availability of an employee to be scheduled for random and follow-up drug testing under the Drug and Alcohol Testing Program.
- **Holding:** Employees are subject to testing while performing safety-sensitive functions (the enumerated on-duty functions); rostered employees can be tested at any time on any day they are on duty performing safety-sensitive functions, including periods actually performing, ready to perform, or immediately available to perform such functions; follow-up testing uses the same criteria; employees at home on approved leave should not be required to travel to a facility for random or follow-up testing.

### Step 4 — 3-Hour Rule, Pritchard from Sgro (2-25-1999)

- **Date:** February 25, 1999
- **Subject:** Scheduling of DOT drug tests; testing on overtime and the three-hour specimen window.
- **Holding:** A drug test may be scheduled at any time during the workday with the expectation of completion by tour's end; if the employee cannot immediately provide a sufficient specimen, the employee consumes up to (but not more than) 40 fluid ounces over a three-hour period while directed to attempt another specimen; testing on overtime is permitted (covered accidents on overtime, reasonable suspicion while on overtime, etc.).

### Operative paragraph
> 
> LABOR RELATIONS WfITEDSMTES POSTAL SERVICE February 25, 1999 Robert C. Pritchard Director, Motor Vehicle Services Division American Postal Workers Union, AFL-CIO 1300 L Street, NW Washington, DC 20005-4107 Dear Mr. Pritchard : This responds to your letter of January 21, 1999 concerning the scheduling of drug tests for employees covered by the Department of Transportation Drug and Alcohol Testing Prugrain . A drug test may be scheduled at any time during an employee's workday with the anticipation that the test will be completed by the end of the employee's tour. There may be circumstances, however, that may cause the employee to continue on overtime while the test is completed . Generally, this occurs when an employee is unable to immediately provide a specimen or a specimen of sufficient quantity for the drug test. In that case, the employee will be required to consume up to, but not more than, 40 fluid ounces in a three- hour period during which time the employee will be directed to attempt to provide another specimen . Other circumstances may also require an employee to be tested while on overtime. These situations include, but are not limited to, an employee having a covered vehicle accident while on overtime; or an employee being sent for reasonable suspicion testing while working overtime . Accordingly, the Postal Service is not precluded from directing an employee to be tested for drugs when overtime is involved . ### MVS Safety-Sensitive Drug Testing — Who Can Be Tested (Q00V-4Q-C 05069237)

- **Case:** Q00V-4Q-C 05069237 / HQTV20052 (Class Action)
- **Subject:** Whether CDL-holding employees who cannot be called upon to operate a commercial motor vehicle must remain in DOT drug and alcohol testing pools.
- **Holding:** The Postal Service will not require an employee to remain in DOT drug and alcohol pools/testing if the employee is unable to be called upon at any time to operate a CMV, even on an occasional or emergency basis; upon being cleared to return to the safety-sensitive position the employee must be ready and able to operate a CMV before returning to the testing pool. Settles the pending national arbitration.
