# NRLCA §34.8 — Issue Before Arbitrator

## Full text

<!-- p.127 -->
### Section 8. Issue Before Arbitrator

The issue before the arbitrator will be whether the national concepts involved in the new work or time standards are fair, reasonable and equitable.

## Summary

The arbitrator decides only whether the national concepts behind the new standards are fair, reasonable, and equitable. A concepts-level review, not installation-level fact-finding.

## Cross-union comparison

The national-concepts framing is the shared standard across the agreements' standards arbitration clauses.
