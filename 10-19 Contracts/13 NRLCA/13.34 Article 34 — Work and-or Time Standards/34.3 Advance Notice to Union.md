# NRLCA §34.3 — Advance Notice to Union

## Full text

<!-- p.126 -->
### Section 3. Advance Notice to Union

The Employer agrees that before changing any current or instituting any new work measurement systems or work or time standards, it will notify the Union as far in advance as practicable.

## Summary

Changes to work measurement systems or standards require notice as far in advance as practicable. The trigger for the §34.4–34.10 resolution machinery.

## Cross-union comparison

The as-far-in-advance-as-practicable notice is the shared formulation across the agreements' standards articles.
