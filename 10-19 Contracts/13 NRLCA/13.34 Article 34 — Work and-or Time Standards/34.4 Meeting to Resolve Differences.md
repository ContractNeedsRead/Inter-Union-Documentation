# NRLCA §34.4 — Meeting to Resolve Differences

## Full text

<!-- p.127 -->
### Section 4. Meeting to Resolve Differences

Within a reasonable time, not to exceed ten (10) days after the receipt of such notice, representatives of the Union and the Employer shall meet for the purpose of resolving any differences that may arise concerning such proposed work measurement systems or work or time standards.

## Summary

The parties must meet within 10 days of the notice to resolve differences over proposed standards. First step of the §34 dispute ladder.

## Cross-union comparison

The 10-day meeting window mirrors the other agreements' standards-resolution steps.
