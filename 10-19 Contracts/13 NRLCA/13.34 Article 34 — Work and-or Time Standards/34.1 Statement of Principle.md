# NRLCA §34.1 — Statement of Principle

## Full text

<!-- p.126 -->
### Section 1. Statement of Principle

The principle of a fair day’s work for a fair day’s pay is recognized by the parties to this Agreement.

## Summary

The fair-day's-work-for-fair-day's-pay principle anchors the work-standards article. One sentence; the machinery follows in §§34.2–34.10.

## Cross-union comparison

The same principle opens the work-standards articles of the other agreements.
