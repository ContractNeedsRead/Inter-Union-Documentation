# NRLCA §34.6 — Grievance at National Level

## Full text

<!-- p.127 -->
### Section 6. Grievance at National Level

If no agreement is reached within ten (10) days after receipt of notification in Section 5, the Union may initiate a grievance at the national level. If no grievance is initiated, the Employer will implement the new work or time standards at its discretion.

If a grievance is filed and is unresolved within ten (10) days, and the Union decides to arbitrate, the matter must be submitted to priority arbitration by the Union within ten (10) days.

## Summary

No agreement within 10 days of the §34.5 notification lets the union file a national-level grievance; silence lets the employer implement. Unresolved grievances go to priority arbitration within a further 10 days — a 10/10/10 sequence.

## Cross-union comparison

The national-grievance-with-priority-arbitration device is the shared standards-dispute machinery; the triple-10-day cadence is the rural articulation.
