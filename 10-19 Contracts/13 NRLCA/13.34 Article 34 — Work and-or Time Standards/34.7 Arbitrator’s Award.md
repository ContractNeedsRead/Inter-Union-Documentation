# NRLCA §34.7 — Arbitrator’s Award

## Full text

<!-- p.127 -->
### Section 7. Arbitrator’s Award

The arbitrator’s award will be issued no later than sixty (60) days after the commencement of the arbitration hearing. During the period prior to the issuance of the arbitrator’s award, the new work or time standards will not be implemented. Data gathering efforts or work or time studies, however, may be conducted during this period in any installation.

## Summary

The award must issue within 60 days of the hearing's start; the new standards stay un-implemented until then, though data gathering and studies may continue. Implementation is frozen during arbitration.

## Cross-union comparison

The 60-day award window with an implementation freeze is the shared standards-arbitration rule.
