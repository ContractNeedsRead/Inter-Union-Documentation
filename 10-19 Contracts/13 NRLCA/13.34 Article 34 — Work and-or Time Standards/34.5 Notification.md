# NRLCA §34.5 — Notification

## Full text

<!-- p.127 -->
### Section 5. Notification

Following such meeting, the Union must be notified, in writing, whether or not the proposed changes will be modified before implementation.

## Summary

After the meeting, the employer must notify the union in writing whether the proposed changes will be modified before implementation. Feeds the §34.6 grievance clock.

## Cross-union comparison

The written post-meeting notification is standard across the standards articles.
