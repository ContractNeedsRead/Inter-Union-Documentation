# NRLCA Article 6 — No Layoffs or Reduction in Force

## Full text

<!-- p.5 -->
It is agreed by the Employer that no employees employed in the career work force will be laid off on an involuntary basis during this Agreement.

## Summary

Career employees cannot be involuntarily laid off for the life of the Agreement. The protection is limited to the career work force — the substitutes, RCAs, RCRs, and ARCs of Article 7.2 are outside it — and to involuntary layoffs.

## Cross-union comparison

The no-layoff guarantee is a fixture of every postal agreement; the career-workforce limitation is the same boundary the other crafts draw, leaving non-career employees exposed to force reductions.
