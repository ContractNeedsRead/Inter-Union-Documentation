# NRLCA §9.2.J — Training Presentations

## Full text

<!-- p.45 -->
### Section 2. Compensation, Allowance, and Fees

- **J.** Training Presentations

  When a rural carrier (Designation 71) is required to view training films, slides or other training presentations which are not incidental to normal daily performance, and has not been provided an equivalent amount of auxiliary assistance, the carrier will be compensated based on the actual time required multiplied by the carrier’s straight time rate (Article 8.3.A.). In those instances when training is received in full-day increments, the carrier will receive compensation for the full day(s) at the daily evaluated rate of the assigned route.

  All other rural carriers (Designations 73, 75, 76, and 78) will be compensated based on actual time used for training multiplied by their individual computed hourly rate.

## Summary

Regular rural carriers (Designation 71) required to view non-incidental training presentations are paid actual time at the §8.3.A straight-time rate — or a full day at the route's daily evaluated rate when training runs in full-day increments — unless they received equivalent auxiliary assistance. All other rural carriers (Designations 73, 75, 76, 78) are paid actual training time at their individual computed hourly rate.

## Cross-union comparison

Training-time compensation exists across the agreements, but the rural twist is the evaluated-rate full-day payment and the auxiliary-assistance offset — again products of the evaluated system with no city-carrier equivalent.
