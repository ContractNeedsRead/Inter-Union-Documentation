# NRLCA §9.2.A — FLSA Compensation

## Full text

<!-- p.16 -->
### Section 2. Compensation, Allowance, and Fees

- **A.** Compensation Pursuant to the Fair Labor Standards Act (FLSA)
  - **1.** FLSA Section 7(b)(2). <!-- transcribed separately: [[9.2.A.1 FLSA 7(b)(2) Annual Guarantee]], pp. 16–21 -->
  <!-- p.21 -->
  - **2.** FLSA Section 7(a)

    The following employees shall be compensated in accordance with FLSA Section 7(a):
    - **a.** Evaluated carriers assigned to routes having less than thirty-five (35) hours of required service per week.
    - **b.** Regular rural carriers receiving non-evaluated compensation assigned to routes having less than thirty-one (31) paid miles.
    - **c.** Part-time flexible rural carriers.
    - **d.** Substitute rural carriers.
    - **e.** Rural carrier associates.
    - **f.** Rural carrier relief employees.
    <!-- p.22 -->
    - **g.** Assistant rural carriers.
    - **h.** Employees appointed as regular rural carriers during the guarantee period not covered by Section 7(b)(2) of the Fair Labor Standards Act.

  In the event the provisions of this section conflict with any Postal Service regulation, manual, or handbook, the provisions of this section shall be deemed controlling.

## Summary

Splits rural carrier compensation between the two FLSA regimes. Section 7(b)(2) — the annual guarantee — is transcribed separately; this note holds the §7(a) side. Eight categories are paid under FLSA §7(a): evaluated carriers on routes under 35 hours, non-evaluated carriers under 31 paid miles, part-time flexible rural carriers, substitutes, RCAs, RCRs, ARCs, and mid-period appointees not covered by §7(b)(2). A conflict clause makes this section controlling over any Postal Service regulation, manual, or handbook.

## Cross-union comparison

The other three crafts have no §7(b)(2) split — their FLSA coverage is uniform overtime-after-40 under §7(a). The rural craft alone divides employees between an annual-guarantee regime and ordinary §7(a), a consequence of the evaluated pay system.
