# NRLCA Article 22 — Bulletin Boards

## Full text

<!-- p.98 -->
The Employer shall furnish a separate bulletin board for the exclusive use of the Union, subject to the conditions stated herein, if space is available. If a bulletin board is not available, some space will be made available for the purpose outlined in this Article. The Union may place their literature rack in swing rooms, if space is available. Only suitable notices and literature may be posted or placed in literature racks. There shall be no posting or placement of literature in literature racks, except upon the authority of officially designated representatives of the Union.

## Summary

The union gets a separate bulletin board for its exclusive use where space allows — or some space if not — plus literature racks in swing rooms where available. Only suitable materials may be posted, and posting is limited to officially designated union representatives' authority.

## Cross-union comparison

The bulletin-board and literature-rack clause is near-uniform across the postal agreements; the rural clause's swing-room placement matches the others' access terms.
