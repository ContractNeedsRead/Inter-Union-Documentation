# NRLCA §7.1 — Career Employees

## Full text

<!-- p.5 -->
### Section 1. Career Employees

- **A.** Regular Carriers (Designation Code 71)
  For administrative and reporting purposes, regular rural carriers who serve on an established rural route on the basis of triweekly, five (5), five-and-a-half (5½), or six (6) days in a service week, are considered to be full-time employees.
<!-- p.6 -->
- **B.** Part-time Flexible Rural Carriers (Designation Code 76)
  Part-time flexible rural carriers are those substitutes or rural carrier associates appointed following an assignment posting. These employees provide service on regular and auxiliary routes as directed by management.

## Summary

Defines the two career categories. Regular carriers (Designation 71) serving triweekly, five, five-and-a-half, or six days a week are full-time for administrative and reporting purposes. Part-time flexible rural carriers (Designation 76) are substitutes or RCAs appointed after an assignment posting, who serve regular and auxiliary routes as directed.

## Cross-union comparison

The rural career roster is unusually granular: a "full-time" rural carrier may work a triweekly route, which has no city-carrier analog. The PTF category is shared with the clerk craft, but rural PTFs are appointment-posting appointees, not schedule-bidder equivalents.
