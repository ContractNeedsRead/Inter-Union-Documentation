# NRLCA §21.5 — Injury Compensation

## Full text

<!-- p.98 -->
### Section 5: Injury Compensation

Employees covered by this Agreement shall be covered by subchapter I of Chapter 81 of Title 5 and any amendments thereto relating to compensation for work injuries.

The Employer will promulgate appropriate regulations which comply with applicable regulations of the Office of Workers’ Compensation Programs and any amendments thereto.

## Summary

FECA coverage (5 U.S.C. ch. 81, subchapter I) applies to covered employees for work injuries, and the employer must promulgate regulations complying with OWCP's. The statutory injury-compensation regime rides above the contract.

## Cross-union comparison

The FECA/OWCP compliance clause is standard in every postal agreement.
