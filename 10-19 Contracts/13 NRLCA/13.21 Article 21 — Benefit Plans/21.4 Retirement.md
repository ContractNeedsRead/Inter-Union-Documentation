# NRLCA §21.4 — Retirement

## Full text

<!-- p.97 -->
### Section 4. Retirement

The provisions of Chapter 83 of Title 5 of the U.S. Code and any amendments thereto shall continue to apply to employees covered by this Agreement.

## Summary

CSRS (5 U.S.C. ch. 83) and its amendments continue to govern retirement for covered employees. The clause preserves the statutory retirement regime without contractual modification.

## Cross-union comparison

All four agreements carry the same Chapter 83 continuation clause.
