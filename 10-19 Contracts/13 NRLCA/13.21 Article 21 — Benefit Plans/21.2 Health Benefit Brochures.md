# NRLCA §21.2 — Health Benefit Brochures

## Full text

<!-- p.97 -->
### Section 2. Health Benefit Brochures

When a new employee who is eligible for enrollment in the Postal Service Health Benefits Program enters the Postal Service, the employee shall be furnished a copy of the Health Benefit Plan brochure of the Union.

## Summary

New PSHBP-eligible employees must be given the union health plan's brochure on entry. A recruitment-channel guarantee for the union's plan.

## Cross-union comparison

Each agreement carries the same union-brochure distribution clause for its own plan.
