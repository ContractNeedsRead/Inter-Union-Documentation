# NRLCA §21.3 — Life Insurance

## Full text

<!-- p.97 -->
### Section 3. Life Insurance

The Employer shall maintain the current life insurance program in effect during the term of this Agreement.

## Summary

Keeps the current life insurance program (FEGLI-based) in effect for the term. A freeze clause; program details live in the federal program itself.

## Cross-union comparison

Identical freeze language across the four agreements.
