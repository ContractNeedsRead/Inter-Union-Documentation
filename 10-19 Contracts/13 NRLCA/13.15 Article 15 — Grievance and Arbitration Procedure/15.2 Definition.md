# NRLCA §15.2 — Definition

## Full text

<!-- p.72 -->
### Section 2. Definition

A grievance is defined as a dispute, difference, disagreement or complaint between the parties related to wages, hours, and conditions of employment. A grievance shall include, but is not limited to, the complaint of an employee or of the Union which involves the interpretation, application of, or compliance with the provisions of this Agreement.

## Summary

Defines a grievance broadly: any dispute between the parties over wages, hours, or conditions of employment, expressly including — but not limited to — employee or union complaints about interpretation, application, or compliance with the Agreement. Union-initiated grievances are recognized by the definition itself.

## Cross-union comparison

The same wages-hours-conditions definition with the interpretation/application/compliance gloss appears in every postal grievance article; the rural version is the shortest of the four.
