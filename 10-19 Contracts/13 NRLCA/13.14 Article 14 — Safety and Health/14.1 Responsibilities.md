# NRLCA §14.1 — Responsibilities

## Full text

<!-- p.70 -->
### Section 1. Responsibilities

It is the responsibility of management to provide safe working conditions in all present and future installations and to develop a safe working force. The Union will cooperate with and assist management to live up to this responsibility.

## Summary

Assigns management the responsibility for safe working conditions in all installations and for developing a safe workforce, with the union committed to cooperate and assist. The clause is the rural safety article's general duty foundation.

## Cross-union comparison

All four agreements open their safety articles with management-responsibility/union-cooperation statements of this kind; the obligations themselves are elaborated in the following sections.
