# NRLCA Article 5 — Prohibition of Unilateral Action

## Full text

<!-- p.5 -->
The Employer will not take any actions affecting wages, hours and other terms and conditions of employment as defined in Section 8(d) of the National Labor Relations Act which violate the terms of this Agreement or are otherwise inconsistent with its obligations under law.

## Summary

Bars the employer from unilateral action on wages, hours, and other terms and conditions of employment — the NLRA §8(d) subject matter — that violates the Agreement or is otherwise unlawful. It is the contractual twin of the statutory duty to bargain.

## Cross-union comparison

All four agreements carry a near-identical prohibition tied to NLRA §8(d); this is the standard status-quo clause, not a rural-specific invention.
