# NRLCA §16.2 — Letter of Warning

## Full text

<!-- p.85 -->
### Section 2. Letter of Warning

A letter of warning is a disciplinary notice in writing, identified as an official disciplinary letter of warning which shall include an explanation of a deficiency or misconduct to be corrected.

## Summary

Defines the letter of warning: a written, official disciplinary notice that must explain the deficiency or misconduct to be corrected. It is the first rung of the §16.1 progressive ladder and stays usable for one year under §16.8.

## Cross-union comparison

The letter-of-warning definition is common form across the postal agreements; the one-year shelf life in §16.8 is the operative rural rule.
