# NRLCA §28.1 — Statement of Principle

## Full text

<!-- p.102 -->
### Section 1. Statement of Principle

The parties agree that continued public confidence in the Postal Service requires the proper care and handling of USPS property, postal funds, and the mails. In advance of any money demand upon an employee for any reason, the employee must be informed in writing and the demand must include the reasons therefor.

## Summary

Frames the employer-side claims article: proper care of USPS property, funds, and mail sustains public confidence. Any money demand on an employee must be preceded by written notice stating the reasons — the due-process floor for debt collection.

## Cross-union comparison

The written-reasons-before-demand rule is common to all four employer-claims articles.
