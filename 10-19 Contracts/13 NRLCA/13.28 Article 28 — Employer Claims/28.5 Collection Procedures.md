# NRLCA §28.5 — Collection Procedures

## Full text

<!-- p.103 -->
### Section 5. Collection Procedures

- **A.** If a grievance is initiated and advanced through the grievance-arbitration procedure or a petition has been filed pursuant to the Debt Collection Act, regardless of the amount and type of debt, collection of the debt will be delayed until disposition of the grievance and/or petition has been had, either through settlement or exhaustion of contractual and/or administrative remedies.
- **B.** No more than 15 percent of an employee’s disposable pay or 20 percent of the employee’s bi-weekly gross pay, whichever is lower, may be deducted each pay period to satisfy a postal debt, unless the parties agree, in writing, to a different amount.

## Summary

Collection of any postal debt is stayed while a grievance or Debt Collection Act petition is pending, until settlement or exhaustion of remedies. Repayment deductions are capped at the lower of 15% of disposable pay or 20% of bi-weekly gross pay per period, unless the parties agree in writing otherwise.

## Cross-union comparison

The collection-stay and the 15%/20% garnishment-style caps are the shared postal debt-collection limits across the agreements.
