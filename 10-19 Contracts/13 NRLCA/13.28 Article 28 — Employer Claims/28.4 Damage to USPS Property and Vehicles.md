# NRLCA §28.4 — Damage to USPS Property and Vehicles

## Full text

<!-- p.103 -->
### Section 4. Damage to USPS Property and Vehicles

An employee shall be financially liable for any loss or damage to property of the Employer, including leased property and vehicles, only when the loss or damage was the result of misconduct of such employee.

## Summary

Liability for USPS property and vehicle damage — including leased property — attaches only on employee misconduct. A higher threshold than the reasonable-care standard of §§28.2–28.3: misconduct, not mere negligence.

## Cross-union comparison

The misconduct-only standard for property and vehicles is the strictest employee-protective liability rule in the four agreements' employer-claims articles — a rural-craft distinctive.
