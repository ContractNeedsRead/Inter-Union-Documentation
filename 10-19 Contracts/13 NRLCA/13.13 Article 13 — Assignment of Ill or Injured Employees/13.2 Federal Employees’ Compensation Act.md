# NRLCA §13.2 — Federal Employees’ Compensation Act

## Full text

<!-- p.70 -->
### Section 2. Federal Employees’ Compensation Act

It is understood that the provisions of this Agreement are subject to the obligations and responsibilities imposed by the Federal Employees’ Compensation Act and its implementing regulations. Recognizing the mutual obligation to be fully responsive to the requirements of the Federal Employees’ Compensation Act, the parties will cooperate in making every effort to insure that employees with job-related illnesses or injuries are returned to duty subject to their medical restrictions.

## Summary

Subordinates Article 13 to FECA and its regulations, and commits both parties to cooperate in returning job-injured or job-ill employees to duty within their medical restrictions. FECA's statutory return-to-work framework sits above the contractual limited-duty effort of §13.1.

## Cross-union comparison

A FECA-subordination clause appears in each agreement's ill/injured article; the return-to-duty cooperation sentence is standard postal language, though other crafts pair it with more robust light-duty programs.
