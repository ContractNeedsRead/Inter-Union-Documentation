# NRLCA §13.1 — Assistance for Employees

## Full text

<!-- p.70 -->
### Section 1. Assistance for Employees

The Employer will make an effort to assist employees who through occupational injury or occupational illness are unable to perform their regularly assigned duties. This effort will consist of possible assignment to limited duty work if such is available.

## Summary

The employer will attempt to assist employees who cannot perform their regular duties due to occupational injury or illness, by possible limited-duty assignment if available. The obligation is an effort, not a guarantee — limited duty is contingent on availability, and §13.3 closes the regular-route option.

## Cross-union comparison

Weaker than the clerk craft's light-duty machinery: the rural craft has no comprehensive limited-duty program; the "possible assignment… if such is available" formulation is the softest of the four agreements' ill/injured articles.
