# NRLCA §13.3 — No Light-Duty Assignments

## Full text

<!-- p.70 -->
### Section 3. No Light-Duty Assignments

In the rural carrier craft, at any local installation, regular rural routes shall not be considered for any light-duty assignment.

## Summary

Regular rural routes can never be used as light-duty assignments at any installation. Because an injured regular carrier cannot be carried on the route, the limited-duty effort of §13.1 must find work off the regular routes — the structural constraint that makes the rural light-duty system the thinnest of the four crafts.

## Cross-union comparison

Unique among the agreements: city carriers, clerks, and mail handlers can be assigned limited duty within their own work units, but the rural craft excludes the core work — the regular route — from light-duty use entirely.
