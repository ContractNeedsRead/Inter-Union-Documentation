# NRLCA §10.7 — Leave Submission

## Full text

<!-- p.48 -->
### Section 7. Leave Submission

- **A.** Requests for annual leave will not be accepted prior to sixty (60) days in advance of the desired leave date or service week. Leave requests may be submitted further in advance for exceptional circumstances. Leave requests received on the same day will be considered in order of seniority.
- **B.** Failure to return a request for leave within three (3) days of receipt will result in the request being automatically approved, provided the rural carrier has obtained proper acknowledgment of the leave request submission.

## Summary

Annual leave requests may not be submitted more than 60 days ahead (except in exceptional circumstances), and same-day requests are ranked by seniority. A request not returned within three days of receipt is automatically approved if the carrier got proper acknowledgment of submission — a silent-approval rule.

## Cross-union comparison

The 60-day window and same-day seniority ranking are standard across the postal agreements; the three-day automatic approval on acknowledgment is the rural version of the clerk and city crafts' deemed-approval devices.
