# NRLCA §10.1 — Leave Funding

## Full text

<!-- p.45 -->
### Section 1. Leave Funding

The Employer shall continue funding the leave program so as to continue the current leave earning level for the duration of this Agreement.

## Summary

Freezes the leave program's funding at the current leave-earning level for the life of the Agreement. It is a funding floor, not an accrual-rate provision — the rates themselves live in the leave-earning rules applied by the employer.

## Cross-union comparison

A funding-maintenance clause; the other agreements' leave articles carry their own leave-funding or leave-earning provisions, but the rural clause commits only to continuing the current level.
