# NRLCA §10.6 — Leave for Part-time Flexible Rural Carriers

## Full text

<!-- p.48 -->
### Section 6. Leave for Part-time Flexible Rural Carriers

Part-time flexible rural carriers shall earn leave based on the number of hours worked and in accordance with the appropriate leave category. It shall be credited as earned. These carriers shall be granted leave in hourly increments.

## Summary

Part-time flexible rural carriers earn leave by hours worked, in the appropriate leave category, credited as earned and granted in hourly increments. Unlike substitutes and RCAs, PTFs get no 90-day retroactive credit — they accrue from day one.

## Cross-union comparison

The hours-worked accrual with hourly increments matches the general postal pattern for PTF employees across the crafts; the rural PTF's evaluated-route pay does not change the leave-accrual basis, which stays on hours actually worked.
