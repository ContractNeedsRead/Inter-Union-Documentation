# NRLCA §10.3 — Sick Leave

## Full text

<!-- p.46 -->
### Section 3. Sick Leave

The Employer agrees to continue the administration of the present sick leave program, which shall include the following specific items:

- **a.** Credit employees with sick leave as earned.
- **b.** Charge to annual leave or leave without pay (at employee’s option) approved absence for which employee has insufficient sick leave.
- **c.** Employee becoming ill while on annual leave may have leave charged to sick leave upon request.
- **d.** Regular rural carriers shall be charged sick leave in minimum units of one day.
- **e.** For periods of absence of three (3) days or less, a supervisor may accept an employee’s certification as reason for an absence.

## Summary

Continues the existing sick leave program: credit as earned; charge insufficient-sick-leave absences to annual leave or LWOP at the employee's option; convert annual leave to sick leave when illness strikes mid-leave; charge regular carriers in one-day minimum units; and allow the employee's own certification for absences of three days or less.

## Cross-union comparison

The five items match the standard postal sick-leave administration across the agreements; the rural-specific piece is the one-day minimum charge unit for regular carriers, a consequence of evaluated (daily) pay rather than hourly pay.
