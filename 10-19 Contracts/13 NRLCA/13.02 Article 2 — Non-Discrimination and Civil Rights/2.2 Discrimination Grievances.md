# NRLCA §2.2 — Discrimination Grievances

## Full text

<!-- p.3 -->
### Section 2. Discrimination Grievances

Grievances arising under this Article may be filed at Step 2 of the grievance procedure unless filed directly at the national level, in which case, the provisions of this Agreement for initiating grievances at that level shall apply.

## Summary

Discrimination grievances skip Step 1: they may be filed at Step 2, or directly at the national level using the national-initiation provisions. The Article 15 procedure otherwise governs.

## Cross-union comparison

The Step 2 entry point for discrimination grievances is standard across the postal agreements; it exists so Article 2 claims are not stalled at the informal step.
