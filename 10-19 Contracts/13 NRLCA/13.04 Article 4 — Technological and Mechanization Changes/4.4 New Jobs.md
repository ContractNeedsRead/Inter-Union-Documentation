# NRLCA §4.4 — New Jobs

## Full text

<!-- p.4 -->
### Section 4. New Jobs

Any new job or jobs created in the rural carrier craft by technological or mechanization changes shall be offered to present regular rural carrier employees capable of being trained to perform the new or changed job, and the Employer will provide
<!-- p.5 -->
such training. During training, the employee will be paid based on hours actually worked at the equivalent of a 40-hour evaluated route, attained step. It is understood that the training herein referred to is on the job and not to exceed sixty (60) days. Certain specialized technical jobs may require additional and off-site training. The obligation herein above set forth shall not be construed to, in any way, abridge the right of the Employer to make such changes.

## Summary

New rural-craft jobs created by technological or mechanization change are offered first to present regular carriers who can be trained, with employer-provided on-the-job training of up to 60 days paid at the attained step for a 40-hour evaluated route; specialized jobs may need more off-site training. The clause preserves management's right to make the changes themselves.

## Cross-union comparison

The first-offer-to-incumbents device with paid training appears in the other agreements' mechanization articles; the rural pay measure — the 40-hour evaluated route at attained step — is the evaluated system's equivalent of the city carrier's straight-time training pay.
