# NRLCA §4.1 — Statement of Principle

## Full text

<!-- p.4 -->
### Section 1. Statement of Principle

Both parties recognize the need for improvement of mail service.

## Summary

One sentence: both parties recognize the need for improved mail service. The principle frames the rest of Article 4 — technological change is legitimate, but it carries notice, resolution, and job-offer obligations.

## Cross-union comparison

The other agreements open their mechanization articles the same way; the operative protections are in the notice and job-offer sections, not in this principle.
