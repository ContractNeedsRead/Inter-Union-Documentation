# NRLCA §35.3 — Employee Referrals

## Full text

<!-- p.129 -->
### Section 3. Employee Referrals

In postal installations having professional medical units, the Employer will insure that the professional staffs maintain a current listing of all local community federally-approved drug treatment agencies for referring employees with such problems.

## Summary

Where professional medical units exist, their staffs must keep current listings of federally approved local drug-treatment agencies for employee referrals. The referral-resource duty of the EAP article.

## Cross-union comparison

A standard referral obligation in the agreements' EAP articles.
