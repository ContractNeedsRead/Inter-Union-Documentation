# NRLCA §35.1 — Statement of Principle

## Full text

<!-- p.128 -->
### Section 1. Statement of Principle

The Employer and the Union express strong support for programs of self-help such as the Employee Assistance Program (EAP).

An employee’s voluntary participation in such programs will be considered favorably in disciplinary action proceedings.

## Summary

Both parties strongly support self-help programs like the EAP, and voluntary participation counts favorably in discipline. The mitigation incentive behind EAP participation.

## Cross-union comparison

The favorable-consideration rule is common to the agreements' EAP articles.
