# NRLCA Article 23 — Rights of Union Officials to Enter Postal Installations

## Full text

<!-- p.98 -->
Upon reasonable notice to the Employer, duly authorized representatives of the Union shall be permitted to enter postal installations for the purpose of performing and engaging in official union duties and business related to the Collective Bargaining Agreement. There shall be no interruption of the work of employees due to such visits and representatives shall adhere to the established security regulations.

## Summary

Authorized union representatives may enter postal installations on reasonable notice for official union and contract business, without interrupting the work, and subject to established security regulations. Access conditioned on notice, purpose, non-disruption, and security compliance.

## Cross-union comparison

The same reasonable-notice access clause appears in all four agreements; it is the contract-level floor beneath the institutional access rights.
