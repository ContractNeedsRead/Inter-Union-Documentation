# NRLCA §25.1 — Salary Computation

## Full text

<!-- p.99 -->
### Section 1. Salary Computation

A regular rural carrier is paid based on the evaluation of the route to which the carrier is assigned for the first 30 days of the temporary higher level assignment. At the beginning of the next pay period following completion of the first 30 days, the carrier’s salary is converted to that of a 40-hour evaluated route, attained step. The salary is then adjusted in accordance with the salary rules for changes under the salary schedule to which assigned.

## Summary

Temporary higher-level pay for regular rural carriers: route-evaluation pay for the first 30 days, then conversion to the 40-hour evaluated route salary at attained step from the next pay period, adjusted under the assigned salary schedule's rules. The 30-day lag mirrors the higher-level schedules of the other crafts.

## Cross-union comparison

The other agreements pay higher-level rates sooner (city carriers from the first day); the rural 30-day evaluation-then-conversion sequence is a rural-specific staging of higher-level pay.
