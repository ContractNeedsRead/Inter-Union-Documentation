# NRLCA §25.4 — Assignments in Excess of 180 Calendar Days

## Full text

<!-- p.100 -->
### Section 4. Assignments in Excess of 180 Calendar Days

Once a regular rural carrier has been detailed to a higher level of work for a period of 180 calendar days, inclusive of the first 30 days of the assignment prior to the salary adjustment, the regular rural carrier must return to the assigned route. The regular rural carrier may not perform another higher-level assignment for a period of 90 calendar days.

## Summary

Higher-level details cap at 180 calendar days (counting the initial 30); the carrier then returns to the route and may not take another higher-level assignment for 90 days. A rotation guard against de facto reassignment through details.

## Cross-union comparison

The 180/90 rotation is the rural version of the other crafts' limits on extended higher-level assignments — city and clerk articles use their own day caps and re-detail windows.
