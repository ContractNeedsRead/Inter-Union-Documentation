# NRLCA §8.6 — Wash-up Time

## Full text

<!-- p.9 -->
### Section 6. Wash-up Time

Additional wash-up time for those rural carriers who perform dirty work or work with toxic materials on a daily or weekly basis may be recorded as miscellaneous time during the mini mail survey, provided the carrier is subject to allergic reaction from such dirty work or toxic materials. The amount of wash-up time granted each employee shall be subject to the grievance procedure.

## Summary

Carriers who do dirty work or work with toxic materials on a daily or weekly basis, and who are subject to allergic reaction from it, may have additional wash-up time recorded as miscellaneous time during the mini mail survey. The amount granted is grievable. The provision protects carriers whose duties carry an allergic-reaction hazard.

## Cross-union comparison

A rural-craft specialty tied to the evaluated system's mini mail survey timekeeping; city carrier and clerk agreements address wash-up time differently (through local practice and timekeeping rules) rather than through a survey-recorded miscellaneous-time mechanism.
