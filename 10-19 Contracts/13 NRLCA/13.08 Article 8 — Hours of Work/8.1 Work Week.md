# NRLCA §8.1 — Work Week

## Full text

<!-- p.7 -->
### Section 1. Work Week

The basic work week for regular rural carrier employees shall be six (6) days, except as relief days are provided for certain carriers and for carriers serving triweekly routes. Regular rural carriers may not work on Sunday.

## Summary

The basic work week for regular rural carriers is six days — longer than the five-day basic weeks of the other three crafts. Two exceptions soften the rule: carriers for whom relief days are provided, and carriers serving triweekly routes. Regular rural carriers may not work on Sunday at all. This is a framework definition; the details of relief days and daily schedules live in §8.2 and §8.5.

## Cross-union comparison

City letter carriers and the clerk and mail handler crafts work five-day, forty-hour basic weeks; the rural craft's six-day basic week is the outlier among the four agreements. The absolute Sunday-work prohibition for regular carriers has no city-carrier counterpart — city carriers can be scheduled on Sundays (e.g., Sunday parcel delivery), while regular rural carriers cannot work Sunday. The rural "relief day" (§8.5) is the functional counterpart of the city non-scheduled day.
