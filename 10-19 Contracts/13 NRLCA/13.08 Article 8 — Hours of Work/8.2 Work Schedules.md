# NRLCA §8.2 — Work Schedules

## Full text

<!-- p.7 -->
### Section 2. Work Schedules

Daily schedules shall be established to coincide with the daily evaluation of the route and adjusted periodically as required.

The carrier’s work day may vary above or below the daily evaluation of the route as mail volume fluctuates and road and weather conditions change.

## Summary

Daily schedules are set to match each route's daily evaluation and adjusted periodically. The actual work day may run above or below the evaluated time as mail volume fluctuates and road and weather conditions change. This is the foundational rule of the evaluated system: schedules track evaluation, not fixed clock hours.

## Cross-union comparison

Unlike the city-carrier eight-hour fixed schedule, the rural work day floats with the route evaluation and the day's actual conditions. The provision has no overtime consequence by itself — variance from evaluation is absorbed in the evaluated compensation system of Article 9.2.C.
