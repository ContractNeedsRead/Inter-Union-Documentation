# NRLCA §8.4 — Guarantees

## Full text

<!-- p.8 -->
### Section 4. Guarantees

Any employee scheduled to work and who does report for work shall be guaranteed two (2) hours work or pay.

## Summary

An employee who is scheduled and reports is guaranteed at least two hours of work or pay. The guarantee is a minimum-pay floor for reporting employees, not an overtime provision. It applies to "any employee," not only regular carriers.

## Cross-union comparison

The city-carrier agreement's guarantee (§8.8) covers the regular carrier's scheduled day; this rural guarantee is narrower — two hours for any employee who reports — and is a minimum-pay floor rather than a full-day guarantee.
