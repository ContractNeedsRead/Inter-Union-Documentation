# NRLCA §20.2 — Parking Security

## Full text

<!-- p.96 -->
### Section 2. Parking Security

Recognizing the need for adequate security for employees in parking areas, and while enroute to and from parking areas, the Employer will take reasonable steps, based on the specific needs of the individual location, to safeguard employee security, including, but not limited to, establishing liaison with local police authorities, requesting the assignment of additional uniformed police in the area, improving lighting and fencing, and where available, utilizing mobile security force patrols.

## Summary

The employer must take location-specific reasonable steps to secure employees in parking areas and en route to and from them: police liaison, extra uniformed police, better lighting and fencing, and mobile security patrols where available. A "reasonable steps" standard keyed to each location's needs.

## Cross-union comparison

Nearly identical to the other crafts' parking-security clauses; the listed measures are the same shared catalogue.
