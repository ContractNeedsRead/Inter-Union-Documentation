# NRLCA §20.3 — Priority Parking

## Full text

<!-- p.96 -->
### Section 3. Priority Parking

Rural carrier vehicles used for mail delivery will be given priority parking over non-delivery official vehicles, customer vehicles and privately owned vehicles not used for the delivery of mail.

## Summary

Vehicles used for mail delivery — the rural carrier's POV among them — park ahead of non-delivery official vehicles, customer vehicles, and other private vehicles. A delivery-first priority rule unique in its reach to the rural POV fleet.

## Cross-union comparison

City carriers have no POV fleet to protect; the priority rule is the rural counterpart of the clerk and mail handler parking provisions but aimed squarely at delivery vehicles.
