# NRLCA §24.1 — Continuation of Benefits

## Full text

<!-- p.99 -->
### Section 1. Continuation of Benefits

Any employee on leave without pay to devote full or part-time service to the Union shall be credited with step increases as if the employee had been in a pay status. Retirement benefits will accrue on the basis of the employee’s step so attained, provided the employee makes contributions to the retirement fund in accordance with current procedure. Annual and sick leave will be earned in accordance with existing procedures based on hours worked.

## Summary

Employees on LWOP for union service keep step increases as if in pay status; retirement accrues on the attained step with employee contributions; and leave accrues by hours worked under existing procedures. Benefit continuity during union leave.

## Cross-union comparison

The step-credit and retirement-accrual device is the shared postal union-leave benefit across the agreements; the hours-worked leave accrual is the standard complement.
