# NRLCA §24.3 — Leave Replacement

## Full text

<!-- p.99 -->
### Section 3. Leave Replacement

In the event a leave replacement is not available on a rural route, the installation head will make every reasonable effort to secure a leave replacement.

## Summary

Where no leave replacement covers the rural route during union leave, the installation head must make every reasonable effort to find one. Backs the convention-leave right with a replacement-recruitment duty.

## Cross-union comparison

A rural-craft necessity — city installations do not depend on leave replacements the way rural routes do; the clause has no direct counterpart in the other agreements.
