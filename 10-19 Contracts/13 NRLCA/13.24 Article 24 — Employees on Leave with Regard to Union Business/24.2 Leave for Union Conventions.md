# NRLCA §24.2 — Leave for Union Conventions

## Full text

<!-- p.99 -->
### Section 2. Leave for Union Conventions

Employees will be granted annual leave or leave without pay at the election of the employee to attend National, State and Area Union Conventions and/or Conferences provided that a request for leave has been submitted by the employee to the installation head as soon as practicable and provided that approval of such leave does not seriously adversely affect the service needs of the installation.

## Summary

Employees may take annual leave or LWOP — their election — for national, state, and area union conventions and conferences, on as-soon-as-practicable request, unless approval would seriously harm installation service needs. The service-needs proviso is the only ground for denial.

## Cross-union comparison

Convention-leave clauses exist in every agreement with the same election and service-needs conditions; the rural version names national, state, and area levels without a delegate-number formula.
