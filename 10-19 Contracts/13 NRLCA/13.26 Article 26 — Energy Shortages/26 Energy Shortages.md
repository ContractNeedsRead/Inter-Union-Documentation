# NRLCA Article 26 — Energy Shortages

## Full text

<!-- p.100 -->
In the event of an energy crisis, the Employer shall make every reasonable attempt to secure a high priority from the appropriate Federal agency to obtain the fuel necessary for the satisfactory maintenance of postal operations. In such a case, or in the event of any serious widespread energy shortage, the Employer and the Union shall meet at the national level to discuss the problems and proposed solutions.

## Summary

In an energy crisis the employer must seek federal fuel priority for postal operations, and in any serious widespread shortage the parties meet at the national level over problems and solutions. A 1970s-era contingency clause preserved verbatim.

## Cross-union comparison

The other agreements carry their own energy-shortage clauses of the same vintage; the fuel-priority and national-meeting structure is the shared form.
