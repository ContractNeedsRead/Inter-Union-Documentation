# NRLCA §11.3 — Part-time Flexible Rural Carriers, and Substitute Carriers

## Full text

<!-- p.50 -->
### Section 3. Part-time Flexible Rural Carriers, and Substitute Carriers

- **A.** The holiday pay for a part-time flexible rural carrier or substitute rural carrier is included in the daily rate. For a part-time flexible rural carrier or substitute serving a six (6) day route, the daily rate for the route is determined by dividing the annual salary by 301; for serving a J route, the daily rate is determined by dividing the annual salary by 275; for serving a K route, the daily rate is determined by dividing the annual salary by 249.
- **B.** For a part-time flexible rural carrier or substitute who is compensated on an hourly basis, the hourly rate is determined by dividing the annual salary by 1,992.

## Summary

PTFs' and substitutes' holiday pay is folded into their daily rate: the rate is annual salary divided by 301 for six-day routes, 275 for J routes, and 249 for K routes. Hourly-paid PTFs and substitutes use the 1,992 divisor. They get no separate holiday pay because the daily rate already carries it.

## Cross-union comparison

The divisors (301/275/249/1,992) are the evaluated system's answer to holiday pay — no other craft computes holiday pay through route-type divisors. City and clerk PTFs instead receive holiday pay on an hours-worked basis under their own formulas.
