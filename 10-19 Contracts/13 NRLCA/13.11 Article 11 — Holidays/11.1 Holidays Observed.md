# NRLCA §11.1 — Holidays Observed

## Full text

<!-- p.49 -->
### Section 1. Holidays Observed

The following eleven (11) days shall be considered holidays for regular rural carriers:

- New Year’s Day
- Martin Luther King, Jr.’s Birthday
- Presidents’ Day
- Memorial Day
- Juneteenth National Independence Day
- Independence Day
- Labor Day
- Columbus Day
- Veterans’ Day
- Thanksgiving Day
- Christmas Day

## Summary

Lists the eleven holidays observed for regular rural carriers. The 2024 agreement's list includes Juneteenth National Independence Day, which earlier rural agreements lacked. The list tracks the standard federal and postal holiday calendar.

## Cross-union comparison

All four agreements observe the same eleven holidays; the rural list is identical in substance to the city carrier, clerk, and mail handler lists.
