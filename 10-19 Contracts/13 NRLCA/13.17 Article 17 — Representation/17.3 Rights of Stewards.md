# NRLCA §17.3 — Rights of Stewards

## Full text

<!-- p.90 -->
### Section 3. Rights of Stewards

When it is necessary for a steward to leave the work area to investigate and adjust grievances or to investigate a specific problem to determine whether to file a grievance, the steward shall request permission from the immediate supervisor, and such request shall not be unreasonably denied. In the event the duties require the steward to leave the work area and enter another area within the post office, the steward must also receive permission from the supervisor from the other area the steward wishes to enter, and such request shall not be unreasonably denied.

The steward or other Union representative properly certified in accordance with Section 2. above may request and shall obtain access through the appropriate supervisor to review the documents, files and other records necessary for processing a grievance or determining if a grievance exists and shall have the right to interview the aggrieved employee(s), supervisors and witnesses during working hours. Such requests shall not be unreasonably denied.

If an employee requests a steward or Union representative to be present during the course of an interrogation by the Inspection Service, such request will be granted. All polygraph tests will continue to be on a voluntary basis.

## Summary

Stewards may leave their work area to investigate and adjust grievances with supervisor permission that cannot be unreasonably denied, including entry into other work areas. Certified representatives get access to documents, files, and records needed for a grievance and may interview grievants, supervisors, and witnesses during working hours. Inspection Service interrogations must honor a request for steward presence, and polygraphs stay voluntary — the rural articulation of Weingarten rights.

## Cross-union comparison

The access, interview, and Inspection-Service rights track the other agreements' steward-rights clauses; the polygraph-voluntary sentence is the shared legacy language across all four.
