# NRLCA §17.1 — Stewards

## Full text

<!-- p.89 -->
### Section 1. Stewards

Stewards may be designated for the purpose of investigating, presenting and adjusting grievances.

## Summary

One sentence: stewards exist to investigate, present, and adjust grievances. The designation machinery and the number formula follow in §17.2.

## Cross-union comparison

The functional definition of a steward is identical across the four agreements; the differences live in the appointment formulas each article carries.
