# NRLCA §1.5 — New Positions

## Full text

<!-- p.2 -->
### Section 5. New Positions

Each newly created position shall be assigned by the Employer to the national craft unit most appropriate for such position within thirty (30) days after its creation. The Union shall be notified promptly regarding assignments made under this provision.

## Summary

New positions must be assigned to the most appropriate national craft unit within 30 days of creation, with prompt notice to the union. This is the unit-boundary mechanism that routes new work among the four crafts.

## Cross-union comparison

The same 30-day craft-assignment device appears in the other agreements; inter-craft disputes over new work are resolved under each craft's Article 1 and, where they persist, through national-level proceedings.
