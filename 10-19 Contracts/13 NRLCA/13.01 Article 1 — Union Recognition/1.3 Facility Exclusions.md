# NRLCA §1.3 — Facility Exclusions

## Full text

<!-- p.1 -->
### Section 3. Facility Exclusions

This Agreement does not apply to employees who work in other Employer facilities which are not engaged in customer services and mail processing, previously understood and expressed by the parties to mean mail processing and delivery, including but not limited to Headquarters, Area Offices, Information Service Centers, William F. Bolger Center for Leadership Development,
<!-- p.2 -->
Technical Training Center, Field Centers, Material Distribution Centers, Mail Equipment Shops, and Mail Transport Equipment Centers/Repair Centers.

## Summary

Carves non-mail facilities out of the Agreement's reach: Headquarters, Area Offices, Information Service Centers, the Bolger Center, Technical Training Center, Field Centers, Material Distribution Centers, Mail Equipment Shops, and Mail Transport Equipment Centers/Repair Centers. Coverage follows facilities engaged in mail processing and delivery.

## Cross-union comparison

Each agreement carries its own facility-exclusion list; the rural list tracks the same Headquarters/Area/ISC set the other crafts exclude, plus the rural-specific MTESC/Repair Centers.
