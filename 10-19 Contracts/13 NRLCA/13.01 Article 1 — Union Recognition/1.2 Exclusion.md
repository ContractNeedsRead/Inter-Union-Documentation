# NRLCA §1.2 — Exclusion

## Full text

<!-- p.1 -->
### Section 2. Exclusion

The employee group set forth in Section 1 above does not include, and this Agreement does not apply to:

- **a.** Managerial and supervisory personnel;
- **b.** Professional employees;
- **c.** Employees engaged in personnel work in other than a purely non-confidential clerical capacity;
- **d.** Security guards as defined in Public Law 91-375, 1201 (2);
- **e.** All Postal Inspection Service employees; and
- **f.** All other bargaining unit craft employees.

## Summary

Lists who is outside the rural carrier unit: managers and supervisors, professionals, confidential personnel workers, security guards under P.L. 91-375, Postal Inspection Service employees, and members of other bargaining unit crafts. The unit is the rural carrier craft alone — §1.2.f keeps the other three crafts out.

## Cross-union comparison

Mirrors the standard craft-unit exclusions in the other agreements; the P.L. 91-375 security-guard exclusion and the "all other bargaining unit craft employees" carve-out are the same devices the NALC, APWU, and NPMHU articles use.
