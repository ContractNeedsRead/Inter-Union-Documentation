# NRLCA §1.4 — Definition

## Full text

<!-- p.2 -->
### Section 4. Definition

Subject to the foregoing exclusions and provisions, this Agreement shall be applicable to all employees covered by Section 1 above at all present and subsequently acquired installations, facilities, and operations of the Employer, wherever located.

## Summary

Subject to §§1.2–1.3, the Agreement applies to all unit employees at every present and future Postal Service installation, facility, and operation, wherever located. The clause makes coverage follow the facility and the employee, not the location.
