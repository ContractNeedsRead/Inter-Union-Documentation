# NRLCA §19.1 — Statement of Principle

## Full text

<!-- p.94 -->
### Section 1. Statement of Principle

Those parts of all handbooks, manuals, and published regulations of the Postal Service, that directly relate to wages, hours or working conditions, as they apply to employees covered by this Agreement, shall contain nothing that conflicts with this Agreement, and shall be continued in effect except that the Employer shall have the right to make changes that are not inconsistent with this Agreement and that are fair, reasonable,
<!-- p.95 -->
and equitable. This includes, but is not limited to, the Employee and Labor Relations Manual (ELM) and Handbook F-21, Time and Attendance.

## Summary

Handbooks, manuals, and published regulations touching wages, hours, or working conditions may not conflict with the Agreement, and continue in effect except for employer changes that are consistent with the Agreement and fair, reasonable, and equitable. The ELM and Handbook F-21 are expressly within the clause.

## Cross-union comparison

This is the shared Article 19 machinery of every postal agreement; the rural clause names the same two instruments (ELM, F-21) the other crafts' clauses do.
