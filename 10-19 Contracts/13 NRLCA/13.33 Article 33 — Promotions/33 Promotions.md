# NRLCA Article 33 — Promotions

## Full text

<!-- p.126 -->
The Employer agrees to place particular emphasis upon career advancement opportunities. First opportunity for promotions will be given to qualified career employees. The Employer will assist employees to improve their own skills through training and self-help programs.

## Summary

Promotion policy: emphasis on career advancement, first opportunity to qualified career employees, and employer assistance through training and self-help programs. The career-first preference in promotions.

## Cross-union comparison

The career-employee-first preference is common to the postal agreements; the rural version is a single un-sectioned article.
