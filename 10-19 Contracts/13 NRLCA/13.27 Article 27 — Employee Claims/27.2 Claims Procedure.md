# NRLCA §27.2 — Claims Procedure

## Full text

<!-- p.101 -->
### Section 2. Claims Procedure

Claims should be documented, if possible, and submitted with recommendations by the Union steward to the Employer at the local level. The Employer will submit the claim, with the employee’s and the steward’s recommendation, to the district office for determination. An adverse determination may be appealed to Step 3 of the grievance-arbitration procedure.

## Summary

Claims are documented where possible and filed locally through the steward with recommendations; the employer forwards them to the district office for decision. Adverse determinations appeal to Step 3 of the grievance-arbitration procedure — skipping Steps 1 and 2.

## Cross-union comparison

The Step 3 entry point for claim appeals is the standard device across the postal agreements' employee-claims articles.
