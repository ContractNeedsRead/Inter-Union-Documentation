# NRLCA §32.2 — Advance Notice

## Full text

<!-- p.125 -->
### Section 2. Advance Notice

The Employer will give advance notification to the Union at the national level when subcontracting which will have a significant impact on bargaining unit work is being considered and will meet to consider the Union’s views on minimizing such impact. No final decision on whether or not such work will be contracted out will be made until the matter is discussed with the Union.

## Summary

Significant-impact subcontracting requires advance national-level notice and a meeting on minimizing impact; no final decision until discussion. A discussion-before-decision gate, not a veto.

## Cross-union comparison

The notice-and-discussion gate is the shared postal subcontracting device across the four agreements.
