# NRLCA §32.1 — Statement of Principle

## Full text

<!-- p.125 -->
### Section 1. Statement of Principle

The Employer will give due consideration to public interest, cost, efficiency, availability of equipment, and qualification of employees when evaluating the need to subcontract.

## Summary

Subcontracting decisions must weigh public interest, cost, efficiency, equipment availability, and employee qualifications. The consideration list that frames the rest of Article 32.

## Cross-union comparison

The same consideration list opens each agreement's subcontracting article.
