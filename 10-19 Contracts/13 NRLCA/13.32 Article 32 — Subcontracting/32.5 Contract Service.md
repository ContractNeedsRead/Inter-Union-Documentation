# NRLCA §32.5 — Contract Service

## Full text

<!-- p.125 -->
### Section 5. Contract Service

No expansion of the Employer’s current national policy on the use of contract service in lieu of rural carriers will be made except through the provisions of this Article, which are intended to be controlling. The parties recognize that individual problems in this area may be made the subject of a grievance under the provisions of Article 15.

## Summary

Contract service in place of rural carriers cannot expand except through Article 32, which controls; individual disputes are grievable under Article 15. Freezes the contracting status quo.

## Cross-union comparison

The no-expansion-except-this-Article formulation is shared with the other agreements' contracting clauses.
