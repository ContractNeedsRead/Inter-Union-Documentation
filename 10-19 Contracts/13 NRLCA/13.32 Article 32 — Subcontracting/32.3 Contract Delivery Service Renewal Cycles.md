# NRLCA §32.3 — Contract Delivery Service Renewal Cycles

## Full text

<!-- p.125 -->
### Section 3. Contract Delivery Service Renewal Cycles

The Employer agrees to provide the Union with a schedule of Contract Delivery Service Renewal Cycles as issued.

## Summary

The employer supplies the union with each CDS Renewal Cycle schedule as issued. Transparency feeding the §32.4 cost comparisons.

## Cross-union comparison

A rural-specific information duty tied to highway contract routes; the other agreements' subcontracting articles lack the CDS-cycle device.
