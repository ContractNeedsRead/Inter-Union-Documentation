# NRLCA §29.3 — Initial Certification

## Full text

<!-- p.104 -->
### Section 3. Initial Certification

An employee shall receive Vehicle Familiarization and Safe Operation Certification when the employee has a valid State driver’s license, has satisfactorily completed the Postal Service driver’s training and has a satisfactory driving history.

## Summary

Vehicle Familiarization and Safe Operation Certification issues on three conditions: valid state license, satisfactory completion of postal driver training, and a satisfactory driving history. The certification baseline for rural-carrier driving privileges.

## Cross-union comparison

The certification device is rural-specific — the POV-driving craft requires its own familiarization certificate; other crafts certify on postal vehicles alone.
