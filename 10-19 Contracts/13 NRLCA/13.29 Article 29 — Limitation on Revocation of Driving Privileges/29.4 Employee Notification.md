# NRLCA §29.4 — Employee Notification

## Full text

<!-- p.104 -->
### Section 4. Employee Notification

An employee must inform the supervisor immediately of the revocation or suspension of the employee’s State driver’s license.

## Summary

Employees must immediately report any state driver's license revocation or suspension to their supervisor. The employee-side duty that pairs with the employer's revocation machinery.

## Cross-union comparison

A duty unique to the license-dependent rural craft; city carriers carry the same practical duty under their own driving rules.
