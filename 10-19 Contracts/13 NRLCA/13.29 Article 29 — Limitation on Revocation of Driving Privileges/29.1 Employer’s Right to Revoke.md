# NRLCA §29.1 — Employer’s Right to Revoke

## Full text

<!-- p.103 -->
### Section 1. Employer’s Right to Revoke

An employee’s driving privileges may be revoked or suspended when the on-duty record shows that the employee is an unsafe driver.

## Summary

Driving privileges may be revoked or suspended only when the on-duty record shows unsafe driving. The on-duty-record trigger is the ceiling on the employer's right — §29.2 defines what counts.

## Cross-union comparison

The on-duty-record standard for POV-driving employees has no city-carrier counterpart in the same form; city carriers face license-based revocation under their own rules.
