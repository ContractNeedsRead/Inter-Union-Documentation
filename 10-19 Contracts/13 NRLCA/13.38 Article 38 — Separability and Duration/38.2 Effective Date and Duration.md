# NRLCA §38.2 — Effective Date and Duration

## Full text

<!-- p.130 -->
### Section 2. Effective Date and Duration

Unless otherwise provided, this Agreement shall be effective on June 16, 2025, and shall remain in full force and effect to and including 12 midnight May 20, 2027, and unless either party desires to terminate or modify it, for successive annual periods. The party demanding such termination or modification must serve written notice of such intent to the other party, not less than 90 or more than 120 days before the expiration date of the Agreement.

## Summary

Effective June 16, 2025 through midnight May 20, 2027, renewing for successive annual periods unless either party serves written notice of termination or modification 90–120 days before expiration. The evergreen-duration clause closing the Agreement.

## Cross-union comparison

The same effective-date/duration structure with the 90–120-day notice window closes every postal agreement.
