# NRLCA §18.3 — Union Liability

## Full text

<!-- p.94 -->
### Section 3. Union Liability

It is agreed that the Union or its local Unions which comply with the requirements of this Article shall not be liable for the unauthorized action of their members or other postal employees.

## Summary

A union that complies with the Article's duties is not liable for unauthorized wildcat action by members or other postal employees. Compliance with §§18.1–18.2 buys the liability shield.

## Cross-union comparison

The compliance-based liability shield is common to all four no-strike articles — the same conditional immunity device throughout.
