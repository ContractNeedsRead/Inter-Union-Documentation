# NRLCA §18.4 — Legal Impact

## Full text

<!-- p.94 -->
### Section 4. Legal Impact

The parties agree that the provisions of this Article shall not be used in any way to defeat any current or future legal action involving the constitutionality of existing or future legislation prohibiting federal employees from engaging in strike actions. The parties further agree that the obligations undertaken in this Article are in no way contingent upon the final determination of such constitutional issues.

## Summary

The no-strike article cannot be used against legal challenges to the constitutionality of the federal strike ban, and the union's obligations under the article do not depend on how those constitutional questions are resolved. The contract obligation stands regardless of the statute's fate.

## Cross-union comparison

This clause appears in substance in the other agreements as well — a shared hedge preserving both the statutory challenge and the contractual commitment.
