# NRLCA §18.1 — Statement of Principle

## Full text

<!-- p.94 -->
### Section 1. Statement of Principle

The Union on behalf of its members agrees that it will not call or sanction a strike or slowdown.

## Summary

The union commits, on behalf of its members, not to call or sanction strikes or slowdowns. The no-strike commitment is the contractual mirror of the statutory ban on federal-employee strikes.

## Cross-union comparison

Verbatim-identical to the no-strike commitments in the other three agreements — this is one of the most uniform clauses in postal bargaining.
